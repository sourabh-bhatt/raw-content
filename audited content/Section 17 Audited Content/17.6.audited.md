# Property Management in Real Estate

<!-- In the realm of real estate, you'll encounter the vital role of **property management**. This involves the **leasing**, **managing**, and **maintenance** of property on behalf of others. Virginia requires property managers to hold a **real estate license** to operate legally. -->

**Property management** involves the **leasing**, **managing**, and **maintenance** of property on behalf of others. Virginia requires property managers to hold a **real estate license** to operate legally.

## Legal Responsibilities of a Property Manager

Property managers must ensure all properties follow these laws:

- **Fair Housing Laws**
- **Equal Credit Opportunity Act (ECOA)**
- **Lead Based Paint Hazard Reduction Act**

Following these laws is required and helps maintain safe and fair housing practices.

## Property Management Agreements

<!-- Let's discuss the **Property Management Agreements** you'll often work with: -->

**Property Management Agreements** establish working relationships between property managers and owners:

<!-- - As a property manager, you enter into a **general agency relationship** with the property owner. This means you have broad authority to act on behalf of the owner in managing the property. -->
- Property managers enter into **general agency relationships** with property owners. This provides broad authority to act on behalf of owners in managing properties.
- This relationship differs from a **listing agreement**, where the broker acts as a **special agent** with a narrowly defined role, typically tasked with executing a specific duty such as selling the property.
