# Understanding Ordinary Care in Property Management

<!-- In the realm of real estate, especially when managing properties on behalf of landlords, it's crucial to grasp the concept of **Ordinary Care**. -->

Property managers must understand the concept of **Ordinary Care** when managing properties for landlords.

## What is Ordinary Care?

<!-- **Ordinary Care** means that your judgment and actions should mirror what a *reasonable person* would do in similar circumstances. When faced with a decision or action, you should take a step back and ask yourself, *"What would a prudent and cautious individual decide to do right now?"* -->

**Ordinary Care** means that judgment and actions should mirror what a *reasonable person* would do in similar circumstances. Property managers should consider what a prudent and cautious individual would decide in any given situation.

## Application of Ordinary Care

- Helps protect your success and reputation as a property manager. -->

- Ensures choices and actions align with expected care levels within the real estate industry.
- Protects professional reputation and standing as a property manager.
