# Home Warranty Plans in Resale Home Transactions

Buyers and sellers of resale homes should understand **home warranty plans**. These plans offer peace of mind by covering major appliances and systems in the home. **Buyers** can purchase them or **sellers** can provide them.

## Cost and Deductibles
- The typical cost for these plans ranges from **$300 to $600** for the initial year.
- Renewal options are available, usually at a higher rate.
- The **deductible** - the amount paid out of pocket for a service call - varies across plans.

## Scope of Coverage
Coverage can differ but generally includes:

- Built-in appliances (note that some may be excluded)
- Air conditioning and heating systems
- Plumbing
- Interior electrical and telephone wiring

Additional amenities such as pools and spas may be covered for an extra premium.

## Considerations and Limitations
<!-- - Understand both the **coverage** and **limitations** of any home warranty plan under consideration. -->
- Home warranty plans have both **coverage** and **limitations** that require review.
- These plans typically do **not** cover replacements for issues that can be repaired.
- There are restrictions regarding which **service providers** must be used.
- **Caps on coverage** may result in only partial payment for major repairs.

### Example:
A pipe leak might be covered under the plan, but the **excavation costs** required to access that pipe would not be covered.
