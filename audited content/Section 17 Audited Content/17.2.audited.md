# Property Management Agreements: Roles, Responsibilities, and Key Elements

<!-- In your journey to effectively manage properties, understanding the roles and responsibilities outlined in **property management agreements** is crucial. As a **property manager**, you engage in leasing, managing, and maintaining properties on behalf of owners. In Virginia, you must be licensed as a **real estate broker** to perform these duties. -->

**Property management agreements** outline roles and responsibilities for effective property management. **Property managers** engage in leasing, managing, and maintaining properties on behalf of owners. In Virginia, property managers must be licensed as **real estate brokers** to perform these duties.

## Agency Relationship

<!-- Your relationship with property owners is typically defined as a **general agency relationship**. This differs from a listing agreement where a broker acts as a **special agent** for a specific task. It is essential to formalize your working relationship with property owners through a **written property management agreement**. This document clearly states your rights and obligations, ensuring there is no ambiguity about your authority. -->

Property managers typically establish **general agency relationships** with property owners. This differs from listing agreements where brokers act as **special agents** for specific tasks. **Written property management agreements** formalize working relationships with property owners. These documents clearly state rights and obligations, ensuring no ambiguity about authority.

## Responsibilities Under the Agreement

<!-- Under such an agreement, your responsibilities are comprehensive and aimed at keeping the property compliant with relevant laws such as the **Fair Housing Act**, **Equal Credit Opportunity Act**, and **Lead-Based Paint Disclosure requirements**. Some of the key duties you'll handle include: -->

Property management agreements establish comprehensive responsibilities aimed at keeping properties compliant with relevant laws such as the **Fair Housing Act**, **Equal Credit Opportunity Act**, and **Lead-Based Paint Disclosure requirements**. Key duties include:

- **Collecting rent** from tenants
- **Managing security deposits**
- **Evicting tenants** when necessary
- **Coordinating and funding repairs**
- **Hiring staff** for property maintenance
- **Screening potential tenants**
- **Negotiating lease terms** with tenants

## Compensation

Property managers are typically compensated through:

- A **flat fee**, or
- A **commission** based on the rental income generated by the property.

## Property Owner's Responsibilities

Property owners are expected to:

<!-- - Cover all **expenses incurred** in the management of the property, including reimbursing you for any costs you've fronted. -->
- Cover all **expenses incurred** in property management, including reimbursing managers for fronted costs.
<!-- - Provide **legal protection** against actions arising from your execution of duties under the management agreement. -->
- Provide **legal protection** against actions arising from manager execution of duties under the management agreement.

## Duration and Termination of Agreement

Most management agreements are set for a **specific period**. However, these agreements often contain clauses allowing for **early termination** under certain conditions, providing a clear exit strategy if needed.

These elements of property management agreements provide the framework to navigate responsibilities and protect interests in effective property management.
