# Leverage in Real Estate Investment

<!-- **Leverage** involves utilizing **borrowed money** to finance your investment, much like getting a **mortgage** to buy a property. When you opt for a higher proportion of borrowed funds in relation to your own investment, you're considered to be *"highly leveraged."* -->
**Leverage** involves utilizing **borrowed money** to finance an investment, much like getting a **mortgage** to buy a property. A higher proportion of borrowed funds in relation to the investor's own funds creates a *"highly leveraged"* position. 

## Key Concepts of Leverage

- A higher leverage means making a smaller **down payment**.
- This results in a higher **Loan to Value (LTV) ratio**.
- The goal is to earn a **return** that surpasses both the original purchase price of the property and the **costs associated with borrowing** the funds.

## Benefits of Leverage

<!-- - Amplifies your investment potential.
- Allows you to aim for higher profits with a lower initial outlay of your own funds. -->
- Amplifies investment potential.
- Allows higher profits with a lower initial outlay of personal funds.

Example: If you purchase a property with a 20% down payment and finance the remaining 80%, your LTV ratio is 80%. If the property's value increases and your return exceeds the mortgage interest and other borrowing costs, your leveraged investment has successfully generated profit beyond your initial investment.
