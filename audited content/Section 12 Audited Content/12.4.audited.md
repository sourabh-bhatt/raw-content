# Net Operating Income (NOI) in Real Estate

<!-- To understand **Net Operating Income (NOI)**, envision evaluating a property's financial performance. The process involves two main components: -->
**Net Operating Income (NOI)** measures a property's financial performance. The process involves two main components:

## Effective Gross Income

- This represents all the money the property generates.
- It is not simply the sum of rent payments but the total income before expenses.

## Operating Expenses

- These are the costs required to keep the property running smoothly.
- Examples include **maintenance**, **management fees**, and **utility expenses**.

## Calculating Net Operating Income

<!-- Your goal is to determine the **NOI** by subtracting the operating expenses from the effective gross income: -->
Determine the **NOI** by subtracting the operating expenses from the effective gross income:


NOI = Effective Gross Income - Operating Expenses


The resulting **NOI** reflects the property's actual income after all necessary expenses have been paid, but before any debts or financing costs are settled. 

<!-- Understanding and calculating **NOI** accurately is vital because it indicates the property's financial health and its investment potential. -->
Calculating **NOI** accurately indicates the property's financial health and its investment potential.
