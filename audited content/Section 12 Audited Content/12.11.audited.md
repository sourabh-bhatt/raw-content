## Key Financial Calculations

<!-- Here's a fundamental breakdown of the equations you'll rely on: -->
Fundamental equations for income property analysis:

- **Annual Effective Gross Income**: Calculate by subtracting any vacancies and credit losses from the Annual Gross Income.
- **Annual NOI**: Subtract Annual Operating Expenses from the Annual Effective Gross Income.
- **Property Value**: Divide the Annual NOI by the Annual Rate of Return.
  - Conversely, multiplying the Property Value by the Annual Rate of Return gives the Annual NOI.
- **Annual Rate of Return**: Calculate by dividing the Annual NOI by the property's Value.

Example: Assessing the value of an office building based on its income. The building generates $156,000 in gross income with annual expenses of $43,000, using a 7% capitalization rate.

Steps:

1. Calculate the annual NOI:
   - $156,000 - $43,000 = $113,000
2. Convert the capitalization rate to a decimal:
   - 7% = 0.07
3. Divide the annual NOI by the capitalization rate to find the property value:
