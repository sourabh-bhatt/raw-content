# Investment vs. Personal Use in Real Estate

<!-- When considering **purchasing property**, it's important to understand the different motivations depending on your purpose. -->
**Purchasing property** involves different motivations depending on the purpose.

## Investor Perspective

- Focus on the property's potential to **generate income** in the future.
- Viewing the purchase not just as acquiring a **physical asset**, but as investing in the promise of **profitable returns** down the line.

## Personal Use Perspective

- Motivated by **immediate tax advantages** associated with **homeownership**.
- Expectation that the property's value will **appreciate over time**, thereby increasing personal **wealth**.
