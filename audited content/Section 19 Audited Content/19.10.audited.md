# Understanding Prorations in Real Estate Transactions

When finalizing the sale of a property, certain expenses need to be divided between the **seller** and the **buyer** as of the **settlement date**. These expenses may include:

- **Property taxes**
- **Utilities**
- **Special assessments**
- **HOA fees**

## The Prorations Clause

The **prorations clause** outlines how these costs are adjusted so that each party pays their share up until the point of **ownership transfer**. 

Example: If the property taxes were paid by the seller at the beginning of the year, but the buyer purchases the house midway through the year, the buyer will be responsible for covering the tax expense for the remainder of the year. The prorations clause ensures this calculation is clear and agreed upon, making the settlement process smoother for both parties.
