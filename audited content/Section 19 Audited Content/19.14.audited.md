# Escrow and Closing in Real Estate Transactions

**Escrow and closing** represents the point in the transaction where **ownership transfers from seller to buyer through a neutral third party**, in return for the buyer's payment of the **purchase price**.

## Timing of Closing
Typically, closing takes place at least **30-40 days** after both buyer and seller have signed a **real estate sales contract**. This gap allows enough time for both parties to fulfill certain mandatory activities required before the exchange of title for payment can occur.

**In Virginia, real estate closings are conducted by attorneys**. Key points include:

- **Virginia law** requires that only licensed attorneys conduct real estate closings.
- The attorney serves as the settlement agent and handles the transfer of funds and documents.
- Settlement agents and transaction parties must adhere to specific laws and regulations.

These laws and regulations include:

- Various **reporting requirements**
- Procedural steps
- The need to **disclose certain information**
