# Title Closing and Disclosure Responsibilities

<!-- In **title closing and settlement**, it's important to understand how **disclosure information** is handled. -->

In **title closing and settlement**, **disclosure information** requires careful handling.

### Seller's Duty to Disclose Material Changes

- If circumstances change and previously disclosed information becomes inaccurate, sellers are *not automatically at fault*.
- However, **sellers** must disclose *any material changes* as soon as they occur.
- Disclosure should happen *up to settlement*.
