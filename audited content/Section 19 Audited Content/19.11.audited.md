# Calculating the Amount Needed at Closing

<!-- When you're assisting a client with understanding how much they need to bring to the closing table, it's important to break down the numbers in an accessible way. Let's go through a typical scenario that shines a light on the arithmetic involved. -->

Calculating how much clients need to bring to the closing table requires breaking down the numbers clearly. The following scenario demonstrates the arithmetic involved.

## Scenario

- **Purchase price:** $247,000
- **Loan amount:** 90% of the purchase price
- **Closing costs:** $1,225
- **Deposit already paid:** $5,000

## Calculation Formula

<!-- To calculate the total amount your client needs to bring to the closing, use the following formula: -->

To calculate the total amount clients need to bring to the closing, use the following formula:

**(Purchase price) - (Amount financed) + (Closing costs) - (Deposit already paid) = (Amount needed at closing)**
