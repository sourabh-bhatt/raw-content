# Understanding the Closing Disclosure

<!-- When you're preparing to finalize a real estate transaction, it's important to familiarize yourself with the **Closing Disclosure**. This crucial document becomes available for your inspection **three business days before the settlement date**. It is your right to review it ahead of time. -->

The **Closing Disclosure** is available for inspection **three business days before the settlement date** in real estate transactions. Borrowers have the right to review it ahead of time.

## Purpose and Preparation of the Closing Disclosure

The Closing Disclosure thoroughly itemizes the services provided throughout the transaction and the corresponding fees that have been charged. This form is prepared by the **lender**, who must provide it to the borrower.

<!-- - The Closing Disclosure must be delivered or mailed to you **at least three business days before the settlement takes place**. -->
- The Closing Disclosure must be delivered or mailed to borrowers **at least three business days before the settlement takes place**.
<!-- - If the transaction concludes **without a formal settlement meeting**, the **escrow agent** is responsible for mailing you the Closing Disclosure **after** the transaction has been finalized. -->
- If the transaction concludes **without a formal settlement meeting**, the **escrow agent** is responsible for mailing borrowers the Closing Disclosure **after** the transaction has been finalized.
<!-- - In such cases, you will **not** have the opportunity to inspect the document before settlement. -->
- In such cases, borrowers will **not** have the opportunity to inspect the document before settlement.
