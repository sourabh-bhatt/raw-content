# Closing: Taking Possession and Transferring Ownership

<!-- **Closing** is the phase where you, as a *buyer*, formally take possession of the property. This is the crucial moment when the *seller* hands over the **title** to you or a neutral third party, solidifying the **transfer of ownership**. -->

**Closing** is the phase where *buyers* formally take possession of the property. This is the moment when the *seller* hands over the **title** to buyers or a neutral third party, solidifying the **transfer of ownership**.

<!-- In return, you complete this transaction by paying the agreed-upon **purchase price** for the property. Understanding this process is vital because it marks the official change of ownership and ensures that all financial obligations are met, allowing you to take full control of your new property. -->

In return, buyers complete this transaction by paying the agreed-upon **purchase price** for the property. This process marks the official change of ownership and ensures that all financial obligations are met, allowing buyers to take full control of their new property.
