# Duty of Accounting in Real Estate

<!-- When you're engaging with clients, it's crucial to understand the significance of maintaining **accurate and up-to-date accounts**. When engaging with clients, agents must understand maintaining **accurate and up-to-date accounts**. This responsibility, known as the **duty of accounting**, applies not only while actively working with a client but also **during and after the agency relationship has concluded or terminated**.

## What the Duty of Accounting Entails

<!-- From the moment you start working with a client until well after your professional relationship ends, you are responsible for: -->

From the moment agents start working with a client until well after their professional relationship ends, they are responsible for:

- Managing and reporting any **financial transactions** related to that client's affairs accurately.
<!-- - Keeping detailed records of all **expenditures**, **receipts**, and any transactions you conduct on their behalf. -->
- Keeping detailed records of all **expenditures**, **receipts**, and any transactions conducted on their behalf.

## Accounting Requirements

<!-- - Ensures you safeguard your **professional integrity**. -->
<!-- - Protects your clients' interests. -->
<!-- - Fosters **trust and confidence** between you and your clients. -->

- Ensures agents safeguard their **professional integrity**.
- Protects clients' interests.
- Fosters **trust and confidence** between agents and their clients.

This meticulous approach to financial management builds a **reputable career in real estate**. Agent commitment to **transparency** and **accuracy** creates professional relationships.
