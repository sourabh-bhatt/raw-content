# Understanding Active Status in Real Estate

<!-- When you're stepping into the world of real estate as a **broker** or **salesperson**, it's important to understand what being in an **"Active Status"** means. **Brokers** and **salespersons** in real estate must understand what being in **"Active Status"** means. Being in active status allows legal engagement in the **regulated activities** that are part of real estate brokerage. This distinction separates those who are currently allowed to perform brokerage activities from those who are not, due to various reasons such as not meeting the required **certifications** or **education**.

## Maintaining Active Status

<!-- To maintain an active status, there are specific requirements you must meet, which include: -->

To maintain active status, specific requirements must be met, which include:

- Continuing education
- License renewal every two years
- Maintaining errors and omissions insurance

<!-- These requirements can vary between **active** and **inactive licenses**. Understanding and adhering to these prerequisites ensures that you remain **compliant** and capable of performing your role effectively in the real estate industry. -->

These requirements can vary between **active** and **inactive licenses**. Understanding and adhering to these prerequisites ensures **compliance** and capability to perform roles effectively in the real estate industry.
