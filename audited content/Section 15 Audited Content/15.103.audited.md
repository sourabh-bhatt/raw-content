# Net Listings

## Understanding Net Listings

<!-- When engaging in a **net listing**, you're entering into a specific type of **agency agreement** with a seller where your role as a **real estate agent** is to find a buyer for their property. In this arrangement, the seller agrees to pay you not a fixed commission, but rather any amount above the net price the seller wants to receive. A **net listing** is a specific type of **agency agreement** with a seller where the agent's role is to find a buyer for their property. In this arrangement, the seller agrees to pay not a fixed commission, but rather any amount above the net price the seller wants to receive. If the agent sells the house for more than the amount the seller has specified, the excess belongs to the agent.

This approach allows agents to maximize earnings by selling the property for as much as possible. However, net listings carry **ethical concerns** because of the potential **conflicts of interest** and the temptation to prioritize personal gains over the best interests of the client. As a result, net listing agreements are illegal in Virginia.
