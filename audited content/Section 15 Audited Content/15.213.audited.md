# Conflicts of Interest in Real Estate Transactions

<!-- When you're handling real estate transactions, it's crucial to **steer clear of conflicts of interest**. Whether you're a salesperson or a broker, and regardless of whether your license is active or inactive, this applies to you. -->

When handling real estate transactions, agents must **steer clear of conflicts of interest**. Whether licensed as a salesperson or a broker, and regardless of whether the license is active or inactive, this applies to all licensees.

## What Constitutes a Conflict of Interest?

<!-- - **Undisclosed Dual Agency**: You must not represent both the buyer and seller in a transaction without making it *crystal clear* to both parties and obtaining their *explicit written consent*. Engaging in undisclosed dual agency is strictly prohibited. -->

- **Undisclosed Dual Agency**: Agents must not represent both the buyer and seller in a transaction without making it clear to both parties and obtaining their explicit written consent. Engaging in undisclosed dual agency is strictly prohibited.

<!-- - **Working for Another Real Estate Firm Without Permission**: You cannot work for another real estate firm without first obtaining *written permission*. Loyalty to your brokerage firm is paramount, and moonlighting without consent can create serious conflicts. -->

- **Working for Another Real Estate Firm Without Permission**: Agents cannot work for another real estate firm without first obtaining written permission. Loyalty to the brokerage firm is required, and moonlighting without consent can create serious conflicts.

<!-- - **Providing Real Estate Services Outside Your Brokerage Firm**: All real estate services you offer should be conducted under the umbrella of your brokerage firm. Providing services outside of your brokerage is another form of conflict of interest to avoid. -->

- **Providing Real Estate Services Outside Brokerage Firm**: All real estate services should be conducted under the umbrella of the brokerage firm. Providing services outside of the brokerage is another form of conflict of interest to avoid.

## Avoiding Conflicts of Interest

<!-- Understanding and avoiding these conflicts is not just about following the rules. It is about ensuring **transparency**, fostering **trust**, and maintaining **professionalism** in all your real estate dealings. Your reputation and integrity in this business are invaluable. -->

Understanding and avoiding these conflicts ensures **transparency**, fosters **trust**, and maintains **professionalism** in real estate dealings.
