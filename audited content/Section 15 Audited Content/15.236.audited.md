# Limited Service Agent in Brokerage and Agency Contracts

<!-- When you work with **brokerage and agency contracts**, you need to understand the concept of a **Limited Service Agent**. This type of agent works under a **brokerage agreement** for a **residential transaction**, but with a unique twist. -->

When working with **brokerage and agency contracts**, understanding the concept of a **Limited Service Agent** matters. This type of agent works under a **brokerage agreement** for a **residential transaction**, but with a unique twist.

## What is a Limited Service Agent?

<!-- A Limited Service Agent is different from a standard agent. This agent is hired to perform only specific duties. The agent does not perform all the duties that a full-service agent would perform. This arrangement is clearly outlined in the brokerage agreement between you (as the agent) and your client. -->

A Limited Service Agent is different from a standard agent. This agent is hired to perform only specific duties. The agent does not perform all the duties that a full-service agent would perform. This arrangement is clearly outlined in the brokerage agreement between the agent and their client.

## Responsibilities and Obligations

<!-- - Your duties as a Limited Service Agent are defined by what has been agreed to in the brokerage agreement.
- Duties can vary significantly based on the needs and expectations of your client. These are documented in your agreement.
- You still have certain **disclosure obligations mandated by law**. These apply even with the limitations on your responsibilities.
- These legal disclosure requirements are *non-negotiable*. You must follow them regardless of what your brokerage agreement says. -->

- Agent duties as a Limited Service Agent are defined by what has been agreed to in the brokerage agreement.
- Duties can vary significantly based on the needs and expectations of the client. These are documented in the agreement.
- Agents still have certain **disclosure obligations mandated by law**. These apply even with the limitations on their responsibilities.
- These legal disclosure requirements are *non-negotiable*. Agents must follow them regardless of what their brokerage agreement says.

## Key Points to Remember

<!-- - The role of a Limited Service Agent requires you to understand the *specific nature* of your duties. These duties are defined by your brokerage agreement.
- You must also have a thorough knowledge of the *legal disclosure requirements* you are expected to fulfill.
- You must balance your contract duties and legal duties. This is essential for working effectively and ethically in this specialized area of real estate. -->

- The role of a Limited Service Agent requires agents to understand the *specific nature* of their duties. These duties are defined by their brokerage agreement.
- Agents must also have a thorough knowledge of the *legal disclosure requirements* they are expected to fulfill.
- Agents must balance their contract duties and legal duties. This helps work effectively and ethically in this specialized area of real estate.
