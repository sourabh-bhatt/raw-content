# Inactive Status in Real Estate Licensing

## Understanding Inactive Status

<!-- *Inactive status* occurs when you, as a **salesperson**, are **not currently affiliated with a Virginia real estate firm**. Brokers may also have inactive status when they choose not to engage in real estate activities. -->

*Inactive status* occurs when a **salesperson** is **not currently affiliated with a Virginia real estate firm**. Brokers may also have inactive status when they choose not to engage in real estate activities.

## Implications of Inactive Status

<!-- - You are **barred from executing any brokerage activities** while in inactive status. -->
- Agents are **barred from executing any brokerage activities** while in inactive status.
- This contrasts with being in an *active status*, where agents are fully engaged and authorized to carry out brokerage tasks.

**Example:**

If a salesperson decides to take a break from working with a real estate firm, they enter inactive status. During this time, they cannot represent clients or perform any real estate transactions until they return to active status by affiliating with a Virginia real estate firm.
