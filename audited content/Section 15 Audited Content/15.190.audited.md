# Agency Relationships in Real Estate

<!-- When you're diving into the world of representing clients in real estate transactions, it's crucial to understand **who's involved in an agency relationship**. Think of an agency relationship as a partnership where each party has a specific role. -->

When representing clients in real estate transactions, understanding **who's involved in an agency relationship** matters. An agency relationship is a partnership where each party has a specific role.

## Key Parties in an Agency Relationship

<!-- - **Principal (Client):** The individual hiring you to represent them in a legal transaction, usually involving buying or selling property. -->
- **Principal (Client):** The individual hiring an agent to represent them in a legal transaction, usually involving buying or selling property.
- **Agent:** The person representing the client's interests in the transaction.

## Role of Brokers and Salespersons

<!-- In real estate, the client is typically paired with a **real estate broker**, not directly with a salesperson. However, as a real estate salesperson, you might act as a **sub-agent** to your broker. This means:

In real estate, the client is typically paired with a **real estate broker**, not directly with a salesperson. However, a real estate salesperson might act as a **sub-agent** to their broker. This means:

- They work under the direction of their broker.
- They represent the interests of both their broker and the client.

## Agent's Responsibilities

<!-- Your job as an agent or sub-agent is to connect your client with a counterpart ready to engage in a transaction. This includes: -->

The job of an agent or sub-agent is to connect their client with a counterpart ready to engage in a transaction. This includes:

- Finding a seller for a client looking to buy.
- Locating a buyer for a client's property.
- Facilitating other types of transactions such as leasing or exchanges.

## Complexity of Agency Relationships

Agency relationships can become more complex when multiple clients and agents are involved. Despite the complexity, the core principle remains:

<!-- > You are there to skillfully represent the interests of your client in the real estate transaction, guided by the overarching leadership of your broker. -->

> Agents are there to represent the interests of their client in the real estate transaction, guided by the leadership of their broker.
