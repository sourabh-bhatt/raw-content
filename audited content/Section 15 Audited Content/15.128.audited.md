# Client Referral Fees and Commission Authority

<!-- When considering **client referral fees**, it is important to understand who has the authority to pay these fees and split commissions. -->

When considering **client referral fees**, understanding who has the authority to pay these fees and split commissions matters.

## Authority to Pay Referral Fees

- Only **brokers** have the authority to pay referral fees and split commissions with other brokers or firms.
- **Salespersons** do not have this authority.

## Role of the Broker

<!-- - If you are working under a broker and encounter a situation involving a referral fee, it is the **broker** who will manage the transaction related to those fees. -->

- If salespersons work under a broker and encounter a situation involving a referral fee, the **broker** will manage the transaction related to those fees.

<!-- Understanding this distinction is crucial as it clearly defines the **responsibilities** and **limitations** of your role compared to that of your broker. -->

Understanding this distinction defines the **responsibilities** and **limitations** of the salesperson's role compared to that of the broker.
