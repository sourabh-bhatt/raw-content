# Understanding the Role of a Special Agent in Brokerage

<!-- When diving into the world of brokerage, it's important for you to understand the role of a **special agent**. This concept is crucial because it shapes the scope and limitations of your responsibilities in any transaction. -->

When working in brokerage, understanding the role of a **special agent** matters. This concept shapes the scope and limitations of responsibilities in any transaction.

## Definition of a Special Agent

A special agent has limited authority to perform specific acts or transactions on behalf of a principal. The relationship is created through an agency agreement.

## Role of the Broker as a Special Agent

<!-- In Virginia real estate transactions, real estate agents typically act as special agents for their clients. This means your authority is limited to specific tasks outlined in your agency agreement with the client. It's a clear boundary that you must not overstep. -->

In Virginia real estate transactions, real estate agents typically act as special agents for their clients. This means agent authority is limited to specific tasks outlined in the agency agreement with the client. Agents must not overstep this boundary.
