# Representing Buyers and Sellers

<!-- When you're working as a **licensee** for a **buyer**, it's essential to understand the following key points regarding your duties and obligations: -->

Licensees working for a **buyer** must understand the following points regarding duties and obligations:

- Licensees are **not committing any breach of duty or obligation** by showing properties the buyer is interested in to other potential buyers, whether they are **clients** or **customers**. However, representing both the buyer and seller in the same transaction creates **dual agency** which requires written disclosure and consent.
- Licensees may **represent other buyers** who are interested in different properties.
- Licensees are also allowed to **represent sellers** concerning other properties.
- The **brokerage must consent** to any dual agency arrangements where the licensee represents both parties in the same transaction.
