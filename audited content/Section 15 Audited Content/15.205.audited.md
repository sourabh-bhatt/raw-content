# Agent Responsibilities When Representing Tenants

<!-- When you act as an **agent for a tenant**, it's important to understand your responsibilities related to **showing properties**. -->

When acting as an **agent for a tenant**, understanding responsibilities related to **showing properties** matters.

## Showing Properties to Multiple Parties

- It is perfectly acceptable to show the same properties to other potential tenants, whether they are **clients** or simply **customers**.
- There is no **breach of duty** if you represent other tenants interested in the same properties or different ones.

## Representing Multiple Parties

<!-- - Representing **landlords** concerning other properties does not conflict with your responsibilities to your tenant clients. -->

- Representing **landlords** concerning other properties does not conflict with agent responsibilities to their tenant clients.

<!-- Understanding these points is crucial as it shapes how you effectively manage your relationships with all parties involved. -->

These points shape how agents effectively manage their relationships with all parties involved.
