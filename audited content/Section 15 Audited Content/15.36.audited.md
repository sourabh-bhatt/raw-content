# Ethical and Legal Responsibilities in Real Estate Practice

<!-- In your career as a real estate professional, it's paramount that you understand and adhere to specific regulations to maintain both *integrity* and *trustworthiness* in your dealings. The following guidance, derived from established real estate law, outlines critical actions and practices you should avoid to prevent misrepresentation or omission, ensuring your success and reputation in the field. -->
Real estate professionals must understand and adhere to specific regulations to maintain both *integrity* and *trustworthiness* in their dealings. The following guidance, derived from established real estate law, outlines actions and practices to avoid to prevent misrepresentation or omission.

## Prohibited Practices

<!-- - **Avoid "bait and switch" tactics:** Do not advertise or offer real property for sale or rent with the intention not to sell or rent at the price or terms advertised. If an advertisement states limited availability or a time frame, ensure you have at least that quantity available at the advertised price or terms at the time of advertising. -->
- **Avoid "bait and switch" tactics:** Do not advertise or offer real property for sale or rent with the intention not to sell or rent at the advertised price or terms. If an advertisement states limited availability or a time frame, ensure having at least that quantity available at the advertised price or terms at the time of advertising.

## Disclosure Obligations

<!-- - **Disclose material adverse facts:** When representing a seller or landlord, disclose all known material adverse facts about the property's physical condition to potential buyers or tenants in a timely manner. Transparency is key. -->
- **Disclose material adverse facts:** When representing a seller or landlord, disclose all known material adverse facts about the property's physical condition to potential buyers or tenants in a timely manner.

<!-- - **Communicate offers promptly:** Ensure every written offer, counteroffer, or rejection related to the property is promptly communicated to both buyer and seller to maintain trust. -->
- **Communicate offers promptly:** Ensure every written offer, counteroffer, or rejection related to the property is promptly communicated to both buyer and seller.

<!-- - **Agency disclosure:** If acting in an agency capacity, disclose all material facts related to the property or transaction to your client promptly. Failure to do so neglects ordinary care as outlined in your brokerage agreement. -->
- **Agency disclosure:** If acting in an agency capacity, disclose all material facts related to the property or transaction to the client promptly. Failure to do so neglects ordinary care as outlined in the brokerage agreement.

## Dual Representation

<!-- - When representing both parties in a transaction, **do not share confidential information** obtained from one client with the other unless legally permitted. This maintains integrity and trust. -->
- When representing both parties in a transaction, **do not share confidential information** obtained from one client with the other unless legally permitted.

## Documentation and Financial Transparency

<!-- - **Include all terms and conditions:** Ensure all terms and conditions of the real estate transaction are included in relevant documents such as leases, property management agreements, or offers to purchase to prevent misunderstandings. -->
- **Include all terms and conditions:** Ensure all terms and conditions of the real estate transaction are included in relevant documents such as leases, property management agreements, or offers to purchase.

<!-- - **Identify deposit holders:** When handling applications, leases, or offers to purchase, clearly identify all parties holding any deposits. Transparency in financial transactions builds trust. -->
- **Identify deposit holders:** When handling applications, leases, or offers to purchase, clearly identify all parties holding any deposits.

## Prohibited Misrepresentations and False Statements

- Avoid knowingly making false statements or misrepresenting the value of any land, property, or security, especially to influence lenders.

- Do not alter terms, extend timeframes, or substitute security without prior written consent from the principals involved.

<!-- - Abstain from making false promises through any channel—agents, advertising, or otherwise. Honesty solidifies your credibility. -->
- Abstain from making false promises through any channel - agents, advertising, or otherwise.

Understanding and following these guidelines helps navigate the complexities of real estate transactions while building a foundation of trust with clients.
