# Handling Escrow Money: Legal and Ethical Responsibilities

<!-- When dealing with **escrow money**, it's essential to understand your responsibilities to avoid legal and ethical pitfalls. The funds you hold in trust for others must be managed with the utmost integrity. -->
When dealing with **escrow money**, understanding responsibilities to avoid legal and ethical pitfalls is necessary. The funds held in trust for others must be managed with integrity.

## Prohibited Uses of Escrow Funds (Conversion)

- Using escrow funds for any purpose other than their intended use is known as **conversion** and is strictly prohibited.
- This includes outright theft.
- It also covers:
  - Using funds from one client to cover expenses or purchases for another client.
  - Releasing escrow money as a commission before the property transaction has successfully closed.

## Separation of Funds (Avoiding Commingling)

<!-- - It is crucial to keep trust funds separate from any personal or business operating funds. -->
- Keeping trust funds separate from any personal or business operating funds is necessary.
- Mixing funds is called **commingling** and is equally prohibited.

**Examples:**

<!-- - Never deposit **earnest money** (money given by a buyer to show serious intent to purchase) into your personal or business account.
- Do not place personal funds into a trust account designated for client funds. -->
- Never deposit **earnest money** (money given by a buyer to show serious intent to purchase) into personal or business accounts.
- Do not place personal funds into a trust account designated for client funds.

- Proper handling of clients' funds maintains their trust and confidence.
- Handling funds with care and respect is a cornerstone of your professional integrity. -->
- Staying vigilant in these practices provides legal and ethical protection.
- Proper handling of client funds maintains trust and confidence.
- Handling funds with care and respect forms a cornerstone of professional integrity.
