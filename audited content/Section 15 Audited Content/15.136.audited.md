# Representing Tenants in Lease Transactions

<!-- When you're representing **tenants** as their agent, your primary responsibility is to champion their interests. This means you're tasked with securing a **lease** that meets their approval in terms of **rent** and other conditions. -->

When representing **tenants** as their agent, the primary responsibility is to champion tenant interests. This means securing a **lease** that meets tenant approval in terms of **rent** and other conditions.

## Agent's Responsibilities

<!-- - You are *not required* to look for other properties for tenants once they have entered into a lease or signed a letter of intent to lease. -->
  <!-- - **Exceptions:** If your **brokerage agreement** specifies otherwise, or if the lease or letter of intent specifically states that you should continue searching. -->

- Agents are *not required* to look for other properties for tenants once they have entered into a lease or signed a letter of intent to lease.
  - **Exceptions:** If the **brokerage agreement** specifies otherwise, or if the lease or letter of intent specifically states that the agent should continue searching.

<!-- - Your role includes aiding in the creation and negotiation of: -->
- The agent's role includes aiding in the creation and negotiation of:
  - Leases
  - Letters of intent to lease
  - Rental applications

<!-- - It is imperative to handle and present all **written offers** or **counteroffers** diligently, even if the tenant has already committed to a lease or letter of intent. -->

- Agents must handle and present all **written offers** or **counteroffers** diligently, even if the tenant has already committed to a lease or letter of intent.

<!-- - Providing necessary support to help your tenant finalize the lease agreement is part of your job. -->

- Providing necessary support to help tenants finalize the lease agreement is part of the agent's job.

## Agent Commitment to Tenants

<!-- As an agent, your commitment is to ensure that the tenant's needs and preferences are at the forefront of your actions. This approach: -->

Agent commitment involves ensuring that tenant needs and preferences are at the forefront of actions. This approach:

- Fosters a positive and trusting relationship with clients
- Upholds the professional standards expected in the real estate industry
