# Representing Landlord Clients as a Standard Agent

<!-- When you are representing **landlord clients** as a **standard agent**, it's crucial that you manage all **money** and **property** you receive on their behalf with utmost responsibility. This means you need to ensure that any funds or properties you handle, which the landlord has an interest in now or could potentially have an interest in the future, are accounted for promptly. -->

When representing **landlord clients** as **standard agents**, agents must manage all **money** and **property** they receive on their behalf with responsibility. This means ensuring that any funds or properties handled, which the landlord has an interest in now or could potentially have an interest in the future, are accounted for promptly.

**Key Responsibilities:**

- Manage all money and property received for the landlord diligently.
- Account for funds and properties promptly, regardless of current or potential future interest.
- Maintain transparency and accuracy in handling client assets.

<!-- This accountability plays a significant role in maintaining the **trust** between you and your client and upholding your **professional integrity**. -->

This accountability maintains **trust** between agents and clients while upholding **professional integrity**.
