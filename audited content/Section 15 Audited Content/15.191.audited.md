# Brokerage and Agency Contracts: Rules on Commissions

<!-- As you dive into the world of **brokerage** and **agency contracts**, it's crucial to understand the rules surrounding **commissions**. -->

When working with **brokerage** and **agency contracts**, understanding the rules surrounding **commissions** matters.

## Key Rules on Compensation

<!-- - As a **licensed salesperson** or **broker**, you can only receive compensation from your **principal broker**. This rule is *non-negotiable*. -->
- A **licensed salesperson** or **broker** can only receive compensation from their **principal broker**. This rule is *non-negotiable*.
<!-- - You cannot pay compensation to anyone who is not licensed for performing real estate brokerage activities. -->
- Agents cannot pay compensation to anyone who is not licensed for performing real estate brokerage activities.

Adhering to these guidelines helps agents operate within the boundaries of the law and maintain **professional integrity**.
