# Managing Conflicts of Interest in Brokerage and Agency Contracts

<!-- When working in Virginia real estate, you must understand how to handle **conflicts of interest**. These situations happen when your duties to different parties clash with each other. -->
When working in Virginia real estate, agents must understand how to handle **conflicts of interest**. These situations happen when duties to different parties clash with each other.

## Common Conflicts of Interest in Virginia

<!-- As a Virginia real estate agent, you may face these conflict situations:

- **Dual agency** - representing both buyer and seller in the same transaction
- **Designated agency** - when your brokerage represents both parties but assigns different agents
- Having a personal interest in a property you're showing to clients -->
Virginia real estate agents may face these conflict situations:

- **Dual agency** - representing both buyer and seller in the same transaction
- **Designated agency** - when the brokerage represents both parties but assigns different agents
- Having a personal interest in a property being shown to clients
- Receiving compensation from multiple parties without proper disclosure

## Required Disclosures and Consent

Virginia law requires specific steps when conflicts arise:

### Written Disclosure
<!-- You must provide **written disclosure** to all parties about:
- Your role in the transaction
- Any potential conflicts of interest
- How the conflict affects your duties to each party -->
Agents must provide **written disclosure** to all parties about:
- The agent's role in the transaction
- Any potential conflicts of interest
- How the conflict affects duties to each party

### Informed Consent
All parties must give **written consent** after receiving full disclosure. This consent must be:
- In writing
- Given before the conflict situation begins
- Based on complete information about the conflict

## Managing Dual Agency

<!-- In **dual agency**, you represent both buyer and seller. Virginia allows this but requires: -->
In **dual agency**, agents represent both buyer and seller. Virginia allows this but requires:

- Written disclosure to both parties
- Written consent from both parties
- Limited representation duties
- Cannot advocate for one party over the other
- Must treat both parties fairly

## Compliance Requirements

The Virginia Real Estate Board requires agents to:

- Follow all disclosure requirements
- Obtain proper written consent
- Maintain records of all disclosures and consents
- Act in good faith toward all parties

Proper handling of conflicts of interest will:

Remember, **honesty** and **full disclosure** are the foundation of ethical real estate practice in Virginia.
