# Representing Clients in Brokerage and Agency Contracts

In **brokerage and agency contracts**, agents must accurately represent clients, especially when acting as an agent for a **buyer**.

## Responsibilities

- Always perform according to the terms outlined in the **brokerage agreement**.
- Maintain professionalism throughout the transaction process.
Fulfill obligations to protect the interests of clients.

Adhering to these guidelines helps ensure successful transactions and safeguards client interests.
