# Role and Responsibilities of a Standard Agent

<!-- When you step into the role of a **standard agent**, you're taking on the responsibility to act for or represent your client in an **agency relationship** during a **residential transaction**. This role is much more comprehensive than that of a **limited service agent**. -->

When agents step into the role of a **standard agent**, they are taking on the responsibility to act for or represent their client in an **agency relationship** during a **residential transaction**. This role is more comprehensive than that of a **limited service agent**.

## Agent Duties

<!-- As a standard agent, it is crucial to fully understand the depth of your **duties** in this capacity. Your primary focus must be ensuring that the interests of those you represent are your priority. -->
As a standard agent, understanding the depth of **duties** in this capacity matters. The primary focus must be ensuring that the interests of those they represent are their priority.

<!-- - Act diligently on behalf of your client -->
- Act diligently on behalf of their client
- Maintain loyalty and confidentiality
- Provide full disclosure and honest communication

This knowledge fosters successful transactions and builds a reputable career in the **real estate sector**.
