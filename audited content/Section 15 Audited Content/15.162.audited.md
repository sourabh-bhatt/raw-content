# Exclusive Right-to-Sell Listings and MLS

<!-- When you list properties in an **MLS (Multiple Listing Service)** database, they are generally secured under an **exclusive right-to-sell listing**. This arrangement is between the **seller** and a specific **member broker**. -->

When brokers list properties in an **MLS (Multiple Listing Service)** database, they are generally secured under an **exclusive right-to-sell listing**. This arrangement is between the **seller** and a specific **member broker**.

## Role of Member Brokers and Agency Relationships

- Other member brokers who become involved in the process can work as **buyer's agents**, **seller's agents**, or **dual agents** depending on their agency relationship.
- Agency relationships in Virginia are established through written **brokerage agreements**, not automatically through MLS participation.
