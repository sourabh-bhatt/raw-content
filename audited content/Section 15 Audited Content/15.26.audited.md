# Independent Contractor Role in Real Estate Transactions

## Understanding the Independent Contractor Role

<!-- When handling various tasks in the real estate field, you may work in a specific role known as an **Independent Contractor**. This means you're hired to complete a particular job using your skill, discretion, and judgment to decide the best way to accomplish it. It's crucial to understand that as an Independent Contractor, you **do not act as an agent**. Thus, you are **not automatically bound by the fiduciary duties** an agent owes to their client. -->
When handling various tasks in the real estate field, professionals may work in a specific role known as an **Independent Contractor**. This means being hired to complete a particular job using skill, discretion, and judgment to decide the best way to accomplish it. Understanding that as an Independent Contractor, professionals **do not act as an agent** is necessary. Thus, they are **not automatically bound by the fiduciary duties** an agent owes to their client.

## Legal Framework and Contractual Agreement

<!-- In Virginia, the law allows real estate professionals to serve their clients as Independent Contractors through **transaction brokerage**. This arrangement must be explicitly outlined in a **written contract** between you and your client. The contract must clearly state that you're serving as an Independent Contractor and **not as an agent**. -->
In Virginia, the law allows real estate professionals to serve clients as Independent Contractors through **transaction brokerage**. This arrangement must be explicitly outlined in a **written contract** between the professional and client. The contract must clearly state service as an Independent Contractor and **not as an agent**.

Under this agreement:

<!-- - Your responsibilities and obligations are limited to those explicitly agreed upon in the contract. -->
- Professional responsibilities and obligations are limited to those explicitly agreed upon in the contract.
- The broader obligations typically associated with **Agency Law** do not apply.

## Limited Representation and Its Implications

<!-- This kind of arrangement is known as **limited representation**. It enables you to assist in the real estate transaction process. You might find yourself aiding the buyer, the seller, or in some cases, both. However, keep in mind: -->
This kind of arrangement is known as **limited representation**. It enables professionals to assist in the real estate transaction process. Professionals might aid the buyer, the seller, or in some cases, both. However:

<!-- - Since you're not offering your services in a **fiduciary capacity**—where your client's interests come above all others—
- Your client is **not receiving the full undivided loyalty** often expected from a real estate licensee. -->
- Since professionals are not offering services in a **fiduciary capacity** - where client interests come above all others -
- Clients are **not receiving the full undivided loyalty** often expected from a real estate licensee.
