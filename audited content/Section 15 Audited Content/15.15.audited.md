# Establishing a Brokerage Relationship with a Seller

When about to enter a **brokerage relationship** with a seller, agents must discuss several points with the prospective client. Virginia law requires specific disclosures about brokerage relationships.

## Points to Discuss

1. **Type of Brokerage Relationship**
   - Clearly explain the specific type of brokerage relationship being proposed.
   - Ensure the seller fully understands the nature of the partnership and the services provided.
   - Virginia requires agents to provide written disclosure of the brokerage relationship.

2. **Compensation Transparency**
   - Be upfront about how the agent will be compensated.
   - This may include a fixed fee, a percentage of the sale price, or another arrangement.
   - Transparency helps avoid misunderstandings or surprises later on.
   - Virginia Real Estate Board regulations require clear disclosure of all compensation arrangements.

3. **Sharing Compensation with Other Brokers**
   - Inform the seller if the agent plans to share compensation with another broker.
   - This is especially relevant if the other broker represents the buyer in the transaction.
   - Such disclosure maintains trust and integrity by ensuring all parties understand the financial aspects of the deal.
   - Virginia law requires disclosure of any cooperative compensation arrangements.

By covering these points, agents lay the groundwork for a well-informed, transparent, and trustworthy relationship with clients.
