# Understanding the Term "Ready, Willing, and Able" in Real Estate

<!-- When you come across the term **"Ready, Willing, and Able,"** it's crucial to understand what it signifies, especially in the realm of real estate transactions. This phrase describes a **buyer** who steps forward with an **offer** that aligns perfectly with the conditions laid out in the **listing agreement**. The term **"Ready, Willing, and Able"** describes a **buyer** who steps forward with an **offer** that aligns with the conditions laid out in the **listing agreement**. This buyer is also genuinely motivated and possesses the **financial means** to follow through with the purchase.
