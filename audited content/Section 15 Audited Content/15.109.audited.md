- Agents are responsible for ensuring that all actions taken on behalf of landlord clients are fully compliant with **Virginia state** and **federal regulations**.

- Compliance protects client interests and maintains professional standards.
- Compliance prevents legal issues that could arise from negligence or ignorance of the law.

Example: If a landlord client requires a lease agreement, you must ensure that the agreement complies with Virginia law, including proper security deposit limits and required disclosures under the VRLTA, to avoid potential disputes or legal penalties.
