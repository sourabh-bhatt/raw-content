# Understanding Ratification in Virginia Real Estate

## Part 1: Ratification of a Contract (A Step in a Transaction)

- **What It Is:** This is the moment a real estate purchase agreement becomes a legally binding contract.  
- **How It Happens:** A contract is ratified when the buyer and seller have both signed and accepted the final written offer.  
- **Function:** The "Date of Ratification" starts the timeline for all contract deadlines, like home inspections, loan applications, and appraisals.  

## Part 2: Ratification of an Agency Relationship (Not Allowed in Virginia)

- **What It Means (in general law):** Approving an agent's unauthorized actions after they have already happened.  
- **The Virginia Rule:** Virginia has abrogated (abolished) this common law principle for real estate.  
**The Correct Way:** In Virginia, agents must have a written brokerage agreement before acting as a client's agent. An agency relationship cannot be created retroactively by ratification.
