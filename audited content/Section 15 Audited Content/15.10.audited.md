# Honesty in Brokerage and Agency Contracts

## Honesty in Practice

Real estate agents must understand **honesty** in the context of **brokerage** and **agency contracts**, especially when representing a **landlord**. Agents must treat all potential tenants with honesty. Agents cannot knowingly provide false information to tenants.

## Ethical Considerations

- Honesty safeguards agent **integrity**.
- Maintaining honesty helps build and preserve the **trust** necessary for successful **real estate transactions**.

By adhering to this ethical principle, agents ensure professionalism and foster positive relationships with clients and tenants alike.
