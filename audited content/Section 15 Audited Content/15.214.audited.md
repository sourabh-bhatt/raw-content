When starting a career in Virginia real estate, understanding how **agency relationships** work matters. These include relationships between a **listing agent and a client** or a **buyer and their broker**. These relationships start when agents sign a written agreement with their client. This means once agents have a signed agreement, they have legal duties to their client.
