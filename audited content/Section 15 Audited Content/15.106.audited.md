When a **buyer's agent** representing a **buyer** assists the **seller** or a prospective seller, understanding the nature of these actions is necessary. Performing ministerial acts - unless explicitly prohibited by law or the **brokerage agreement** - does **not** automatically create a **brokerage relationship** between the agent and the seller.

In Virginia, ministerial acts are defined in Virginia Code Â§ 54.1-2130 as acts that do not require real estate expertise or judgment.

### Characteristics of Ministerial Acts

- These tasks are considered **routine or clerical**.
- Examples include passing along information or filing paperwork.
- Such acts do **not** imply the agent is acting as an agent for the seller.
- They do not require real estate expertise or professional judgment.
