# Representing the Seller as Their Agent

<!-- When you're representing a **seller** as their agent, your primary duty is to carry out **marketing efforts** aligned with what's outlined in the **brokerage agreement** you both agreed upon. When representing **sellers** as their agent, the primary duty is to carry out **marketing efforts** aligned with what's outlined in the **brokerage agreement**. This means working to close a sale at either the price and terms the seller has indicated or at another set of conditions they're comfortable with.

> Agents aren't required to seek additional offers once the property is under contract - unless this is explicitly included in the brokerage agreement or the sales contract itself specifies it.

## Responsibilities in Offer Management and Negotiations

<!-- Your responsibilities extend to helping draft and negotiate not just the initial **offer**, but also any subsequent **counteroffers**, **amendments**, and **addenda** to the property's sales contract. Agent responsibilities extend to helping draft and negotiate the initial **offer**, and subsequent **counteroffers**, **amendments**, and **addenda** to the property's sales contract. Agents also brainstorm and execute strategies aimed at meeting seller goals.

<!-- - **Present all written offers and counteroffers promptly to the seller**. This ensures the seller has the option to consider all potential deals. -->

- **Present all written offers and counteroffers promptly to the seller**. This ensures the seller has the option to consider all potential deals.

Assistance doesn't stop with negotiations. Agents also provide support to sellers in fulfilling their **obligations under the contract** and aid in ensuring a smooth process leading up to **settlement**. This could involve a variety of tasks, all aimed at making sure the seller's experience is trouble-free and that the deal progresses to a successful close.

## Agent Role as Guide and Supporter

The agent's role is both guide and supporter for sellers through the process of selling property. By diligently fulfilling these responsibilities, agents uphold their end of the brokerage agreement while maintaining their reputation as dependable and effective real estate professionals.
