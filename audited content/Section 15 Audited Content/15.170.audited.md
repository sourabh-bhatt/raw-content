When working as a real estate agent in Virginia, agents must use **ordinary care** in all their **brokerage** and **agency contracts**. Ordinary care means using the same level of skill and care that other reasonable real estate agents would use.

## Ordinary Care Requirements

- **Ordinary care** requires agents to be competent and careful in their work
- Agents must share material facts they know with their clients
- Information known by the agent is considered known by the client in many situations
