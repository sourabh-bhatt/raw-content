# Brokerage and Agency Contract Obligations

## Confidentiality and Disclosure Duties

The responsibilities that come with **brokerage** and **agency contracts** include certain obligations, especially concerning the **duty to maintain confidentiality** for clients. However, there are instances where **state laws** or common **legal principles** can override this confidentiality commitment.

### Disclosure of Latent and Material Defects

For example, if there are **latent (hidden)** and **material defects** in a property that you know about, you must disclose this information under Virginia law. This means that, despite your responsibility to keep your client's information confidential, your **duty to disclose known significant property defects** takes precedence to ensure transparency and honesty in the real estate transaction. Virginia's Property Disclosure Act requires this disclosure to protect buyers.

This is a critical point to grasp as it underscores the importance of balancing your obligations to your client with your legal responsibilities.
