# Agency Disclosure in Brokerage and Agency

When working in **brokerage** and **agency**, agents must understand **disclosing representation**. This is required by law in Virginia. As a broker or salesperson, agents have a responsibility to clearly tell potential buyers and sellers **who they are representing**.

## Agency Disclosure Requirements

<!-- - The purpose of agency disclosure goes beyond simply following rules; it is about **integrity**. -->
- The purpose of agency disclosure goes beyond simply following rules; it is about **integrity**.
- Making a proper and timely **agency disclosure** helps eliminate confusion or misleading situations for those interested in buying or selling.
- It ensures transparency by making everything clear so everyone knows where they stand.

## Risks of Not Disclosing Properly

<!-- - Without the right disclosures made at the right time, you could accidentally create an **implied agency relationship**. -->
<!-- - Implied agency carries additional **duties and liabilities** that you may not be prepared for or aware of. -->

- Without the right disclosures made at the right time, agents could accidentally create an **implied agency relationship**.
- Implied agency carries additional **duties and liabilities** that agents may not be prepared for or aware of.

## Virginia Requirements and Timing

<!-- - In Virginia, you must make agency disclosures at the first substantive contact with a consumer. -->
- In Virginia, agents must make agency disclosures at the first substantive contact with a consumer.
- Virginia has specific requirements for how and when to make these disclosures.
- Disclosures must happen **early in the process**:
  - Before engaging in deep conversations about the property or transaction.
  - Before any **confidential information** is exchanged.

## Agency Disclosure Benefits

Agency disclosure follows **legal requirements** and builds **trust** and **transparency** with clients. Keeping this principle helps agents handle their responsibilities with confidence and integrity.
