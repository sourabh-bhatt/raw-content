# Confidentiality When Representing Tenant Clients

When representing **tenant clients** as a **standard agent**, agents must keep all **personal** and **financial information** received during the brokerage relationship **confidential**. Exceptions are only when **the law requires disclosure** or when the **client provides written consent** to share specific information.
