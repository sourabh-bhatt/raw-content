# Understanding Escrow Accounts in Real Estate Transactions

<!-- When you're handling transactions that involve **holding money or documents** for another party until certain conditions are met, it's essential to understand the role of **escrow accounts**. -->
When handling transactions that involve **holding money or documents** for another party until certain conditions are met, understanding the role of **escrow accounts** is necessary.

## Role and Requirements of Escrow Accounts

<!-- - In Virginia, you must place client funds into an escrow account. -->
- In Virginia, agents must place client funds into an escrow account.
- In Virginia, attorneys, title companies, or licensed real estate brokers can serve as **escrow agents**.

## Role of the Salesperson

<!-- - As a Virginia salesperson, you cannot act as an escrow agent. Only your supervising broker can handle escrow accounts.
- However, you can accept earnest money deposits on behalf of your broker, who will place them in the escrow account. -->
- Virginia salespersons cannot act as escrow agents. Only supervising brokers can handle escrow accounts.
- However, salespersons can accept earnest money deposits on behalf of their broker, who will place them in the escrow account.
