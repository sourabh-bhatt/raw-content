In understanding the framework of **brokerage** and **agency contracts**, understanding who is eligible to provide **compensation** is necessary. Only the **employing broker** has the authority to compensate, or pay, the **salesperson**.

This setup is designed to maintain clear financial and managerial lines within real estate operations, ensuring that transactions and payments are streamlined through the appropriate channels for **accountability** and **transparency**.
