# Operating a Brokerage Firm as an Individual Broker

<!-- If you're planning to run a **brokerage firm** by yourself, you should know that as long as you, the **individual broker and owner**, hold a **valid license**, you do **not** need a separate **firm license**. -->

When planning to run a **brokerage firm** individually, as long as the **individual broker and owner** holds a **valid license**, a separate **firm license** is **not** needed.

## Business Name Requirements

<!-- - You may operate under your **legal name** or choose a **fictitious name**. -->
- Individual brokers may operate under their **legal name** or choose a **fictitious name**.
- Any fictitious name must be **registered with the State Corporation Commission (SCC)** before use.

This provision allows flexibility in how brokers present and manage their business in the real estate industry. Understanding this responsibility helps in navigating the **regulatory landscape** and ensuring that **brokerage operations** remain compliant.
