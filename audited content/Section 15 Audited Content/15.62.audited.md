# Disclosure Responsibilities in Real Estate Transactions

<!-- When preparing for a home sale, it is crucial to understand your role regarding **disclosure forms** and **seller disclosures**. -->
When preparing for a home sale, understanding the agent's role regarding **disclosure forms** and **seller disclosures** is necessary.

<!-- ## Your Role as a Real Estate Professional -->
## Real Estate Professional Role

- **Do not fill out disclosure forms on behalf of the seller.**
- Advise sellers about **Virginia's disclosure laws**.
- Inform sellers of their **obligations under Virginia law**.

<!-- ## Importance of Disclosure Forms -->
## Disclosure Forms

- Disclosure forms act as an additional protective measure for all parties involved in the transaction.
<!-- - However, these forms do **not** relieve you of your independent **duty to disclose** any **material defects** in the property that you are aware of. -->
- However, these forms do **not** relieve agents of their independent **duty to disclose** any **material defects** in the property that they are aware of.
- In Virginia, the Property Disclosure Act requires sellers to disclose known material defects.

<!-- ## Your Duty to Disclose -->
## Agent Duty to Disclose

<!-- - You must ensure **honesty and transparency** throughout the transaction process.
- This responsibility remains fully on your shoulders, regardless of the disclosure forms. -->
- Agents must ensure **honesty and transparency** throughout the transaction process.
- This responsibility remains fully with agents, regardless of the disclosure forms.
- Virginia law requires real estate agents to disclose any material defects they know about.

Example: If you know of a significant roof leak or foundation issue, you must disclose this information even if the seller's disclosure form does not mention it.
