# Licensing Requirements for Real Estate Activities

In Virginia, individuals must obtain a real estate license unless they are exempt. Licensing is required for activities such as:

- Selling or proposing the sale of real estate for compensation
- Buying or suggesting buying real estate for compensation
- Negotiating purchase or exchange of real estate for compensation
- Renting, leasing, or negotiating leases for real estate on behalf of another for compensation

These requirements are outlined in Virginia Code § 54.1-2105 and reinforced by the Real Estate Board regulations 18VAC135-20-280, which mandate written consent and disclosure for any brokerage activities.
