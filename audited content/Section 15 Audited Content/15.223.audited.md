# Earning Commissions in Real Estate

<!-- When it comes to earning commissions, it's crucial for you to understand how this works. You, as a **salesperson**, can earn a commission under specific conditions. Remember that as a salesperson, you must be associated with a broker. -->

When it comes to earning commissions, understanding how this works matters. A **salesperson** can earn a commission under specific conditions. As a salesperson, they must be associated with a broker.

## Key Conditions for Earning a Commission

<!-- - **Procuring Cause of the Sale**: This means that your actions directly lead to the sale of the property. Your efforts and involvement make the sale happen. -->

- **Procuring Cause of the Sale**: This means that agent actions directly lead to the sale of the property. Agent efforts and involvement make the sale happen.

<!-- - **Introducing a Ready, Willing, and Able Buyer**: You need to find a buyer who is prepared and legally capable of purchasing the property under the terms that the seller has laid out. The buyer must be: -->

- **Introducing a Ready, Willing, and Able Buyer**: Agents need to find a buyer who is prepared and legally capable of purchasing the property under the terms that the seller has laid out. The buyer must be:
  - Ready and eager to proceed with the transaction
  - Legally able to complete the purchase
  - Financially capable of fulfilling the purchase terms
