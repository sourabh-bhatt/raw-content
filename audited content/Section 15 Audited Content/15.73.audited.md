# Understanding Fiduciary Duties in Real Estate

<!-- As you dive into the world of real estate, understanding the concept of **fiduciary duties** is crucial. Imagine yourself being trusted by someone to act on their behalf â€” that's exactly what being a **fiduciary** means. In real estate, understanding the concept of **fiduciary duties** is necessary. A **fiduciary** is someone trusted to act on another person's behalf. The fiduciary is in a position of trust and confidence, representing another person's interests, which in this domain is referred to as the **client**.

## The Nature of Fiduciary Duty

<!-- When you become a fiduciary, the law places a special responsibility on your shoulders. This responsibility is known as a **fiduciary duty**, which requires you to act in the *best interests* of your client at all times. Whether you're actively serving a client or have completed your obligations, certain duties persist. -->
When someone becomes a fiduciary, the law places a special responsibility on them. This responsibility is known as a **fiduciary duty**, which requires acting in the *best interests* of the client at all times. Whether actively serving a client or having completed obligations, certain duties persist.

When the brokerage relationship ends - whether through **termination**, **expiration**, or after fulfilling responsibilities - obligations do not necessarily end. Exceptions include:

<!-- - You must **account for all money and property** related to your brokerage relationship.

- The fiduciary must **account for all money and property** related to the brokerage relationship.
- Ensure that any **pending transactions** are finalized correctly.
- Hand over all assets as agreed.

Throughout dealings, fiduciaries will encounter various personal and financial information belonging to their client. Maintaining the **confidentiality** of this information is not just a courtesy; it is a *mandatory obligation*. Even after the relationship has ended, disclosure of this information is prohibited unless:

- The law requires disclosure.
<!-- - Your client provides **written consent** to share specific details. -->
- The client provides **written consent** to share specific details.

## Legal Foundation and Professional Importance

<!-- These fiduciary duties are rooted in the **common laws of agency** and are designed to protect both you and your client. Upholding these duties ensures legal compliance and helps build **trust** and **credibility** in your professional relationships. -->
These fiduciary duties are rooted in the **common laws of agency** and are designed to protect both the fiduciary and the client. Upholding these duties ensures legal compliance and helps build **trust** and **credibility** in professional relationships.
