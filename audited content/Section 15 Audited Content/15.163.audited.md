# Confidentiality When Representing Buyer Clients

<!-- When you're representing **buyer clients** as their **agent**, it's crucial to understand the importance of **confidentiality**. This means that all **personal** and **financial information** you receive from them during your **brokerage relationship** must be kept strictly confidential. -->

When representing **buyer clients** as their **agent**, agents must understand **confidentiality**. This means that all **personal** and **financial information** received from them during the **brokerage relationship** must be kept strictly confidential.

<!-- This confidentiality applies not only to information explicitly shared with you but also to any information your clients ask you to keep private. -->

This confidentiality applies not only to information explicitly shared with the agent but also to any information their clients ask them to keep private.

## Exceptions to Confidentiality

There are three exceptions to this rule:

1. If the **law requires** the information to be disclosed.
<!-- 2. If your client gives you **written consent** to share specific details. -->
2. If the client gives the agent **written consent** to share specific details.
<!-- 3. If disclosure is necessary to defend yourself in a legal proceeding. -->
3. If disclosure is necessary to defend the agent in a legal proceeding.

<!-- Maintaining this trust is fundamental in building and maintaining a strong, professional relationship with your clients. -->

Maintaining this trust helps in building and maintaining a strong, professional relationship with clients.
