# Disclosure of Brokerage Relationship When Representing Tenant Clients

<!-- When you're acting as a **tenant's agent** representing **tenant clients**, it's essential to properly disclose your **brokerage relationship** to any **unrepresented landlords**. This disclosure must be made at first contact. This step ensures **transparency** in your dealings and maintains professional integrity throughout the transaction process. -->

When acting as **tenant agents** representing **tenant clients**, agents must properly disclose their **brokerage relationship** to any **unrepresented landlords**. This disclosure must be made at first contact. This step ensures **transparency** and maintains professional integrity throughout the transaction process.

<!-- In Virginia, you must use the Disclosure of Brokerage Relationship form to make this disclosure to unrepresented parties. -->

In Virginia, agents must use the Disclosure of Brokerage Relationship form to make this disclosure to unrepresented parties.

<!-- Understanding and executing this disclosure correctly is vital for: -->

Understanding and executing this disclosure correctly enables:

- Creating trust between parties
- Fostering good relationships in **real estate transactions**

**Key Points:**

<!-- - Always disclose your brokerage relationship when representing tenants to landlords who do not have representation. -->
- Always disclose the brokerage relationship when representing tenants to landlords who do not have representation.
- This disclosure must be made at first contact with the unrepresented landlord.
- Proper disclosure helps avoid conflicts of interest and misunderstandings.
- Transparency promotes ethical practices and professionalism in real estate dealings.
