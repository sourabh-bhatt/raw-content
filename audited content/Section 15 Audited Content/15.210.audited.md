# Subagency in Real Estate

<!-- When you step into the role of a real estate agent, you'll learn about an important concept known as a **subagent**. This is an agent who represents the same client as another agent through cooperation between brokerages. -->

A **subagent** is an agent who represents the same client as another agent through cooperation between brokerages.

## Subagency

- The arrangement requires the **client's approval** before proceeding.
- In Virginia, subagency relationships must be disclosed in writing and agreed to by all parties.
- A typical example is when a listing agent's brokerage cooperates with a buyer's agent from another brokerage who agrees to represent the seller.
- Subagency is less common in modern real estate practice.
- This structure allows for a broader range of services and support for the client.
- The **primary agent** oversees the transaction's integrity and ensures compliance with the client's wishes.
