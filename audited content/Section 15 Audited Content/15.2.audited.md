# Disclosure of Material Facts for Buyer's Agents

**Disclosure** of **material facts** when representing buyers is a concept for real estate professionals.

## The Role of the Buyer's Agent

When acting as a buyer's agent, agents hold a responsibility that goes beyond finding the right property. Agents must ensure clients are fully informed about aspects of their potential new home. These aspects are known as **material facts**.

## Understanding Material Facts

**Material facts** encompass a wide range of information that could influence a client's decision to:

- Proceed with the purchase
- Reconsider their offer
- Back out of the deal entirely

These facts may relate to the property itself or the transaction.

## Obligation to Disclose

Agents are required to disclose all material facts that they **actually know**. Examples include:

- Issues with the **foundation**
- Problems with **plumbing**
- **Legal issues** tied to the property
- **Neighborhood plans** that could affect the property's value

Agents must share what they know, not what they speculate.

## Disclosure Functions

Disclosure functions:

- Ensuring buyers are fully informed protects their interests.
- It builds a relationship grounded in **trust** and **transparency**.
- It helps clients make informed decisions.
- It establishes agent reputation as reliable and ethical.

The disclosure of material facts maintains integrity and trust in real estate practice.
