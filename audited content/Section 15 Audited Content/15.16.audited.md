# Brokerage and Agency Contracts in Real Estate

<!-- When you're venturing into the world of real estate, you need to understand brokerage and agency contracts. -->
Real estate professionals must understand brokerage and agency contracts.

## Types of Business Entities

Real estate professionals may work within or interact with various forms of business entities in this field. These entities include:

- **Partnerships**
- **Associations**
- **Corporations**
- **Limited Liability Companies (LLCs)**
- **Sole Proprietorships**

All these entities must be managed or owned by a **principal broker**.

## Purpose and Licensing Requirements

These firms operate with the main goal of facilitating the sale of real estate on behalf of others, aiming to generate profit from these transactions. These firms must be **licensed**. This licensing:

- Authenticates their legitimacy
- Confirms their capacity to operate within the real estate market

### Exception to Licensing

There is an exception to this rule:

- When the entity is a **sole proprietorship owned by a principal broker**, the business entity itself does not need a separate license because the principal broker's license covers the business operations.

These concepts lay the groundwork for real estate practice, providing a clear picture of the operational framework within which these entities function.
