# Disclosure Obligations When Representing a Seller as a Standard Agent

<!-- When you represent a seller as a **standard agent**, you must disclose all material facts about the property that you know. You do not disclose material facts about the buyer to the seller. -->

When representing a seller as a **standard agent**, agents must disclose all material facts about the property that they know. Agents do not disclose material facts about the buyer to the seller.

## Understanding Material Facts

**Material facts** are any details that could influence the buyer's decision to purchase. They could also affect the terms and conditions the buyer might agree to. Examples of material facts include:

- Structural issues in the property
- Legal disputes related to the property  
- Environmental hazards or defects

## Duty as a Standard Agent

<!-- Your duty is to ensure transparency. You must protect your client's interests by sharing all material facts about the property. Holding back such information could lead to: -->
Agent duty is to ensure transparency. Agents must protect their client's interests by sharing all material facts about the property. Holding back such information could lead to:

- Legal consequences
<!-- - Damage to your professional reputation -->
- Damage to professional reputation

<!-- Always remember the importance of **honesty** and **full disclosure**. This builds trust and maintains the integrity of the transaction. -->
**Honesty** and **full disclosure** build trust and maintain the integrity of the transaction.
