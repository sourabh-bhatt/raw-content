# Establishing a Brokerage Relationship with a Landlord

<!-- Before you start a brokerage relationship with a landlord, you must clearly communicate several key details right from the start. -->
Before starting a brokerage relationship with a landlord, agents must clearly communicate several details right from the start.

## Nature of the Brokerage Relationship

<!-- - Explain to your potential client the type of brokerage relationship you are proposing.

- Explain to the potential client the type of brokerage relationship being proposed.
- Clarify whether the agent will be acting as a *client's agent*, which means they will have specific **duties and responsibilities** toward them.
- Agents must provide the Disclosure of Brokerage Relationship form at the first substantive contact with the landlord.

## Compensation

<!-- - Discuss how you will be paid for your services. -->
- Discuss how the agent will be paid for services.
- Clear communication about compensation helps set proper expectations and avoids confusion later.

## Sharing Compensation

<!-- - You must tell the landlord if you plan to share any portion of your compensation with another broker representing the other party in the transaction. -->
- Agents must tell the landlord if they plan to share any portion of their compensation with another broker representing the other party in the transaction.
- Virginia law requires this disclosure to ensure transparency in the brokerage relationship.

<!-- Remember, these discussions are required by Virginia law for establishing a professional and transparent relationship. All parties must be fully aware of the arrangement details. -->
These discussions are required by Virginia law for establishing a professional and transparent relationship. All parties must be fully aware of the arrangement details.
