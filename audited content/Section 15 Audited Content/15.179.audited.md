# Personal Service Contracts in Real Estate

When entering into **personal service contracts**, such as a **listing agreement**, these agreements are uniquely tied to the individual responsible for performing the services. This means that if the person obligated to fulfill the duties under the contract passes away, the contract **does not survive**.

## Nature of Personal Service Contracts

- These contracts depend on the **continuing ability and existence** of the individual who has promised to perform the services.
- The obligations of the contract are linked to the **personal capabilities and life** of the service provider.

This principle ensures that the contract's duties cannot be transferred or continued by another party if the original service provider is no longer able to perform them.
