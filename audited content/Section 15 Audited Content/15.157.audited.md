# Understanding Obedience in Real Estate Agency

<!-- Obedience is a **fiduciary duty** that requires you to follow all *lawful instructions* given by your client. This duty is fundamental in managing the relationship between you and your client. -->

Obedience is a **fiduciary duty** that requires agents to follow all *lawful instructions* given by their client. This duty manages the relationship between agents and their clients.

## Key Points About Obedience

<!-- - You may *suggest* different courses of action, but the **final decision** always rests with your client. -->
<!-- - You must *never* comply with **illegal requests** from your client, as doing so would violate your legal and ethical obligations. -->
<!-- - If you are unable to follow through with a *lawful instruction*, the proper response is to *gracefully exit* the agency relationship. -->

- Agents may *suggest* different courses of action, but the **final decision** always rests with their client.
- Agents must *never* comply with **illegal requests** from their client, as doing so would violate legal and ethical obligations.
- If agents are unable to follow through with a *lawful instruction*, the proper response is to *gracefully exit* the agency relationship.

<!-- Maintaining obedience in this way ensures you uphold **professionalism** and **integrity** in your role as a real estate professional. -->

Maintaining obedience in this way ensures agents uphold **professionalism** and **integrity** in their role as real estate professionals.
