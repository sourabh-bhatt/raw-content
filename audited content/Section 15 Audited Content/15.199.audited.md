# Responsibility and Liability in Real Estate Transactions

<!-- When navigating real estate transactions, it's crucial to understand how **responsibility** and **liability** work, especially regarding **misrepresentations** and **negligence**. This knowledge is essential when working with clients and other brokers. -->

When navigating real estate transactions, understanding how **responsibility** and **liability** work matters, especially regarding **misrepresentations** and **negligence**. This knowledge helps when working with clients and other brokers.

## Misrepresentation and Liability

<!-- - If a **misrepresentation** occurs during a brokerage relationship and it is tied to something you, as a **licensee**, say or do, your client will *not* automatically be held responsible. -->
- If a **misrepresentation** occurs during a brokerage relationship and it is tied to something the **licensee** says or does, their client will *not* automatically be held responsible.
- This holds true unless the client was aware or reasonably should have been aware of the misrepresentation and failed to take prompt corrective action.
<!-- - Similarly, clients are not liable for any **negligence**, **extreme negligence**, or **intentional wrongdoing** committed by you or any broker you are associated with. -->
- Similarly, clients are not liable for any **negligence**, **extreme negligence**, or **intentional wrongdoing** committed by the licensee or any broker they are associated with.

## Liability for Actions of Other Brokers

<!-- - When you involve another broker to assist with brokerage services, you are generally *not* liable for that broker's misrepresentations unless: -->
- When licensees involve another broker to assist with brokerage services, they are generally *not* liable for that broker's misrepresentations unless:
  <!-- - You knew or should have known about the misrepresentation, and -->
  - They knew or should have known about the misrepresentation, and
  <!-- - You failed to correct it promptly. -->
  - They failed to correct it promptly.
- The same applies to any negligence or deliberate misconduct by the assisting broker.

## Actual Knowledge vs. Implied Knowledge

<!-- - The knowledge or information possessed by you or your clients is considered **actual knowledge**. -->
- The knowledge or information possessed by licensees or their clients is considered **actual knowledge**.
- This means that what is known by one party is *not* automatically assumed to be known by the other.
- Therefore, the principle of **implied knowledge** does *not* apply in these brokerage relationships.

## Exceptions and Additional Considerations

- These rules do *not* limit accountability regarding **unlawful discrimination** in housing; that is governed separately.
- The right to **rescind** a real estate deal or liability for any misrepresentation, negligence, or intentional acts in a transaction remains unaffected except as specifically stated.
<!-- - Both you and your clients may still face consequences for wrongful actions directly connected to a deal. -->
- Both licensees and their clients may still face consequences for wrongful actions directly connected to a deal.
- However, these rules do *not* create new grounds for lawsuits over real estate deals gone wrong.

<!-- Understanding these points helps you navigate your responsibilities and liabilities in real estate transactions, ensuring you maintain professional integrity and protect both your clients and yourself. -->

Understanding these points helps licensees navigate their responsibilities and liabilities in real estate transactions, ensuring they maintain professional integrity and protect both their clients and themselves.
