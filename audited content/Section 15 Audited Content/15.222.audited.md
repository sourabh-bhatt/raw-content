In real estate, understanding the dynamics of a **buyer-broker agreement** matters. Consider forming a partnership with a buyer, aiming to help them find their property. This relationship is bound by an **agency agreement**, sometimes referred to as a **buyer agency employment contract**. As the broker, agents may also have a salesperson working under them, essentially acting as a **subagent** for the buyer.

## Nature of the Buyer Representation Contract

The contract to represent a buyer can be quite flexible:

- It can be an **oral** or **written agreement**.
- It might be **bilateral** (where obligations are on both sides) or **executory** (binding one to future actions).
<!-- - Despite being a **personal service agreement**, it is not transferable; it is tailor-made for the relationship between you and the buyer. -->

- Despite being a **personal service agreement**, it is not transferable; it is tailor-made for the relationship between the agent and the buyer.
- While not always necessary, including an **expiration date** is common. If left open-ended, the agreement will last for a **reasonable period**, as determined by Virginia law.
