# Agent Responsibilities to Clients and Customers in Real Estate

<!-- As you dive into the world of real estate, understanding the distinction between **customers** and **clients**, along with the responsibilities you hold towards each, is crucial. -->

When working in real estate, understanding the distinction between **customers** and **clients**, along with the responsibilities agents hold towards each, matters.

## Duties to Clients

<!-- When representing **clients**, your role necessitates a deeper commitment, carrying a bundle of **fiduciary duties**. These duties are paramount and ensure that you act in the **best interests of your clients above all else**. -->

When representing **clients**, the agent role necessitates a deeper commitment, carrying a bundle of **fiduciary duties**. These duties ensure that agents act in the **best interests of their clients above all else**.

## Duties to Customers

<!-- Your obligation to uphold **integrity** and **fairness** doesn't stop with those you formally represent. Even those not under your formal representation, referred to as **customers**, deserve a level of honesty and fairness from you. This includes: -->

Agent obligations to uphold **integrity** and **fairness** don't stop with those they formally represent. Even those not under formal representation, referred to as **customers**, deserve a level of honesty and fairness from agents. This includes:

- Never providing false information, whether dealing with buyers or sellers.
- Disclosing any **material adverse facts** that could affect the property's physical condition. In Virginia, this means any facts that adversely and significantly affect the use, value, or enjoyment of the property.

## Ministerial Acts

<!-- There are certain **ministerial acts** you're expected to perform. These are basic duties that: -->

There are certain **ministerial acts** agents are expected to perform. These are basic duties that:

- Do not require discretion or the provision of advice.
<!-- - Ensure you provide necessary assistance where it's allowed. -->
- Ensure agents provide necessary assistance where it's allowed.

Agent roles might also involve navigating situations where they represent multiple clients over time. This requires a careful balance to maintain **impartiality** and **fairness**.
