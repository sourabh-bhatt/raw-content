# Understanding Brokerage and Agency Contracts

<!-- In your journey into the world of real estate, one crucial concept you'll need to grasp is the nature of **brokerage and agency contracts**, specifically understanding the various roles you will play and the duties associated with each. -->
In real estate, understanding the nature of **brokerage and agency contracts** is necessary, specifically understanding the various roles agents play and the duties associated with each.

## Defining the Client

<!-- The term **client** refers to the person, often called the **principal**, who enters into an **agency relationship** with you, the **agent**. -->
The term **client** refers to the person, often called the **principal**, who enters into an **agency relationship** with the **agent**.

## Roles and Responsibilities of the Agent

<!-- Picture yourself wearing different hats in real estate: -->
Agents take on different roles in real estate:

- **Buyer's Agent**
  - Represents someone looking to purchase a home.
  - The client is the buyer.
  - Responsibilities include serving the buyer's best interests, helping navigate the home buying process from the search to negotiations and closing.

- **Seller's Agent**
  - Represents the individual or entity selling the property.
  - The client is the seller.
  - Duties involve marketing the property effectively, securing the best possible sale price, and facilitating the sale process efficiently.

- **Property Manager**
  - Represents a property owner.
  - The client is the property owner.
  - Role includes managing the property on behalf of the owner, such as finding and vetting tenants, handling maintenance and repairs, and managing leases and tenant relationships.
