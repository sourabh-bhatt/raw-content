# Dual and Designated Agency: Disclosure and Consent

<!-- When dealing with **dual and designated agency**, it's crucial that you, as an agent, handle **disclosures** with utmost care and transparency. -->
When dealing with **dual and designated agency**, agents must handle **disclosures** with utmost care and transparency.

## Disclosure Requirements

- Disclosures must be **clearly written**, **easily noticeable**, or presented on **standardized forms**.
- Disclosures must be made at the **earliest practical time**, but no later than the time of **contract signing**.
<!-- - You are responsible for obtaining **written consent** from both parties involved:
  - From **buyers** before you prepare an offer on their behalf.
  - From **sellers** before you present an offer to them. -->
- Agents are responsible for obtaining **written consent** from both parties involved:
  - From **buyers** before preparing an offer on their behalf.
  - From **sellers** before presenting an offer to them.

This step involves following protocol, fostering **trust**, and ensuring that all parties are fully aware of the agent's role in the transaction.

**Timely and clear communication** helps navigate these situations effectively.
