# Responsibilities of a Standard Agent Representing Tenant Clients

<!-- When you're representing **tenant clients** as a **standard agent**, it's essential to understand your responsibilities towards actual or prospective **landlord customers** in a **rental transaction**. Here's what you owe to these landlords to maintain professionalism and adhere to your duties. -->

When representing **tenant clients** as **standard agents**, agents must understand their responsibilities towards actual or prospective **landlord customers** in **rental transactions**. These responsibilities maintain professionalism and adhere to duties.

## Key Duties to Landlord Customers

<!-- - **Honesty and Fairness**: Always prioritize honesty and fairness in your dealings. This means being truthful and fair in all your dealings. -->

- **Honesty and Fairness**: Prioritize honesty and fairness in dealings. This means being truthful and fair in all dealings.

<!-- - **Reasonable Skill and Care**: Exercise reasonable skill and care throughout the process. Be careful and thorough in your work. -->

- **Reasonable Skill and Care**: Exercise reasonable skill and care throughout the process. Be careful and thorough in work.

<!-- - **Proper Accounting**: Properly account for all money and property received during your transactions. Handle all money and property correctly. Mistakes can cause legal problems. -->

- **Proper Accounting**: Properly account for all money and property received during transactions. Handle all money and property correctly. Mistakes can cause legal problems.

<!-- - **Disclosure of Material Facts**: While focusing on your tenant clients' best interests, disclose all material facts you are aware of that could affect the landlords' decisions. You must share important facts without breaking your duty to your tenant clients. -->

- **Disclosure of Material Facts**: While focusing on tenant clients' best interests, disclose all material facts that could affect landlords' decisions. Agents must share facts without breaking their duty to tenant clients.

<!-- Understanding and fulfilling these duties will enhance your professionalism and build your reputation as a fair and trustworthy agent in the real estate industry. -->

Understanding and fulfilling these duties enhances professionalism and maintains agent reputation as fair and trustworthy in the real estate industry.
