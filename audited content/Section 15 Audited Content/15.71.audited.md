## Commission Compensation Points

<!-- - If you are a **salesperson** or an **associate broker**, you can only receive compensation **directly from your employing broker**.

- If a person is a **salesperson** or an **associate broker**, they can only receive compensation **directly from their employing broker**.
- The employing broker is the broker under whose name the **license** has been issued.
- The license must be **currently active** to legally receive any kind of commission.

This rule helps maintain clarity and accountability within brokerage operations, ensuring that financial transactions align with legal and ethical standards.
