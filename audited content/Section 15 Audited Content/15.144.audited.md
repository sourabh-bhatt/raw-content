In brokerage and agency contracts, understanding how **agent relationships** work matters. They must also follow specific **disclosures** and **limitations** regarding their **brokerage relationships**.

## Standard Duties Apply to All Licensees

All Virginia real estate licensees have the same duties under state law. The duties they owe to clients are set by Virginia Code. These duties cannot be changed or removed by agreement. The **brokerage agreement** clients sign will outline the scope of services. However, the basic agency duties remain the same for all licensees.

## Required Agency Duties

- All Virginia licensees must follow the same **agency duties** set by state law.
- These duties include loyalty, confidentiality, disclosure, obedience, reasonable care, and accounting.
- The **brokerage agreement** defines the services provided but cannot change these basic duties.
- This ensures consistent protection for consumers in all **real estate transactions**.

Example: When you work with any Virginia real estate licensee, they must provide fiduciary responsibilities as required by Virginia law. These duties are automatically part of the relationship and cannot be removed from your brokerage agreement.
