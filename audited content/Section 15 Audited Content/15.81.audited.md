# Brokerage Relationships: Commencement and Termination

<!-- As you delve into the intricacies of real estate, understanding how **brokerage relationships** commence and terminate is fundamental. Let's explore these critical junctures together. -->

Understanding how **brokerage relationships** commence and terminate forms the basis of professional practice.

## Beginning a Brokerage Relationship

<!-- A brokerage relationship officially starts when you, as a future real estate licensee, are engaged by a client. This professional commitment lasts until either: -->

A brokerage relationship officially starts when a licensee is engaged by a client. This professional commitment lasts until either:

- The goals outlined in the brokerage agreement are achieved, or
- It ends sooner under specific conditions, including:
  - Reaching the agreed expiration date in the contract
  - Mutual agreement to part ways
  - A breach of contract by any party
  - Certain situations prescribed by law

## The Brokerage Agreement

<!-- It's imperative that the **brokerage agreement** is in writing and includes key elements such as: -->

The **brokerage agreement** must be in writing and include elements such as:

- A clearly defined **termination date**
  - *Note:* If an exact end date is omitted, the agreement automatically terminates 90 days after signing.
- Detailed **fees** for brokerage services, specifying the amount, method, and timing of payment
- Enumeration of the services the licensee will provide
- Any other terms agreed upon with the client

In cases involving **dual representation**, specific **disclosures** must be made to ensure transparency and compliance with legal standards.

## Obligations Upon Termination

<!-- Upon termination, expiration, or completion of your services, your obligations to the client significantly reduce. However, two critical responsibilities persist: -->

Upon termination, expiration, or completion of services, obligations to the client reduce. However, two responsibilities persist:

1. **Accounting for all moneys and properties** related to the professional relationship.
2. Maintaining **confidentiality** of all personal, financial, or otherwise sensitive information learned during the brokerage relationship, including any data the client explicitly requested to be kept confidential.

These obligations remain unless:

- The law demands disclosure, or
- The client provides written consent to share specific information.
