# Duties Owed to Seller Customers When Representing Buyer Clients

<!-- When you work as a standard agent representing **buyer clients**, there are specific duties you owe to potential and actual **seller customers** in a residential transaction. You must understand these responsibilities. -->
When working as a standard agent representing **buyer clients**, specific duties are owed to potential and actual **seller customers** in a residential transaction. Understanding these responsibilities is necessary.

## Key Duties to Seller Customers

<!-- - **Honesty:** Treat seller customers with honesty by providing truthful information and avoiding any form of misleading statements. Honesty builds trust and is fundamental in all professional interactions, especially in real estate transactions. -->
- **Honesty:** Treat seller customers with honesty by providing truthful information and avoiding misleading statements. Honesty builds trust in all professional interactions, especially in real estate transactions.

<!-- - **Presenting Offers:** You have an obligation to present all offers from your buyer clients to the sellers in a timely manner. Quick communication of offers helps the transaction move forward. It also maintains transparency and fairness. -->
- **Presenting Offers:** Agents have an obligation to present all offers from buyer clients to sellers in a timely manner. Quick communication of offers helps transactions move forward and maintains transparency and fairness.

<!-- - **Disclosure of Material Facts:** Disclose any material facts that could influence the seller's decisions. This includes information that may affect the property's value or the seller's willingness to proceed with the transaction. Sharing this information maintains integrity and trust. -->
- **Disclosure of Material Facts:** Disclose any material facts that could influence seller decisions. This includes information that may affect property value or seller willingness to proceed with the transaction. Sharing this information maintains integrity and trust.

<!-- - **Accounting for Money and Property:** It is your responsibility to account for all money and property exchanged during the transaction. Losing or mishandling funds or property can lead to serious legal problems. It can also damage your professional reputation. -->
- **Accounting for Money and Property:** Agents are responsible for accounting for all money and property exchanged during transactions. Losing or mishandling funds or property can lead to serious legal problems and damage professional reputation.

<!-- - **Compliance with Laws:** Comply with all applicable state and federal laws related to the transaction. This includes real estate laws and other regulations about discrimination and fair treatment. -->
- **Compliance with Laws:** Comply with all applicable state and federal laws related to transactions. This includes real estate laws and other regulations about discrimination and fair treatment.

<!-- These duties underscore the importance of **professionalism**, **transparency**, and **ethics** when representing buyer clients in residential transactions. Following these responsibilities helps ensure smooth transactions. It also helps build lasting relationships with buyers and sellers. -->
These duties emphasize **professionalism**, **transparency**, and **ethics** when representing buyer clients in residential transactions. Following these responsibilities helps ensure smooth transactions and builds lasting relationships with buyers and sellers.
