# Responsibilities of Independent Contractor Brokers in Real Estate

<!-- Understanding different brokerage relationships is important in real estate. You need to know the responsibilities that come with each type. Let's focus on **independent contractor brokerage relationships**. -->
Understanding different brokerage relationships in real estate is necessary. Knowing the responsibilities that come with each type is required. This section focuses on **independent contractor brokerage relationships**.

## Duties of an Independent Contractor Broker

<!-- As an independent contractor broker, you are *not automatically bound* by all the duties typically expected when promoting the interests of clients. However, there are still several critical duties you must adhere to, including: -->
As an independent contractor broker, the broker is *not automatically bound* by all the duties typically expected when promoting the interests of clients. This only changes if they have explicitly agreed to take on such responsibilities in their **brokerage relationship disclosure**. However, there are still several duties they must adhere to, including:

- **Maintaining confidentiality** of client information
<!-- - Exercising **ordinary care** in your dealings
- **Accounting** for any money and property belonging to your client
- **Disclosing any material facts** that could influence decisions -->
- Exercising **ordinary care** in dealings
- **Accounting** for any money and property belonging to the client
- **Disclosing any material facts** that could influence decisions
- Ensuring compliance with **fair housing laws**

These obligations align with what standard and limited agents also owe to their clients.

## Treatment of Customers (Non-Clients)

<!-- When it comes to customers—those with whom you do *not* have an agency relationship—the expectations remain high. You should: -->
When it comes to customers - those with whom the broker does *not* have an agency relationship - the expectations remain high. The broker should:

- Always treat customers with **honesty**
- Avoid sharing **false information**
<!-- - Openly **disclose your brokerage relationship** with any non-clients who are unrepresented -->
- Openly **disclose the brokerage relationship** with any non-clients who are unrepresented

## Brokerage Relationship Disclosure Requirements

<!-- Your written brokerage relationship disclosure must clearly indicate that you are functioning as an **independent contractor broker**, *not* as an agent. This disclosure must outline the services you will provide. You must stick to what has been agreed upon. -->
The written brokerage relationship disclosure must clearly indicate that the broker is functioning as an **independent contractor broker**, *not* as an agent. This disclosure must outline the services to be provided. The broker must stick to what has been agreed upon.
