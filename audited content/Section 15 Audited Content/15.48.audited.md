# Misrepresentation vs. Puffing in Real Estate

## Distinction

Agents must understand the difference between making an **incorrect factual statement** (misrepresentation) and **puffing** (exaggerated opinion). Misrepresentation is prohibited under 18VAC135-20‑300, which defines actions constituting misrepresentation or omission, including knowingly making a material misrepresentation.

## Puffing

Puffing involves subjective opinions that a reasonable person would not rely on as factual, such as calling a property "the most beautiful house in the city." This is not considered a misrepresentation under Virginia law.

## Legal Implications

- **Misrepresentation** can lead to disciplinary action and liability under Virginia regulations.
- **Puffing** is generally permissible as long as it does not cross into false statements of fact.
