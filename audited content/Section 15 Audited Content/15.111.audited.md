# Confidentiality Duties When Representing a Seller

<!-- When you're representing a **seller** as their **agent**, you have a critical duty to keep all their **personal** and **financial information** completely **confidential**. This includes anything they share with you during your **brokerage relationship**. -->

Agents representing a **seller** have a duty to keep all their **personal** and **financial information** completely **confidential**. This includes anything they share during the **brokerage relationship**.

## Respecting Confidentiality Requests
- If the seller specifically asks that certain details remain confidential, agents must respect those requests.

## Exceptions to Confidentiality
The only exceptions to this rule are:

1. If the **law requires** agents to disclose such information.
2. If the seller gives the agent **written permission** to share it.

This practice of **confidentiality** protects the seller's **privacy** and forms part of agent **responsibilities**.
