# Establishing Client Relationships in Real Estate

<!-- When you're stepping into the world of real estate, understanding how to establish a relationship with your clients is crucial. One of the primary ways you'll form these connections is through something called an **express agreement**. -->

In real estate, understanding how to establish client relationships matters. One of the primary ways agents form these connections is through an **express agreement**.

## Express Agreement

<!-- An express agreement is a clear and definitive arrangement between you and your client, stating that you're working together with a specific goal in mind, like selling a piece of real estate. While you can agree verbally, it's always better to put everything in writing. This not only clarifies expectations but also protects both you and your client. -->

An express agreement is a clear arrangement between agents and clients, stating that they are working together with a specific goal in mind, like selling a piece of real estate. While parties can agree verbally, putting everything in writing is better. This clarifies expectations and protects both agents and clients.

## Listing Agreement

<!-- Think of a time when you might be helping a client sell their home. To officially represent them, you'll enter into what's known as a **listing agreement**. This document is the heart of your relationship, laying out the details of your arrangement, such as: -->

When helping clients sell their homes, agents must enter into a **listing agreement** to officially represent them. This document is the heart of the relationship, laying out arrangement details, such as:

<!-- - How long you'll be working together -->
<!-- - Your responsibilities as their agent -->

- How long the parties will be working together
- Agent responsibilities

<!-- Although you might work closely with other salespersons in your brokerage, the listing agreement ties the client directly to your **brokerage**, not to you personally as a salesperson. -->

Although agents might work closely with other salespersons in their brokerage, the listing agreement ties the client directly to the **brokerage**, not to the agent personally as a salesperson.

## Beginning Agency Relationships

<!-- Agency relationships begin the moment a client decides to work with you. However, it's important to understand the following: -->

Agency relationships begin the moment a client decides to work with an agent. However, understanding the following matters:

<!-- - **Payment does not automatically create a brokerage relationship.** Money changing hands without a clear agreement about representation means there's no official link between you and the client. -->
- **Payment does not automatically create a brokerage relationship.** Money changing hands without a clear agreement about representation means there's no official link between agent and client.
- Using tools like the **Multiple Listing Service (MLS)** to find properties for a buyer does **not** mean the agent is in a brokerage relationship with the sellers listed in the MLS.

<!-- In essence, you can assist a buyer in finding potential homes through the MLS without representing the sellers of those homes. -->

Agents can assist buyers in finding potential homes through the MLS without representing the sellers of those homes.

<!-- By grasping these concepts, you're laying a strong foundation for establishing clear and effective relationships with your clients. Remember, **clarity and documentation** are your allies in avoiding misunderstandings and ensuring both you and your clients have a positive experience. -->

Understanding these concepts lays a foundation for establishing clear and effective client relationships. **Clarity and documentation** avoid misunderstandings and ensure positive experiences for both agents and clients.
