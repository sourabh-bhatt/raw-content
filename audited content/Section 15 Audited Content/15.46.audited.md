# Referral Fees and Written Consent in Real Estate

When a real estate agent receives a **referral fee** from a **home inspector** because they referred them to a client, Virginia law requires **written consent** from the client and **full disclosure** to all parties before the referral is made. This aligns with 18VAC135-20‑e (negotiating referral fees) and the licensing law provision that permits such arrangements only with proper client authorization.
