# Professional Responsibilities When Representing a Buyer

<!-- When you're working as a real estate agent for a buyer, it's important to understand your professional responsibilities. Here's what you need to do: -->
When working as a real estate agent for a buyer, understanding professional responsibilities is necessary. The following outlines these responsibilities:

## 1. Follow the Brokerage Agreement

<!-- - Everything you do should align with the terms laid out in the agreement you have with your buyer. -->
- All actions should align with the terms laid out in the agreement with the buyer.

<!-- ## 2. Act in Your Buyer's Best Interest -->
## 2. Act in the Buyer's Best Interest

This includes:

<!-- - **Finding properties** that meet your buyer's specifications in terms of type, price, and terms. Remember, you're not required to look for other properties if your buyer is already under contract, unless it's part of your agreement.
- **Assisting with the creation and negotiation** of offers, counteroffers, and any amendments or addenda to the contract. Your goal is to help your buyer achieve their objectives.
- **Promptly handling all offers and counteroffers** between the buyer and the seller, even if a contract is already in place.
- **Helping your buyer fulfill their contractual obligations** and supporting them up to the settlement of the purchase. -->
- **Finding properties** that meet the buyer's specifications in terms of type, price, and terms. Agents are not required to look for other properties if the buyer is already under contract, unless it's part of the agreement.
- **Assisting with the creation and negotiation** of offers, counteroffers, and any amendments or addenda to the contract. The goal is to help the buyer achieve their objectives.
- **Promptly handling all offers and counteroffers** between the buyer and the seller, even if a contract is already in place.
- **Helping the buyer fulfill their contractual obligations** and supporting them up to the settlement of the purchase.

## 3. Maintain Confidentiality

<!-- - Protect all personal and financial information provided by your client during your working relationship.
- Only disclose this information if the law requires it or if your buyer gives written consent. -->
- Protect all personal and financial information provided by the client during the working relationship.
- Only disclose this information if the law requires it or if the buyer gives written consent.

## 4. Exercise Care

<!-- - Show a standard level of professionalism and carefulness in your work. -->
- Show a standard level of professionalism and carefulness in work.

## 5. Account for Money and Property

<!-- - Ensure you manage and report any money or property belonging to your buyer or held on their behalf in a timely manner. -->
- Ensure proper management and reporting of any money or property belonging to the buyer or held on their behalf in a timely manner.

## 6. Disclose Material Facts

<!-- - If you're aware of any important details about the property or the transaction that could affect your buyer's decision, you must share this information with them. -->
- If aware of any details about the property or the transaction that could affect the buyer's decision, agents must share this information with them.

## 7. Comply with Laws and Regulations

- Follow all relevant laws, including **fair housing laws**, in residential real estate transactions.

- When dealing with prospective sellers, honesty is required. Do not provide false information.
- Specifically, if aware of **defective drywall** in a property, this must be disclosed as required by Virginia law.
- Inform the seller if the buyer plans to occupy the property as their main residence.
- Agents may assist the seller with minor tasks unless doing so is against the law or the brokerage agreement. These tasks should not interfere with the commitment to the buyer.
- Showing properties the buyer is interested in to other potential buyers, or working with other buyers interested in the same or different properties, **does not violate obligations** to the buyer.
- Representing sellers for other properties also **does not constitute a breach of duty**.
- Always **reveal brokerage relationships** in residential transactions as required by law.
