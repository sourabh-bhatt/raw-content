- **Salespersons** or **associate brokers** receive compensation directly from the **principal broker**.

- The payment flow is as follows: the **client pays the broker**, and then the **broker pays the agent**.

## Negotiating Your Compensation

Negotiating compensation takes place between agents and the **principal broker**. This discussion determines payment for transactions or services provided.
