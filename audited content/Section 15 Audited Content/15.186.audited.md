# Managing Escrow Accounts and Avoiding Commingling

<!-- When handling **escrow accounts**, it is essential to carefully manage these funds to avoid **commingling**. -->

When handling **escrow accounts**, agents must carefully manage these funds to avoid **commingling**. 

## Understanding Commingling

- Commingling occurs when escrow funds are mixed with personal or business funds.
- Escrow accounts must be separate interest-bearing accounts.
- For example, if agents use escrow funds to purchase a **certificate of deposit**, commingling happens if:
  - They pledge these certificates,
  - Or if the **principal broker** does not directly control the original certificate.
