# Disclosures in Real Estate Transactions

All **disclosures** must be in **writing** and **signed by all parties**. This shows that everyone received and understood the disclosure. Agents must make most of these disclosures **before entering into a brokerage agreement or providing brokerage services**. This step maintains **transparency** and upholds the principles of **fairness** in their dealings.
