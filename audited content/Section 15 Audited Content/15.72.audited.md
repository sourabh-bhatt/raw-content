# Understanding Procuring Cause in Real Estate Transactions

<!-- When working in real estate transactions, understanding the concept of **_procuring cause_** is crucial. -->
When working in real estate transactions, understanding the concept of **_procuring cause_** is necessary. 

## What is Procuring Cause?

Sometimes there is a dispute over which broker brought a buyer who is ready, willing, and able to purchase. The heart of the matter lies in figuring out which broker is the rightful **procuring cause** of the sale.

## Why Procuring Cause Matters

<!-- - **Listing agreements**, the contracts that bind sellers to their agents, promise **compensation** to you, the agent, if you are indeed the procuring cause of the sale. -->
- **Listing agreements**, the contracts that bind sellers to their agents, promise **compensation** to the agent if they are indeed the procuring cause of the sale.
- Being the procuring cause means initiating a sequence of events that, without interruption, leads directly to the sale or lease of a property.

## Legal Guidance and Practical Implications

- Virginia law and the Virginia Real Estate Board guide the determination of procuring cause, but the essence remains the same: if a broker's actions led directly to the sale without interruption, they have established themselves as the procuring cause.
