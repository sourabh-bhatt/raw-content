# Representing Landlords: Disclosure and Liability Responsibilities

## Duty to Disclose Physical Condition
<!-- When representing **landlords** as an agent, you have a key duty to ensure the **tenant** is fully aware of any significant negative aspects related to the property's **physical condition** that you are already aware of. -->
When representing **landlords** as an agent, agents have a duty to ensure the **tenant** is fully aware of any significant negative aspects related to the property's **physical condition** that they are already aware of. 

- *Physical condition* refers specifically to the actual state of the land and any structures on it.
- This duty **does not** extend to issues beyond the property's borders or related to neighboring lands.
<!-- - You are **not required** to disclose matters concerning government land use policies or information about public roads and highways. -->
- Agents are **not required** to disclose matters concerning government land use policies or information about public roads and highways.

## Liability and Passing Along Information
<!-- It is critical to understand when liability applies to you as an agent: -->
Understanding when liability applies to agents matters:

<!-- - You are **not liable** if you pass along incorrect information to the tenant under these conditions: -->
- Agents are **not liable** if they pass along incorrect information to the tenant under these conditions:
  <!-- - The landlord provided you with inaccurate details. -->
  - The landlord provided them with inaccurate details.
  - The misinformation originated from a governmental body or a licensed professional (e.g., architect or engineer) recognized in the Commonwealth.
  <!-- - You had no knowledge that the information was wrong and did not willfully ignore the truth. -->
  - They had no knowledge that the information was wrong and did not willfully ignore the truth.

## Legal Obligations and Tenant Rights
<!-- - There may be situations where the law **requires** you to disclose specific details; fulfilling these obligations does **not** expose you to liability. -->
- There may be situations where the law **requires** agents to disclose specific details; fulfilling these obligations does **not** expose them to liability.
- Prospective tenants retain the right to personally **inspect** the physical state of the property.
