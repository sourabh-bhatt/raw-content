# Referral Fees and Consent in Real Estate Transactions

<!-- When you're engaging in **real estate transactions**, you might have opportunities to accept **referral fees** for sharing **client information**. This information can include details about the property, parties involved, or financial aspects. -->

In **real estate transactions**, licensees may have opportunities to accept **referral fees** for sharing **client information**. This information can include details about the property, parties involved, or financial aspects.

## Key Guidelines for Accepting Referral Fees

<!-- - Before proceeding, you **must gain written consent from your principal broker**. -->
<!-- - You **must also obtain written consent from all clients** whose information you plan to share. -->

- Before proceeding, licensees **must gain written consent from their principal broker**.
- Licensees **must also obtain written consent from all clients** whose information they plan to share.
- **Referral fees can only be paid to licensed real estate professionals**.
<!-- - This protocol ensures **transparency** and maintains the **integrity of the brokerage** you're associated with. -->
- This protocol ensures **transparency** and maintains the **integrity of the brokerage**.

## Responsibilities of an Active Licensee

<!-- Whether you are a **salesperson** or a **broker**, it is your responsibility to: -->

**Salespersons** and **brokers** have the responsibility to:

- Adhere to this guideline.
- Ensure all actions comply with the **professional standards** expected in the real estate industry.
- Follow **Virginia Real Estate Board regulations** regarding referral fees.

Example: If you receive a referral fee offer for sharing information about a property's financial terms, you must first obtain written approval from your principal broker AND written consent from your clients before accepting the fee. This protects both you and your brokerage from potential ethical violations.
