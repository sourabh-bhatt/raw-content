# Managing Trust Monies in Real Estate Brokerage

<!-- When you work in **brokerage** and **agency work**, particularly in real estate, one of your key responsibilities will revolve around **trust monies**. You'll usually be the one in charge of the **escrow account**, which means you must handle it with the utmost care and integrity. -->

In **brokerage** and **agency work**, particularly in real estate, a primary responsibility involves managing **trust monies**. The agent typically oversees the **escrow account** and must handle it with care and integrity.

## Responsibilities in Handling Trust Monies

- **Safeguarding Funds:** Keep the funds safe and maintain detailed records of every transaction.
- **Record Keeping:** Maintain transparent and accurate records to ensure accountability.
