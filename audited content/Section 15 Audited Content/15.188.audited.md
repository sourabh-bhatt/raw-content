# Representing Sellers as Standard Agents

When representing **sellers** as **standard agents**, agents have the option (though it's not mandatory) to assist actual or potential **buyers** with everyday **administrative tasks**. These tasks are considered minor and do not involve giving **material advice** or significant support, provided there is no law or **contractual agreement** within the brokerage that prohibits such assistance.

### Key Points About Administrative Tasks

- These tasks are limited to routine, non-advisory activities.
- Performing these tasks alone does **not** establish a **brokerage relationship** with the buyer.
<!-- - It is essential to be aware of any brokerage policies or legal restrictions that may apply. -->
- Agents should be aware of any brokerage policies or legal restrictions that may apply.
