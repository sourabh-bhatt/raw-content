# Exclusive Agency Listing Agreement in Brokerage and Agency Contracts

<!-- When diving into the responsibilities within brokerage and agency contracts, let's focus on an essential concept: the **Exclusive Agency Listing Agreement**. -->

Within brokerage and agency contracts, the **Exclusive Agency Listing Agreement** is a concept worth examining.

## Key Features of the Exclusive Agency Listing Agreement

- The agent represents a seller who chooses them as their **exclusive agent**.
<!-- - If you manage to sell the property, you are entitled to a commission. If any other broker achieves the sale, **you are also entitled to a commission**. -->
- If the agent manages to sell the property, they are entitled to a commission. If any other broker achieves the sale, **the agent is also entitled to a commission**.
<!-- - However, if the owner decides to sell the property personally, bypassing any brokers, **they are not required to pay any commission**. -->
- However, if the owner decides to sell the property personally, bypassing any brokers, **they are not required to pay any commission**.

## Differences from Open Listing Agreement

- The exclusive agency agreement **must be in writing**, reinforcing its seriousness.
- It is a **two-way agreement**, committing both the seller and the agent to specific responsibilities.
<!-- - It is tailored to **personal service**, meaning you cannot simply delegate this duty to someone else. -->
- It is tailored to **personal service**, meaning agents cannot simply delegate this duty to someone else.
- The agreement must have a **definite expiration date**, emphasizing urgency and commitment from both parties.

## Executory Nature of the Agreement

<!-- - The agreement is **executory**, meaning its full potential is realized only if you, as the broker, bring in a buyer. -->
- The agreement is **executory**, meaning its full potential is realized only if the broker brings in a buyer.
- Only then do the obligations tied to the commission become activated.
<!-- - This arrangement encourages you to actively seek potential buyers to fulfill your part of the agreement successfully. -->
- This arrangement encourages brokers to actively seek potential buyers to fulfill their part of the agreement.

**Example:** If an agent is the exclusive agent and finds a buyer, they earn the commission. If another broker finds the buyer, the exclusive agent still earns the commission. But if the seller sells the property on their own, no commission is owed.
