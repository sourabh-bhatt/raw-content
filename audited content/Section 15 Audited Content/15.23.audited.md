## Payment Procedures for Salespersons

- In Virginia, the law prohibits **salespersons** from directly accepting payment from clients.
<!-- - Any payment received must be handed over to your **employing broker**, who acts as your **principal** in this relationship. -->
- Any payment received must be handed over to the **employing broker**, who acts as **principal** in this relationship.
- Virginia Real Estate Board regulations require that all salesperson compensation must be paid through the employing broker.

This guideline ensures:

- **Transparency** in transactions
- **Accountability** for all parties involved
