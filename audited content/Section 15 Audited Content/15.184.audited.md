# Disclosure of Brokerage Relationship in Residential Real Estate Transactions

<!-- When you work in real estate transactions, you must understand **disclosure of the brokerage relationship**. As a **salesperson** or **broker**, you must clearly tell people in writing whom you represent. You must do this at the first contact for business. -->

When working in real estate transactions, agents must understand **disclosure of the brokerage relationship**. As a **salesperson** or **broker**, agents must clearly tell people in writing whom they represent. Agents must do this at the first contact for business.

## Initial Disclosure Requirements

<!-- - If you talk about specific property details with someone, you must tell them who you represent. -->
- If agents talk about specific property details with someone, they must tell them who they represent.
- This initial disclosure can be **verbal** when agents first realize the conversation involves specific property details.

## Written Disclosure Requirement

- Do not rely only on verbal disclosure.
<!-- - You must provide this written disclosure at the first contact for business. -->
- Agents must provide this written disclosure at the first contact for business.
<!-- - You must provide written disclosure before showing property, preparing a CMA, or providing other real estate services. -->
- Agents must provide written disclosure before showing property, preparing a CMA, or providing other real estate services.

## Disclosure Requirements

- This process ensures **clarity and fairness** in the transaction. It protects the interests of all parties involved.
<!-- - It shows your professionalism and adherence to the **ethical standards** expected in the real estate industry. -->
- It shows agent professionalism and adherence to the **ethical standards** expected in the real estate industry.
