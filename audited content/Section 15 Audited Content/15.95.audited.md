Agents navigating brokerage and agency contracts must understand the **responsibilities owed by agents to their clients**. This understanding ensures legal and ethical adherence. These foundational duties can be remembered using the acronym **"OLD CAR."**

## The OLD CAR Duties Explained

1. **Obedience**
   - When agents are given lawful instructions by clients, the duty is to follow them meticulously.
   - Actions must align with client directions, provided they don't conflict with legal statutes or ethical standards.

2. **Loyalty**
   - Agent commitment should be to client interests, placing them above all others.
   - Avoid any conflicts of interest.
   - Ensure that any information that could benefit the client is communicated to them promptly.

3. **Disclosure**
   - Agents have the duty of keeping clients fully informed.
   - This includes any facts or information that could influence the client's decision-making process or transaction outcomes.
   - Examples include disclosing property defects or revealing your commission sources.
   - Transparency is key.

4. **Confidentiality**
   - Protecting client private information is part of the agent role.
   - This duty extends beyond the contract duration, meaning even after the professional relationship ends, client information remains confidential.
   - Agents must be vigilant against unintentionally sharing sensitive information that could disadvantage clients.

5. **Accountability**
   - When handling client money or property, meticulousness is required.
   - Whether earnest money, down payments, or other financial aspects, every transaction must be accurately recorded.
   - Manage all funds with the highest level of integrity.

6. **Reasonable Care and Diligence**
   - Agents have the responsibility of being knowledgeable and well-informed about the market and all pertinent legal procedures.
   - Advice should be based on thorough research and due diligence.
   - Safeguard client interests throughout every step of the process.
