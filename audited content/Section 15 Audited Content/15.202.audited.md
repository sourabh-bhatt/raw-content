# Customers in Brokerage and Agency Relationships

<!-- In the context of **brokerage and agency contract responsibilities**, it's important to understand the distinction between **customers** and **clients**. -->

In the context of **brokerage and agency contract responsibilities**, understanding the distinction between **customers** and **clients** matters.

## Definition of a Customer

<!-- Imagine you are involved in a real estate transaction but do **not** have a brokerage relationship with a particular broker. In this scenario, you are considered a **customer**. -->
Consider someone involved in a real estate transaction but who does **not** have a brokerage relationship with a particular broker. In this scenario, they are considered a **customer**.

<!-- As an agent, you may still perform specific tasks for this customer, known as **ministerial acts**. These are basic duties that you can carry out without implying a **fiduciary relationship** or advocacy on behalf of the customer. -->
As an agent, they may still perform specific tasks for this customer, known as **ministerial acts**. These are basic duties that agents can carry out without implying a **fiduciary relationship** or advocacy on behalf of the customer.

## Example

<!-- - If you represent a **seller** in a transaction, any potential **buyer** who does not have an **agency agreement** with you is classified as a **customer**. -->
- If agents represent a **seller** in a transaction, any potential **buyer** who does not have an **agency agreement** with them is classified as a **customer**.

## The Distinction

<!-- - This distinction is crucial because it highlights that you owe a different level of duty to a customer compared to a **client**. -->
- This distinction highlights that agents owe a different level of duty to a customer compared to a **client**.
<!-- - A **client** is someone you are contractually obligated to represent. -->
- A **client** is someone agents are contractually obligated to represent.
<!-- - Understanding this difference helps you navigate your responsibilities effectively and ensures you maintain professional integrity and compliance with real estate regulations. -->
- Understanding this difference helps agents navigate their responsibilities effectively and ensures they maintain professional integrity and compliance with real estate regulations.
