# Responsibilities After the End of a Brokerage Relationship

Agents must understand their responsibilities after the end of a brokerage relationship. Unless the agent and client have agreed on different terms in writing, obligations conclude once the agreement is terminated, reaches its expiration, or the brokerage duties have been fully performed. However, there are two duties that continue beyond the termination of the agreement:

## Continuing Duties After Termination

1. **Accounting for Money and Property**
   - Agents must ensure that all money and property related to the brokerage relationship are fully accounted for.
   - This means keeping track of any funds, properties, or other assets involved in the relationship with the client.
   - These assets must be handled or returned according to the agreement or applicable law.

2. **Maintaining Confidentiality**
   - Any personal or financial information received from the client during the brokerage relationship must be kept confidential.
   - This includes information given in confidence and any other details the client specifically requested to keep private.
   - The only exceptions are if the law requires disclosure or if the client provides written consent to share the information.

Fulfilling these duties protects client interests and maintains professional standards. The agent role extends beyond executing transactions; it involves respecting and safeguarding client privacy and assets, even after direct involvement has concluded.
