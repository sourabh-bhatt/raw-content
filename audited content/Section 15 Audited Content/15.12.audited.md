# Buyer Representation Agreements: Exclusive Right to Represent vs. Exclusive Buyer Representation

When helping clients purchase a property, agents encounter two primary forms of agreements for representing buyers exclusively. These are known as the **exclusive right to represent buyer agreement** and the **exclusive buyer representation agreement**.

## Exclusive Right to Represent Buyer Agreement

With an **exclusive right to represent buyer agreement**, the client agrees to work solely with the agent for their property hunting needs. However, there is an exception:

- If the client finds a property on their own without agent assistance, they may purchase it without owing the agent a commission.

This agreement establishes the agent as the primary guide in the real estate journey, while clients retain the freedom to explore independently.

## Exclusive Buyer Representation Agreement

The **exclusive buyer representation agreement** is more definitive in establishing the agent's role as the sole professional advocate for the client. Key points include:

- The agent is guaranteed a commission regardless of how the property is found — whether through agent efforts, client efforts, or another source.
- This agreement reflects a deeper commitment from the client to rely entirely on agent expertise and services throughout their buying process.
- The commission amount and terms must be clearly specified in writing as required by Virginia law.

## These Agreements in Practice

Both types of agreements are tools in professional practice. They:

- Shape how agents approach client representation
- Influence negotiation strategies
- Determine how agents will be compensated for their work and dedication

Understanding and navigating these agreements helps agents provide service to clients while ensuring their efforts are compensated.
