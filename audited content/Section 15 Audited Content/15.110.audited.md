# Understanding the Principal in Transactions

<!-- When diving into the world of real estate transactions, it's crucial to understand **who the principal to a transaction is**. -->

Professionals must understand **who the principal to a transaction is**. 

## Definition of Principal

A *principal* refers to any party directly involved in the real estate deal. This includes:

- **Seller**
- **Buyer**
- **Landlord**
- **Tenant**
- Someone holding or granted an option, known respectively as an **optionor** or **optionee**

## Role of Brokers Under Virginia License Law

Under **Virginia License Law**, being a **listing broker** or **selling broker** does *not* make the broker a principal in the transaction. Instead:

- The broker's role is defined by the **brokerage relationship**.
- This distinction separates brokers from the direct parties involved in the deal.

Understanding this boundary supports effective performance of responsibilities without overstepping the line that separates broker duties from those of represented clients.
