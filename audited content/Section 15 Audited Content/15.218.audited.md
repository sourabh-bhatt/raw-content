# Agent Authority and Responsibilities

<!-- As a **real estate agent**, you have the authority to **represent** clients. However, you cannot bind clients to **agreements** without their consent. This role is critical as it involves: -->

As a **real estate agent**, agents have the authority to **represent** clients. However, agents cannot bind clients to **agreements** without their consent. This role involves:

- Acting on behalf of others
- Helping clients make informed decisions
- Helping clients enter into **contracts** with proper authorization

<!-- Understanding the depth of this **responsibility** is crucial for effectively managing your duties and maintaining the trust placed in you by those you represent. -->

Understanding the depth of this **responsibility** helps effectively manage duties and maintain the trust placed in agents by those they represent.
