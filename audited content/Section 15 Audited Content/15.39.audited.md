# Disclosure and Confidentiality in Brokerage Relationships

<!-- When you're embarking on a **brokerage** or **agency relationship**, it's crucial to understand the importance of **disclosure**. -->
When embarking on a **brokerage** or **agency relationship**, understanding **disclosure** is necessary. 

## Responsibility to Maintain Confidentiality

<!-- - You must **not disclose confidential information** received from your client to other parties without permission.

- Agents must **not disclose confidential information** received from clients to other parties without permission.
- This rule applies **even if the client does not explicitly ask for secrecy**.
- If any information is confided with a specific request for confidentiality, agents **must honor this request** and keep that information away from the other party involved in the transaction.
- Confidentiality obligations **continue even after the brokerage relationship ends**.
- Agents may only disclose confidential information if **required by law** or with **written client consent**.

- Be particularly vigilant **before any confidential information is exchanged**.
- Most disclosures need to be **clarified and agreed upon at the beginning** of the brokerage relationship.
