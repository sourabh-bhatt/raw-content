## Obligations in Listing Agreements

<!-- In real estate, when you work with **listing agreements**, you have several obligations. Key points include: -->

Agents working with **listing agreements** have several obligations. Key points include:

- The **principal** (the client) holds the ultimate power to:
  - Decide on the **listing price**
  - Accept or reject any offer

<!-- - As an agent, especially if you're acting as a **special agent**, you are required to **present all offers** to the principal. This ensures: -->

- Agents, especially those acting as **special agents**, are required to **present all offers** to the principal. This ensures:
  - **Transparency**
  <!-- - Maintenance of the **trust** placed in you as the representative -->
  - Maintenance of the **trust** placed in the representative

<!-- Your role is not just about facilitating transactions but upholding the highest standard of **integrity** and **authenticity** throughout the process. -->

The agent's role involves facilitating transactions while upholding the highest standard of **integrity** and **authenticity** throughout the process.
