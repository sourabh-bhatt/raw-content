## Individual Licensing Requirements

<!-- - If you or anyone else who owns the business or holds an officer position actively engages in **real estate transactions**, each person must hold an individual license as a **salesperson** or **broker**. -->

- If anyone who owns the business or holds an officer position actively engages in **real estate transactions**, each person must hold an individual license as a **salesperson** or **broker**.
- Having a **business entity license** does *not* eliminate the need for each involved individual to have their own license.
- A business entity can have multiple licensed participants.
