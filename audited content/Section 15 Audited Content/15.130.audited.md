# Duties of a Standard Agent Representing Landlords in Residential Transactions

<!-- As a **standard agent** who represents **landlords**, you have specific duties to actual or prospective **tenants** in a residential transaction. -->

**Standard agents** who represent **landlords** have specific duties to actual or prospective **tenants** in residential transactions.

## Key Responsibilities

- **Honesty and Fairness**
  - Treat all tenant customers with honesty and fairness.
  - This means all interactions must be conducted with integrity.

- **Disclosure of Material Defects**
  <!-- - Disclose all **material defects** in the property that are known to you. -->
  <!-- - You must tell tenants about any issues or flaws that could affect their decision to lease the property. -->
  - Disclose all **material defects** in the property that are known to the agent.
  - Agents must tell tenants about any issues or flaws that could affect their decision to lease the property.
  - This helps tenants make informed decisions.

- **Confidentiality**
  - Maintain confidentiality regarding any discussions, negotiations, or information shared by the tenant that could affect their bargaining position.
  <!-- - You still must disclose known property defects. But you cannot use personal information the tenant shares against them in negotiations. -->
  - Agents still must disclose known property defects. However, agents cannot use personal information the tenant shares against them in negotiations.

<!-- Following these duties helps create fair transactions and successful **rental agreements**. Maintaining these standards benefits the tenant and reinforces your reputation as a trustworthy and reliable agent in the industry. -->

Following these duties helps create fair transactions and successful **rental agreements**. Maintaining these standards benefits tenants and reinforces agent reputation as trustworthy and reliable in the industry.
