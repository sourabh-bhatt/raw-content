# Managing Real Estate Advertising: Compliance Guidelines

Virginia regulations require that all real estate advertising be supervised by the principal or supervising broker. Advertisements must prominently display the firm's licensed name and include any required disclosures. The guidelines apply across all media types, including internet, print, and broadcast, to ensure transparency and compliance.
