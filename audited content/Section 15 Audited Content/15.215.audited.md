When learning about **brokerage** and **agency contracts**, understanding **liabilities** and **responsibilities** matters. This includes understanding the actions of other **agents** associated with the brokerage. As **salespersons** working under a broker, they represent the broker and work with clients according to their agency agreements. This means the broker shares responsibility for their actions while they are working within their licensed capacity. Understanding this relationship and its implications helps navigate responsibilities effectively.
