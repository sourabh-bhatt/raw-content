In **brokerage and agency contracts**, understanding the concept of **fiduciary responsibility** matters. This involves a role where someone places trust and confidence in an agent - the core of being an **agent** for a **principal**. The law, derived from centuries of common practice, outlines specific **fiduciary duties** that agents owe to clients.

In interactions and decisions, agents are expected to act with **loyalty**, **integrity**, and in the **best interest** of the person who has entrusted them with their affairs. This involves more than completing transactions; it means embodying the trust someone places in agents to manage aspects of their financial or personal life. Prioritizing client interests above agent interests or anyone else's is the guiding principle. This understanding guides agents as they navigate their responsibilities in this entrusted position.
