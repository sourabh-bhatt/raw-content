# Representing Landlord Clients: The Brokerage Agreement

<!-- When you're representing **landlord clients**, it's crucial that you always act in accordance with the terms laid out in the **brokerage agreement**. -->

Agents representing **landlord clients** must act in accordance with the terms laid out in the **brokerage agreement**. 

## Brokerage Agreement Overview

- This document outlines what is expected of the agent.
- It ensures that both agent and client understand the **responsibilities**.
