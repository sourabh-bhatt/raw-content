# Handling Third-Party Funds in Real Estate Transactions

<!-- When handling funds entrusted to you by a third party, such as **security deposits** or **earnest money deposits**, you are required to deposit these amounts into a **trust account**. -->

When handling funds entrusted by a third party, such as **security deposits** or **earnest money deposits**, agents are required to deposit these amounts into a **trust account**.

## Legal Requirements and Timeframes

<!-- - **Virginia law** requires you to deposit these funds into an **escrow account** by the end of the **fifth banking day** after you receive them. -->
- **Virginia law** requires agents to deposit these funds into an **escrow account** by the end of the **fifth banking day** after they receive them.
