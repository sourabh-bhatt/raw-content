# Exclusive Buyer Agency Agreement

<!-- Understanding the **Exclusive Buyer Agency Agreement** is essential for representing buyers effectively in real estate transactions. This agreement establishes an **exclusive buyer agency relationship** between you, the real estate agent, and a prospective buyer. -->

The **Exclusive Buyer Agency Agreement** establishes an **exclusive buyer agency relationship** between the agent and a prospective buyer.

## Key Features of the Exclusive Buyer Agency Agreement

- Once the agreement is signed, the buyer is obligated to compensate the agent if they purchase a property that matches the description outlined in the contract.
- This obligation applies regardless of how the buyer finds the propertyâ€”whether independently or through another agent.

## Relationship to Seller Representation

The **Exclusive Buyer Agency Agreement** serves as the buyer's counterpart to the seller's **Exclusive Right-to-Sell Listing Agreement**. Both agreements:

- Define clear expectations and responsibilities between parties.
- Operate on opposite sides of the transaction.
