# Confidentiality in Representing a Landlord

<!-- As a **real estate agent** representing a **landlord**, it's crucial to understand the importance of **confidentiality**. Any **personal** and **financial information** you receive from the landlord during your **brokerage relationship** must be kept confidential. -->

As a **real estate agent** representing a **landlord**, understanding **confidentiality** matters. Any **personal** and **financial information** agents receive from the landlord during the **brokerage relationship** must be kept confidential.

## Scope of Confidentiality

<!-- - This confidentiality applies not only to information you are directly told but also to any other information the landlord requests to be kept private. -->
- This confidentiality applies not only to information agents are directly told but also to any other information the landlord requests to be kept private.
- Maintaining confidentiality is mandatory unless:
  - The law requires disclosure, or
  <!-- - You have **written consent** from the landlord to share specific information. -->
  - Agents have **written consent** from the landlord to share specific information.
