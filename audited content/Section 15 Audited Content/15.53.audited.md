# Disclosure of Representation in Brokerage and Agency Contracts

Virginia law requires agents to provide a **Disclosure of Brokerage Relationship** form to clients at the first substantive contact about a specific property. This disclosure explains the types of representation available and must be given in writing before any dual agency arrangement.
