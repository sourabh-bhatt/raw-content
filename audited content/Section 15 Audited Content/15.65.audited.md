# Understanding Brokerage and Agency Contracts in Virginia

<!-- ## Key Areas to Know

## Areas to Know
When studying **brokerage and agency contracts**, understanding two areas is necessary:

- The **common law of agency**, which is part of the national portion of the exam.
- The specific **Virginia statutes** regarding agency, which will be on the state portion of the exam.

## Virginia Law vs. Common Law of Agency
<!-- Virginia law can sometimes differ from or outright replace parts of the common law of agency. Whenever there is a conflict between the two, **Virginia law takes precedence**. However, understanding both is essential for your exams and your real estate practice. -->
Virginia law can sometimes differ from or outright replace parts of the common law of agency. Whenever there is a conflict between the two, **Virginia law takes precedence**. However, understanding both is necessary for exams and real estate practice.

## The Common Law of Agency
<!-- The common law of agency establishes the foundation for the relationship between you, as an agent, and your clients. It covers: -->
The common law of agency establishes the foundation for the relationship between agents and their clients. It covers:

- **Responsibilities** of the agent
- **Rights** of both agent and client
- Expectations of **confidentiality**
- Duty of **loyalty**

## Virginia-Specific Rules
Virginia law may introduce specific rules or requirements that adjust or expand on these general principles. For example:

- Special **disclosures** that must be made to clients
- Specific ways to handle certain situations that differ from the common law
