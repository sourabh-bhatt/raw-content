# Honesty and Integrity When Representing Tenant Clients

<!-- When representing **tenant clients**, agents must always treat **prospective landlords** with *honesty*. Never knowingly provide false information to them. When representing **tenant clients**, agents must always treat **prospective landlords** with *honesty*. Never knowingly provide false information to them. This principle forms the cornerstone of **professional integrity** and fosters **trust** and **reliability** in relationships with both tenants and landlords.

<!-- Remember, **honesty** is not just a moral obligation; it is a fundamental part of your role as an agent, ensuring that all interactions and transactions are conducted **fairly** and **transparently**. -->

**Honesty** is a moral obligation and a part of the agent role, ensuring that all interactions and transactions are conducted **fairly** and **transparently**.
