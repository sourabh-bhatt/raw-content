# Fiduciary Duties and Disclosure in Brokerage and Agency

Real estate agents must understand **fiduciary duties** towards clients. One aspect of these duties is the requirement to **disclose all relevant information** that could affect the transaction. Agents must disclose all material facts they know or should have known. This includes facts, rumors, or information that might affect the transaction.

## Understanding Material Information

"Material information" covers a broad spectrum, including but not limited to:

- The existence of any **offers on the table** or the potential for offers to come in.
- Indications that a buyer is open to **increasing their offer**.
- Details about the **financial conditions** of the opposing party in the transaction.

All such information must be disclosed to clients to ensure they are fully informed.

## Disclosure of Compensation and Comparable Sales

Additional disclosures include:

- Any arrangements related to the **sharing of compensation** between brokers or agents involved in the transaction.
- Information about **sales of similar properties**. This information can influence client decisions.

## Upholding Transparency and Ethical Standards

Agents uphold principles of **transparency** and **honesty**. Agents must ensure clients are fully informed at every stage of the transaction. This approach:

- Fosters **trust** between agents and clients.
- Ensures compliance with the **ethical guidelines** set forth for real estate professionals.

Following these responsibilities protects client interests. It also enhances agent **reputation** and **integrity**.
