# Responsibilities of Real Estate Licensees Working with Landlords

<!-- When you, as a **real estate licensee**, are engaged by a **landlord**, it's important to understand that your responsibilities towards them do not prevent you from serving other clients effectively. -->

When **real estate licensees** are engaged by a **landlord**, their responsibilities towards them do not prevent them from serving other clients effectively.

## Serving Multiple Clients

<!-- - Even if you're working exclusively for a landlord, you are **not breaking any rules** or failing in your duties if you show different properties to prospective tenants. -->
<!-- - You may represent properties that belong to **other landlords**. -->
<!-- - You must provide proper brokerage relationship disclosure to all parties as required by Virginia law. -->

- Even if agents are working exclusively for a landlord, they are **not breaking any rules** or failing in their duties if they show different properties to prospective tenants.
- Agents may represent properties that belong to **other landlords**.
- Agents must provide proper brokerage relationship disclosure to all parties as required by Virginia law.

## Industry Practice and Professionalism

- This practice is standard in the real estate industry.
<!-- - Understanding this helps you navigate your role with confidence. -->
- Understanding this helps agents navigate their role with confidence.
<!-- - It ensures you can cater to the needs of **multiple clients simultaneously** without conflict or compromise in your professional obligations. -->
- It ensures agents can cater to the needs of **multiple clients simultaneously** without conflict or compromise in their professional obligations.
- Always follow Virginia brokerage relationship requirements when working with multiple parties.
