# Disclosure of Brokerage Relationship When Representing Landlords

<!-- When you're representing the interests of **landlords** as a **listing agent**, it is crucial to properly disclose your **brokerage relationship** to **tenants** who are not represented. Agents representing the interests of **landlords** as a **listing agent** must properly disclose their **brokerage relationship** to **tenants** who are not represented. This disclosure must be made in writing using the **Disclosure of Brokerage Relationship** form. This step ensures:

- **Transparency** and clarity within the transaction
- Trust between all parties involved
- Professional standards are maintained
