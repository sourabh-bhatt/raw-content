# Understanding Conflict of Interest and Dual Agency in Transactions

<!-- When you're entering into transactions as an agent, it's crucial to understand the concept of **conflict of interest** to uphold ethics and maintain trust. A particularly important scenario to be aware of is **dual agency**. -->

Agents entering transactions must understand the concept of **conflict of interest** to uphold ethics and maintain trust. A scenario requiring attention is **dual agency**.

## What is Dual Agency?

<!-- Dual agency occurs when you represent both the buyer and the seller in the same transaction. This situation requires careful handling to ensure ethical compliance. -->

Dual agency occurs when an agent represents both the buyer and the seller in the same transaction. This situation requires careful handling to ensure ethical compliance.

## Key Points About Dual Agency

- **Dual agency itself is not forbidden.** The main concern arises when the arrangement is not transparent.
- Agents **must obtain written consent from all parties involved before or as soon as reasonably practicable after knowing or should know that dual agency exists**.
- Without clear, documented agreement from everyone, the agent would be engaging in **undisclosed dual agency**, which is a violation.
- In dual agency, duties are limited. The agent cannot advocate for one party over the other or share confidential information.

## Why Transparency and Consent Matter

<!-- Your primary responsibility is to serve the best interests of your clients. When representing both sides, maintaining transparent communication and securing written consents: -->

The primary responsibility is to serve the best interests of clients. When representing both sides, maintaining transparent communication and securing written consents:

- Respects the trust clients place in agents.
- Ensures adherence to professional and ethical standards.
- Protects reputation and career over the long term.

<!-- Think of it this way: transparency and consent safeguard both your clients' interests and your professional integrity. -->

Transparency and consent safeguard both clients' interests and professional integrity.
