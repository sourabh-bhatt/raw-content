When representing **buyer clients** as their **agent**, agents must treat all **prospective sellers** with *honesty*. This means agents should never knowingly provide false information. Maintaining honesty is a matter of **professional integrity** and lays the foundation for **trust** and respect in any **transaction**.

The agent's role is to facilitate a smooth and transparent process for both the **buyer** and the **seller**, ensuring a fair and **ethical exchange** for all parties involved.
