# Brokerage Agreements and Licensee Relationships

<!-- When you work as a **licensee** in Virginia, you have two important relationships to understand. You work under a **broker** as an **independent contractor**. You also serve clients through **brokerage agreements**. -->

**Licensees** in Virginia have two relationships to understand. They work under a **broker** as an **independent contractor**. They also serve clients through **brokerage agreements**.

## Independent Contractor Status

<!-- - As a **licensee**, you work as an **independent contractor** for your broker. -->
<!-- - This means you are not an employee of the brokerage. -->
<!-- - You still must follow your broker's supervision and policies. -->
<!-- - Your broker is responsible for your real estate activities. -->

- **Licensees** work as **independent contractors** for their broker.
- This means they are not employees of the brokerage.
- Licensees still must follow their broker's supervision and policies.
- The broker is responsible for licensee real estate activities.

## Client Relationships

<!-- - You represent clients through **brokerage agreements**. -->
<!-- - These agreements create **agency relationships** between the brokerage and the client. -->
<!-- - You act as the broker's agent when serving clients. -->
<!-- - You have specific duties and responsibilities to your clients. -->

- Licensees represent clients through **brokerage agreements**.
- These agreements create **agency relationships** between the brokerage and the client.
- Licensees act as the broker's agent when serving clients.
- Licensees have specific duties and responsibilities to clients.

## Clear Agreement Requirements

<!-- - Your role and responsibilities must be clearly stated in brokerage agreements. -->
<!-- - This clarity helps clients understand what services you will provide. -->
<!-- - It helps you understand your duties and any limitations. -->
<!-- - Clear agreements protect both you and your clients. -->

- Licensee roles and responsibilities must be clearly stated in brokerage agreements.
- This clarity helps clients understand what services will be provided.
- It helps licensees understand their duties and any limitations.
- Clear agreements protect both licensees and clients.
