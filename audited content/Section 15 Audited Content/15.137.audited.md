# Dual Agency in Real Estate Transactions

<!-- Understanding **Dual Agency** is essential for real estate professionals. This situation arises when you, as the **agent** or **broker**, represent both the **buyer** and the **seller** in the same transaction. -->

Understanding **Dual Agency** matters for real estate professionals. This situation arises when **agents** or **brokers** represent both the **buyer** and the **seller** in the same transaction.

## Key Points About Dual Agency in Virginia

- **Virginia Law:** Virginia law allows dual agency with proper disclosure and consent from both parties.
<!-- - **Written Disclosure:** You must provide written disclosure of dual agency to both parties before they sign a purchase agreement. -->
<!-- - **Written Consent:** You must obtain written consent from both the buyer and the seller before proceeding with dual agency. -->
- **Written Disclosure:** Agents must provide written disclosure of dual agency to both parties before they sign a purchase agreement.
- **Written Consent:** Agents must obtain written consent from both the buyer and the seller before proceeding with dual agency.
- **Timing:** Disclosure and consent must happen before any party signs a purchase agreement.
<!-- - **Neutral Position:** As a dual agent, you must remain neutral and cannot advocate for one party over the other. -->
<!-- - **Virginia Real Estate Board:** You must follow all Virginia Real Estate Board regulations regarding dual agency. -->
- **Neutral Position:** Dual agents must remain neutral and cannot advocate for one party over the other.
- **Virginia Real Estate Board:** Agents must follow all Virginia Real Estate Board regulations regarding dual agency.
- **Documentation:** Keep all disclosure and consent forms in transaction files.
