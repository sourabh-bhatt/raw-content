# Conflict of Interest in Brokerage and Agency Contracts

<!-- When you're navigating through your responsibilities in **brokerage and agency contracts**, it's essential to understand the concept of **conflict of interest**, particularly who can enter into agreements. -->

In **brokerage and agency contracts**, understanding **conflict of interest** is necessary, particularly who can enter into agreements.

## Authority to Enter Brokerage Agreements

<!-- Imagine a situation where there's a need to establish a brokerage agreement. -->

Brokerage agreements can be established with:

- Seller
- Buyer
- Landlord
- Tenant

<!-- Here's the critical part you need to remember: as a **salesperson** or an **associate broker**, you cannot directly enter into a brokerage agreement with any of these parties. -->

A **salesperson** or **associate broker** cannot directly enter into a brokerage agreement with any of these parties.

### Who Can Enter Into Brokerage Agreements?

Only an **employing broker** can enter into brokerage agreements. The employing broker is the only one who can make agreements with clients.

The reason this is structured this way is to prevent any **conflict of interest**.

## Avoiding Conflicts of Interest

<!-- Why is avoiding conflicts of interest so crucial? -->

Avoiding conflicts of interest provides several benefits:

- It ensures that every party's interests are protected.
- It guarantees a clear, unbiased path in all transactions and agreements.
- Breaking this rule can harm **integrity** and **fairness** in real estate.

<!-- Maintaining professionalism and adhering to these guidelines not only helps in fostering trust but also in building your reputation as a reliable agent in this field. -->

Maintaining professionalism and adhering to these guidelines fosters trust and builds reputation as a reliable agent.
