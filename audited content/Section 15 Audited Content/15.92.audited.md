"Ready, Willing, and Able" is a well-established common law standard used to determine when a broker has earned their commission. Under this doctrine, a broker is entitled to commission when they produce a buyer who:
1. Is ready (prepared to proceed with the transaction)
2. Is willing (desires to purchase on the stated terms)
3. Is able (has the financial capacity to complete the purchase)

This principle is recognized in Virginia and nationwide. When a broker produces such a buyer who makes an offer conforming to the listing agreement terms, the broker's commission is earned regardless of whether the seller chooses to accept the offer or complete the sale.

## Vetting Buyers

This concept outlines broker **responsibilities** and **rights**. The broker role involves not just attracting offers but **vetting potential buyers**, ensuring they are genuinely capable of completing the transaction. Once accomplished, commission rights are established based on fulfilling the brokerage agreement terms.

This applies even in cases where the seller might decide not to follow through with the sale.
