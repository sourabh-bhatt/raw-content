# Broker Responsibilities and Compliance in a Brokerage Firm

<!-- As a broker, you play a critical role in maintaining the **integrity** and **compliance** of your brokerage firm. If you are stepping into the shoes of the **principal broker**, you need to ensure that your **license is in good standing**. This position carries substantial responsibilities covering a wide spectrum of operations within the firm. -->

As a broker, brokers play a role in maintaining the **integrity** and **compliance** of their brokerage firm. When serving as the **principal broker**, brokers must ensure that their **license is in good standing**. This position carries substantial responsibilities covering a wide spectrum of operations within the firm.

## Key Duties of the Principal Broker

<!-- - Ensure that both you and all licensees under your brokerage comply with all applicable **laws and regulations**, regardless of where your firm operates. -->

- Ensure that principal brokers and all licensees under their brokerage comply with all applicable **laws and regulations**, regardless of where the firm operates.
<!-- - Take appropriate action if any licensees violate regulations, especially if you were aware or should have reasonably been aware of the misconduct. Failure to act can lead to discipline, including suspension, revocation, or denial of license renewal for you and your licensees. -->

- Take appropriate action if any licensees violate regulations, especially if principal brokers were aware or should have reasonably been aware of the misconduct. Failure to act can lead to discipline, including suspension, revocation, or denial of license renewal for principal brokers and their licensees.
- Maintain open and responsive **communication with the Board** regarding any matters affecting the firm or its licensees.
- Safeguard all **financial records**, including the source of funds, disclosures, and executed documents, for a minimum of **three years** from the date of closing or ratification. These records must be kept in an accessible location, whether in the main or a branch office.
- Oversee the firm's **advertising efforts** to ensure all promotional materials comply with regulatory standards and reflect the firm's values.
- Manage **escrow accounts** with accuracy and integrity.

## Supervisory Responsibilities and Branch Offices

- In larger operations with branch offices, you may delegate supervisory responsibilities to other **supervising brokers**.
- Supervising brokers ensure that associate brokers, salespersons, and real estate teams adhere to the firm's standards and regulations.
- Their role includes:
  - Being available to oversee activities
  - Approving documents
  - Ensuring access to necessary training on topics such as escrow deposits and fair housing laws
- If a supervising broker is located more than **50 miles** from a branch office, they must provide **quarterly certifications** to the Board affirming compliance with supervision rules.
<!-- - You may designate agents to be supervised by a chosen supervising broker, extending the hierarchical structure for effective management and oversight. -->

- Principal brokers may designate agents to be supervised by a chosen supervising broker, extending the hierarchical structure for effective management and oversight.

## Role of the Associate Broker

<!-- - If you hold a broker's license but are **not serving as the principal broker**, you are recognized as an **associate broker**. -->

- If brokers hold a broker's license but are **not serving as the principal broker**, they are recognized as **associate brokers**.
- Associate brokers engage in brokerage activities under the guidance of the principal broker.
- They must adhere to the same standards and protocols established for the firm's operations.

<!-- Understanding these roles and responsibilities is crucial for ensuring the smooth and lawful operation of your brokerage firm. -->

Understanding these roles and responsibilities helps ensure the smooth and lawful operation of brokerage firms.
