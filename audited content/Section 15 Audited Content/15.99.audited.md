# Establishing a Brokerage Relationship with a Tenant Client

<!-- When you're preparing to establish a **brokerage relationship** with a **tenant client**, Virginia law requires you to provide written disclosure and clearly communicate several key pieces of information. -->

When establishing a **brokerage relationship** with a **tenant client**, Virginia law requires agents to provide written disclosure and clearly communicate several pieces of information.

## Key Points to Communicate

- **Written Disclosure Statement**: Agents must provide a written **brokerage relationship disclosure statement** at first substantive contact. This statement explains the different types of brokerage relationships available under Virginia law.

- **Type of Brokerage Relationship**: Explain the specific type of brokerage relationship being proposed. This sets clear expectations for both parties.

- **Compensation**: Be transparent about how the agent will be paid, including the source and amount when known. The client needs to understand how the agent earns the **fee** and what services to expect in return.

- **Sharing Compensation**: Disclose whether the agent will be sharing any compensation with another **broker**, especially if that broker represents the other party in the transaction. This transparency helps maintain fairness and trust.
