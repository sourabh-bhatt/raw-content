# Broker License Requirements in Virginia

<!-- If you're looking to advance your career in real estate and become a **broker**, here are the specific requirements you'll need to meet in addition to the general qualifications previously discussed: -->
To advance careers in real estate and become a **broker**, specific requirements must be met in addition to the general qualifications previously discussed:

## Educational Standards

<!-- - You must adhere to the educational standards outlined in **§ 54.1-2105 of the Code of Virginia**. This ensures that you're grounded in the necessary principles and practices to operate effectively within the industry. -->
- Candidates must adhere to the educational standards outlined in **§ 54.1-2105 of the Code of Virginia**. This ensures grounding in the necessary principles and practices to operate effectively within the industry.

## Experience Requirements

<!-- - You should have been actively working as a **real estate salesperson** for at least **36 months out of the 48 months** immediately before you apply for your broker license. -->
- Candidates should have been actively working as a **real estate salesperson** for at least **36 months out of the 48 months** immediately before applying for broker license.
<!-- - This period allows you to refine your skills, deepen your understanding, and prepare for the broader responsibilities of a broker. -->
- This period allows skill refinement, deeper understanding, and preparation for the broader responsibilities of a broker.
- Under certain circumstances and at the discretion of the board, this experience requirement can be waived as per **§ 54.1-2105 of the Code of Virginia**.

## Validation of Experience

<!-- - Your experience must be verified by the **principal or supervising broker** under whom you've gained your experience.
- This verification confirms to the board your active engagement in the field and readiness to step up as a broker. -->
- Experience must be verified by the **principal or supervising broker** under whom experience was gained.
- This verification confirms to the board active engagement in the field and readiness to step up as a broker.

These regulations and requirements ensure thorough preparation for broker responsibilities. They ensure that candidates entering this role are equipped with knowledge and practical experience to succeed and uphold industry standards.
