# Exclusive Listing Agreement

When a property owner enters into an **exclusive listing agreement**, they form a contract with just one **broker**. The broker becomes the exclusive agent. For a set period, only this broker has the right to market the property and earn a **commission** if it sells.

This arrangement gives the broker exclusive marketing rights. The broker earns the commission even if someone else finds the buyer during the listing period.
