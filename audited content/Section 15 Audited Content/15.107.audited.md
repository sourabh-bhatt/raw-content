Professionals should understand the concept of **limited service agents**. These are professionals who choose to represent a client but do so with certain limitations. They commit to performing a subset of the responsibilities that are typically expected of standard agents. This means that they explicitly exclude one or more duties in their **brokerage agreement**, and they are only held accountable for the tasks that have not been omitted.
