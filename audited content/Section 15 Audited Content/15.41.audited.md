# Roles of Agents in Virginia Real Estate Transactions

In Virginia real estate transactions, agents can serve in three distinctive representation roles, depending on preferences and client needs. These roles are:

- **Seller's Agent**
- **Buyer's Agent**
- **Dual Agent**
