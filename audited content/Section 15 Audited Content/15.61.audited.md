# Responsibilities of a Real Estate Agent Representing a Tenant

<!-- When a tenant hires you as their real estate agent, you must understand and fulfill specific responsibilities. Here's how you can ensure you're serving your client's best interests and adhering to the law. -->
When a tenant hires an agent as their real estate representative, agents must understand and fulfill specific responsibilities. The following guidelines ensure agents serve their client's best interests and adhere to the law.

## Brokerage Agreement

<!-- - Always act according to the **brokerage agreement** you've entered into. This agreement lists your duties to your client. -->
- Always act according to the **brokerage agreement** entered into. This agreement lists duties to the client.

## Promoting Tenant-Client Interests

<!-- - Your primary goal is to promote your **tenant-client's interests**.
- Seek a lease that meets the tenant's requirements regarding **rent** and **terms**.
- You must look for other properties unless your brokerage agreement says otherwise or a lease already exists. -->
- The primary goal is to promote the **tenant-client's interests**.
- Seek a lease that meets the tenant's requirements regarding **rent** and **terms**.
- Agents must look for other properties unless the brokerage agreement says otherwise or a lease already exists.
- Assist in drafting and negotiating leases.
- Present all offers swiftly.
- Aid in finalizing the lease agreement.

## Confidentiality

<!-- - Protect your client's **confidentiality**, including safeguarding personal and financial information received during your relationship.
- Disclosure is only allowed if required by law or if your client agrees in writing. -->
- Protect the client's **confidentiality**, including safeguarding personal and financial information received during the relationship.
- Disclosure is only allowed if required by law or if the client agrees in writing.

## Duty of Care and Disclosure

<!-- - Apply **ordinary care** in your dealings.
- Promptly account for all money and property in which your client has an interest.
- Disclose to your client any **material facts** about the property or transaction that you are aware of. -->
- Apply **ordinary care** in dealings.
- Promptly account for all money and property in which the client has an interest.
- Disclose to the client any **material facts** about the property or transaction that the agent is aware of.

## Legal Compliance

- Adhere strictly to legal requirements, including:
  - This section of the law.
  - **Fair housing regulations**.
  - Any other relevant statutes and regulations.

## Honesty with Prospective Landlords

- Be honest in your interactions with prospective landlords.
- Do not provide false information.
<!-- - Inform your client of any known defects, such as **defective drywall**.
- This openness builds trust and protects your client's interests while avoiding legal issues. -->
- Inform the client of any known defects, such as **defective drywall**.
- This openness builds trust and protects the client's interests while avoiding legal issues.

## Ministerial Acts and Dual Representation

<!-- - You may perform ministerial acts for other parties as long as these do not conflict with your tenant-client's interests. Ministerial acts are routine tasks that do not require judgment or discretion.
- You can show properties of interest to your tenant-client to other potential tenants.
- You may represent other tenants and landlords provided there is no conflict with the loyalty owed to your current client. -->
- Agents may perform ministerial acts for other parties as long as these do not conflict with the tenant-client's interests. Ministerial acts are routine tasks that do not require judgment or discretion.
- Agents can show properties of interest to the tenant-client to other potential tenants.
- Agents may represent other tenants and landlords provided there is no conflict with the loyalty owed to the current client.

## Disclosure of Brokerage Relationships

<!-- - Always disclose your **brokerage relationships** in residential real estate transactions as required by law. -->
- Always disclose **brokerage relationships** in residential real estate transactions as required by law.
- Showing properties to prospective tenants does **not** immediately require a written brokerage agreement.

<!-- Keeping these responsibilities in mind will help you effectively represent your tenant-clients while staying compliant with legal obligations. -->
Keeping these responsibilities in mind helps agents effectively represent tenant-clients while staying compliant with legal obligations.
