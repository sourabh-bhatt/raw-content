# Agency Relationships and Fiduciary Duties in Real Estate Transactions

Virginia law requires agents to establish a clear **agency relationship** with a written agreement and to uphold **fiduciary duties**—including loyalty, confidentiality, disclosure, and ordinary care—when representing clients. These duties are outlined in 18VAC135-20-280 and related statutes, ensuring agents act in the best interest of their principals.
