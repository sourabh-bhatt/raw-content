# Licensing Law: Understanding Exemptions

<!-- Learn about the **Real Estate Licensing Law** and understand the exceptions to who needs to adhere to its chapter. As you learn about real estate practices, understand these important legal rules. -->

The **Licensing Law** has exceptions to who needs to adhere to its chapter. Professionals should understand these legal rules.

## Exemptions to Licensing Requirements

Not everyone who works with property needs a real estate license. The following are key exemptions:

- **Property Owners and Lessors:** If managing their own property and performing tasks typical for management or investment, the rules do not apply. This exemption also extends to certain affiliated entities under specific conditions.

- **Acting Without Compensation:** People who work for free under a power of attorney to complete final contract tasks for property sales, leases, purchases, or exchanges do not need a license.

- **Legal Professionals and Court-Appointed Individuals:**
  - Attorneys performing their professional duties.
  - Individuals appointed as receivers, trustees in bankruptcy, administrators, executors.
  - Those selling real estate under a court order.
  - Trustees working under a trust, deed of trust, or will, along with their regular salaried employees.

- **Corporations Managing Rental Housing:** If a corporation only manages rental housing for its members and does not manage property for others, the rules do not apply.

- **Tenants Referring Others:** Tenants who refer someone to their landlord or landlord's agent and receive a referral fee are exempt.

- **Auctioneers:** Auctioneers holding a valid license and conducting public real estate auctions for owners follow a different set of regulations. They cannot advertise real estate selling capabilities outside the context of a specific auction.

- **Out-of-State Licensed Brokers and Salespersons:** Those helping with commercial real estate deals for out-of-state clients have specific exemptions.

- **Salaried Employees of Licensed Brokers:** Employees who manage property and lease or show residential units for their broker employer do not need separate licenses, provided they adhere to conditions regarding handling security deposits and rentals.

## Regulatory Notes

- Licensees must follow the **board's regulations** even if they fall into an exempt category.

- Attorneys referring clients to licensees **cannot receive compensation** from a listing firm or through information shared among cooperating brokers unless they are also licensed as real estate brokers or salespersons.
