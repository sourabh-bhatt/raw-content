# Entering a Brokerage Relationship with a Buyer

<!-- When you're about to enter into a **brokerage relationship** with a **buyer**, there are several key pieces of information you need to share upfront. Virginia law requires a written brokerage agreement for buyer representation. -->

When entering into a **brokerage relationship** with a **buyer**, there are several pieces of information agents need to share upfront. Virginia law requires a written brokerage agreement for buyer representation.

## Information to Share

- **Type of Brokerage Relationship**: Explain the type of brokerage relationship proposed. This explains what services will be provided.

<!-- - **Compensation**: Be transparent about how you will be paid for your services. This ensures there are no surprises later on and helps build trust with your client. -->

- **Compensation**: Be transparent about how agents will be paid for their services. This ensures there are no surprises later on and helps build trust with clients.

<!-- - **Compensation Sharing**: Communicate whether you will share your compensation with another broker if their client is the other party in the transaction. This disclosure helps manage expectations and maintains transparency throughout the process. -->

- **Compensation Sharing**: Communicate whether agents will share their compensation with another broker if their client is the other party in the transaction. This disclosure helps manage expectations and maintains transparency throughout the process.

<!-- - **Dual Representation**: Disclose if you might represent both buyer and seller in the same transaction. Virginia law requires this disclosure. -->

- **Dual Representation**: Disclose if agents might represent both buyer and seller in the same transaction. Virginia law requires this disclosure.

<!-- - **Material Relationship Facts**: Share any material facts about your relationship with other parties in the transaction. -->

- **Material Relationship Facts**: Share any material facts about agent relationships with other parties in the transaction.

<!-- Covering these points clearly and early on in your relationship with a client sets a solid foundation for **trust** and **professionalism**. -->

Covering these points clearly and early on in the relationship with a client sets a foundation for **trust** and **professionalism**.
