# Representing Landlords: Disclosure of Material Facts

<!-- When you're representing **landlords** as their **agent**, it's crucial that you **disclose all material facts** that could affect the landlord's interests, both about the **property itself** and any details concerning the **transaction**. -->
When representing **landlords** as their **agent**, **disclosing all material facts** that could affect landlord interests is necessary, both about the **property itself** and any details concerning the **transaction**.

## Understanding Material Facts

<!-- Material facts are any information that could significantly impact the property's **value**, desirability, or the landlord's negotiating position. -->
Material facts are any information that could impact the property's **value**, desirability, or the landlord's negotiating position.
