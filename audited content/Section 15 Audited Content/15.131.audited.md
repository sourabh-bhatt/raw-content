# Understanding Liability in Common Law Agency

When **principal brokers** delegate tasks to **employees** or hire **independent contractors**, they place trust in them. Principal brokers may be held **liable** for wrongful actions, but only if those actions occur within the **scope of their authority** or during **employment**.

## Key Points to Consider

- Liability applies when wrongful acts happen within the scope of authority or employment.
- The extent of liability can vary depending on **Virginia law and Real Estate Board regulations**.
- Understanding these principles helps principal brokers manage their **responsibilities** and potential **risks** when working with clients and other professionals.
- In Virginia, principal brokers are also liable for supervising all licensees under their brokerage.
