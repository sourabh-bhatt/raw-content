# Designated Agency in Real Estate

<!-- In real estate, you'll encounter a scenario called **Designated Agency**. -->

In real estate, **Designated Agency** occurs when, under **Virginia law**, a **brokerage** proposes to represent both the **buyer** and the **seller** in the same transaction by appointing different **licensees** within the same brokerage to represent each party.

## Key Points of Designated Agency

- The brokerage must **disclose this arrangement in writing** to both parties.
- The brokerage must **obtain written consent** from both parties before proceeding.
- Each designated agent owes full fiduciary duties to their assigned client.
- Designated agents cannot share confidential information about their clients with each other.
- This disclosure and consent process ensures that everyone is fully informed and agrees to the arrangement.
