When representing **tenant clients** as an agent, agents must promptly account for all **money** and **property** received that the tenant has or may have an interest in. This accountability is a core responsibility, ensuring that any **funds** or **assets** that come into their possession on behalf of their client are tracked and reported.

## Accountability Requirements

<!-- - Ensures **professional integrity** in handling client assets. -->
- Ensures **professional integrity** in handling client assets.
- Fulfills a **legal requirement** designed to protect the tenant's interests.
<!-- - Provides assurance to clients that their assets are managed with the utmost **care** and **transparency**. -->
- Provides assurance to clients that their assets are managed with **care** and **transparency**.

<!-- By maintaining strict accountability, you safeguard your client's interests and uphold the standards expected of real estate professionals. -->

By maintaining strict accountability, agents safeguard their client's interests and uphold the standards expected of real estate professionals.
