# Exercising Ordinary Care When Representing Buyer Clients

When representing **buyer clients** as a **buyer's agent**, agents must exercise judgment in a way that a reasonable person would under similar circumstances. This standard, known as **ordinary care**, is required by Virginia regulations (18VAC135-20-280) to ensure agents act in the best interest of their clients and avoid negligence.
