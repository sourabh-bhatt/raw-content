In **real estate brokerage** and **agency contracts**, understanding who has the **authority** to engage in specific transactions, especially regarding **compensation**, matters.

## Key Points About Authority and Compensation

<!-- - As a **salesperson**, you do **not** have the authority to: -->

- As a **salesperson**, salespersons do **not** have the authority to:
  - Directly receive compensation from a client.
  - Enter into contracts with a client.

<!-- - This authority is **exclusively reserved** for your **broker**.
- Your **broker** will: -->

- This authority is **exclusively reserved** for the **broker**.
- The **broker** will:
  - Contract directly with the client.
  - Compensate you accordingly.
