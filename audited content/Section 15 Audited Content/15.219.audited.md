In understanding the concept of **brokerage** and **agency contract responsibilities**, the **Disclosure of Agency** is a component to understand. This pertains to an agent's obligation, under **Virginia law**, to inform consumers about whom they truly represent. In Virginia, this disclosure must be made at the time of first substantial contact with a consumer. This disclosure is typically carried out through mandatory forms required by the Virginia Residential Property Disclosure Act.
