# Understanding Limited Service Agency in Real Estate

<!-- As a real estate agent, understanding the specific duties you owe to your clients is crucial, especially when it comes to the type of representation they've chosen. -->

Real estate agents must understand the specific duties owed to clients, especially regarding the type of representation clients have chosen.

## Limited Service Agent Responsibilities

<!-- When working as a **limited service agent**, there are certain responsibilities you must uphold, similar to those of a standard agent. When working as **limited service agents**, agents have certain responsibilities to uphold, similar to those of standard agents. However, the key distinction lies in the agreement with clients, which can explicitly exclude some obligations typically required to actively promote client interests.

### Example of Limited Service Role

<!-- For instance, if you are representing a seller, the brokerage agreement might specify your role as solely listing the property on the **Multiple Listing Service (MLS)**. This means other typical efforts such as: -->

Example: If agents represent sellers, the brokerage agreement might specify the role as solely listing the property on the **Multiple Listing Service (MLS)**. This means other typical efforts such as:

- Aiding in the creation of a marketing strategy
- Helping negotiate and present offers
- Facilitating the final transaction settlement

Given the nature of limited service agency, agents must communicate clearly with clients about what their role will and will not include. At the onset of such agreements, agents are required to provide clients with all relevant disclosures mandated by federal, state, or local laws. This documentation covers aspects such as:

1. **Client rights and duties under the Virginia Residential Property Disclosure Act** - ensuring clients understand what is expected from them and what they can expect from others.
2. **Condominium considerations** - detailing client responsibilities and rights concerning the distribution or receipt of a **condominium resale certificate**.
3. **Property Owners' Association Act obligations** - clarifying what must be disclosed to potential buyers or what they should expect to receive in terms of an association disclosure packet.
