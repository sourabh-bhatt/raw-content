# Entity-Specific Rules in Brokerage and Agency

<!-- When working in **brokerage and agency**, you need to know the **"Entity-Specific" Rules**. -->

Professionals working in **brokerage and agency** must know the **"Entity-Specific" Rules**.

## Federal Do Not Call Regulations

Federal Do Not Call regulations require professionals to maintain their own Do Not Call lists. These regulations place clear responsibility on professionals.

## Maintaining an Entity-Specific Do Not Call List

<!-- You need to maintain a distinct **entity-specific Do Not Call list**. This list should include the details of consumers who have directly told you they do not want to receive calls made by or on behalf of your real estate business. -->

Professionals must maintain a distinct **entity-specific Do Not Call list**. This list should include details of consumers who have directly stated they do not want to receive calls made by or on behalf of the business.

<!-- - If a consumer tells you they do not want to be contacted, you must:
  - Record their preference on your Do Not Call list
  - Not call them in the future -->

- If a consumer states they do not want to be contacted, professionals must:
  - Record their preference on the Do Not Call list
  - Not call them in the future

This practice:

- Respects consumer rights
- Upholds professional integrity within the brokerage and agency landscape

**Example:** If a homeowner explicitly requests no phone calls regarding the sale of their property, you must add their information to your entity-specific Do Not Call list. You must ensure no calls are made to them by you or your representatives.
