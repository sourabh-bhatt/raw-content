# The Role of the Supervising Broker

Real estate professionals must understand the role of the **Supervising Broker**.

## Appointment and Responsibilities

- The **Supervising Broker** is appointed by the **principal broker** to oversee the activities of **associate brokers** and **salespersons**.
- In some cases, the **principal broker** may take on the supervisory role themselves.
- The **principal broker** may also appoint another licensed broker as a **Supervising Broker** to oversee agents in the firm.

## Supervision Structure

This structure of supervision maintains the standards and ethics expected in the real estate industry. It ensures that all transactions are conducted professionally and in accordance with **Virginia real estate laws and regulations**.

## Role as a Mentor

The **Supervising Broker** serves as a mentor and guide, helping agents navigate their responsibilities effectively.
