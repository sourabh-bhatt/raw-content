## Types of Buyer-Broker Agreements

<!-- Here are the types of buyer-broker agreements you will work with: -->

The types of buyer-broker agreements include:

1. ### Exclusive Right-to-Represent Agreement
   - This is the most common form of buyer-broker agreement.
   <!-- - Under this contract, you have the **exclusive right to represent the buyer** in purchasing a property. -->
   - Under this contract, the agent has the **exclusive right to represent the buyer** in purchasing a property.
   <!-- - If the buyer purchases a property during the agreement term, you earn a **commission**. This is true regardless of who found the property. -->
   - If the buyer purchases a property during the agreement term, the agent earns a **commission**. This is true regardless of who found the property.

2. ### Exclusive Agency Agreement
   <!-- - This arrangement allows you to represent the buyer exclusively. -->
   - This arrangement allows the agent to represent the buyer exclusively.
   - However, the buyer keeps the right to find and purchase a property on their own.
   <!-- - If the buyer finds a property without your help, you **do not earn a commission**. -->
   - If the buyer finds a property without the agent's help, the agent **does not earn a commission**.
   <!-- - This arrangement requires clear communication between you and the buyer. -->
   - This arrangement requires clear communication between the agent and the buyer.

3. ### Non-Exclusive Agreement
   <!-- - This agreement is more flexible for the buyer but riskier for you as the agent. -->
   - This agreement is more flexible for the buyer but riskier for the agent.
   - Under this contract, the buyer can work with multiple brokers.
   <!-- - The buyer is **not obligated to purchase a property through you**. -->
   - The buyer is **not obligated to purchase a property through the agent**.
   <!-- - You only get paid if you are the agent who finds the property the buyer purchases. -->
   - The agent only gets paid if they are the agent who finds the property the buyer purchases.

4. ### Open Buyer Agency Agreement
   - This is similar to the Non-Exclusive Agreement.
   - The buyer can work with multiple agents.
   <!-- - This type of agreement is rarely used because it offers **no guarantee of compensation** for your work. -->
   - This type of agreement is rarely used because it offers **no guarantee of compensation** for agent work.
