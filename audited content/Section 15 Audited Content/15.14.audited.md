# Brokerage and Agency Contracts: Disclosure Responsibilities

**Brokerage and agency contracts** in real estate carry certain responsibilities. One obligation involves making **proper disclosures**.

## Disclosure Requirements

Before any **agency relationship** begins, Virginia law requires agents to disclose specific details about the nature of the proposed agency relationship to all parties involved. The Virginia Real Estate Board regulations outline these disclosure requirements.

These disclosures serve a function:

- Ensure **transparency** between all parties
- Protect the interests of everyone involved
- Clearly outline the expectations and responsibilities of the proposed relationship

This step establishes **trust** and **integrity** in professional interactions.

## Impact on Client Relationships

A well-informed client is a confident and cooperative partner throughout the real estate transaction process. Therefore, making the necessary disclosures is:

- A **legal requirement**
- Part of **ethical real estate practice**

By fulfilling these disclosure obligations, agents build strong, transparent relationships that facilitate smoother transactions and uphold professional standards.
