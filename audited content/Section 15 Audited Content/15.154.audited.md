# Single Agency in Brokerage and Agency Contracts

<!-- When you're entering into **brokerage** and **agency contracts**, it's crucial to understand the concept of **Single Agency**. -->

When entering into **brokerage** and **agency contracts**, understanding the concept of **Single Agency** matters.

## What is Single Agency?

<!-- In this arrangement, you, as a **broker** or **real estate agent**, will represent just *one party* in a transaction. This means you'll either stand for the **buyer** or the **seller**, but *not both simultaneously*. -->

In this arrangement, **brokers** or **real estate agents** represent just *one party* in a transaction. This means they either represent the **buyer** or the **seller**, but *not both simultaneously*.

<!-- - The party you represent is known as your **client**. -->
- The party the agent represents is known as the **client**.
- The other party involved in the transaction is referred to as the **customer**.

<!-- This distinction is essential for: -->

This distinction matters for:

<!-- - Ensuring that you're providing the best possible service and advocacy for your client. -->
<!-- - Adhering to your **fiduciary responsibilities**. -->
<!-- - Maintaining clarity about whom you're representing in the transaction. -->

- Ensuring that agents provide service and advocacy for their client.
- Adhering to **fiduciary responsibilities**.
- Maintaining clarity about whom agents are representing in the transaction.
