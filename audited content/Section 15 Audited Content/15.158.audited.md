# Procuring Cause in Real Estate Transactions

<!-- Let's explore the concept of **procuring cause** and its significance as you learn the ropes in real estate. -->

The concept of **procuring cause** and its role in real estate compensation matters.

## What is Procuring Cause?

<!-- When you're working as an **agent** or **broker**, you earn your compensation (commission) by being the procuring cause of a sale. This means that if you, or someone under your supervision like a salesperson, bring about a sale, you're entitled to a commission for your hard work. -->

When working as an **agent** or **broker**, compensation (commission) is earned by being the procuring cause of a sale. This means that if an agent, or someone under their supervision like a salesperson, brings about a sale, they are entitled to a commission.

## Procuring Cause Requirements

<!-- Disagreements can arise over which broker actually brought in a **ready, willing, and able buyer**, leading to a sale. This is where the concept of procuring cause becomes crucial. -->

Disagreements can arise over which broker actually brought in a **ready, willing, and able buyer**, leading to a sale. The concept of procuring cause addresses these situations.

To determine which broker was the procuring cause, it is necessary to identify who initiated a series of events that directly led to the transaction without any interruptions.

## Determining Procuring Cause

- Determining which agent was the procuring cause of a sale is not always straightforward, as criteria can vary.
- Generally, **state laws** look for an **unbroken chain of events** that a broker initiated, culminating in the sale or lease of a property.
