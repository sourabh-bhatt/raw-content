# Broker Relationship Disclosure in Residential Real Estate Transactions

<!-- When you engage in a substantive discussion about a specific property or properties in a residential real estate transaction with someone who isn't already your client or represented by another agent, it's crucial to **disclose any broker relationship** you may have with another party in the transaction. This goes beyond simple courtesy; it's a **legal requirement**. -->

When agents engage in substantive discussions about specific properties in residential real estate transactions with someone who isn't already their client or represented by another agent, agents must **disclose any broker relationship** they may have with another party in the transaction. This represents a **legal requirement**.

- **Transparency builds trust**: Knowing who represents whom clarifies expectations and reduces potential conflicts of interest.
- Trust enables smoother transactions for everyone involved.

- The law requires agents to make this disclosure **in writing at the earliest practical time**, but **no later than when they start providing specific real estate assistance**.
- Disclosure can be included as part of other documents or provided with additional information.

## Required Information in Disclosure

Agent disclosures should generally include:

<!-- - Your **name** as the broker or salesperson -->
<!-- - The **name of your brokerage firm** -->
<!-- - A clear statement of whether you represent the **seller(s), buyer(s), landlord(s), or tenant(s)** in the transaction -->

- Agent **name** as the broker or salesperson
- The **name of the brokerage firm**
- A clear statement of whether the agent represents the **seller(s), buyer(s), landlord(s), or tenant(s)** in the transaction
- The **dates** and **names of the involved parties** for clarity and record-keeping

## Disclosure in Lease Situations

- Written disclosure of brokerage relationships is required as part of all **lease applications** or the **lease itself**, whichever comes first.
- **Exception:** No disclosure is required for leases of single or multifamily residential units lasting **less than two months**.

## Changes in Relationship

<!-- - Any changes in your relationship with a client or customer must be **documented and disclosed in writing** to all involved parties **as soon as such changes occur**. -->
- Any changes in agent relationships with clients or customers must be **documented and disclosed in writing** to all involved parties **as soon as such changes occur**.
- This ensures all parties are up to date and can adjust their expectations accordingly.

## Record Keeping Requirements

- Keeping copies of disclosures related to **fully executed purchase contracts** is a **legal requirement**.
- These records must be kept for **three years** as proof of compliance.
- This applies **whether or not the disclosure was acknowledged in writing** by the receiving party.

## Limited Service Agent Disclosures

- If operating as **limited service agents**, specific disclosure requirements apply.
- These disclosures ensure all parties understand the **nature and scope of the services** being provided.
