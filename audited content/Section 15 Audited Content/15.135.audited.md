# Understanding Sub-Agency in Real Estate Brokerage

<!-- When learning about the complexities of **brokerage and agency contracts**, it's essential to understand the concept of **sub-agency**. Think of it as an agent representing another agent. -->

When learning about the complexities of **brokerage and agency contracts**, understanding the concept of **sub-agency** matters. Sub-agency involves an agent representing another agent.

## What is Sub-Agency?

**Brokers** might need to appoint **subagents (other brokers and their salespersons)** to help carry out their duties. This arrangement usually happens under two conditions:

<!-- - You have **explicit permission** from your client. -->
- The broker has **explicit permission** from the client.
- The **law allows** for it.

## Role and Responsibilities of a Subagent

<!-- As a subagent, your role is to perform tasks on behalf of the primary agent's clients as if you were the primary agent yourself. For example: -->

Subagents perform tasks on behalf of the primary agent's clients as if they were the primary agent. For example:

<!-- - If you're a subagent under a **listing broker**, you take on the responsibility to represent the **seller**â€”your clientâ€”just as faithfully and diligently as the listing broker would. -->

- If subagents work under a **listing broker**, they take on the responsibility to represent the **seller** - their client - just as faithfully and diligently as the listing broker would.

## Agency Relationship and Fiduciary Duties

<!-- This creates an **agency relationship** between the subagent and the **seller**, who is your client. Such a relationship is bound by mutual obligations and duties: -->

This creates an **agency relationship** between the subagent and the **seller**, who is the client. Such a relationship is bound by mutual obligations and duties:

<!-- - Both you and your client owe each other **fiduciary duties**. -->
- Both subagents and clients owe each other **fiduciary duties**.
- These duties require placing the **client's interests above all else** in the real estate transaction process.

<!-- Understanding and honoring these obligations is pivotal to your role and effectiveness as an agent or subagent. -->

Understanding and honoring these obligations is central to the role and effectiveness of agents or subagents.
