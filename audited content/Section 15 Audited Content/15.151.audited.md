# Reasonable Care and Due Diligence in Real Estate Practice

<!-- When you guide clients through the complex process of buying, selling, or leasing property, you must carry out your duties with a level of **care** and **diligence** that's expected of any competent professional in the real estate field. This responsibility is a **fiduciary duty** known as **reasonable care and due diligence**. When guiding clients through the process of buying, selling, or leasing property, agents must carry out duties with a level of **care** and **diligence** expected of competent professionals in the real estate field. This responsibility is a **fiduciary duty** known as **reasonable care and due diligence**. This means agents must work to ensure that clients are well-informed and represented with competence.

## Role and Expectations of a Real Estate Agent

<!-- Being a real estate agent means using standard tools and available information to serve your clients effectively. While you are expected to have a deeper understanding of the **market** and the **legalities** involved than the average person, remember, you're not expected to possess the specialized knowledge that legal, engineering, or accounting professionals have. -->

Real estate agents use standard tools and available information to serve clients effectively. While agents are expected to have a deeper understanding of the **market** and **legalities** involved than the average person, they are not expected to possess the specialized knowledge that legal, engineering, or accounting professionals have.
