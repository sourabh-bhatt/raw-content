# Duties Owed to Tenant Clients in Residential Transactions

<!-- When you're representing tenant clients in a residential transaction as a **tenant's agent**, there are several key duties you owe them. Understanding these responsibilities is crucial for effectively guiding your clients through the rental process. Here's what you need to know: -->
As a **tenant's agent** representing clients in residential transactions, agents have specific duties to fulfill. These responsibilities guide agents through the rental process and help ensure effective client representation.

## Key Duties

1. **Loyalty**
   <!-- - Your primary allegiance is to your tenant clients.
   - Prioritize their interests above all others, including your own.

   - Primary allegiance belongs to tenant clients.
   - Prioritize client interests above all others, including personal interests.
   - Advocate for clients, negotiate beneficial terms, and work to secure optimal outcomes.

2. **Confidentiality**
   <!-- - Keep any information your tenant clients share with you confidential, even after the lease agreement is signed.
   - This includes personal details, financial information, and any specific needs or concerns regarding the property or lease terms. -->
   - Keep all tenant client information confidential, even after lease agreement completion.
   - This includes personal details, financial information, and specific needs or concerns regarding the property or lease terms.

3. **Disclosure**
   <!-- - Inform your tenant clients of any **material facts** that could affect their decision to rent a property.
   - This includes known physical defects and any legal or financial nuances impacting their use and enjoyment of the premises. -->
   - Inform tenant clients of any **material facts** that could affect their rental decision.
   - This includes known physical defects and legal or financial factors impacting property use and enjoyment.

4. **Obedience**
   <!-- - Act in accordance with your tenant clients' lawful instructions.
   - Follow their directives during negotiation or leasing, provided they do not conflict with legal or ethical standards. -->
   - Act in accordance with tenant clients' lawful instructions.
   - Follow client directives during negotiation or leasing, provided they do not conflict with legal or ethical standards.

5. **Accounting**
   - Handle all funds related to the transaction with accuracy and transparency.
   - Securely manage deposits, rent payments, and other financial transactions.
   - Maintain accurate records of all client funds and provide proper accounting.

6. **Reasonable Care and Diligence**
   - Demonstrate professionalism, knowledge, and attention to detail throughout the transaction.
   - Conduct thorough property searches.
   - Ensure all paperwork is correctly completed and filed.
   <!-- - Provide guidance and advice based on your expertise and experience in the real estate market. -->
   - Provide guidance and advice based on expertise and experience in the real estate market.
