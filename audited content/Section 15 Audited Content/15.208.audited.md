# Net Listings in Real Estate

<!-- Let's explore the concept of a **net listing** together. -->

A **net listing** is a concept in real estate.

## What is a Net Listing?

A **net listing** is a type of listing agreement where the seller sets a minimum amount they want to receive from the sale. The real estate agent keeps any amount above this minimum as their **commission**.

**Example:** If a seller wants to net $200,000 from their home sale, and the agent sells it for $220,000, the agent would keep the $20,000 difference as commission.

## Legal Considerations in Virginia

- **Net listings are prohibited in Virginia**.
- **Virginia Real Estate Board regulations prohibit net listings** because they can create conflicts of interest.
- Net listings can encourage agents to act against their clients' best interests.
