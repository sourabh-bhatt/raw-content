# Buyer-Broker Agreements

When representing buyers, brokers encounter a **buyer-broker agreement**. This agreement is parallel to **listing agreements** but from the buyer's perspective. It forms the contractual bond between the **broker** and the client, who is the **buyer**.

## Role of the Broker and Salesperson

- This agreement often includes a broker's **salesperson**, functioning as an **agent of the broker**.
- Whether serving as the broker or the salesperson working under a broker, professionals commit to abiding by the terms set forth in this agreement.
- The role is to represent the **buyer's interests** throughout the transaction.

## Buyer-Broker Agreement Elements

- These agreements outline responsibilities toward the client.
- They set the foundation for the **professional relationship** with the buyer.
- Understanding buyer-broker agreements supports effective buyer representation in transactions.
