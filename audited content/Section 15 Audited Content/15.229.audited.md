In real estate, real estate agents earn **commissions**, which are typically a percentage of the **sales price** of a property. **Commission rates**, along with any additional fees, can always be negotiated. However, price fixing with other brokers violates **antitrust laws** and should be avoided.

## How to Calculate Commissions

When calculating commissions, there are two basic formulas you'll use regularly:

1. **Calculating the commission amount:**
   - **Example:** Suppose an agent sells a property for $120,000 and their commission rate is 6%.
   - Convert the commission rate from a percent to a decimal by moving the decimal two places to the left: 6% becomes 0.06.
   - Multiply the sales price by the commission rate:

2. **Calculating the commission rate:**
   - **Example:** Suppose an agent has earned a commission of $7,200 from a sale priced at $120,000 and wants to find their commission rate.
   - Divide the commission amount by the sales price:

   - Convert the decimal back to a percentage by moving the decimal two places to the right or multiplying by 100:
     
     
     0.06 Ã— 100 = 6%
