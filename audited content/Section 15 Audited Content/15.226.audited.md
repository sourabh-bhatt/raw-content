# Duty of Ordinary Care When Representing Tenant Clients

<!-- When representing **tenant clients**, it is essential that you, as their agent, exercise what is known as **ordinary care**. When representing **tenant clients**, agents must exercise what is known as **ordinary care**. This means that in their actions and decisions, agents must demonstrate the kind of **judgment** and **prudence** expected of a reasonable person placed in similar situations.

**Key aspects of ordinary care include:**

- Acting with a level of care and attention that any sensible person would use.
<!-- - Serving your clients' best interests with the **due diligence** they rightfully expect.
- Incorporating this principle as a cornerstone of your professional responsibilities. -->

- Serving client best interests with the **due diligence** they rightfully expect.
- Incorporating this principle as a cornerstone of professional responsibilities.

<!-- By adhering to ordinary care, you ensure that your conduct meets the standards required to protect and promote your tenant clients' interests effectively. -->

By adhering to ordinary care, agents ensure that their conduct meets the standards required to protect and promote their tenant clients' interests effectively.
