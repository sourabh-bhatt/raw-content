# Duty of Loyalty in Client Representation

<!-- When you're representing a client, one of the most crucial responsibilities you hold is the **duty of loyalty**. This duty is fundamental and underscores the **fiduciary relationship** between you and your client. It mandates that you, as the agent, must always put your **client's interests** ahead of anyone else's, including your own. -->

When representing a client, one of the key responsibilities agents hold is the **duty of loyalty**. This duty underscores the **fiduciary relationship** between agents and their client. It mandates that agents must always put their **client's interests** ahead of anyone else's, including their own.

## Demonstrating Loyalty

<!-- You demonstrate loyalty by actively promoting your client's best interests above all others. Consider the following key points: -->
Agents demonstrate loyalty by actively promoting their client's best interests above all others. Consider the following key points:

<!-- - Always prioritize your client's interests over your personal gain or profit. -->
- Always prioritize client's interests over personal gain or profit.
<!-- - Avoid any actions that could disfavor your client in favor of yourself. -->
- Avoid any actions that could disfavor the client in favor of the agent.
<!-- - Recognize that prioritizing personal gain at the expense of your client is a clear **breach of your duty of loyalty**. -->
- Prioritizing personal gain at the expense of the client is a clear **breach of duty of loyalty**.

## Loyalty

<!-- Acting with unwavering loyalty is not just about fulfilling a legal obligation; it's about building **trust** and **integrity** in your professional relationships. -->
Acting with loyalty fulfills a legal obligation and builds **trust** and **integrity** in professional relationships.
