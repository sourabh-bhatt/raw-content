# Confidentiality in Brokerage and Agency Contracts

<!-- When you engage in **brokerage** and **agency contracts**, it's crucial to understand your obligation concerning the **confidentiality of information**. This duty remains in place not only while the **agency relationship** exists but also after it concludes or terminates. 

Remember, any information you learn from or about your client is to be kept strictly **confidential**, safeguarding their interests and privacy. -->

Agents engaged in **brokerage** and **agency contracts** must understand their obligation concerning the **confidentiality of information**. This duty remains in place while the **agency relationship** exists and after it concludes or terminates.

Any information learned from or about clients must be kept strictly **confidential**, safeguarding their interests and privacy.

## Disclosure of Information

<!-- There might be situations where disclosing certain information is necessary or beneficial. In such cases, it is essential that you obtain **explicit authorization** from your client before sharing any details.

This responsibility underscores the importance of **trust** and **discretion** in your role, highlighting why maintaining confidentiality is fundamental to the profession. -->

Situations may arise where disclosing certain information is necessary or beneficial. In such cases, agents must obtain **explicit authorization** from their client before sharing any details.

This responsibility demonstrates the role of **trust** and **discretion** in agency relationships, showing why maintaining confidentiality supports professional practice.
