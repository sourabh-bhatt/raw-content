# Representing Buyers in Transactions

<!-- When you're representing **buyers** in real estate transactions, it's crucial that you prioritize their interests above all. Agents representing **buyers** in transactions must prioritize their interests. This involves actively searching for properties that meet their preferences in terms of **type**, **price**, and **terms**. Unless specifically outlined in the **brokerage agreement**, agents are not required to continue searching for other properties once the buyer has entered into a **sales contract**.

## Key Responsibilities

### Property Search
- Actively look for properties that align with the buyer's preferences.
- The obligation to search ends once a sales contract is signed, unless otherwise specified.

### Drafting and Negotiating
- Assist buyers in crafting **offers**, **counteroffers**, **amendments**, and **addenda** to the contract.
- Develop negotiation strategies that align with the buyer's goals, using their objectives as a guide.

### Handling Offers and Counteroffers
- Present all written proposals to buyers promptly.
- Ensure no opportunity is missed, even if the buyer is already under a sales contract.

### Supporting the Buyer Through the Process
- Help buyers fulfill their **contractual duties**.
- Guide and support them throughout the transaction until **closing** and the keys change hands.

The agent role extends beyond finding the right property; it encompasses guiding and supporting buyers every step of the way to ensure a successful transaction.
