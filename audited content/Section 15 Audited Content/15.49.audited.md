# Compensation for Agency Services

When dealing with the **compensation for agency services**, it is typically based on a **percentage of the selling price** that the broker and client agree upon. The **commission rate** and any additional fees are fully negotiable.

## Important Rules

- **Negotiating Commission**: Virginia regulations (18VAC135-20‑e) require that any commission, commission split, management fee, or referral fee be negotiated and agreed upon in writing.
- **Escrow Requirements**: Under 18VAC135-20‑300, a licensee may not receive any portion of the earnest money or other funds until the transaction is consummated, unless otherwise agreed in writing.
- **Prohibited Practices**: Accepting commissions or fees from anyone other than the principal broker or supervising broker at the time of the transaction is prohibited (18VAC135-20‑300).

---

## Additional Guidance

- Commission structures (percentage, flat fee, hourly rate) can be customized per client needs.
- All compensation agreements must be documented in the brokerage agreement.
- Ensure compliance with escrow and commission rules to avoid disciplinary action.
