Real estate professionals must understand the role of a **Principal Broker**. In Virginia, every real estate firm is required to have a designated Principal Broker. This role comes with significant responsibilities that directly impact the firm's operations and compliance.

## Key Responsibilities of the Principal Broker

- **Ensuring Compliance with Virginia License Law**
  - The Principal Broker's primary duty is to ensure that the firm adheres to the **Virginia License Law**.
  - They make sure every practice and transaction carried out by the firm is legal and follows the rules set forth by the state.
  - This responsibility safeguards both the firm and the clients.

- **Liaison with the Virginia Real Estate Board**
  - Acts as the primary point of contact between the firm and the **Virginia Real Estate Board**.
  - Responsible for receiving and addressing communications, updates, or compliance checks from the board.
  - Ensures the firm remains aligned with the board's regulations and guidelines.

- **Supervision of Affiliated Licensees and Firm Activity**
  - Oversees the work of real estate agents and other affiliated licensees.
  - Ensures that everyone performs their duties correctly and ethically.
  - Creates an environment where professionals can thrive while upholding the highest standards of practice.
