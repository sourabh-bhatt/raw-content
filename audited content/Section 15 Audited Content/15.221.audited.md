# Managing Buyer Client Assets as a Standard Agent

<!-- When you're representing **buyer clients** as **standard agents**, it's crucial that you **manage and account for all money and property received on their behalf promptly**. This includes anything in which your buyer has or might have an interest. -->

When representing **buyer clients** as **standard agents**, agents must **manage and account for all money and property received on their behalf promptly**. This includes anything in which the buyer has or might have an interest.

## Key Responsibilities

- **Prompt management** of client assets
- **Accurate accounting** of all money and property

## Proper Management

<!-- - Upholds your **responsibility and trust** as an agent
- Ensures **transparency** in all transactions
- Maintains the **integrity** of your professional relationship with clients -->

- Upholds agent **responsibility and trust**
- Ensures **transparency** in all transactions
- Maintains the **integrity** of professional relationships with clients

<!-- By following these practices, you demonstrate professionalism and protect both your clients' interests and your reputation as a real estate agent. -->

Following these practices demonstrates professionalism and protects both client interests and agent reputation.
