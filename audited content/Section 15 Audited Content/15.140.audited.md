# Exclusive Agency Buyer Agency Agreement

<!-- When working with a prospective buyer as a real estate agent, one key agreement you might enter into is called the **Exclusive Agency Buyer Agency Agreement**. -->

When working with prospective buyers, real estate agents might enter into the **Exclusive Agency Buyer Agency Agreement**.

## What Is the Exclusive Agency Buyer Agency Agreement?

This is a relationship where the agent will only be compensated by the buyer if the agent finds a suitable property for them. However, if the buyer finds a suitable property on their own or purchases through another agent, they are not obligated to pay the agent.
