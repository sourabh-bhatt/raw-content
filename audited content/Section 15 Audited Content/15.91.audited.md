# Responsibilities of a Standard Agent Representing a Seller Client

<!-- When you become a **standard agent** representing a **seller client**, it is crucial to manage all **money** and **property** involved in the transaction with the utmost responsibility. This includes the following key duties: -->

A **standard agent** representing a **seller client** must manage all **money** and **property** involved in the transaction responsibly. This includes the following duties:

- Any **funds** or **property** that come into the agent's possession, in which the seller client has an interest, must be **accounted for promptly to the seller client**.
- Maintain **transparency** and **diligence** in tracking and reporting these assets.
- Ensure that the client is always fully informed and that their **interests are protected** throughout the **sales process**.

<!-- Your role demands careful management and clear communication to uphold the trust placed in you by your seller client. -->

The agent role demands careful management and clear communication to uphold the trust placed in them by the seller client.
