# Working in a Real Estate Team

<!-- When you work in real estate, you might consider joining a **real estate team**. This means you'll be part of a group of two or more individuals who collectively function as a single unit within the same **brokerage firm**. -->
Real estate professionals may join a **real estate team**. This means becoming part of a group of two or more individuals who collectively function as a single unit within the same **brokerage firm**.

## Team Structure and Operation

<!-- - You and your teammates present yourselves to the public under a unified front. -->
- Team members present themselves to the public under a unified front.
- Often, the team chooses a **fictitious name** to represent the group.
- For the team to operate legally, all members who engage in real estate activities must hold valid **real estate licenses**.

## Benefits of Joining a Real Estate Team

- Access to **shared resources**
- Benefit from **collective expertise**
<!-- - Receive support from fellow team members as you navigate your real estate career -->
- Receive support from fellow team members during real estate career development
