# Brokerage Relationship and Written Agreements

<!-- When you're entering into a **brokerage relationship**, it's crucial to have a **written agreement** in place. This document sets the foundation of your relationship with your client. -->

Agents entering a **brokerage relationship** must have a **written agreement** in place. This document sets the foundation of the relationship with the client.

In this agreement, agents specify how they will represent the client:

- Acting as an **agent** for the client, offering **standard** or **limited services**
- Acting as an **independent contractor** providing specific services

Agents must understand this distinction. It outlines **responsibilities**, **duties**, and services they will provide.
