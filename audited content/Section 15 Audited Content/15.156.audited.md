# Dual Agency in Brokerage and Agency Contracts

<!-- When navigating through **brokerage and agency contracts**, it's essential to understand the responsibilities involved with **dual agency**. -->

In **brokerage and agency contracts**, understanding the responsibilities involved with **dual agency** matters.

## Client Consent and Disclosure

<!-- - You must obtain **written consent** from your clients before dual agency begins. -->
- Agents must obtain **written consent** from clients before dual agency begins.
- This consent requires clear **disclosure** of the nature and potential risks of the **dual agency** relationship.
<!-- - The disclosure must explain the limitations on services you can provide in dual agency. -->
- The disclosure must explain the limitations on services agents can provide in dual agency.
- Clients must provide this written consent, confirming they understand the arrangement.

## Proper Consent Requirements

<!-- - Proper consent is not just a formality; it is a crucial step in maintaining **transparency** and **trust** with your clients. -->
<!-- - As a real estate professional, you will see that clear communication about dual agency protects all parties involved. -->

- Proper consent maintains **transparency** and **trust** with clients.
- Clear communication about dual agency protects all parties involved.

## Handling Client Hesitation or Refusal

<!-- - If a client hesitates or refuses to agree to dual agency, you must follow proper procedures to **terminate the existing agency relationship**. -->
<!-- - Termination in this case, when done properly, does not expose you to **liability**. -->
<!-- - This approach safeguards the interests of all parties and upholds the **integrity** of your professional role. -->

- If clients hesitate or refuse to agree to dual agency, agents must follow proper procedures to **terminate the existing agency relationship**.
- Termination in this case, when done properly, does not expose agents to **liability**.
- This approach safeguards the interests of all parties and upholds the **integrity** of the professional role.
