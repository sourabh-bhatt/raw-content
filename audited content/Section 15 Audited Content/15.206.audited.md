# Active Engagement in Real Estate

<!-- When you dive into the world of real estate, understanding your responsibilities in regard to **brokerage** and **agency contracts** is crucial. Let's break down a key concept you need to grasp: being *actively engaged* in the real estate profession. -->

Understanding responsibilities regarding **brokerage** and **agency contracts** involves the concept of being *actively engaged* in the real estate profession.

## What Does "Actively Engaged" Mean?

Being *actively engaged* means:

- Holding an **active license**
- Dedicating at least **35 hours per week** to **regulated real estate activities**

<!-- This concept is especially relevant if you're focusing on meeting the **broker experience requirement**. -->

This concept is especially relevant when meeting the **broker experience requirement**.

## Broker Experience Requirement

<!-- To qualify for the broker license, you should have: -->

To qualify for the broker license, agents should have:

- Worked as a **salesperson** for at least **24 months**
- Completed this experience within a **60-month timeframe**
