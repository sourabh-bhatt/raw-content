# Representing the Landlord in Leasing

## Responsibilities in Marketing and Tenant Selection

<!-- As your responsibility to represent the **landlord**, your main goal is to uphold their interests. When representing the **landlord**, the main goal is to uphold their interests. This means agents must carry out **marketing efforts** specifically tailored to attract **tenants**, in line with what the agent and landlord have agreed upon in the **brokerage contract**. The aim should be to secure a tenant who agrees to the **rent** and terms set out in this agreement, or at least terms the landlord finds acceptable.

<!-- - You are *not required* to keep looking for more leasing offers once the property is under a **lease agreement** or a **letter of intent to lease** has been signed, -->
- Agents are *not required* to keep looking for more leasing offers once the property is under a **lease agreement** or a **letter of intent to lease** has been signed,
- unless the **brokerage agreement**, **lease**, or **letter** specifically states otherwise.

## Duties in Lease and Offer Negotiations

<!-- Your duties also include aiding in the creation and negotiation of **leases** and **letters of intent to lease**. This involves: -->

Agent duties also include aiding in the creation and negotiation of **leases** and **letters of intent to lease**. This involves:

- Promptly handling all written **offers** or **counteroffers** for leasing,
- Even if there is already a lease or letter of intent in place.

<!-- It is your job to facilitate the process for the landlord, ensuring they have the assistance needed to successfully conclude the lease agreement. By focusing on these areas, you'll be effectively promoting and supporting the landlord's interests throughout the leasing process. -->

Agents must facilitate the process for the landlord, ensuring they have the assistance needed to conclude the lease agreement. By focusing on these areas, agents promote and support the landlord's interests throughout the leasing process.
