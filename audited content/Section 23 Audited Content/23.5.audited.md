# The HOME Federal Program and Its Impact on Low-Income Homebuyers

<!-- When exploring housing legislation and its impact on low-income homebuyers, it's essential to become familiar with the **HOME Federal Program**. -->

Housing legislation impacts low-income homebuyers through the **HOME Federal Program**. Initiated in 1990, this program plays a pivotal role in assisting individuals and families who struggle to purchase a home due to financial constraints.

## How the HOME Program Works

- The HOME program channels funds to states, cities, and counties, known as **participating jurisdictions (PJs)**.
- These PJs have the autonomy to tailor their assistance programs to meet local needs while operating within a common framework.
- To qualify for assistance, potential homeowners must have incomes that do not exceed **80% of the median income** in their area.

## Scope of Assistance Provided

<!-- The HOME program offers a broad range of financial support critical to purchasing a home, including: -->

The HOME program offers a broad range of financial support for purchasing a home, including:

- **Down payment assistance**: Reduces the upfront costs of buying a house.
- **Closing costs**: Covers fees associated with finalizing a home purchase.
- **Property rehabilitation**: Funds allocated to improve the safety and livability of homes for low-income buyers.
- **Primary financing options**: Supports initial home loan financing.
- **Interest rate subsidies**: Makes loans more affordable by reducing interest costs.
- **Loan guarantees**: Provides a safety net for lenders, encouraging them to offer loans to qualified buyers.
