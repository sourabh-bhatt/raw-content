# Americans with Disabilities Act (ADA) and Accessibility Requirements

<!-- You're going to learn about the **Americans with Disabilities Act (ADA)**, a crucial piece of federal legislation that impacts access to public buildings and the workplace for individuals with disabilities. -->

The **Americans with Disabilities Act (ADA)** is federal legislation that impacts access to public buildings and the workplace for individuals with disabilities. 

## Understanding ADA Regulations

<!-- It is important to grasp the essentials of **ADA regulations**, which include: -->

**ADA regulations** include:

- Ensuring **wheelchair accessibility** for parking and buildings
- Knowing the specifics about **handicapped parking requirements**
- Recognizing necessary **accessibility accommodations** for:
  - Bathrooms
  - Public telephones
