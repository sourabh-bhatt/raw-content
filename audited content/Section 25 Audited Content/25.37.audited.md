# Maintaining an Active Real Estate License

<!-- To maintain an active real estate license, you must understand and comply with certain regulations regarding **license renewal** and **reinstatement**. Here's what you need to know to keep your license in good standing and avoid any interruptions in your ability to operate as a real estate professional. -->
Maintaining an active real estate license requires understanding and compliance with certain regulations regarding **license renewal** and **reinstatement**. These requirements keep licenses in good standing and avoid interruptions in real estate operations.

## License Renewal and Reinstatement Requirements

<!-- - When your license needs renewing, it is crucial to meet all the necessary requirements outlined in the regulations. -->
- When a license needs renewing, meeting all necessary requirements outlined in the regulations is required.
- If seeking to **reinstate an active license that has lapsed**, fulfilling the **continuing education** obligations before license reactivation is required.
- However, if a license is **inactive** and reinstatement is desired, continuing education may not be necessary.

## Reinstatement Fees

If failing to renew a license on time, including not paying the renewal fee by the expiration date, a **reinstatement fee** applies. The fees vary depending on role and the type of license held:

- **Salespersons:** $100
- **Salespersons or brokers operating as a business entity:** $135
- **Brokers:** $120
- **Concurrent brokers:** $120
- **Firms:** $245
- **Branch offices:** $135

## Time Limits for Reinstatement

- A license can be reinstated up to **two years after its expiration** by paying the appropriate reinstatement fee.
- Beyond this two-year period, reinstatement is no longer an option.
- After two years, the full process of meeting current educational and examination requirements to apply for a new license must be undergone.

## Legal Considerations

- Engaging in any real estate activities with an **expired license** is **illegal**.
- Any transactions or services performed after a license has expired could be considered **unlicensed activity**, exposing the individual to potential legal action.
