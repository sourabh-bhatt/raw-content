# Virginia Real Estate Licensing Requirements

<!-- To ensure that you're well prepared for a career in real estate and fully understand the qualifications needed to obtain a license as a **real estate salesperson** or **broker** in Virginia, it's important to grasp the criteria set by the **Virginia Real Estate Board**. Obtaining a license as a **real estate salesperson** or **broker** in Virginia requires meeting qualifications set by the **Virginia Real Estate Board**. The criteria include:

## Integrity and Competence

- Maintaining a reputation for **honesty**, **truthfulness**, and **fair dealing** is paramount.
- Applicants must be seen as **competent** in managing real estate transactions in a way that protects the interests of the public.
- <!-- This foundation of **integrity** and **capability** is crucial for anyone entering the field. --> This foundation of **integrity** and **capability** is required for anyone entering the field.

## Educational and Exam Requirements

- Applicants are required to meet specific **educational requirements** before sitting for the licensing exam.
- For salesperson licensing, this includes completing 60 hours of pre-licensing education as outlined by law to ensure necessary knowledge and skills.
- Applicants must pass a **written examination** within 12 months after completing education.
  - The exam is administered by the board or an appointed testing service.
  - It assesses readiness to enforce and adhere to real estate laws and practices.
- <!-- Your behavior during the examination process is evaluated; following all established procedures and maintaining **professional conduct** is crucial. --> Behavior during the examination process is evaluated; following all established procedures and maintaining **professional conduct** is required.

## Licensing and Disciplinary Standing

- Standing in other jurisdictions where licensed matters.
- Applicants should have no **disciplinary actions** that resulted in **license suspension**, **revocation**, or **surrender**.
- Before applying in Virginia, ensure compliance with all board orders, including:
  - Paying any fees or penalties.
  - Completing any required additional education.

## Criminal Background and Fingerprinting

<!-- - An important step in the application process involves **fingerprinting** and disclosing any past **criminal convictions**. --> - The application process involves **fingerprinting** and disclosing any past **criminal convictions**.
- Disclosures must include:
  - All misdemeanors involving **moral turpitude**, **sexual offenses**, **drug distribution** (non-marijuana), or **physical injury** in the past five years.
  - Any **felony convictions** over the applicant's lifetime.
- Openness in this process is key, as the board has the discretion to deny licensure based on these disclosures.

## Basic Eligibility Criteria

- Applicants must be at least **18 years old**.
- Applicants must have a **high school diploma** or its equivalent, ensuring a baseline of general education.

## Board Discretion

- Even if some requirements related to past conduct or disciplinary actions are not fully met, the board **may still consider the application**.
- This is a discretionary process, with each case reviewed individually.
