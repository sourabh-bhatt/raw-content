Before installing real estate signs, including **"for sale" signs**, licensees must call **Miss Utility at 811**. **Safe digging practices** protect underground utility lines, ensure public safety, and prevent severe consequences.

## Background: Virginia Underground Utility Damage Prevention Act

Back in 1979, the **Virginia Underground Utility Damage Prevention Act** was enacted. Its goal was straightforward: to safeguard the network of underground utility lines crisscrossing Virginia, providing essential services to homes and businesses. Despite this, there's been a troubling uptick in damages caused by the installation of real estate signs, often because these signs are placed on **utility easements** without proper precautions.

Utility companies rely on these easements to bury their lines out of sight. Digging here without checking can lead to damages that range from disrupting services to causing injuries or even fatalities, plus economic and environmental tolls. Real estate industry professionals must ensure that any signs installed comply with state laws to prevent such outcomes.
