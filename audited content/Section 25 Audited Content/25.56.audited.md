Navigating **conflicts of interest** in real estate requires understanding specific regulations. This guidance stems from regulations outlined in the **Real Estate Board regulations**, with a focus on ensuring actions align with both **ethical** and **legal standards** in the industry. The following scenarios constitute conflicts of interest:

## Key Conflict of Interest Scenarios

1. **Working With Other Brokers**
   - If wanting to work with another real estate broker while having a principal broker, **written consent** from the principal broker must be obtained before taking any steps.
   - This keeps the relationship with the principal broker honest and clear.

2. **Representing Multiple Clients in the Same Transaction**
   - Licensees may represent more than one client in the same transaction only with proper disclosure and consent.
   - **Written consent** from *all* clients must be obtained before starting.
   - This ensures all clients know about the conflict and agree to it.

3. **Working Outside Your Brokerage Firm**
   - Licensees cannot do real estate work for clients outside their brokerage firm.
   - There are very few exceptions. Brokerage rules and Virginia Real Estate Board rules must be followed.
