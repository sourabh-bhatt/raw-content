# Escrow Account Management and Oversight in Real Estate

<!-- When dealing with **escrow accounts** in real estate, it's crucial to understand the potential consequences of mismanagement. The **Virginia Real Estate Board** has the authority to intervene if these accounts are not properly handled. -->
Escrow account mismanagement in real estate has potential consequences. The **Virginia Real Estate Board** has the authority to intervene if these accounts are not properly handled.

## Board and Court Intervention

- If an escrow account is mismanaged, the Virginia Real Estate Board can request a court to take control of the account.
- Upon court intervention, the real estate professional responsible for the account will be **barred from accessing it**.
- The court will appoint a **receiver**, a neutral third party, to manage the escrow account on behalf of all parties involved until proper distribution can be made.

## Purpose of Intervention

This process ensures that:

- The funds within the escrow account are handled properly.
- The interests of all parties involved are protected.
