# Maintaining Communication and Compliance with the Real Estate Board

## Updating Personal and Professional Information

<!-- When working as a real estate agent, it's essential to keep in constant communication with the **Real Estate Board** regarding your **professional credentials**. This involves always ensuring they have your most recent **name** and **home address** on file. -->
Real estate agents must maintain communication with the **Real Estate Board** regarding their **professional credentials**. This involves ensuring the board has the most recent **name** and **home address** on file. 

- Whenever there's a change, such as a new address or a name change, licensees are required to inform the board through **written communication within 10 days** of the change.
- Failing to keep the address up-to-date could result in missing notices or correspondence from the board, for which licensees will be responsible.
- Licensees are allowed to use a **professional name** different from their legal name, but this must also be communicated to the board before use.
- The professional name should be either your first or last name, without any titles added.

## License and Business Address Requirements

<!-- Your **license** is specifically tied to your **place of business**. Important points include:

The **license** is specifically tied to the **place of business**. Key requirements include:

- The license will only reflect the address of the business entity or sole proprietorship the licensee is actively working with.
- Any changes in the business address or firm switches must be reported to the board **within 10 days**.
- The board requires a **physical address**; a P.O. Box is not accepted as a valid business address.

## Protocols When Leaving a Firm

<!-- Clear procedures apply when you leave a firm, whether you are a salesperson, broker, or principal broker: -->
Clear procedures apply when leaving a firm, whether as a salesperson, broker, or principal broker:

- If a **salesperson or broker** ends their association with a business, the **sole proprietor or principal broker** must return the individual's license to the board **within 10 days** of termination.
  - The license must indicate the termination date and bear a signature upon return.
- If a **principal broker** leaves a firm, the **firm** must notify the board and return the license **within 10 days**.
  - The license must be signed and dated before being sent back.
