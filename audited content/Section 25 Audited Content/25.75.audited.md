# Real Estate License Regulations and Professional Conduct

<!-- In navigating the intricacies of becoming a proficient real estate agent, it's crucial that you familiarize yourself with specific regulations that govern your professional conduct. Real estate agents must understand specific regulations that govern professional conduct. The **Virginia Real Estate Board** has outlined a series of prohibitions that, if violated, could severely impact licenses and standing as real estate professionals.

## Key Prohibitions and Requirements

1. **Accuracy in License Applications and Maintenance**
   - Do not provide the board with information that is inaccurate or incomplete when applying for, renewing, reinstating, or maintaining licenses.

2. **License Limitations**
   - Licensees cannot hold more than one license as a real estate broker or salesperson at the same time.

3. **Examination Restrictions**
   - If holding a real estate salesperson license, the salesperson exam cannot be taken again.
   - If holding a real estate broker license, the broker exam cannot be taken again.

4. **Experience Verification Forms**
   - Only sign an experience verification form if directly supervising the applicant or having firsthand knowledge of their work.
   - Licensees cannot unreasonably refuse to sign the form if they have the required knowledge.

5. **Disclosure of Criminal Convictions**
   - Licensees must tell the board about any conviction for misdemeanors involving **moral turpitude**, sexual offenses, drug distribution, physical injury, or any felony.
   - This disclosure is required even if not appealing or unable to appeal anymore.

6. **Timely Notification of Pleas and Convictions**
   - If pleading guilty or nolo contendere, or being convicted of these crimes, licensees must tell the board in writing within 30 days.

7. **Disclosure of License Disciplinary Actions**
   - If a license was ever suspended, revoked, or surrendered anywhere due to disciplinary action, licensees must tell the board within 30 days.

8. **Safeguarding Public Interest**
   - Properly supervise financial transactions and **escrow accounts**.
   - Make sure people under supervision follow licensing rules.
   - Give accurate reports to the board.

9. **Prohibition of Improper Conduct**
   - Licensees cannot engage in improper, fraudulent, or dishonest conduct.
   - Examples include:
     - Changing documents to mislead people
     - Signing documents without permission
     - Lying about who owns a property
     - Not telling clients where **earnest money deposits** are kept
