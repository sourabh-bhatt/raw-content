# License Reinstatement and Denial by the Virginia Real Estate Board

The Virginia Real Estate Board has rules about regaining a license once it has been lost, expired, or revoked. This section explains why the board may deny license reinstatement.

## Reasons for Denial of License Reinstatement

The Virginia Real Estate Board may deny the reinstatement of a license based on several key factors:

1. **Same Grounds as Initial Denial or Discipline**
   - The board can deny reinstatement for the same reasons it would deny or discipline a license.
   - Examples include **unethical behavior**, failure to meet **education requirements**, or other infractions that affect eligibility.

2. **Non-Compliance with Prior Agreements or Orders**
   - If there has been a prior agreement for licensure or other board orders, failure to meet the terms of these agreements can result in denial.
   - Compliance demonstrates responsibility and commitment to professional standards.

3. **Unpaid Monetary Penalties and Costs**
   - Failure to fully pay any **monetary penalties**, costs imposed by the board, and any accrued interest can block reinstatement.
   - Meeting all **financial obligations** to the board is a prerequisite.

## Compliance Requirements

Board regulations require:

- Maintaining **good standing** with the board
- Adhering to **professional conduct** standards
- Fulfilling all **financial responsibilities**

Compliance reduces the risk of license reinstatement denial.
