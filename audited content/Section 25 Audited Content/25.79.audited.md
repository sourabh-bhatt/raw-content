# Limitations on Recovery from the Real Estate Fund

<!-- When it comes to understanding the limitations on recovery from the **real estate fund**, it's essential to grasp some key concepts outlined in the **Real Estate Licensing Law**. These regulations are designed to ensure fairness and accountability in the real estate profession. Let's break them down into clearer terms. -->
The limitations on recovery from the **real estate fund** involve specific concepts outlined in the **Real Estate Licensing Law**. These regulations are designed to ensure fairness and accountability in the real estate profession.

## Limits on Claims for a Single Transaction

- If a real estate agent engages in improper or dishonest behavior during a sale, lease, or management of property that results in unpaid judgments, the total amount paid out to all claimants from the fund for a single transaction **cannot exceed $50,000**.
- When multiple claimants are affected by the same transaction and claims may exceed $50,000, the board can **pause payouts for up to one year**.
- After the pause, if total claims still exceed $50,000, the fund amount will be **divided proportionally among claimants** based on their unpaid judgment amounts.
- For an individual claimant, the **maximum recovery from the fund for a single transaction is capped at $20,000**, regardless of the judgment size or number of claimants.

## Limits on Claims Across Multiple Transactions

- When one agent's actions affect **more than one transaction**, the total claims against them **cannot exceed $100,000 during any two-year license period** (ending June 30 of every even-numbered year).
- Similar to the single transaction rule, if claims might exceed $100,000, payments can be **paused for up to one year**.
- If claims exceed the $100,000 limit after the pause, funds will be **proportionally distributed among claimants**.

## Additional Provisions

- Claims against the fund **cannot include amounts for interest or punitive damages** from the judgment.
- However, **legal costs and attorney's fees are eligible** to be included in claims.
- If the fund lacks sufficient money to cover claims at any time, claims will be **paid in the order they were filed once funds become available**.
- Failure to meet specific **procedural requirements** does **not automatically disqualify** a claimant from recovery if they are otherwise entitled.
