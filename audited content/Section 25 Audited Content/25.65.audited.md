# Changing Your Professional Affiliation as an Active Licensee

<!-- When you're an **active licensee**, whether a **broker** or **salesperson**, and you decide to move your **professional affiliation** from one firm to another, you must inform the **Board** about this change. -->
When an **active licensee**, whether a **broker** or **salesperson**, moves their **professional affiliation** from one firm to another, the **Board** must be informed about this change.

## Steps to Update Your License

- Submit a **license transfer application** to the Board within **30 days** of changing your affiliation.
- The new **supervising broker** must also submit the required documentation to the Board.
- Pay the designated **fee** associated with this process.

This step ensures the **license** accurately reflects the current place of practice, maintaining **compliance** with the Board's regulations. This procedure keeps **professional credentials** up to date.
