# Limitations of the Virginia Real Estate Board

<!-- When delving into the regulations of the **Virginia Real Estate Board**, it's crucial to grasp a couple of key limitations you'll encounter. -->
The **Virginia Real Estate Board** has specific limitations in its regulatory scope.

## Dispute Resolution

- The Board **does not resolve disputes between licensees or handle contract disputes** between buyers and sellers.
- In case of disagreements or conflicts, resolution must be sought through **civil court or mediation** outside the Board's jurisdiction.

## Commission Rates and Sales Agreements

- The Board **does not set commission rates** nor provide standardized agreement templates, including sales contracts.
- Licensees are responsible for **negotiating commission rates** directly with clients or through their brokerage.
- Licensees must either **develop sales agreements** or use approved forms from their brokerage or other sources.

These boundaries define the scope of the Board's regulatory authority in real estate practice.
