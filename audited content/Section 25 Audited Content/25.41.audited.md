# License Application Timeline After Passing the State Exam

<!-- When you're embarking on the journey of becoming a licensed real estate agent, one critical milestone involves passing a **state exam**. However, successfully clearing the exam is just a part of the process. It's important to understand that once you've passed, you're on a timeline. -->
After passing the **state exam** for real estate licensing, applicants must follow specific timeline requirements for license application submission.

## 24-Month Window to Submit Your License Application

- Applicants have a **24-month window** to submit their **license application**.
- The application can be for either an **active** or **inactive license**, depending on career plans.

## Consequences of Missing the Deadline

- Failing to submit your application within the 24-month period means you must retake the **state exam**.
- This rule ensures that only those who are up-to-date with current real estate practices and regulations become licensed agents, maintaining **professional standards** within the industry.

<!-- Keeping track of this deadline is crucial as you move forward in your career. -->
Tracking this deadline prevents the need to retake the state exam.
