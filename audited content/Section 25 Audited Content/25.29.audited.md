# Responding to Board Inquiries: Timelines and Responsibilities

<!-- When you are approached by the **Board** with an inquiryâ€”whether it is a request for information or for specific recordsâ€”it is crucial that you respond in a **timely manner**. -->
When approached by the **Board** with an inquiry - whether it is a request for information or for specific records - licensees must respond in a **timely manner**. This responsibility applies to all real estate licensees, including both **salespersons** and **brokers**, regardless of whether the license is **active or inactive**.

## Response Timelines

- For most inquiries, a **21-day window** exists to provide the necessary information or documents.
- If the request specifically concerns **financial records**, the timeline is shorter, and compliance is required within **10 days**.
