# Ongoing Education Requirements for Virginia Real Estate Licensees

<!-- As part of maintaining your qualifications as a real estate agent, it's essential to engage in **ongoing education**. This commitment to continuous learning ensures that you're always up-to-date on the latest industry standards, regulations, and best practices. Here's a breakdown of what you need to know: -->
Virginia real estate licensees must engage in **ongoing education** to maintain their qualifications. This education keeps licensees current on industry standards, regulations, and best practices.

## 1. Post-License Education (PLE) for New Salespersons

- New salespersons are required to complete **30 hours of post-license education** within their first 12 months of licensure.
- This applies regardless of whether you are in an active or inactive status.
- This initial phase of education builds upon foundational knowledge to help navigate real estate transactions effectively.

## 2. Continuing Education (CE) for Established Licensees

After the initial year, licensees must complete continuing education every two years. Requirements differ depending on role:

### Salespersons

- Must complete a minimum of **16 hours** of continuing education every two years.
- This education keeps licensees informed about changes in real estate laws, emerging market trends, and other aspects that influence daily operations.

### Brokers

- Brokers have a broader scope of responsibility and must complete **24 hours** of continuing education every two years.
- This additional education equips brokers with advanced knowledge and skills to support their sales teams and manage complex transactions.
