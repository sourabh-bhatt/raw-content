# Continuing Education Requirements for Virginia Real Estate Licensees

<!-- Understanding and fulfilling Continuing Education (CE) requirements is essential for maintaining an active real estate license in Virginia. -->
Continuing Education (CE) requirements must be fulfilled to maintain an active real estate license in Virginia. The Real Estate Board sets these regulations to ensure brokers and salespersons remain knowledgeable about current trends, laws, and ethical standards.

## CE Requirements for Salespersons

- After completing Post-License Education (PLE) during the first renewal cycle, salespersons must complete **16 credit-hours** of continuing education every two years.
- **Mandatory topics (8 credit-hours):**
  - Fair housing laws (2 hours)
  - Ethics (3 hours)
  - Legal updates (1 hour)
  - Agency principles (1 hour)
  - Contracts (1 hour)
- The remaining **8 credit-hours** can be fulfilled through approved elective courses, allowing flexibility to explore specialized areas within real estate.

## CE Requirements for Brokers

- Brokers must complete **24 credit-hours** of continuing education every two years.
- **Mandatory topics (16 credit-hours):**
  - Broker supervision & management (8 hours) - covering knowledge for managing a real estate office or agents.
  - Fair housing laws (2 hours)
  - Ethics (3 hours)
  - Legal updates (1 hour)
  - Agency principles (1 hour)
  - Contracts (1 hour)
- Brokers also have the option to complete **8 credit-hours** of approved electives.

## Reciprocity CE Requirements

Licensees holding a license in another jurisdiction who wish to practice in Virginia must meet reciprocity CE conditions:

- Focus areas include fair housing laws, legal updates (including flood zone areas and the National Flood Insurance Program), ethics and standards of conduct, and fundamentals of real estate agency and contracts.
- **Salespersons:** 8 hours of study.
- **Brokers:** 16 hours, with emphasis on managing a real estate brokerage firm.

## Documentation and Compliance

- Licensees must retain proof of CE completion for **three years**.
- Proof must be presented upon request by the Real Estate Board.
- Failure to provide documentation can result in **non-renewal of license**, **disciplinary action**, or both.

## Inactive License Status

- No annual CE requirements while the license is inactive.
- To **reactivate** a license, all CE requirements must be completed beforehand.
- Reactivation is allowed within a **five-year window** without retaking pre-license education.
- Inactive salespersons must still complete their PLE within the first year of obtaining their license.

## Exemptions and Reductions

- In cases of certified illness or undue hardship, licensees may request an **exemption or reduction** in CE requirements from the Board.
