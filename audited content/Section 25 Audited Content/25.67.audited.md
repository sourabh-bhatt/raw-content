# Licensee Responsibilities and Compliance in Virginia Real Estate

<!-- In the realm of real estate, every **licensee**, whether you're a salesperson or broker, active or inactive, must uphold a standard of *worthiness* and *competence* when providing real estate services to the public. Every **licensee**, whether a salesperson or broker, active or inactive, must uphold a standard of *worthiness* and *competence* when providing real estate services to the public. Licenses come with specific responsibilities, and certain actions are strictly prohibited.

## Key Prohibitions and Requirements

- **Providing Accurate Information:** It is prohibited to provide substantially inaccurate or incomplete information to the **Real Estate Board**. Transparency and factual accuracy are essential in all dealings.

- **Single License Rule:** Licensees cannot hold multiple licenses simultaneously. This ensures focus and responsibility are not divided, allowing for the best service to clients.

- **Retaking the Licensing Exam:** Retaking the licensing exam is only allowed for valid causes. Attempting to retake it without proper reason is not permitted.

- **Document Signing and Experience Verification:** Always be fully aware of the contents before signing any document. Unreasonably refusing to sign an **experience verification form** is not allowed.

- **Reporting Criminal Convictions:** Criminal convictions related to **moral turpitude**, sex offenses, or drug distribution (excluding marijuana) must be reported to the Board within 60 days of the plea or conviction. Failure to report can lead to severe consequences for the license.

- **Fair Housing Compliance:** Fair housing laws guarantee equal access to housing opportunities. Violating these laws or failing to report violations can result in disciplinary action.

- **Interstate License Compliance:** If operating in multiple states, licensees must respect and comply with all applicable license laws and regulations in each jurisdiction.
