# Understanding Firm Licenses and Branch Office Regulations in Virginia Real Estate

## Broker Owned Sole Proprietorship

<!-- When operating as a **Broker Owned Sole Proprietorship**, if you choose to run your firm independently as a sole proprietor with a single location and no branch offices, you still need a **firm license**. This structure may simplify operations and allows for direct control over your business. -->
When operating as a **Broker Owned Sole Proprietorship**, if running the firm independently as a sole proprietor with a single location and no branch offices, a **firm license** is still needed. This structure may simplify operations and allows for direct control over the business.

## Branch Offices

<!-- If you plan to expand your operations to multiple locations, each **branch office** must: -->
If expanding operations to multiple locations, each **branch office** must:

- Be properly licensed.
- Be under the diligent supervision of a **broker**.
- Display the branch license prominently at each location (this is a mandatory requirement).

## Use of Names

<!-- Operating under a **fictitious name** is allowed for all firms. However, if you are a single broker owner, you must:

Operating under a **fictitious name** is allowed for all firms. However, single broker owners must:

- Register their **legal name** with the Virginia Real Estate Board.
- Register their chosen fictitious name.
- Provide their residential and business addresses.

This registration promotes transparency and maintains public trust.

## All Other Firms (Corporations, Partnerships, Associations, LLCs, Multiple Brokers)

For firms involving multiple brokers, non-brokers, or organized as a **corporation, partnership, association, or limited liability company (LLC)**:

- Securing a **firm license** is mandatory.
- Individual licenses are required for all professionals involved.
- The firm must be authorized to conduct business by the **Virginia State Corporation Commission** to ensure compliance with state laws.

### Branch Offices in Complex Firms

Similar to sole proprietorships, every branch office in these firms must:

- Hold a valid license.
- Have broker supervision.
- Display the branch license prominently.

### Naming Requirements for Complex Firms

- Operating under a fictitious name is common.
- Licensing for **non-broker owned firms** depends on using the company's **legal name**, which may also be fictitious.

Adhering to these regulations ensures proper navigation of the licensure process and maintains compliance in Virginia real estate practice.
