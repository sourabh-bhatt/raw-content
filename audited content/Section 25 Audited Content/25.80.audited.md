# Composition of the Virginia Real Estate Board (VREB)

<!-- The **Virginia Real Estate Board (VREB)** plays a significant role in the real estate industry. Understanding its composition is essential to appreciate how it functions and makes decisions. -->
The **Virginia Real Estate Board (VREB)** plays a role in the real estate industry. The board's composition determines how it functions and makes decisions.

## Board Membership

- The board consists of **nine members** in total.
- **Seven members** must be **licensed brokers or salespersons**.
- These licensed professionals are required to have at least **five consecutive years of experience** in the real estate field. This requirement ensures the board benefits from a high level of expertise and a deep understanding of the real estate market.

## Consumer Representation

- In addition to the seven licensed professionals, the board includes **two citizen members**.
- These citizens are not active brokers or salespersons but represent the **consumer's perspective**.
- Their role is to contribute to the board's decisions from a layman's point of view, ensuring that the board considers the wider public's interests.

## Board Diversity

The diverse makeup of the Real Estate Board includes:

- **Professional expertise** from experienced brokers and salespersons
- **Public representation** through citizen members

This balance ensures that the board's decisions consider the interests of all stakeholders in the real estate market.
