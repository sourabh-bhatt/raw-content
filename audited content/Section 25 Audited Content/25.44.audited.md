# Financial Responsibilities and the Real Estate Transaction Recovery Fund

<!-- When you start your journey as a real estate professional, you'll be introduced to certain financial responsibilities and the importance of a particular fund that supports the regulatory framework of your profession. Let's explore how this operates and why it's crucial for you. -->
Real estate professionals have financial responsibilities related to the **Real Estate Transaction Recovery Fund** that supports the regulatory framework.

## Initial License Fee and Fund Contribution

- Upon receiving a license, a **$20 fee** is assessed.
- This fee is specifically allocated to the **Real Estate Transaction Recovery Fund**.
- The fee can be part of the application process and must be transferred to the fund by the regulatory authority within **ten days**.

## Handling and Security of Fund Contributions

- All subsequent contributions to this fund after the initial fee are:
  - Deposited into **secured, federally insured financial institutions** within **three working days** of receipt.
  - Managed with a high level of trust and responsibility, ensuring security beyond federal insurance levels through specific security measures.

## Fund Balance Requirements and Management

- The fund must maintain a **minimum balance of $400,000**.
- If the balance drops below this threshold:
  - The regulatory authority must inform the Real Estate Board immediately.
  - Solutions to replenish the fund are suggested, including transferring **interest earnings** to meet the minimum.

## Additional Fee Assessments

- If the fund dips below the required minimum and interest earnings are insufficient:
  - Each professional may be assessed an **additional fee**.
  - This fee is capped at **no more than $20 during any two-year license period** to ensure fairness.

## Surplus Fund Allocation

- At the end of each fiscal year, if the fund's balance exceeds **$2,000,000**:
  - The surplus is transferred to **the general fund of the state treasury**.
