# Virginia Real Estate Board Regulations: Ethical Standards and Compliance

<!-- Under **Virginia Real Estate Board regulations**, it's critical to understand what actions are strictly off-limits to maintain professional integrity. -->
Under **Virginia Real Estate Board regulations**, certain actions are strictly prohibited to maintain professional integrity.

## Prohibited Actions

- **Permitting someone else to sit for the license examination in place of the actual applicant is prohibited.** This violates integrity and violates Virginia Real Estate Board rules.

- **Providing truthful information when filling out examination or license applications is required.** Falsifying information on these documents violates Virginia Real Estate Board regulations.

Following these rules is required to maintain a Virginia real estate license.
