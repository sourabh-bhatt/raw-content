# Changing Real Estate Brokerage Affiliation in Virginia

<!-- When you're licensed as a real estate agent, you might find yourself needing to change the brokerage you're associated with. Here's how you do it, based on the regulations set forth in the *Code of Virginia*. -->
Licensed real estate agents may need to change their brokerage affiliation. The process follows regulations set forth in the *Code of Virginia*.

## Transfer Procedure

To make this transfer possible, follow these steps:

- **Complete and submit a transfer application.**
- **Pay the required fee** as outlined in another section of the *Code of Virginia*.
<!-- - Ensure the transfer application includes the **signature of your new principal broker**. This signature is crucial because this individual, vested with signature authority, will be overseeing your real estate activities once the transfer is approved. -->
- Ensure the transfer application includes the **signature of the new principal broker**. This signature is required because this individual, vested with signature authority, will be overseeing the agent's real estate activities once the transfer is approved.

## Broker's Signature

<!-- The effectiveness of the transfer hinges on the execution of the transfer application by your new principal broker. Their signature:

- Sets the wheels in motion for the transfer process.
- Signifies their responsibility for your professional activities henceforth. -->
The effectiveness of the transfer depends on the execution of the transfer application by the new principal broker. Their signature:

- Initiates the transfer process.
- Signifies their responsibility for the agent's professional activities.
