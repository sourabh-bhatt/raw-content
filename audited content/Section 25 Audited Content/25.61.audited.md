# Real Estate Licensing Requirements and Roles in Virginia

<!-- When working in real estate, it's essential to grasp the roles and licensing requirements set by the **Virginia Real Estate Board**. These rules ensure businesses and individuals operate within legal boundaries. Here's what's required for different entities and individuals in the real estate business. -->
Real estate work requires understanding the roles and licensing requirements set by the **Virginia Real Estate Board**. These rules ensure businesses and individuals operate within legal boundaries. The following requirements apply to different entities and individuals in the real estate business.

## Licensing Requirements for Real Estate Firms

- If part of any business structure **other than a sole proprietorship**, entities must secure a **real estate firm license** from the Virginia Real Estate Board before engaging in any brokerage activities.
- This requirement includes advertising your services as a real estate firm.
- A firm may operate under a **fictitious name** provided it has the license to do so.
- To be eligible for a firm license, every individual with a significant role in the firm's brokerage activities (such as managing members or officers) must hold a valid **real estate broker license**.
- Anyone employed or contracted by the firm to act as a salesperson must possess either a **salesperson** or **broker license**.

## Sole Proprietorship Brokers

- Brokers who own a real estate brokerage as a **sole proprietorship** may operate without obtaining a firm license from the Virginia Real Estate Board.
- However, operating under a **fictitious name** is not permitted without the necessary licensure.
- Brokers can own a separate business entity for their real estate dealings, provided it complies with licensing requirements.

## Broker Roles and Licensing

- No individual may act as a broker without obtaining the appropriate **broker license** from the Virginia Real Estate Board.
- Once licensed, a broker can:
  - Function as a salesperson for another broker.
  - Hold an ownership or management position in a business entity acting as a salesperson.

## Salesperson Licensing

- Anyone wishing to engage in real estate sales must obtain a **salesperson license**.
- A business entity can act as a salesperson if it obtains a distinct **business entity salesperson license**.
- This license requires that any active participant in the brokerage business hold a relevant **salesperson** or **broker license**.

## Real Estate Teams

- Teams composed of brokers, salespersons, or both must secure a **business entity salesperson license** to operate legally.
- Such teams are allowed to employ **unlicensed assistants** under specific legal provisions.

## Branch Office Licensing

- If a **principal broker** operates multiple businesses in Virginia, a separate **branch office license** is required for each location.
- A copy of the branch office license must be prominently displayed at each site.
