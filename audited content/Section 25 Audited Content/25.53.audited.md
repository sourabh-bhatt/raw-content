# Operating a Real Estate Firm: Key Responsibilities and Office Management

## Place of Business

<!-- When running a real estate firm, you must carefully consider the location of your business. You cannot operate your firm out of your home living room if your living and working spaces are mixed. If you want your firm at your residential property, ensure there is a **clear separation** between your personal living space and your business office, including **separate entrances**. -->
When running a real estate firm, the location of the business must be carefully considered. Firms cannot operate out of home living rooms if living and working spaces are mixed. If the firm is at a residential property, there must be a **clear separation** between personal living space and business office, including **separate entrances**.

- If a broker lives in Virginia, they are **required** to have at least one office location within the state.
- If a broker does not live in Virginia, they are **not mandated** to maintain a location there.

## Main Office Management

<!-- The main office serves as more than just a gathering place for you and your team; it is the repository for critical documents and records. -->
The main office serves as more than just a gathering place; it is the repository for critical documents and records.

<!-- As the **principal broker**, you are responsible for:

- Keeping your firm's license, your broker's license, and the licenses of all salespeople and associate brokers at the main office.
- Ensuring these licenses are **readily available for public viewing upon request**.
- Maintaining **all financial records** for all locations consolidated in one place, whether at the main or a branch office. -->
The **principal broker** is responsible for:

- Keeping the firm's license, the broker's license, and the licenses of all salespeople and associate brokers at the main office.
- Ensuring these licenses are **readily available for public viewing upon request**.
- Maintaining **all financial records** for all locations consolidated in one place, whether at the main or a branch office.

## Main Office Supervision

Supervision is essential for compliance and smooth operation.

<!-- - Either you, as the principal broker, or an appointed broker must oversee all real estate brokerage services performed by licensees at the main office. -->
- Either the principal broker or an appointed broker must oversee all real estate brokerage services performed by licensees at the main office.
- If a **designated supervising broker** is appointed, their **name and license number** must be communicated to the Virginia Real Estate Board.

## Branch Office Management

Each branch office must be licensed separately.

<!-- - You must designate one location as your **main office** among all offices. -->
- One location must be designated as the **main office** among all offices.

## Branch Office Records

At each branch office:

- The supervising broker must display the **office license** and a **list of assigned licensees** in a visible location.
- Only the list of licensees needs to be displayed at the branch; the actual licenses are kept at the main office.

## Branch Office Supervision

- Each branch office requires a supervising broker to oversee all licensees working at that location.
- The supervising broker must notify the board of their **name and license number**, along with the names and license numbers of all licensees at the branch.

<!-- Understanding and adhering to these operational duties will ensure your firm runs smoothly and complies with Virginia real estate board regulations. -->
Adhering to these operational duties ensures firms run smoothly and comply with Virginia real estate board regulations.
