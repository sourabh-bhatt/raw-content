# Timely and Accurate Communication in Real Estate Transactions

In real estate, **timely and accurate communication** is mandated by regulations designed to protect all parties involved, ensuring fairness and transparency. Below is a breakdown of requirements:

## Delivery of Instruments and Documents

- Every principal in a transaction must receive **complete and legible copies** of all instruments and documents, including:
  - Listings
  - Leases
  - Offers and counteroffers
  - Addenda
  - Ratified agreements
  - Any other contract-required documents
<!-- - **Prompt delivery** of these documents is essential. Delaying or failing to provide them violates regulations. -->
- **Prompt delivery** of these documents is required. Delaying or failing to provide them violates regulations.

## Communication of Material Changes

- Any **material changes** to the transaction must be communicated **in writing** to all principals **in a timely manner**.
- This ensures all parties are immediately informed of significant developments.

## Closing Statements

- At the close of a transaction, both the seller and buyer must receive a **detailed statement** of all monies received and disbursed by the licensee.
- This statement must be **signed by the licensee or supervising broker**.
- If a **settlement agent outside the brokerage** handles the closing and includes these details in their settlement statement, the licensee is not required to provide a separate accounting.

## Return of Documents and Instruments

- If holding any documents or instruments that belong to someone else, licensees must **return them upon request**, unless there is a valid reason not to.
- Holding onto another party's property without cause can result in disciplinary action.
