# Liability for Disseminating False Information in Real Estate

<!-- When navigating the complexities of real estate, it's crucial for you to understand the circumstances under which you might be held liable for disseminating false information. To shield yourself effectively, grasp these specific conditions where liability is **not** applicable to you: -->
Real estate licensees must understand the circumstances under which they might be held liable for disseminating false information. Specific conditions exist where liability is **not** applicable:

## Conditions Protecting Licensees from Liability

1. **Information Received from Client**
   - If the information that turned out to be false was given by the client, the licensee is protected from liability.
   - This emphasizes maintaining clear, documented communications with clients.

2. **Information from Governmental Entities**
   - Licensees are protected if the false information was procured from any governmental entity.
   - Since these sources are generally considered reliable, reliance on them is not held against the licensee.

3. **Information via Nongovernmental Sources from Governmental Entities**
   - If information is received indirectly through a nongovernmental person or entity, which originally obtained it from a governmental source, the licensee is not liable for the misinformation.
   - Verifying the direct source as a governmental entity provides protection.

4. **Reliance on Professionals**
   - Provided the false information was acquired from a person who is licensed, certified, or registered to offer professional services within the Commonwealth, and reasonable grounds existed to rely on this information, the licensee is not at fault.
   - This underscores consulting with and relying on certified professionals for information critical to transactions.

## Exceptions to Liability Protection

Licensees are **not** protected under these conditions if:

- They *knew* the information was false (having actual knowledge), or
- They acted with *reckless disregard* for the truth (not caring whether the information was true or false).
