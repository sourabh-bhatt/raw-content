# Understanding the Term "Regulant" in Real Estate

<!-- As you delve into the world of real estate, understanding the terminology used by the **Virginia Real Estate Board** is crucial. One term you'll often come across is **"Regulant"**. -->
The **Virginia Real Estate Board** uses specific terminology in regulatory contexts. The term **"Regulant"** applies to regulated entities. 

## What is a Regulant?

A *regulant* refers to any person or entity who is subject to regulation by the Virginia Real Estate Board. This includes **salespersons**, **brokers**, real estate firms, property management companies, and other entities licensed or regulated by the Board.
