# Managing Financial and Escrow Records in Real Estate

<!-- As a future real estate professional, one of your key responsibilities involves managing **financial and escrow records** meticulously. Let's delve into how you should handle these duties effectively. -->
Real estate professionals have responsibilities involving managing **financial and escrow records** meticulously. The following covers how to handle these duties effectively.

## Duration for Keeping Records

- Maintain records of all real estate transactions, including those involving escrow, for a period of **three years**.
- This timeframe applies from:
  - The date of closing, or
  - If no closing occurred, from when the contract was ratified, terminated, or when involvement in the transaction concluded.
- Retain **disclosures** and **brokerage agreements** for three years from their execution date or the date they were presented to a party.

## Accountability and Timely Remittance

- Account for **all funds held on behalf of parties**, including escrow funds.
- Remit these funds within a **reasonable timeframe**, meaning promptly and without unnecessary delay.

## Compliance with Board Requests

- Submit requested records to the Board **within twenty-one days** of the request.
- This ensures transparency and compliance with regulatory standards.

## Managing Escrow Accounts

<!-- As the **principal broker**, you are primarily responsible for the integrity of escrow accounts. Supervision also extends to any licensed real estate agent with authority over these accounts. -->
The **principal broker** is primarily responsible for the integrity of escrow accounts. Supervision also extends to any licensed real estate agent with authority over these accounts.

### Violations to Avoid

- **Commingling:** The illegal practice of mixing escrow funds with personal or business funds. Always keep escrow funds separate to ensure transparency and integrity.
- **Conversion:** Misappropriating escrow funds for personal use or other unauthorized purposes; considered theft.
- **Inappropriate Disbursement:** Using funds from one escrow account to cover shortages in another is prohibited. Each account must be managed independently.

## Acknowledging Receipt

- Whenever receiving escrow funds or property, **acknowledge receipt** in the relevant contract, such as an offer or lease agreement.

## Reporting Violations

- If becoming aware of any escrow-related violations, report them promptly.
- As a principal broker, if suspecting a violation has occurred, notify the Board **within three business days**.
- This commitment to ethical practice protects all parties involved and upholds the integrity of the real estate profession.
