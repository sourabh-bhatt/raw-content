# Recovery from the Virginia Real Estate Recovery Fund

When obtaining a **final judgment** in a Virginia court against a **real estate licensee** for **improper** or **dishonest actions** in selling, leasing, or managing property, claimants have the right to seek recovery from a designated fund. This applies specifically if these actions occurred while the individual or entity was acting in their **licensed capacity**, rather than for personal dealings.

## How to Make a Claim from the Recovery Fund

To make a claim, follow these steps:

1. **Serve Legal Process to the Virginia Real Estate Board**
   - When starting legal action against a real estate licensee, serve a copy of the legal process to the **Virginia Real Estate Board**.
   - Include an **affidavit** detailing the improper or dishonest conduct.

2. **Submit Subsequent Legal Documents**
   - Any legal documents filed after the initial ones must also be sent to the Board.
   - Deliver these via **certified mail** or a **similar delivery method**.

3. **File a Verified Claim**
   - File a **verified claim** to request compensation from the fund within **12 months** after the judgment becomes final.
   - This applies to judgments entered on or after **July 1, 1996**.

4. **Eligibility Restrictions**
   - Claimants cannot be licensed real estate professionals, related to one, or represent one.
   - Banks and people in construction or development cannot claim from the fund.

5. **Complete Search for Debtor Assets**
   - Before filing for recovery, do a complete search to determine if the debtor has any assets that could satisfy the judgment.
   - Document this search in an affidavit, including any debtor interrogatories and checks on assets or commissions.

6. **Use All Legal Ways to Satisfy Judgment**
   - If assets are found, use all legal ways to collect from these assets toward satisfying the judgment.
   - Document efforts and their outcomes.

7. **Bankruptcy Considerations**
   - If the debtor has declared bankruptcy, file a complaint in the bankruptcy court to determine **whether the debt can be discharged**.

## Review and Decision Process

- The Department will review the claim and investigation.
- If there is a good case for payment, the Department will notify the licensee who caused the harm, giving them a chance to respond.
- If no contest is raised within a specified period, the claim proceeds for the Board's recommendation.

## Important Notes on Claims

- Claims are **not invalidated** by the absence of a court's explicit finding of dishonest or improper conduct, as long as evidence supports such a conclusion.
- The Board determines eligibility based on the substance of the verified claim.

## Payment and Appeals

- Once the Board confirms all conditions for recovery are met, it will issue an order for payment, following **legal limits**.
- Claimants will receive written notice of the Board's decision.
- This decision can be reviewed judicially under certain conditions.
- The Board reserves the right to appeal any court decision conflicting with its recommendation or authorized distribution.
