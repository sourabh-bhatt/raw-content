# Trust Accounts in Real Estate Transactions

<!-- When you're navigating through the world of real estate transactions, you'll encounter something known as **trust accounts**, also referred to as **escrow accounts**. -->
In real estate transactions, **trust accounts**, also referred to as **escrow accounts**, are specific types of bank accounts set up to ensure that money belonging to others - buyers, sellers, tenants, or landlords - is held securely until certain conditions are met.

## State Rules and Common Elements

<!-- You need to understand that different states have their own rules around these accounts. -->
Different states have their own rules around these accounts. In Virginia, specific requirements must be followed:

- **Earnest money deposits** must be deposited within 5 banking days after acceptance of a contract.
- Trust accounts must be maintained in Virginia-based financial institutions.
- Rules regarding the **interest** that might accumulate on the funds in these trust accounts.

## Interest on Trust Account Funds

In Virginia, interest earned on trust account funds typically belongs to the parties who deposited the funds unless otherwise agreed in writing. Brokers must understand whether they are entitled to the interest earned on the funds or if it remains with the **client's funds**.
