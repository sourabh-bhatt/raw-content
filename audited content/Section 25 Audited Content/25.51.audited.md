# Business Entity Salesperson License in Virginia Real Estate

Real estate professionals have various **licensing options** available. The **Business Entity Salesperson License** is suited for **salespersons** or **associate brokers** who aim to reduce their personal liability and enjoy tax benefits by creating a **business entity**, such as a **corporation**, **partnership**, or **limited liability company**. This can be helpful for sales teams.

## Choosing the Business Entity Salesperson License

<!-- - You can decide if operating under this business model fits your career objectives.

- Professionals can evaluate if operating under this business model fits their objectives.
- If forming a **real estate team**, securing a Business Entity Salesperson License is **required** before working together at the brokerage firm.

## Limitations of the Business Entity Salesperson License

- This license **does not replace** the need for individual or firm licenses.
- It **does not permit** conducting brokerage activities.
- To perform brokerage activities, a **Broker License** is mandatory.

## Qualification Criteria for the Business Entity Salesperson License

To qualify, the following criteria must be met:

1. **Active Participation**: Owners or officers who take an active role in the business must hold a valid license as either a salesperson or an associate broker. This ensures that key decision-makers are qualified and adhere to industry standards.

2. **Authorization to Operate in Virginia**: The business entity must be authorized to operate within Virginia by the **Virginia State Corporation Commission**. This legal prerequisite confirms the business's legitimacy and compliance with state regulations.

These requirements help ensure that business entities in real estate follow Virginia laws and maintain proper licensing standards.
