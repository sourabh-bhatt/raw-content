# Responding to Board Inquiries

<!-- When you receive an inquiry from the **board**, or its agents, it's crucial to respond within **21 days**, except for inquiries specified under a different regulation. -->
Licensees must respond to inquiries from the **board** or its agents within **21 days**, except for inquiries specified under a different regulation. 

## Regulatory Background

- This practice has been in place for quite some time, with its foundations reaching back to regulations established as early as **June 28, 1995**.
- The guideline has undergone amendments, notably on **January 1, 1999**, and again on **April 1, 2003**.

## Importance of Timely Response

<!-- Understanding and adhering to this regulation is key to ensuring that you remain in good standing within the **professional boundaries** set by the board. Maintaining **timely communication** with the board helps uphold your professional responsibilities and compliance. -->
Adhering to this regulation ensures good standing within the **professional boundaries** set by the board. **Timely communication** with the board upholds professional responsibilities and compliance.
