# Licensure by Reciprocity for Virginia Real Estate License

<!-- If you're looking to obtain a **Virginia real estate license** and already hold a real estate salesperson or broker license in another jurisdiction, you'll need to meet specific requirements to qualify for **licensure by reciprocity**. Here's what you need to know: -->
To obtain a **Virginia real estate license** when already holding a real estate salesperson or broker license in another jurisdiction, specific requirements must be met to qualify for **licensure by reciprocity**:

## Eligibility Requirements

1. **Age Requirement:** Applicants must be at least *18 years old*.
2. **Education:** Applicants must have a *high school diploma or its equivalent*.
3. **Previous License Examination:** The salesperson's or broker's license held from another jurisdiction must have been obtained by passing a *written examination* considered as rigorous as the Virginia examination.

## Application and Examination Process

4. **Statement of Understanding:** Applicants are required to sign a statement verifying understanding of the specific chapters and provisions relevant to *Virginia real estate laws and regulations*.
5. **Virginia Law Examination:** Within *12 months before or after* submitting the complete application, applicants must have passed a written examination focused on *Virginia real estate license law* and the specific regulations of the *Virginia Real Estate Board*. This exam may be administered by the board or an authorized testing service.
6. **Examination Conduct:** Compliance with all procedures regarding behavior during the examination is essential. Failure to comply may result in denial of the application.

## License Standing and Professional Conduct

7. **Good Standing:** Applicants must be in *good standing* as a licensed real estate broker or salesperson in every jurisdiction where they hold a license. This means:
   - No license suspensions, revocations, or surrenders due to disciplinary action.
   - No prior disciplinary actions before applying in Virginia.
   - Compliance with all board orders, including monetary penalties and education requirements.

8. **Educational and Experience Requirements:**
   - For a *salesperson's license*, the educational background must closely match Virginia's requirements.
   - For a *broker's license*, applicants must have a similar educational background and have been actively engaged in the real estate profession for at least *36 months of the last 48 months*. This experience must be verified by someone familiar with the professional activities.

9. **Character and Competence:** A reputation for *honesty, truthfulness, and fair dealing* is crucial, along with the competence to conduct real estate business in a manner that protects the *public interest*.

## Additional Requirements

10. **Fingerprinting and Background Disclosure:** Applicants must undergo fingerprinting as part of the application process and disclose certain misdemeanor and felony convictions as specified in the regulations. This ensures that only qualified individuals are granted licensure.

11. **Board Discretion:** If applicants do not meet the requirements related to license standing or background disclosure, the board may still approve licensure after a thorough review.
