# Structure and Governance of the Real Estate Board

## Composition of the Board
The **Real Estate Board** is composed of **nine members** designed to represent a broad spectrum of the real estate sector:

- **Seven members** must be individuals who have held licenses as **real estate brokers or salespersons** for at least **five consecutive years** prior to their appointment. This ensures the board benefits from experienced practitioners with deep industry insight.
- **Two members** are **citizens** who are not licensed real estate professionals, providing perspectives from outside the industry and representing consumer interests.

## Term Length
Each board member serves a term of **four years**. This duration allows members to contribute meaningfully to the board's objectives while enabling periodic introduction of new ideas through new appointments.

## Leadership

The board elects a **chairman** from among its members. The chairman:

- Guiding board discussions and decisions
- Ensuring meetings are productive
- Aligning the board's actions with its responsibilities and goals

## Board Seal
The board adopts an official **seal** that serves as a mark of authenticity for its proceedings and decisions. Documents bearing this seal indicate official approval and represent the board's authority.
