# Reporting Changes to Real Estate Licenses

<!-- In the realm of real estate, keeping both individual and firm licenses updated is crucial. Let's dive into how you should report any changes regarding these licenses, according to **Real Estate Board** regulations. -->
Keeping both individual and firm licenses updated is required. Changes regarding these licenses must be reported according to **Real Estate Board** regulations.

## Reporting Changes and Deadlines

Different types of changes have distinct deadlines for notifying the Board. Understanding these deadlines ensures compliance and prevents any unintended license issues.

- **Changes in Status (Within 10 Days)**: If there is a change in working status - such as terminating association with a firm or voluntarily inactivating a license - the Board must be informed within *10 days* from the day the change occurs.

- **Voluntary Inactivation (Within 10 Days)**: Choosing to inactivate a license voluntarily also requires notification to the Board within the same *10-day* window.

- **Principal Broker Responsibilities for Termination**: When a salesperson or associate broker is terminated from the firm, the principal broker must return the licensee's credentials to the Board within *10 days*.

- **Changes in Personal Information (Within 30 Days)**: Updates to personal information, such as a change in name or address, must be reported to the Board within *30 days* after the change.

- **Changes in Firm Information (Within 30 Days)**: The principal broker is responsible for submitting any modifications to the firm's informationâ€”including firm and branch addresses or ownership changesâ€”to the Board within *30 days*.

- **Termination of Principal Broker (Within 3 Days)**: If a firm ends its association with a principal broker, the broker's license must be returned to the Board within *3 days*.

## Certification of Firm's Compliance

Upon renewal of a firm or sole proprietor license, the principal or supervising broker must **certify** that they have conducted a thorough audit of the firm's operations, policies, and procedures. This certification:

- Ensures the firm aligns with licensing laws.
- Confirms adherence to the Board's regulations.
- Maintains the integrity and trustworthiness of the real estate profession.
