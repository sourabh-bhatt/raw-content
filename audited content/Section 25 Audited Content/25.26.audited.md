# Real Estate Licensing Regulations and Compliance

<!-- In real estate regulations, there are specific actions that are **not allowed**, and it's crucial you understand these to ensure you're always in **compliance**. -->
Real estate regulations specify actions that are **not allowed**. Understanding these ensures **compliance**.

## Licensing Examination Rules

- Once a **salesperson's license** is obtained, retaking the salesperson's examination is **not permitted**.
- However, if necessary qualifications are met, attempting the **broker's examination** is allowed.

## License Status and Renewal

<!-- - It is important to keep track of your **license status**. -->
- Tracking **license status** is required.
- If a license **lapses for more than 3 years**, retaking the licensing exam is **required** to regain active status.
