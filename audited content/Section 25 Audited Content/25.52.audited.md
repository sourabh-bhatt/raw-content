# Role of the Virginia Real Estate Board in Legal Proceedings and Claims

<!-- When you're diving into the world of real estate, understanding the involvement of the **Virginia Real Estate Board** is crucial. Below are key points to remember about the Board's role in legal matters, claims, and disciplinary actions. -->
The **Virginia Real Estate Board** has involvement in legal proceedings, claims, and disciplinary actions. Below are key points about the Board's role in legal matters, claims, and disciplinary actions.

## Intervention in Legal Proceedings

- The **Virginia Real Estate Board**, its **Director of the Department of Professional and Occupational Regulation (DPOR)**, or authorized representatives have the right to intervene in legal cases related to real estate.
- They can request permission from the court to step into a case, especially when disputes involve the Board's regulations or affect the integrity of the real estate community.

## Compensation from the Real Estate Transaction Recovery Fund

- A **claimant** may receive compensation from the **Real Estate Transaction Recovery Fund** managed by the Virginia Real Estate Board.
- The **Director of DPOR** is authorized to make payments to the claimant as directed by the Board.
- When compensation is paid, the claimant must transfer their rights to pursue the offender (the person responsible for the claim) to the Board.
  - This means the Board can then seek to recover the paid amount from the offender.

## Consequences of Claims Paid from the Fund

- If the Board pays a claim due to someone's improper or dishonest actions, that person's **real estate license is revoked** after Board determination.
- License revocation means the individual can no longer legally operate in real estate.
- The revoked licensee cannot apply for a new license until they:
  1. Repay the full amount paid out from the fund because of their actions.
  2. Include any applicable interest on that amount.
