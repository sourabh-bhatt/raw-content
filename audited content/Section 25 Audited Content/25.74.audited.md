# Post Licensing Education (PLE) for Virginia Real Estate Salespersons

<!-- Once you've passed the State exam and entered the world of real estate sales, it's crucial to understand the importance of **Post Licensing Education (PLE)**. -->
After passing the State exam and entering real estate sales, understanding **Post Licensing Education (PLE)** is required. 

## PLE Requirement

- All new salespersons must complete **thirty hours of PLE within their first year** of licensure.
- This requirement applies specifically to **salespersons**, not brokers.

## Continuing Education During First Renewal

- During the first renewal period as a salesperson, **PLE is the main focus**.
- There is no need to complete any other Continuing Education requirements at this time.

## Compliance and License Status

- The Virginia Real Estate Board has the authority to place licenses on **inactive status** if salespersons fail to meet the PLE requirement on time.
- This regulation ensures that all new entrants are well-prepared and maintain a high standard of knowledge and professionalism in the field.
