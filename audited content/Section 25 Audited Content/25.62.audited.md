# Using Professional and Fictional Names in Real Estate

<!-- When you're working in real estate, you might choose to operate under a **professional** or even a **fictional name**, something that might resonate more with your clientele or stand out in the market. However, it's crucial to remember the following: -->
Real estate professionals may choose to operate under a **professional** or **fictional name**. However, the following requirements apply:

- Professionals must register their **legal name** with the **Virginia Real Estate Board** when obtaining a license.
- This registration keeps professional activities documented and legal.
- Maintaining this compliance helps uphold **transparency** and **trust** in professional relationships.
