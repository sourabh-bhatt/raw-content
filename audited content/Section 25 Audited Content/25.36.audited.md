# License Restrictions in Virginia Real Estate

<!-- When you work in real estate, it's important to understand the specific rules about **holding licenses**. -->
Real estate professionals must understand the specific rules about **holding licenses**.

## License Ownership Rules

<!-- In **Virginia**, there is an important restriction to keep in mind: -->
In **Virginia**, a restriction exists:

- A person can only hold **one active real estate license** at a time in Virginia.

This means individuals cannot hold multiple real estate licenses at the same time, whether they are salesperson or broker licenses.

## Why This Rule Exists

This regulation ensures clear accountability and prevents conflicts in real estate practice.
