# Responsibilities and Liabilities of Real Estate Licensees in Virginia

<!-- When it comes to your responsibilities and liabilities as a **real estate licensee in Virginia**, there are several key points you need to understand to protect yourself and operate effectively within the legal boundaries. -->
As a **real estate licensee in Virginia**, several points regarding responsibilities and liabilities exist for operating within legal boundaries.

## Liability for Misinformation

- Licensees are **not liable** for incorrect information from clients, government bodies, or other licensed professionals if they did not know the information was false and were not willfully ignorant.
- This means if false details are given and passed along without knowledge of their falsity, the licensee is **not at fault**.
- If the law requires disclosure of certain information, doing so **does not make the licensee liable**.
- Licensees are not liable for **misrepresentation or misconduct by another licensee assisting them**, as long as they are **unaware** of the inaccuracies or misconduct.

## Translation Assistance

- During a real estate transaction, if someone requests a document to be translated into a different language, licensees can:
  - Facilitate finding a translator.
  - Direct them to an electronic translation service.
- Licensees **cannot charge a fee** for this assistance.
- Facilitating translation **does not make the licensee liable** for any potential translation errors.

## Liability for Negligence and Harmful Actions

- Licensees can still be **liable for gross negligence or intentional wrongdoing**, especially when dealing with:
  - Customers who represent themselves.
  - Customers represented by a **limited service licensee**.
- When interacting with clients of a limited service licensee, licensees have the opportunity to enter into a **brokerage agreement for limited services** with them for additional services.
- A client may have agreements with **multiple limited service licensees simultaneously**.

## Purpose of These Guidelines

These guidelines aim to:

- Protect consumers.
- Ensure real estate licensees are **not unfairly penalized** for misinformation or the actions of others outside their control.
