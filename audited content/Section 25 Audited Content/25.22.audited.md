# Licensure Transition: Broker to Salesperson in Virginia

<!-- When navigating the real estate profession, it's essential to understand the nuances of **licensure transitions**, especially if you are considering a shift from being a **broker** to a **salesperson**. -->
Under the regulations set forth in the **Code of Virginia**, specifically **18 VAC 135-20-55**, a process exists for **licensure transitions** from **broker** to **salesperson**.

## Application Process

To initiate this change, applicants must:

- Submit a fully completed application.
- Include the appropriate fee with your application.

## Considerations

- By opting for this exchange, the applicant agrees to **relinquish their current broker's license**.
- This decision is **not reversible by a simple choice**.

## Reverting to Broker Status

If later wishing to return to broker status, the individual must:

1. Complete the **requalification process**.
2. Pass the latest version of the **broker examination**.
3. Fulfill the current **education and experience prerequisites** applicable at the time of reapplication.

## Regulatory Context

- This procedure has been effective since **November 1, 2015**.
