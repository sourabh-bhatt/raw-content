# Limitations on Recovery from the Transaction Recovery Fund (TRF)

## Eligibility for Compensation

<!-- It is essential to understand who is eligible for compensation from the **Transaction Recovery Fund (TRF)** and the extent of damages covered. -->
Eligibility for compensation from the **Transaction Recovery Fund (TRF)** has specific limitations. The following groups are **not eligible** to receive payments from the TRF:

- Any licensee or their personal representative
- Spouses and children of licensees found at fault
- Banks and lenders
- Construction and development companies

<!-- Knowing who cannot claim from the TRF helps focus on the individuals and entities the fund is truly intended to protect. -->
Understanding eligibility restrictions clarifies the fund's intended beneficiaries.

## Types of Damages Covered

The TRF compensates for **actual damages only**, which include:

- Attorney fees
- Court costs

These must be costs that a final judgment has not already reimbursed. The TRF **does not cover**:

- Pain and suffering
- Punitive damages

<!-- Understanding the scope of what can be claimed ensures realistic expectations. -->
The scope of claimable damages has specific parameters.

## Compensation Limits

The TRF imposes limits on compensation amounts as follows:

- **Single transaction:** Up to $20,000 per party seeking compensation.
- **Multiple aggrieved parties in a single transaction:** Total compensation cannot exceed $50,000.
- **Multiple transactions involving the same licensee:**
  - Up to $20,000 per individual claimant per licensee.
  - Maximum total payout remains $100,000 for all claims against one licensee.

<!-- Being aware of these caps is crucial for setting realistic expectations for clients. -->
These compensation caps establish clear limits for claims.

## Withholding of Payments

The Board may decide to withhold payment from the TRF if:

- It anticipates additional claims against the same licensee within the year.
- There is a risk of the TRF being depleted.

<!-- This knowledge can influence how quickly or aggressively claims are pursued. -->
This policy may affect claim timing and strategy.

## Consequences for Licensees Causing TRF Payments

Licensees who cause the TRF to make payments face serious repercussions:

- **Automatic license revocation** upon TRF compensation for their actions.
- Reapplication for a license is only possible after reimbursing the TRF, including interest.
- Failure to pay required assessments to the TRF on time results in **automatic suspension** of the licensee's privilege to operate.
