# Disciplinary Procedures by the Virginia Real Estate Board

The **disciplinary procedures** set by the **Real Estate Board** guide how actions are taken against any real estate agent or broker who doesn't adhere to the established **standards of conduct**.

## Reporting and Investigation of Misconduct

- **Written Complaint:** Misconduct can be reported to the Board through a written complaint by anyone, including clients, customers, or other licensees.
- **Board-Initiated Investigation:** The Board also has the authority to initiate an investigation if it deems necessary.
- **Notification:** Once an investigation is decided upon, the accused person and their principal broker are notified.
- **Investigative Committee:** This committee interviews witnesses, reviews evidence, and presents findings to the Board.

## Board's Decision and Disciplinary Actions

After the investigation, the Board decides whether to:

1. **Dismiss the case** if there is insufficient evidence.
2. **Proceed with prosecution** if the accusations have merit.

The accused has two options:

- **Acknowledge misconduct** and accept the proposed punishment.
- **Dispute the allegations** and undergo an informal trial called an **informal fact-finding conference**.

The Board can enforce various **disciplinary measures**, including:

- Imposing fines up to **$2,500** for violations.
- **Suspending** or **revoking** the license.
- Deferring findings or dismissing the case if certain conditions are met.

## Time Limits: Statute of Limitations

- Violations must be prosecuted within **one year** from when they were discovered.
- No action can be initiated if more than **five years** have passed since the alleged misconduct occurred.

This time frame is known as the **statute of limitations**.

## Appeals Process

If a disciplinary ruling is received, the decision may be appealed to:

- The **Circuit Court of the city or county where the Board's principal office is located**
- Higher courts

**Exceptions:** Certain decisions cannot be appealed, such as:

- Suspensions for failure to pay **transaction recovery fund** assessments.
- Revocations due to **transaction recovery fund** payments.
