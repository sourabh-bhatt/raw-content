# Firm Licenses and Business Registration in Virginia Real Estate

<!-- When you're diving into the world of real estate, it's crucial to understand the rules about **firm licenses**, especially if you're looking at operating in Virginia. -->
Understanding **firm license** rules is necessary for operating in Virginia real estate.

## Firm License Requirements

- Every firm requires its own **firm license**.
- **Exception:** **Sole proprietor principal brokers** (the only licensed broker operating from one location) do not need a separate firm license.

## Business Registration in Virginia

For all other business types, getting a firm license is the first step. Businesses must also be officially **registered in Virginia**. This applies to all types of business entities, including:

- **Corporations**
- **LLCs (Limited Liability Companies)**
- **Partnerships**
- Other business structures

<!-- Business registration is more than just paperwork. It makes your business legal in Virginia and is a fundamental step toward operating legally and successfully within the Virginia real estate sector. -->
Business registration makes the business legal in Virginia and allows legal operation within the Virginia real estate sector.
