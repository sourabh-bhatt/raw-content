# Enforcement Authority of the Real Estate Board

<!-- When you're learning about real estate regulations, it's important to understand the role of the **Real Estate Board**, particularly regarding enforcement. -->
The **Real Estate Board** enforces both its own regulations and the **license law**. This enforcement authority includes several key powers.

## Powers of the Real Estate Board

1. **License Withdrawal**
   - The Board can withdraw a license either temporarily or permanently.
   - Temporary withdrawal is known as **suspension**.
   - Permanent withdrawal can occur in two ways:
     - Denying the **renewal** of a license.
     - **Revoking** the license outright.
   - These actions serve as mechanisms to ensure compliance with the law and regulations.

2. **Monetary Penalties**
   - The Board can impose **monetary penalties**, commonly referred to as **fines**.
   - Fines are issued for violations of the law and regulations.

> **Note:** While the Board can impose fines and affect the status of a license, it does *not* possess criminal authority.

## Compliance

<!-- Understanding these enforcement powers is fundamental as they underscore the importance of adhering to both the law and Board regulations. -->
Adhering to both the law and Board regulations:

- Supports a fair and orderly real estate market.
- Safeguards the ability to practice within the market.
