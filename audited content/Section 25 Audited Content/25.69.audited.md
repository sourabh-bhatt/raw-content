# Continuing Education Requirements for Virginia Real Estate License Renewal

Licensed real estate professionals in Virginia must meet specific **continuing education requirements** to renew their license. These requirements are mandated by **Virginia Real Estate Board regulations**, which require ongoing learning within the real estate field.

## Education Requirements for Salespersons and Brokers

- **Salespersons:**
  - Must complete a minimum of **16 hours** of education during each licensing period.
  - Education can be completed through classroom settings, correspondence, or other approved distance learning methods.
  - **Exception:** For first-time license renewals, **30 hours** of post-license education is required within the first renewal period, regardless of whether the license is active or inactive.

- **Brokers:**
  - Required to complete at least **24 hours** of education across similar learning modalities.

- **Active Military Personnel:**
  - Active military personnel can complete education requirements within **six months after release** from active duty.

- **Inactive License Holders:**
  - Continuing education requirements do **not** apply for license renewal if the license is inactive.

## Specific Areas of Study

### For Salespersons (16 Hours Required):
- **2 hours** on *fair housing laws*
- **3 hours** on *ethics and standards of conduct*
- At least **1 hour each** on:
  - *Legal updates*
  - *Emerging trends* (including flood zone areas and the National Flood Insurance Program)
  - *Real estate agency*
  - *Real estate contracts*

### For Brokers (24 Hours Required):
- **16 hours** focused on *supervision and management* of real estate agents and brokerage firm management.
  - This includes **2 hours** specifically on *broker supervision requirements*.
- Additional hours dedicated to:
  - *Fair housing laws*
  - *Ethics and standards of conduct*
  - Core areas covering *legal updates*, *emerging trends*, and essentials of *real estate agency* and *contracts*.

## Additional Topics for Continuing Education

The remaining education hours should cover a broad range of topics relevant to real estate, including but not limited to:

- Property rights
- Contracts
- Mortgages and financing
- Environmental issues impacting real estate
- Building codes
- Local laws
- Ethical considerations
