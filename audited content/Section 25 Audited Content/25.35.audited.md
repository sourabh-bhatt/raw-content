# Honesty and Full Disclosure in Real Estate Transactions

<!-- When engaging in real estate transactions, it is critical for you to understand the importance of **honesty** and **full disclosure**. -->
In real estate transactions, **honesty** and **full disclosure** are required. **Misrepresentation** or **omission** of any kind is strictly prohibited. This rule applies to all licensed real estate salespersons and brokers.

## Key Practices to Avoid

- **Bait and switch tactics**: Offering a different property after luring potential buyers or renters with a more attractive or unavailable option.
- Concealing **material facts** about a propertyâ€”these are facts that could influence a person's decision to proceed with a transaction.

## Responsibilities in Transactions

- Always disclose and never conceal material facts.
- Present any offers received to the client in a timely manner.
- Protect the **confidentiality** of any information provided by clients; do not disclose anything without their consent.
- When drafting contracts, ensure no material information is omitted. Be transparent and include all relevant details.
- Clearly identify the persons holding any **deposits** made.
- Avoid making any **material misrepresentations** or false statements about properties, transactions, or related matters.
