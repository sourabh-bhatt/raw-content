# Understanding Trade Organizations vs. Real Estate Boards

<!-- When learning about the real estate landscape, you'll encounter various organizations and boards. Each plays a distinct role in shaping your responsibilities and operations. Understanding the difference between **trade organizations** and **real estate boards** is crucial. -->
The real estate landscape includes various organizations and boards. Each plays a distinct role in shaping responsibilities and operations. The difference between **trade organizations** and **real estate boards** is notable.

## Trade Organizations

Trade organizations, such as **NVAR**, offer membership on a *voluntary basis*. This means:

- Joining such an organization comes with perks:
  - Access to valuable resources
  - Networking opportunities
  - Adherence to a set of standards established by the organization itself

- However, the decision to join or not join these organizations *does not* directly impact the ability to get or maintain a **real estate license**.
- These organizations focus on *enrichment* and *professional growth* without directly influencing licensure status.

## Real Estate Boards

On the other hand, **real estate boards**, like the **VREB**, operate differently:

- The rules and orders they establish are *mandatory* for legal operation within the field.
- Compliance with their regulations is *not optional* but a *requirement*.
- They control the requirements necessary to both obtain and maintain **real estate licenses**.

## Key Distinction

- **Trade organizations** offer voluntary membership and benefits aimed at professional development.
- **Real estate boards** are authoritative bodies whose regulations *must* be followed to legally practice in the industry.
