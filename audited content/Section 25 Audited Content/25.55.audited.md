# Managing and Presenting Records to the Real Estate Board

<!-- In the realm of real estate, understanding how to manage and present records to the **Real Estate Board** is crucial. Here's what you need to know: -->
Managing and presenting records to the **Real Estate Board** has specific requirements:

## Legal Requirements for Document Submission

- Under the guidelines defined by the board, unless a specific exception is mentioned or outlined in **Â§ 54.1-2108 of the Code of Virginia**, real estate professionals are required to provide certain documents to the Real Estate Board or its representatives promptly.
- If the board requests it, real estate professionals have a **5-day window** to present items such as:
  - Signature cards
  - Bank records
  - Any documents, books, or records related to any real estate transaction they were part of
  - Records they are obligated to keep accessible for inspection and duplication by the board or its delegates

## Extensions for Document Submission

- Life can be unpredictable, and there may be situations where providing these documents within 5 days is challenging.
- The board may grant an **extension** if professionals can demonstrate that **extenuating circumstances** make it difficult to comply within the original timeframe.
