# Prompt Judicial Action in Real Estate Regulation

<!-- Understanding the concept of **prompt judicial action** is essential for navigating the complexities of real estate regulations. -->
The concept of **prompt judicial action** applies to real estate regulations.

## Role of the Real Estate Board and Attorney General

- After a complaint is filed, the **Virginia Real Estate Board** reviews the matter.
- The Board may consult with the **Office of the Attorney General** to determine if immediate legal intervention is necessary.
- If so, the Board may request the **Attorney General to initiate a civil action**.

## Purpose of Legal Intervention

- The goal is to seek **temporary** or **preliminary relief** to address urgent issues.
- This ensures problems are handled swiftly and efficiently, maintaining integrity and order within the real estate sector.

## Process Overview

- When requested by the Board, the **Attorney General may act**.
- The Attorney General commences and diligently pursues the necessary legal actions.

This framework protects stakeholders in the real estate market by ensuring fairness and strict adherence to regulations at all times.
