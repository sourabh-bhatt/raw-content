# Virginia Real Estate Prelicensing Educational Requirements

## Becoming a Real Estate Salesperson

<!-- If you're aiming to become a **real estate salesperson**, it's important to understand the initial educational steps required before qualifying for the **state license exam**. One of the key requirements is completing a course on the **principles of real estate**. -->
To become a **real estate salesperson**, initial educational steps are required before qualifying for the **state license exam**. A requirement is completing a course on the **principles of real estate**. This foundational course:

- Requires **60 credit hours**
- Can be completed through classroom lessons, correspondence, or distance learning
- Provides the basic knowledge needed to navigate the real estate industry effectively

## Advancing to a Real Estate Broker

To become a **real estate broker**, the pre-licensing educational requirements are more extensive:

- Complete at least **60 hours of study**
  - At least **15 hours** must focus specifically on **real estate brokerage**
  - Complete additional required coursework, which may cover topics such as:
    - **Real estate law**
    - **Real estate finance**
    - **Real estate appraisal**
  - Remaining hours can be fulfilled through **board-approved elective courses**

## Practical Experience Requirement

Before applying for a **broker's license**, candidates must have:

- Been an actively **licensed salesperson** for at least **24 months** out of the preceding 36 months

This requirement ensures that broker candidates possess not only theoretical knowledge but also substantial practical experience in the real estate field.
