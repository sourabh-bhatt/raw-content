# Qualifications for Obtaining a Virginia Real Estate License

## Personal Qualifications

<!-- When aiming to acquire a real estate license, whether as a **broker** or **salesperson**, it's crucial to meet certain standards set by the **Real Estate Board**. -->
To acquire a real estate license as a **broker** or **salesperson**, applicants must meet certain standards set by the **Real Estate Board**. These qualifications include:

- Holding at least a **high school diploma** or an equivalent certificate.
- Being **18 years or older**.
- Maintaining a good standing in every state where licensed.
- Demonstrating a reputation for being **honest, truthful, fair, and competent**.

For those seeking a **broker's license**, there is an additional requirement:

- Having been a licensed, active **salesperson for 36 months within the last five years**.

## Disclosure of Prior Convictions

<!-- Transparency about your past is essential. -->
The board requires disclosure of:

- Any **misdemeanor convictions within the past five years** involving:
  - Moral turpitude
  - Sexual offenses
  - Distribution of drugs other than marijuana
  - Causing physical injury
- Any **felony convictions** at any time in your lifetime.

Applicants must also provide **fingerprints** for a comprehensive background check conducted by the **Virginia Central Criminal Records Exchange**. This process helps the board obtain both **state and national criminal history records**.

Additionally, having a previous license that was **suspended, revoked, or surrendered in any state** is considered a red flag.

## Education Requirements

Before taking the state examination, applicants are required to:

- Successfully complete an **approved pre-license education course**. This foundational step ensures proper preparation for the examination.

## State Examination

The state examination consists of two parts:

1. **National portion**
2. **State portion**

This exam tests knowledge and readiness to begin a career in the real estate industry.
