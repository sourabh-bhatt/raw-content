Applicants seek approval from the regulatory board to practice within the field, demonstrating that they meet the necessary qualifications and standards set forth by the regulating body.

## Associate Broker

The term **"associate broker"** refers to a specific role within the real estate landscape:

- They hold a **broker's license**, which is a notch above a salesperson's license in the hierarchy of real estate qualifications.
- Unlike the **principal broker**, who is responsible for overseeing the operations and compliance of a real estate firm, associate brokers do not carry that weight of responsibility.
- Their focus might be more on conducting transactions, similar to what agents do, but with the added knowledge and credentials that come with the broker's status.

<!-- This role is ideal for those who seek higher qualification and capability in their real estate career but are not yet looking to manage their own firm or take on supervisory duties. -->
This role provides higher qualification and capability in real estate practice without the responsibility of managing a firm or supervisory duties.

## The Board (Virginia Real Estate Board - VREB)

The term **"Board"** refers specifically to the **Virginia Real Estate Board (VREB)**, the governing body responsible for:

- Overseeing all real estate practices within Virginia.
- Setting regulations.
- Handling licensing.
- Ensuring that all operating agents, brokers, and firms adhere to established legal and ethical standards.

<!-- Your interaction with the VREB will be crucial throughout your career, as they are the authority on all matters related to your licensure, continuing education, and professional conduct in the real estate sector. -->
Interaction with the VREB is ongoing throughout real estate careers, as they are the authority on all matters related to licensure, continuing education, and professional conduct in the real estate sector.
