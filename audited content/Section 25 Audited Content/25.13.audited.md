# Improper Dealing in Real Estate: Ethical and Legal Guidelines

<!-- In the realm of real estate, it's vital to adhere to certain **ethical guidelines** and **legal regulations** to avoid what's known as **improper dealing**. Real estate licensees must adhere to certain **ethical guidelines** and **legal regulations** to avoid **improper dealing**. Understanding what constitutes improper dealing maintains integrity and legality.

## Consent for Sale or Lease

- A licensee must not offer real property for sale or lease without the owner's written consent.
- All terms must match what the owner authorized in writing.
- Going beyond authorized terms violates Virginia Real Estate Board regulations.

## Sign Placement on Property

- Placing signs on property requires written consent from the property owner.
- Placing signs without consent violates Virginia Real Estate Board regulations.

## Advertising Requirements

- When advertising properties for sale, rent, or lease, all advertisements must include the licensed firm's name as it appears on the Virginia Real Estate Board license.
- This transparency ensures the advertisement can be traced back to a legitimate source, protecting the integrity of the real estate process.
