# Activating or Transferring Real Estate Licenses in Virginia

<!-- If you're looking to **activate** or **transfer** your real estate license, it's key to understand the regulations that guide this process. -->
To **activate** or **transfer** a real estate license, understanding the regulations that guide this process is necessary.

## Activation Process

- If a license is currently **inactive** but the licensee wishes to align it with a **licensed real estate firm** or work as a **sole proprietor**, an **activation form** provided by the board must be completed.
- **No business may be conducted** with the firm or as a sole proprietor until the application has been processed and the board has officially issued the **activated license**.

## Continuing Education Requirement

- For licenses inactive for **over one year**, **continuing education** must be completed within the two years prior to activation.
- This requirement is outlined in **Virginia Real Estate Board regulations**.
- The purpose is to ensure licensees are up to date with the latest real estate knowledge and practices.

## Requalification After Extended Inactivity

- If a license has not been associated with any licensed real estate firm or operated as a sole proprietorship for **more than three years**, the board requires meeting the current **pre-license education requirements** again.
- This ensures licensees are fully prepared and knowledgeable about current real estate standards and practices before re-entering the field.
