# Real Estate License Renewal and Reinstatement in Virginia

## License Renewal Requirements

<!-- To ensure you maintain your legal ability to work in real estate, it's crucial to keep your licenseâ€”whether for a firm or an individual, active or inactiveâ€”**renewed on time**. You are required to renew your license every **two years**, counting from the date you first received it. -->
To maintain legal ability to work in real estate, licenses - whether for a firm or an individual, active or inactive - must be **renewed on time**. Licenses are required to be renewed every **two years**, counting from the date first received. To renew a license, licensees must:

- Pay a **renewal fee**
- Complete any **mandatory education** required by the regulatory board

Those serving in the military may qualify for an **extension** on these requirements.

## Consequences of License Expiration

If a license is not renewed before the two-year deadline, it will **expire**. An expired license means the licensee is no longer permitted to perform any activities regulated by the real estate board. In real estate, **timely compliance with renewal deadlines is required** to continue practicing without interruption.

## License Reinstatement Process

If a license expires, there is a window of opportunity to get it **reinstated**:

- Up to **one year after the expiration date** exists to reinstate the license.
- Reinstating within the **first 30 days** after expiration requires paying the **renewal fee**.
- Reinstating **after 30 days but within one year** requires paying both the **renewal fee** and an additional **reinstatement fee**.

The regulatory board has the final authority and may **deny reinstatement requests at their discretion**.

## License Lapse and Reapplication

If the one-year reinstatement period is missed, the license will **lapse**. At this point:

- The license cannot be renewed or reinstated.
- A **new license application** must be submitted from scratch.
- This involves completing the **initial application process** again and meeting the **current licensure criteria**, which may have changed since the original license.
