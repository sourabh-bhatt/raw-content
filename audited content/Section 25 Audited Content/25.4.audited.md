# License Reciprocity for Real Estate Agents in Virginia

<!-- When you're already licensed as a real estate agent in another state and looking to extend your practice into Virginia, **obtaining a license by reciprocity** is a key pathway. This process is designed to simplify your transition by acknowledging your existing qualifications and potentially easing some requirements. -->
For agents already licensed in another state, **obtaining a license by reciprocity** provides a pathway to practice in Virginia. This process acknowledges existing qualifications and may ease some requirements.

## Differences Between Initial Licensing and Reciprocity

- Unlike applying for an initial license, reciprocity may allow bypassing certain steps.
- For example, the Virginia Real Estate Board may waive some pre-license education requirements.
- Exemptions from the national portion of the licensing exam may apply for active license holders, focusing only on the state-specific portion.

## Core Qualifications and Standards

Despite these potential waivers, the **core qualifications** remain unchanged. Applicants must meet the same personal attributes and conduct standards required for initial licensure, including:

- Possessing a high school diploma or equivalent
- Meeting minimum age requirements
- Demonstrating honesty, good standing, and reputable character
- Adhering to fair housing laws
- Maintaining a clean criminal record

## Broker's License Reciprocity Requirements

Applicants for a **broker's license** through reciprocity must meet an experience requirement:

- Active engagement in real estate as a salesperson or broker for **36 of the last 48 months** before applying.

This ensures practical experience, not just licensure.

## Education and Exam Requirements

- Some exams from your original jurisdiction may be recognized as equivalent to Virginia's.
- However, applicants must certify that they have studied and understood **Virginia's specific license laws**.
- Applicants must pass the Virginia state portion of the licensing exam.

## Application Process

- There is an **application fee** associated with pursuing license reciprocity.
- This fee covers the assessment and processing of your application.
