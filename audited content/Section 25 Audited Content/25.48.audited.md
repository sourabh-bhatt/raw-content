# Handling Firm Affairs When the Principal Broker Is Unavailable

If the **principal broker** of a firm passes away or becomes incapacitated, the **Board** can authorize someone who does **not** have a license to wrap up the firm's affairs. This authorization is restricted to a maximum of **180 days**.
