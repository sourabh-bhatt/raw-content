# Role of the Virginia Real Estate Board in Education and Licensing

<!-- When you're preparing to enter the field of real estate, it's important to understand that the **Virginia Real Estate Board** plays a crucial role in ensuring that all aspiring agents, like yourself, and those already practicing meet certain **educational standards**. This follows Virginia law to maintain **professional standards** within the industry. -->
The **Virginia Real Estate Board** ensures that all aspiring agents and those already practicing meet certain **educational standards**. This follows Virginia law to maintain **professional standards** within the industry.

## Pre-Licensing Education

<!-- Before you can obtain your license, you'll need to complete a **60-hour pre-licensing course**. This is your foundational step, designed to equip you with the knowledge and skills necessary for starting your career in real estate. Think of it as the groundwork that prepares you for the practical aspects of the profession. -->
Before obtaining a license, applicants must complete a **60-hour pre-licensing course**. This is the foundational step, designed to equip applicants with the knowledge and skills necessary for starting a career in real estate.

## Post-Licensing and Continuing Education

<!-- Once you've successfully entered the field and are a licensed agent, your education doesn't stop there. The Virginia Real Estate Board mandates ongoing learning to ensure that you stay updated on the latest trends, laws, and best practices within the industry. This includes:

- **Post-licensing education (PLE):** You must complete 30 hours of post-licensing education within 12 months of getting your license. This focuses on enhancing your skills during the initial phase of your career.
- **Continuing education (CE):** You must complete 16 hours of continuing education every two years. This ongoing requirement keeps you informed and competent throughout your career. -->
Once licensed, education continues. The Virginia Real Estate Board mandates ongoing learning to ensure licensees stay updated on the latest trends, laws, and best practices within the industry. This includes:

- **Post-licensing education (PLE):** Licensees must complete 30 hours of post-licensing education within 12 months of getting their license. This focuses on enhancing skills during the initial phase of their career.
- **Continuing education (CE):** Licensees must complete 16 hours of continuing education every two years. This ongoing requirement keeps licensees informed and competent throughout their career.

<!-- Both PLE and CE are vital for your **professional growth** and **legal compliance**. -->
Both PLE and CE ensure **professional growth** and **legal compliance**.

## Regulation of Education Providers

The Virginia Real Estate Board also oversees the institutions that provide these educational programs, known as **real estate education providers** or schools. This regulation ensures that education meets the high standards set by the Board and is relevant to professional development. Board-regulated education providers offer curriculum that is comprehensive, up-to-date, and designed for real estate industry preparation.
