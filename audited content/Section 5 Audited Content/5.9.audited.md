# 5.9 Covenant of Warranty in Real Estate

A covenant of warranty is a promise in a deed. It assures the **grantee** about the possession and ongoing validity of the **title**. Phrases like *"the grantor warrants and will forever defend the title"* identify this type of **covenant** in the deed's language.

## What is the Covenant of Warranty?

The covenant of warranty assures the grantee that the person transferring the property, known as the **grantor**, will:

-   Ensure the **title's validity**
-   Pay the costs of defending the **title** if there are any **third-party claims** against it

This resembles **quiet enjoyment**. The property belongs to the grantee and the grantor will pay for any legal disputes about the title.
