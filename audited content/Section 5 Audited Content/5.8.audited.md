# 5.8 Gift Deeds in Real Estate Transactions

**Gift Deeds** involve the **transfer of real property** given without payment or other valuable consideration. This characteristic of a Gift Deed means that, unlike typical transactions, the **grantee** has limited legal remedies against the **grantor**.

## Key Considerations for Gift Deeds

-   A Gift Deed cannot be validly used as a tool to hide property from **creditors**.
-   If the purpose of the gift deed is to avoid creditors, the **legality and validity** of the deed come into question.

The specific nature and limitations of Gift Deeds affect real estate transactions.
