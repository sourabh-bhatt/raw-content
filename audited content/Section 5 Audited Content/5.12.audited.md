# 5.12 Bargain and Sale Deed in Property Transactions

Property transactions involve different types of **deeds**. The **Bargain and Sale Deed** represents one such type.

## What is a Bargain and Sale Deed?

A Bargain and Sale Deed offers limited warranties from the seller (also known as the **grantor**) regarding the quality or status of the **property title** being transferred.

-   With a **Bargain and Sale Deed**, the seller indicates they believe they have the right or interest to transfer the property.
-   However, they only warrant against **encumbrances** they created during their ownership of the property.
-   They do not warrant against problems that existed before they owned the property.

While it implies a level of **ownership interest** that can be transferred, the buyer must ensure that the title is clear of any issues from previous owners.

Note that Bargain and Sale Deeds are not commonly used in Virginia residential real estate transactions. Most Virginia transactions use General Warranty Deeds or Special Warranty Deeds.
