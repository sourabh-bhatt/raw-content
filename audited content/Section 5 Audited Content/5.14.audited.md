# 5.14 Deed in Partition: Dividing Jointly Owned Property

## Deed in Partition

A **Deed in Partition** is a legal document used when dealing with properties owned by multiple parties. This applies when two or more people own a property together as **joint tenants** or **tenants in common**.

## When is a Deed in Partition Used?

Co-owners may need a Deed in Partition when they want to end their joint ownership. A court issues this deed to divide the property or its sale proceeds among the owners during a **partition sale**.

## Role of the Court and the Deed

-   The court issues the Deed in Partition to divide the property.
-   The court makes sure the property or sale money is divided fairly.
-   Each owner gets their share based on how much of the property they own.

## Deed in Partition Benefits

-   Protects the interests of all co-owners.
-   Gives a legal way to end joint property ownership.
-   Lets former co-owners separate their ownership and move on.

**Example:** If you and a friend own a house as tenants-in-common and decide to sell, the court can issue a Deed in Partition to ensure the sale proceeds are divided fairly according to your ownership shares.
