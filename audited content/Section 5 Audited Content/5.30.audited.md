# 5.30 Legal Descriptions in Real Estate: Metes and Bounds

Real estate involves key components of **legal descriptions** for **deeds** and **titles**. This concept pinpoints the exact boundaries of any property.

## Metes and Bounds Method

The **metes and bounds** method is a classic approach for legal real estate descriptions. This method focuses on identifying the outer limits of a property and involves the following key elements:

-   **Point of Beginning (POB):** A well-defined starting point, typically marked by a lasting artificial monument such as a survey stake.
-   **Boundary Description:** From the POB, the property's boundaries are described by detailing the distance and direction they run.
-   **Closure:** The description must loop back to the POB; failure to do so renders the description flawed.

This technique is especially useful for properties with irregular shapes because it allows boundary descriptions to navigate in any direction from the POB, unlike other methods that assume more regular property shapes.

## Key Terminology

-   **Metes:** Refers to measuring the distance, which can be in inches, feet, yards, or miles, typically guided by a compass for directional accuracy.
-   **Bounds:** Denote the boundaries themselves, which can be marked by:
    -   Artificial monuments such as iron pipes or brass disks set in concrete.
    -   Natural markers like lakes, large boulders, or significant trees.

## Compass Angles and Surveying

**Compass angles** represent another tool. Surveyors use these angles, along with precision instruments and identified boundaries (both natural and artificial), to determine the exact dimensions of a property. This methodology ensures that every stake in the ground contributes to a clear boundary line.

The nuances of legal descriptions such as metes and bounds, alongside the roles played by measurements, compass angles, and boundary markers, provide knowledge to navigate real estate transactions.
