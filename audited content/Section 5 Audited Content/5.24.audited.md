# 5.24 Express Easements: Conveyance and Legal Requirements

**Express easements** need to be conveyed *in writing*, often through a **deed**.

## Requirements for an Express Easement Deed

-   The deed must include a **clear description** of both the **easement itself** and the **property it affects**.
-   The deed must be **signed**, **acknowledged**, and **properly recorded** to ensure legal validity.

## Distinction Between Easements and Licenses

-   Attempting to establish an easement through **verbal agreements** may instead create a **license**.
-   Unlike an easement, a **license**:
    -   Does *not* create a **dominant estate**.
    -   Can be **revoked at any time**.

This distinction affects **legal and enduring rights** to access or use someone else's land.
