# 5.26 Trustee's Deed and Deed of Trust Foreclosure in Real Estate

A **Trustee's Deed** is given to someone who buys a property at a **deed of trust foreclosure sale**. This buyer is different from the borrower who defaulted on the loan.

## Deed of Trust Foreclosure Process

Virginia is a **deed of trust state**. This means most real estate loans use a deed of trust instead of a mortgage.

-   A **deed of trust foreclosure sale** is a legal process where property is sold to pay off a debt after the borrower defaults on their loan.
-   The **trustee** acts as a neutral party in this process. The trustee conveys the property to the new owner.
-   Unlike a regular sale where ownership transfers directly from seller to buyer, a foreclosure sale transfers ownership through the trustee.
-   In Virginia, most foreclosure sales happen outside of court through the trustee.

Properties can change hands in different ways. The **trustee's deed** and **deed of trust foreclosure** demonstrate how ownership transfers in foreclosure situations.
