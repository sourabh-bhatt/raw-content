# 5.2 Correction Deed in Real Estate

When there is a mistake in a **deed** to a property, a **correction deed** rectifies the issue. This document corrects minor errors, which can include:

-   An improper **legal description** of the property
-   A simple misspelling of a name

## Correction Deeds

Employing a **correction deed** ensures that **property records** are accurate and reliable. This practice supports the integrity of real estate transactions and safeguards the interests of all parties involved.
