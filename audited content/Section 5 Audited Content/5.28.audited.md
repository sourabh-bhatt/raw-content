# 5.28 Deeds in Lieu of Foreclosure

A **deed in lieu of foreclosure** is a voluntary transfer of property from a borrower to a lender. The borrower gives the property to the lender to avoid going through the **foreclosure process**.

This happens when the borrower cannot make their **mortgage payments**. Instead of the lender starting foreclosure proceedings, the borrower agrees to transfer ownership directly to the lender.

This process is different from a **foreclosure sale**. In a foreclosure sale, the court orders the property to be sold at auction. With a deed in lieu, there is no court involvement or public sale.

Real estate professionals must understand this process. It involves different steps and parties compared to a standard property sale.
