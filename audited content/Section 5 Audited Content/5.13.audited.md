# 5.13 The Testimonium Clause in Deeds

The **Testimonium Clause** appears at the end of a deed. This clause contains a statement that says something like, _"In witness whereof the parties to these present have hereunto set their hands and seal."_

## The Testimonium Clause

The Testimonium Clause shows that all parties agree to the terms in the deed. They show this agreement by **signing (or setting their hands)** and **sealing** the document. Signing and sealing **legally binds** the parties to the deed.

## Key Points to Remember

-   Always ensure the **Testimonium Clause** is present when reviewing deeds.
-   Check that it has proper signatures and seals.
-   This clause proves that parties **agree** to the deed terms.
-   It protects all parties in **real estate transactions**.
