# 5.27 The Premises Clause in Deeds

In **deeds**, the **Premises Clause** identifies the parties and describes what is being transferred. The **granting clause** is a separate part of the deed that contains the actual words of conveyance.

## Key Elements of the Premises Clause

When reviewing or preparing the Premises Clause:

-   Clearly identify **all parties involved** in the transaction.
-   Detail the **type of ownership** being transferred (such as fee simple).
-   Specify the **consideration** (which is essentially the price or value exchanged for the property).
-   Provide a **legal description** of the property itself.

The Premises Clause lays the groundwork for a legal **property transaction**. This knowledge ensures every property sale or transfer is executed accurately.
