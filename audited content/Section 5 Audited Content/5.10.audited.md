# 5.10 Deed of Trust Reconveyance Process in Virginia

A **Deed of Trust** in **Virginia (a title theory state)** follows a specific procedure once a borrower fulfills their payment obligation.

## Steps in the Reconveyance Process

1.  **Lender Marks Note as Paid**
    -   The lender marks the borrower's note as "paid".
    -   The lender sends this note to the trustee along with a formal request for reconveyance of the title back to the borrower.

2.  **Trustee Issues Release Deed**
    -   Upon receiving the note from the lender, the trustee issues a **release deed** (also called a **deed of release** or **certificate of satisfaction**).
    -   This document transfers full title back to the borrower, indicating they now own the property outright, free from the lien placed by the Deed of Trust.

3.  **Borrower Records the Release Deed**
    -   The borrower must record the release deed on the public record.
    -   This step officially cancels the lien and clears the title, ensuring the property is free from the previous encumbrance.

## Real Estate Professional Role

Real estate professionals need to understand this process to help their clients.
