# Verifying Title and Property Condition Before Closing

<!-- Before the official closing of a property transaction, it's essential to verify the **title's status** and assess the **property's condition**. This process involves reviewing title evidence, arranging for a land survey, and ensuring all necessary inspections are conducted. -->

Before the official closing of a property transaction, parties must verify the **title's status** and assess the **property's condition**. This process involves reviewing title evidence, arranging for a land survey, and ensuring all necessary inspections are conducted.

## Title Search

- Examine an **abstract** that documents the property's title before closing.
- A title search is typically conducted before closing to confirm clear title.
<!-- - This step is crucial to ensure the title is clear of any issues or **encumbrances** that could affect the transfer of ownership. -->
- This step ensures the title is clear of any issues or **encumbrances** that could affect the transfer of ownership.

## Property Inspection

- Various inspections may be required or requested by the buyer or lender, including:
  - **Termite inspections**
  - Assessments of **structural integrity**
  - **Appraisals** (e.g., those required by the Department of Veterans Affairs)
- Perform a **final walk-through inspection** as the closing date nears to:
  - Confirm the property is in the condition agreed upon in the sales contract.
  - Verify that any concerns identified in earlier inspections have been addressed satisfactorily.

## Status of Existing Loans

- Request a **payoff statement** or **reduction certificate** to obtain documented evidence of the status of any current loans against the property.
