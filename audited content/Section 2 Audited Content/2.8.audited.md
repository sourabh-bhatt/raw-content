# Understanding Market Value in Real Estate

## Factors Influencing Market Value

Consider the following scenarios that illustrate how market value can be affected:

- **Urgent Sale by Seller:** A seller who needs to sell quickly due to personal or business urgencies may accept a lower offer than the typical market price.

- **Buyer's Lack of Local Knowledge:** A buyer from out of state unfamiliar with the local real estate landscape might pay more or less than the property's actual market value.

- **Family Transactions:** When a parent sells property to a son or daughter at a reduced cost, the sale price is below market value due to personal reasons.

These examples highlight that many factors can cause a property to sell for more or less than its market value.
