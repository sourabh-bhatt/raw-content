# Market Price and Market Rent in Real Estate

## Definition of Market Price and Market Rent

- **Market Price** refers to the actual amount of money a buyer will pay for a property.
- **Market Rent** is the actual amount a tenant pays to rent a property.

## Important Distinctions

The market price or rent can differ from other property valuations, such as:

- **Market Value**
- **Appraised Value**
- **Assessed Value**
- The result of a **Comparative Market Analysis**
