# Understanding Geodetic Surveys in Real Estate

## What Are Geodetic Surveys?

Geodetic surveys refer to a method that encompasses both **horizontal** and **vertical descriptions** of land. This method relies heavily on the use of **permanent ground markers**, often called **monuments** or **benchmarks**.

## Key Components of Geodetic Surveys

- **Permanent Ground Markers (Monuments/Benchmarks):** Physical markers placed on the land to provide fixed reference points.
- **Datum:** An imaginary baseline used for all horizontal and vertical measurements in the surveying process.
