# Physical Characteristics of Land in Real Estate

The core **physical characteristics of land influence** value, ownership rights, and legal considerations in real estate. Each characteristic is addressed individually below.

## Immobility

- Land **cannot be moved**; its location is fixed.
- The value of land is heavily influenced by its surroundings.
- Changes in the neighborhood can either enhance or reduce property value.
- Natural processes like **erosion** or **accretion** can slightly alter the land's physical characteristics over time, but the land's fixed position remains the same.

## Indestructibility

- Land itself **cannot be destroyed**.
- Buildings and landscapes may change or fall, but the land endures.
- Because of this, land is typically **not insured** or **depreciated** in financial terms.
- However, its economic value might fluctuate due to external conditions.

## Uniqueness (Heterogeneity)

- Every piece of real estate is **one of a kind**.
- No two parcels are exactly alike in size, shape, location, or appearance.
- This uniqueness is why specific legal actions, such as **specific performance lawsuits**, may be pursued if a seller backs out of a valid sale agreement.
- Since a substitute property might not be acceptable or available, the originally agreed-upon land is critical to the buyer.
