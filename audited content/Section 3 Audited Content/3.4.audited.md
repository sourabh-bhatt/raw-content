# Understanding Encroachments in Property Rights

An encroachment is any **unauthorized physical intrusion** that impacts someone else's land. This can happen in different ways, such as a fence or driveway physically extending onto another person's property, which is considered a form of **trespass**.

## Types of Encroachments

Encroachments are not limited to just physical space. They can also breach into the **airspace** of a neighboring property. Examples include:

- Overhanging tree branches

Other issues like loud noises and intrusive bright lights are forms of **nuisances** that infringe upon a neighbor's comfort and use of their property, but they are not encroachments.

## Identifying Encroachments

To identify potential encroachments, a **property survey** is typically conducted. This process helps in:

- Mapping out the precise **property boundaries**
- Detecting any areas where unwelcome intrusions might exist
