# Understanding Eminent Domain in Property Rights

## What is Eminent Domain?

Eminent domain refers to the government's power to take private property for **public use** with **just compensation**. The government must pay fair market value for the property taken.

## Impact on Leased Properties

- If the government decides to take over a piece of **leased land** for public use, the existing **lease agreement** will be terminated.
- This means any lease that are managing or involved with can be ended due to eminent domain.
- Both the property owner and tenant may be entitled to compensation for their losses.
