# Understanding Escheat in Property Rights

<!-- Removed you -->

<!-- When you're dealing with **property rights**, it's essential to understand the concept of **escheat**.  -->

## What is Escheat?

Escheat is the government's power to transfer **unclaimed property** back to the state. In Virginia, real property escheats to the Commonwealth.

## When Does Escheat Apply?

- It occurs when someone dies without a will (intestate) AND has no legal heirs.
- It applies when there are no known **heirs**—individuals legally entitled to inherit the deceased's assets.

## Purpose of Escheat

The principle behind escheat is to address the issue of property that would otherwise become ownerless. It ensures that assets are not left in limbo and have a clear path of ownership, reverting to the state's control in the absence of heirs or a will.
