# Understanding Illegality in Property Rights and Contracts

## Illegality in Contracts

Imagine entering into an agreement. If the subject matter of this agreement involves illegal activities, such as:

- Hiring someone for a crime
- Buying prohibited items or services

then such contracts are considered **void from the outset**. This means that the law views these contracts as if they never existed, rendering them completely **unenforceable**.

## Importance of Legal Compliance

Why does this matter? In dealings, need to ensure that every contract engage with is **legally compliant**. This:

- Protects from potential legal repercussions
- Safeguards the sanctity of transactions
- Ensures every agreement can be legally enforced if necessary

Remember, the essence of a **valid contract** is not just in the **mutual agreement** but also in the **legality of its purpose**.

Keep this in mind to maintain the integrity and legality of engagements.
