# Understanding Covenants in Property Rights

<!-- When diving into the realm of **property rights**, one essential concept you will encounter is **covenants**.  -->

## What Are Covenants?

Think of **covenants** as unconditional and binding promises that are embedded within **contracts**, **deeds**, or other legal documents.

## Importance of Covenants

Learning about covenants is crucial because they play a significant role in:

- How properties can be used
- Restrictions that may apply
- The obligations of the parties involved

## Role in Property Transactions

In **property transactions**, covenants define the obligations and rights tied to properties. They help clarify responsibilities such as:

- Advising clients accurately
- Ensuring transactions comply with any existing covenants on the property
