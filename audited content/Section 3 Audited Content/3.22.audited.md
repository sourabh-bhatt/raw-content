# Private Restrictions and Restrictive Covenants in Real Estate

Private restrictions are land use controls created by private parties rather than government entities. The primary tool for this is the restrictive covenant.

## Restrictive Covenants and CCRs

A **restrictive covenant**, along with **conditions and restrictions** (often abbreviated as **CCRs**), can be implemented on a piece of real estate by the property owners or developers. This is done through various legal documents such as:

- **Deeds**
- **Leases**
- **Condominium declarations**

It's important to grasp that these private controls can actually play a significant role in enhancing the value of a property. They do this by promoting a sense of uniformity within a community or development.

## Examples of Restrictive Covenants

For example, in a residential context, restrictive covenants might:

- Dictate the minimum lot size
- Prescribe certain architectural styles or building materials that must be used
- Outline other specifications related to the exterior appearance of properties
