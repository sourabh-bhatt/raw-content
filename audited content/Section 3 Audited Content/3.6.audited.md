# Concurrent Ownership in Real Property

In real property, **concurrent ownership** allows two or more individuals to hold ownership at the same time. This form of **co-ownership**, or **co-tenancy**, is divided into three main types:

- **Joint Tenancy**
- **Tenancy in Common**
- **Tenancy by the Entireties**

Each of these forms has its unique set of rules and implications for the co-owners.
