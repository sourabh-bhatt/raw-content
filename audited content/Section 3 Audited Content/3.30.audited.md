# Understanding Personal Property in Real Estate

<!-- When discussing **property rights**, it's essential to understand the concept of **personal property**.  -->

## Definition of Personal Property

**Personal property** refers to items that are movable and not permanently attached to real estate. Personal property includes both physical items and intangible assets.

### Types of Personal Property

<!-- - **Tangible personal property**: Physical items you can touch, such as cars, boats, jewelry, furniture, and clothing.

- **Tangible personal property**: Physical items such as cars, boats, jewelry, furniture, and clothing.
- **Intangible personal property**: Non-physical assets such as stocks, bonds, bank accounts, and patents.

## Additional Elements of Personal Property

Personal property also includes:

- **Fructus industriales** (often called **emblements**)

Emblements are important in real estate transactions involving farmland.

## Terminology

Personal property is sometimes referred to as **personalty** or **chattel**. These terms mean the same thing as personal property.
