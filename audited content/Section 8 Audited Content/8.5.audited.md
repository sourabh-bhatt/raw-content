# Option Clause in Leases

An **option clause** grants the tenant the right, but not the obligation, to make a specific decision in the future based on certain conditions. This clause provides flexibility in **rental agreements**.

## How the Option Clause Works

- **Lease Extension:** An option clause may allow the tenant the opportunity to extend the lease for more time under the terms already agreed upon. This provides value for tenants who want to avoid moving when the initial lease term expires.

- **Leasing Additional Space:** An option clause might enable the tenant to lease additional space in the property if needed. This supports business growth without the need to relocate. Example: If a business is growing and suddenly needs more room, this type of option allows expansion without the complication of finding a new place and moving operations.

- **Purchase Option:** An option clause can contain a provision that allows the tenant to purchase the leased property outright. This is set at a **predetermined price**, timeframe, and specific terms and conditions.

## Benefits of the Option Clause

The option clause provides flexibility to make decisions about:

- Extending the lease
- Expanding space
- Buying the property

These options are available under specific circumstances agreed to beforehand, providing flexibility for future decisions.
