# Lease Rights: Exclusive Possession and Landlord Ownership

<!-- When you're engaging with leases, it's crucial to understand the concept of the **Exclusive Right of Possession**. The **Exclusive Right of Possession** transfers to the tenant the privilege of possessing and using the property for a specified duration. This is backed by a **covenant of quiet enjoyment**, which means that tenants have the right to enjoy the property without undue disturbance.

## Tenant's Rights
- The tenant holds the **Exclusive Right of Possession**, allowing use and enjoyment of the property during the lease term.
- The **covenant of quiet enjoyment** ensures the tenant can use the property without interference.

## Landlord's Rights
- Despite the tenant's possession rights, the **landlord still owns the property**.
- This ownership during the lease is called the **leased fee estate**.
- The landlord retains a **reversionary interest**, meaning once the lease ends, the landlord has the full right to take back possession of the property.
