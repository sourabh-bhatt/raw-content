# Lessee's Interest in Leases

<!-- When you're delving into **leases**, it's important to grasp what exactly a **lessee's interest** means. Think of it as the exclusive right you gain to **use and possess** a property. A **lessee's interest** is the exclusive right to **use and possess** a property. This right is granted through a **lease contract**. This right is considered **personal property**, often referred to as a **leasehold estate** or **non-freehold estate**.

## Key Concepts of Lessee's Interest

- The lessee acquires a **limited interest** in the real property.
- This interest grants **possession rights**, but **not ownership** of the property.
<!-- - You can **live in or use** the property, but you do **not own** the property itself. -->
- The lessee can **live in or use** the property, but does **not own** the property itself.

<!-- This distinction is vital because it lays the groundwork for understanding various types of leases and how **property rights** are transferred **without transferring ownership**. Recognizing the difference between possession and ownership is a fundamental concept in real estate. -->
This distinction lays the groundwork for understanding various types of leases and how **property rights** are transferred **without transferring ownership**. The difference between possession and ownership is a concept in real estate.

---

Example: If you rent an apartment, your lease gives you the right to live there (possession), but the landlord retains ownership of the property.
