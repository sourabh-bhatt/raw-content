# Restrictive Covenants in Leases

<!-- As you delve into the world of leases, one important concept you'll need to understand is **restrictive covenants**. When dealing with leases, it's crucial to be aware that the **landlord** has the authority to set specific limitations on how you, as a **tenant**, can use the premises. This means that certain activities or modifications that you might consider beneficial or necessary for your space could actually be prohibited under the terms of your lease. -->
**Restrictive covenants** are lease provisions where the **landlord** sets specific limitations on how a **tenant** can use the premises. Certain activities or modifications may be prohibited under the terms of the lease.

## Benefits of Understanding Restrictive Covenants

<!-- - Knowing about restrictive covenants equips you with the knowledge to **negotiate lease terms more effectively**, ensuring that your use of the property aligns with your needs while adhering to the landlord's restrictions.
- Understanding these limitations helps you avoid potential **disputes with the landlord**, which could lead to unnecessary complications or, in extreme cases, **eviction**. -->
- Knowledge of restrictive covenants enables more effective **lease term negotiation**, ensuring property use aligns with needs while adhering to landlord restrictions.
- Understanding these limitations helps avoid potential **disputes with the landlord**, which could lead to complications or **eviction**.

## Reviewing Restrictive Covenants Before Leasing

<!-- When assessing a potential leasing situation, it's important to carefully review the restrictive covenants to ensure they won't significantly hinder your planned use of the space. These restrictions may include: -->
When assessing a potential leasing situation, restrictive covenants should be reviewed to ensure they don't hinder planned use of the space. These restrictions may include:

- The type of business you can operate
- Modifications you can make to the space
- Hours of operation

<!-- Such covenants can have a significant impact on your **operational flexibility**. -->
Such covenants can impact **operational flexibility**.
