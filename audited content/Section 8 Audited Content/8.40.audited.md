# Percentage Lease in Real Estate

A **percentage lease** requires a tenant to pay a **fixed minimum rent**, regardless of their business's sales. However, there is an additional component to this lease type:

- Besides the fixed minimum rent, the tenant must also pay a **percentage of gross sales** that exceed a predetermined **breakpoint**.

This structure allows landlords to benefit from the business success of their tenants. Both **gross leases** and **net leases** can incorporate the percentage lease structure as an additional method to calculate the **monthly rent**.
