# Estate from Year to Year (Periodic Tenancy)

An **Estate from Year to Year**, also known as a **periodic leasehold** or **periodic tenancy**, is a lease agreement set for a specific duration that automatically renews itself from one period to the next unless one of the parties decides to terminate it.

## Duration

- The term "year to year" can be misleading. It does not mean the lease must be for a minimum of one year.
- These leases can be flexible, operating on a **month-to-month** or even **week-to-week** basis.
- Example: A month-to-month rental agreement does not end after one month; it continues into the next month and so forth, until terminated.

## Termination Requirements

- Unlike an **estate for years** lease, which has a fixed expiration date, a **periodic tenancy** requires a **formal notice** to terminate.
- The automatic renewal feature means the tenancy continues from one period to the next until proper steps are taken to end it.
- This setup ensures continuous lease coverage for both the **landlord** and **tenant**, but also requires clear communication when it's time to end the lease.
