# Leases: Clauses and Covenants

**Leases** contain specific **clauses** and **covenants** that frequently appear. These elements shape the agreement between the **landlord** and **tenant**, detailing their rights, responsibilities, and the terms of the lease itself.

## Common Lease Clauses

Each lease is unique, but many share common clauses that define the framework of the agreement. These clauses include:

- **Description of the property**
- **Term of the lease**
- **Amount of rent**
- Conditions regarding **renewal**
- Conditions regarding **subletting**
- Conditions regarding **termination**

These clauses provide the framework for lease interpretation and management.

## Lease Covenants

**Covenants** are promises embedded within the lease. They can obligate either the **landlord (lessor)** or the **tenant (lessee)** to perform certain actions or abstain from doing something. Common covenants include:

- **Covenant to pay rent**: The tenant's promise to pay the agreed-upon rent on time.
- **Covenant of quiet enjoyment**: Ensures the tenant can use the property without interference from the landlord.

## Clauses and Covenants Overview

Clauses and covenants ensure agreements are legally sound and address concerns that might arise during the lease term.
