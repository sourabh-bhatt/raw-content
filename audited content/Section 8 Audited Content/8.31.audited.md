A **gross lease** is an agreement where tenants pay a **fixed rental amount** to the landlord. This is also known as a **fixed** or **flat lease**. Example: In this arrangement, you pay a set amount every period, such as monthly, for the duration of your lease.

## Key Features of a Gross Lease

<!-- - You pay a **fixed rental amount** regularly. -->
- Tenants pay a **fixed rental amount** regularly.
- The **landlord** is responsible for:
  - Property **taxes**
  - **Insurance**
  - All **maintenance** and **repairs**

This arrangement simplifies budgeting for tenants because they don't have to worry about fluctuating costs for property taxes or sudden repair expenses affecting monthly payments.
