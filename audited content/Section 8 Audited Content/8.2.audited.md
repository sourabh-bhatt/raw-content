# Restrictive Covenants in Leases

**Restrictive covenants** are lease provisions that limit tenant activities or operations within a property.

## What Are Restrictive Covenants?

Restrictive covenants are lease provisions that limit the activities or operations of tenants within a property. Example: In a shopping center:

- One tenant running a restaurant might have a restrictive covenant limiting them to selling only **seafood items**.
- Another tenant could be restricted to selling just **hamburgers and hot dogs**.

## Purpose of Restrictive Covenants

These covenants serve to:

- Maintain a balanced **tenant mix**
- Prevent direct **competition** between tenants in the same shopping center
- Ensure a variety of offerings to customers
- Create a harmonious operating environment for tenants

## Lease Management Applications

Restrictive covenants help manage lease agreements and tenant relationships in commercial properties.
