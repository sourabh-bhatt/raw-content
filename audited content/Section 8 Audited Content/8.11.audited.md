# Lease Types and Termination

<!-- When you're navigating through the world of leases, it becomes essential to understand the dynamics of different lease types and the implications of their termination. Let's break this down together. -->
Different lease types have varying termination requirements and implications.

## Fixed-Term Leases (Tenancy for Years)

- These leases are fixed for a definite period, also known as **tenancy for years**.
- They have a clear **start date** and **end date**.
- Once the end date arrives, the lease **expires automatically** without any action required from either party.

## Periodic Leases

- These leases operate in cycles, renewing at set intervals such as **month-to-month** or **year-to-year**.
- Unlike tenancy for years, periodic leases **do not terminate automatically** at the end of each cycle.
- To terminate a periodic lease, **proper notice** must be given, which is often required to be **in writing**.

## Notice Requirements and Legal Considerations

- **Virginia law** dictates the **minimum notice period** required to terminate periodic leases.
- In Virginia, **month-to-month leases** require **30 days written notice**. **Year-to-year leases** require **120 days written notice**.
- However, the **specific lease agreement** can legally require a **longer notice period** than Virginia law.
- If the lease document specifies a particular notice timeframe, that requirement **overrides** the Virginia law minimum.
- Failure to provide notice according to the lease terms or not giving notice at all results in the lease **automatically renewing**.

## Summary
