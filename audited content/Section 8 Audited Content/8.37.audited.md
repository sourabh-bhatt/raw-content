Landlord and tenant rights and obligations are governed by state laws. These laws outline the duties, rights, and responsibilities of landlords and tenants. These laws cover a broad spectrum of issues, including:

- The maintenance of fit and habitable living conditions
- The handling of **security deposits** (such as their collection, usage, and eventual repayment)
- Tenant obligations, which encompass **rent payment**, keeping the premises in good condition, and adhering to any rules and regulations tied to the property

## Lease Contracts

<!-- Lease contracts are important documents. They transfer the leasehold to the tenant and outline the terms of occupancy. Important points include: -->
Lease contracts transfer the leasehold to the tenant and outline the terms of occupancy. Key points include:

- Under Virginia law, any lease for more than one year must be in writing.
- Common types of lease agreements include:
  - **Gross lease**: tenant pays a fixed or flat rate.
  - **Net lease**: tenant also covers taxes, insurance, and maintenance.
  - **Percentage lease**: rent tied to the tenant's business profits.
  - **Graduated lease**: rent increases over time.
  - **Ground lease**: lease for the land alone.
  - **Proprietary lease**: used in cooperatives.
  - **Revaluation lease**: rent based on property value re-assessment.
