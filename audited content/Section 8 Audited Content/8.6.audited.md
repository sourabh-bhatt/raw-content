# Lessor's Interest in Leases

<!-- As part of understanding **leases**, it's important to know about the **lessor's interest**. -->
The **lessor's interest** refers to the property owner's rights in a lease agreement.

- The **lessor** is the person or entity that owns the property and **retains the title**, meaning they keep the **ownership rights**.
- However, the lessor transfers the right of **exclusive use and possession** to someone else, known as the **lessee**.
- The **lessee** can use the property as if it were their own, but only for a **limited time**.
- This arrangement is made in exchange for something valuable, typically **rent**.

<!-- It is crucial to grasp this balance of rights and obligations to effectively manage and negotiate leases. -->
This arrangement creates a balance of rights and obligations between lessor and lessee.
