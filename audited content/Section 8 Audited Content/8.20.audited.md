Example: A situation arises where someone wants to make use of a piece of land without actually owning it. A **ground lease**, sometimes referred to as a **land lease** or **pad lease**, is a rental agreement for a piece of vacant land rather than a building.

## What is a Ground Lease?

- The **owner of the land** agrees to let someone else, called the **tenant**, use this piece of ground.
- The tenant has the opportunity to **build their own building** on the leased land.
- This arrangement is particularly popular for **commercial properties**.

## Duration of Ground Leases

- Ground leases are typically **long-term agreements**, often ranging from **50 to 99 years**.
- The long duration allows the tenant (also called the lessee or builder) to **recoup their investment** in the property they develop.

## Financial Structure: Net Lease Elements

- Ground leases often include elements of a **net lease**.
- This means the tenant takes on some or all of the **property expenses**, such as:
  - Property taxes
  - Insurance
  - Maintenance
- This structure makes ground leases especially appealing for **commercial developments**.
