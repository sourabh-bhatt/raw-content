A lease acts as a **contract**. Like any other contract, it requires all the essential elements that a typical contract demands. What makes a lease specific to real estate is that it transfers a limited interest - namely, the **possession** and **use** - of **real property**.

This transfer occurs from the **lessor**, who is the owner or landlord, to the **lessee**, known as the renter or tenant.

## Key Concepts of Lease Contracts

- A lease is a legally binding contract.
- It transfers a limited interest in real property, specifically possession and use.
- The **lessor** is the property owner or landlord.
- The **lessee** is the renter or tenant who receives possession and use rights.
