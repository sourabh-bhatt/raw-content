## Types of Leaseholds and Termination Scenarios

Each type of **leasehold** presents unique scenarios for termination:

- **Fixed-term lease**: Naturally expires after its predetermined duration.
- **Periodic lease** (such as month-to-month lease): Requires proper notice to terminate. In Virginia, month-to-month leases require 30 days written notice.

## Methods of Lease Termination

Leases can end through various means:

- **Mutual agreement** between landlord and tenant.
- **Fulfillment of contract terms**, such as reaching the lease expiration.
- **Eviction** due to **breach of contract** or other legal grounds under the Virginia Residential Landlord and Tenant Act (VRLTA).
