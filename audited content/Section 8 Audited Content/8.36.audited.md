# Lease Termination Due to Tenant Abandonment

<!-- When a **lessee** decides to abandon the leased premises without going through a formal **surrender and acceptance** process, it leads to the **termination of the lease**. At this point, as the **lessor**, you regain full possession and control of the premises. -->
When a **lessee** decides to abandon the leased premises without going through a formal **surrender and acceptance** process, it leads to the **termination of the lease**. At this point, the **lessor** regains full possession and control of the premises.

## Financial Responsibilities of the Lessee

<!-- It is crucial to understand that the lessee remains responsible for: -->
The lessee remains responsible for:

- Any **rent that is past due**
- **Future rent payments**, but the lessor must try to re-rent the property to reduce damages
