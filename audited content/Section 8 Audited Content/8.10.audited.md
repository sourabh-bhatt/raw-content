# Early Termination of Lease Agreements

<!-- When you come across a situation where either you or the other party wants to end a lease agreement early, it's important to know that this can be done without penalty if both parties agree. Imagine you're leasing a property and decide you want out before the lease term is up. If you propose to end the lease (also known as *surrendering the lease*) and the owner of the property (the *lessor*) agrees, the lease can be terminated. A lease agreement can be ended early without penalty if both parties agree. If a tenant proposes to end the lease (also known as surrendering the lease) and the property owner (the lessor) agrees, the lease can be terminated, ending future rent payment obligations.

Leases commonly include a **cancellation clause**. This clause is a pre-agreed condition that allows either the tenant or the lessor to terminate the lease under specific conditions, which might include paying a certain amount of money.

## Cancellation Clauses in Commercial and Industrial Properties

<!-- In the world of commercial or industrial properties, cancellation clauses are particularly useful. In commercial or industrial properties, cancellation clauses give both parties - the lessee or the lessor - the capability to cut the lease short as long as certain agreed-upon conditions are met or a predetermined sum is paid.

### Typical Payments and Costs Covered

The payment required upon cancellation is usually calculated to cover potential losses or expenses the non-canceling party might face due to the cancellation. These may include:

- Brokerage fees
- Costs associated with finding a new tenant
- Anticipated rental income lost because of the ended lease
