# Protective Covenants in Commercial Leases

**Protective covenants** are restrictions included in **commercial lease agreements** to protect the interests of both the **landlord** and **tenant**. These safeguards ensure the commercial space remains conducive to **business operations**.

## What Are Protective Covenants?

Protective covenants can regulate various factors, including:

- The type of activities allowed on the premises
- Modifications that can be made to the property
- Specifics about maintenance and repairs
- Restrictions on competing businesses
- Operating hours and noise levels
