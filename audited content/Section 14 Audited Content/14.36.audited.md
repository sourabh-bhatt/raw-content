# Understanding Loan Qualification: Down Payment and Financing

<!-- To understand how to qualify for a loan, it's essential to grasp the concept of a **down payment** and the **amount to be financed**. -->
Loan qualification involves understanding the **down payment** and the **amount to be financed**.

### What is a Down Payment?

- <!-- The down payment is the amount of money you must invest upfront into the property. -->
- The down payment is the amount of money borrowers invest upfront into the property.
- It represents the difference between the **purchase price** of the property and the amount the lender is willing to finance.

### Down Payment Function

- <!-- Demonstrates your **financial commitment** to the property. -->
- Demonstrates the borrower's **financial commitment** to the property.
- Influences the lender's decision to provide financing.

<!-- Understanding these concepts is crucial for successfully qualifying for a loan and securing the necessary financing to purchase a property. -->
These concepts help borrowers qualify for loans and secure financing for property purchases.
