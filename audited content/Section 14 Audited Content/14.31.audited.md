# Servicing Disclosure Statement in the Loan Application Process

<!-- When you're guiding your clients through the **loan application process**, it's crucial to understand the role of the **Servicing Disclosure Statement**. -->
Real estate agents must understand the role of the **Servicing Disclosure Statement** in the **loan application process**.

## Legal Requirement Under RESPA

According to the **Real Estate Settlement Procedures Act (RESPA)**, lenders or mortgage brokers must notify borrowers in writing - either at the time they apply for a loan or within three business days - about who will service their loan. This means informing borrowers who will collect their loan payments.

## Placement of the Notification

- This notification appears at the end of the **Loan Estimate Form**.
