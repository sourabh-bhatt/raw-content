# Rural Real Estate Financing: USDA Loan Programs

Real estate financing in rural areas involves two entities: the **Rural Development** and **Farm Service Agency**. These federal agencies administer rural development and conservation programs under the U.S. Department of Agriculture. They offer **direct loans** and **guarantee loans** made by conventional lenders under specific circumstances, making them key players in rural real estate financing.

## Rural Housing Direct Loans

The **Rural Housing Direct Loans** are provided by the **Rural Housing Service (RHS)**, a sub-agency within the Office of Rural Development. These loans are specifically designed to support **low- and very low-income households** in achieving homeownership. The financing can be used for:

- Purchasing an existing dwelling
- Acquiring a site and constructing a dwelling
- Buying newly constructed homes located in rural areas

What makes these loans appealing is that **mortgage payments are tailored based on the household's adjusted income**, making homeownership more accessible to those with limited financial means.

### Eligibility Criteria for Direct Loans

- Applicants must fall within the **very low-income** (below 50% of the area median income, or AMI) or **low-income** (between 50% and 80% of AMI) brackets.
- The property must be **modest in size, design, and cost**.
- Properties must adhere to **voluntary national model building codes** and **RHS standards**, ensuring affordability and quality.

## Guaranteed Loans

The RHS also supports low-income individuals or households through **guaranteed loans**, which involve:

- Offering **100% financing** from approved lenders.
- RHS providing a **90% guarantee** to lenders to mitigate lending risks.

These guarantees facilitate:

- Construction, repair, renovation, or relocation of homes.
- Site preparation expenses, including water and sewage facilities.

### Eligibility and Property Standards for Guaranteed Loans

- Applicant's income can be up to **115% of the median income** for the area.
- Homes financed must be **modest** and meet both **state and RHS standards**, ensuring quality living environments.

## Role of Approved Lenders

Approved lenders include state housing agencies and lenders approved by major housing and financial institutions such as **HUD**, the **Department of Veterans Affairs**, **Fannie Mae**, and **Freddie Mac**. Their responsibilities include:

- Determining the **repayment feasibility** of applicants.
- Ensuring the financial stability of the loan programs.
