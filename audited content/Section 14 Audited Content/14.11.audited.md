Working with buyers in real estate requires accurate estimation of **monthly mortgage costs** to determine affordable payment levels. This process involves using a **loan repayment factor**, also known as an **amortization factor**, which helps calculate the monthly **principal and interest** payment with reasonable precision.

## What is a Loan Repayment Factor?

- A loan repayment factor is typically expressed as a dollar amount per $1,000 of the loan.
- For example, it might be stated as "$5.00 per $1,000."
- This factor simplifies the calculation of monthly payments by standardizing the amount owed per thousand dollars borrowed.
