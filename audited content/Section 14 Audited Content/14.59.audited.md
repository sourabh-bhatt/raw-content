# Understanding Sales Proceeds and Profit Calculation in Real Estate

<!-- When you're dealing with the financial aspects of selling a home, understanding **sales proceeds** is crucial. -->
**Sales proceeds** refer to the total amount of money received from a home sale. <!-- However, it's the **net proceeds** that tell you the profit made. -->
**Net proceeds** show the profit made. **Net proceeds** are calculated by subtracting various **selling expenses** such as **commissions** and **closing costs** from the sales proceeds. <!-- This difference essentially represents your **seller's profit**. -->
This difference represents the **seller's profit**.

## Calculating Seller's Profit

<!-- Let's break down how you can calculate this profit step by step: -->
Steps to calculate seller's profit:

1. **Identify the purchase price and sale price.**
2. **Convert the commission rate from a percentage to a decimal.** For example, 6% becomes 0.06.
3. **Calculate the commission amount:** Multiply the sale price by the commission rate.
4. **Calculate the profit:** Subtract both the purchase price and the commission from the sale price.

### Example:

- Purchase price: $120,000
- Sale price: $200,000
- Commission rate: 6% (0.06 decimal)

Calculate commission:

$200,000 Ã— 0.06 = $12,000

Calculate profit:

$200,000 - $120,000 - $12,000 = $68,000

The **seller's profit** is $68,000.

## Calculating Percent Profit

To find the **percent profit**, follow these steps:

1. Calculate the profit amount as shown above.
2. Divide the profit by the purchase price.
3. Multiply the result by 100 to convert it to a percentage.
4. Round to the nearest hundredth for standard presentation.

### Example:

Using the previous example:

0.56666 Ã— 100 = 56.6666%

Rounded to the nearest hundredth:

56.67%

Therefore, the **percent profit** on the property is **56.67%**.
