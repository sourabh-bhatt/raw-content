# Understanding the Pre-Approval Process in Real Estate Financing

<!-- In real estate financing, one essential concept you need to understand is the **Pre-Approval** process. -->
The **Pre-Approval** process in real estate financing involves a lender giving a **conditional commitment** to lend money to a specific borrower.

## How Pre-Approval Works

- <!-- Initially, you or your prospective buyer will provide the lender with a variety of **financial records**. -->
Initially, prospective buyers provide the lender with **financial records**. This could include:
  - *Income statements*
  - *Credit reports*
  - Any other documentation that can give a clear picture of financial health

- After receiving these documents, the lender starts a **preliminary approval process**. They review the financial records to determine the borrower's **creditworthiness**.

- <!-- It is important to note that this commitment is *not a guarantee* but a strong indication that the loan is likely to be approved, if the information stays true and accurate throughout the process. -->
This commitment is *not a guarantee* but a strong indication that the loan is likely to be approved, if the information stays true and accurate throughout the process.
