# Understanding Freddie Mac and Its Role in Real Estate Financing

<!-- You're about to dive into the world of real estate financing, and understanding **Freddie Mac** will be crucial on this journey. -->
**Freddie Mac** plays a key role in real estate financing. 

## What is Freddie Mac?

Freddie Mac, officially known as the *Federal Home Loan Mortgage Corporation* (FHLMC), is a government-sponsored yet privately operated corporation. It operates under the oversight of the **Federal Housing Finance Agency (FHFA)**.

## Freddie Mac's Role

Freddie Mac has a unique role in the housing finance system:

- It raises money by issuing debt securities in the capital markets.
- It buys mortgages from lenders.
- It bundles these mortgages together.
- It sells bonds on the open market, using these bundled mortgages as security.

<!-- **Important:** Freddie Mac guarantees the timely payment of principal and interest on the mortgage-backed securities it issues. -->
Freddie Mac guarantees the timely payment of principal and interest on the mortgage-backed securities it issues.

## Types of Mortgages Freddie Mac Purchases

Freddie Mac purchases a variety of loans, including:

- **Conventional mortgages**
- Mortgages backed by the **Federal Housing Administration (FHA)**
- Mortgages backed by the **Department of Veterans Affairs (VA)**
- Conventional mortgage-backed securities

These mortgages are primarily purchased from **savings and loan associations (S&Ls)** and **banks** operating within the **secondary market**.

## Freddie Mac's Impact on the Housing Market

Freddie Mac functions to:

- Support the availability and affordability of **homeownership** and **rental housing**.
- Stabilize and provide **liquidity** to the mortgage market.
- Make financing more accessible to a broader range of people.

This role has a ripple effect on the overall housing market.
