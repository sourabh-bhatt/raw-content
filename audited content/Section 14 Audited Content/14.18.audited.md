## What is a Percentage?

A **percentage** represents a part out of 100. Tasks involving percentages may require converting numbers from decimal form to percentage or vice versa. This skill applies to calculating various financial aspects in real estate transactions.

## Converting Percentages and Decimals

<!-- To convert a percentage to a decimal, you have two easy methods: -->
Converting a percentage to a decimal involves two methods:

- Move the decimal point two places to the left
- Divide by 100

For example, **1%** becomes **1/100** or **0.01** when converted into a decimal.

Conversely, turning a decimal into a percentage involves:

- Moving the decimal point two places to the right
- Multiplying by 100

Thus, **0.01** becomes **1%** after conversion.
