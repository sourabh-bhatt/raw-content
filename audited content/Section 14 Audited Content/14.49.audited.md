# Government-Backed Loans in Real Estate Financing

<!-- When exploring real estate financing, it's important to understand the concept of **government-backed loans**. -->
**Government-backed loans** are loans in real estate financing. These loans are initially made by **primary market lenders**, such as banks or credit unions, but carry an extra layer of security for the lender in the form of **government insurance** or **guarantees**.

## Types of Government-Backed Loans

### Federal Housing Administration (FHA) Loans
- Insured by the **Federal Housing Administration (FHA)**.
- If the borrower defaults, the FHA covers the lender's losses.
- This insurance encourages lenders to approve loans for individuals who may not qualify for conventional loans due to **lower credit scores** or a **smaller down payment**.

### Department of Veterans Affairs (VA) Loans
- Guaranteed by the **Department of Veterans Affairs (VA)**.
- The government guarantees a portion of the loan amount if repayment issues arise.
- Specifically designed for **service members**, **veterans**, and eligible surviving spouses.
- Provides an opportunity for these groups to become homeowners.

- Differentiates between **conventional loans** and government-backed programs.
- Highlights financing options for borrowers who might not qualify through traditional means.
