# Understanding Subprime Loans in Real Estate Financing

<!-- When you're exploring the realm of **real estate financing**, one unique category you'll come across is the **subprime loan**. This is a loan made to borrowers who do not meet standard lending criteria. -->
**Real estate financing** includes the **subprime loan** category. This is a loan made to borrowers who do not meet standard lending criteria.

## What Are Subprime Loans?

Subprime loans have a higher risk of **default**. These loans go to borrowers who do not qualify for conventional loans. These borrowers usually have lower credit scores, higher debt-to-income ratios, or limited credit history.

## Characteristics of Subprime Loans

Subprime loans carry certain features that set them apart and contribute to their riskier nature:

- **Interest-Only Payments:** Some subprime loans allow borrowers to make only interest payments for a set time period.

- **Teaser Rates:** These are low starting interest rates that increase after a short time period. This makes payments much higher later.

- **Pay Option or Negative Amortization Loans:** These loans have adjustable rates. Borrowers can choose to make a full payment, interest-only payment, or minimum payment.

  <!-- - *Important:* The minimum payment may be less than the interest owed. This causes the loan balance to grow larger over time. -->
  - The minimum payment may be less than the interest owed. This causes the loan balance to grow larger over time.

## Risk and Compensation for Lenders

Lenders face higher risk with subprime loans. They charge higher interest rates and fees to offset this risk.
