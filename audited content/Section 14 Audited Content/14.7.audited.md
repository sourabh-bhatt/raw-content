# Rate Lock in Real Estate Financing

In real estate financing, a **rate lock** represents a promise from a **lender** to the **borrower** to hold a specified **interest rate**, which may or may not include a certain number of **points**, for a specific period of time. This period typically spans the duration the lender takes to process the **loan application**. This may also be referred to as a **"lock-in"** or **"rate commitment.""

## Rate Lock Applications

Utilizing a rate lock provides financial security, ensuring that the interest rate won't increase while the loan application is being processed. This can potentially save money over the term of the loan, making it a valuable method in real estate financing.
