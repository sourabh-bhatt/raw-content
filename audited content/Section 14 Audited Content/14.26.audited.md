# Understanding the Promissory Note in Real Estate Financing

<!-- When you delve into real estate financing, it's essential to understand the concept of a **promissory note**. Imagine yourself signing a written promise where you agree to pay back the money you owe to someone who has lent it to you. This document will detail exactly how much you owe, the **interest rate** attached to that amount, and both when and how you're supposed to pay it back. -->
Real estate financing includes the concept of a **promissory note**. A promissory note represents a written promise where the borrower agrees to pay back money to the lender. This document details the amount owed, the **interest rate** attached to that amount, and both when and how repayment occurs.

## Roles in a Promissory Note

<!-- - You are the **maker**, or the **borrower**, who commits to repaying the lender. -->
- The **maker**, or the **borrower**, commits to repaying the lender.
- The lender is also known as the **payee**.

<!-- The terms "or order" or "or bearer" can make notes **negotiable**. This means that the original lender has the option to sell your promissory note to someone else. If that happens, this new individual steps into the shoes of the original lender, gaining the legal authority to demand repayment from you. -->
The terms "or order" or "or bearer" can make notes **negotiable**. This means that the original lender has the option to sell the promissory note to someone else. When this occurs, the new individual steps into the shoes of the original lender, gaining the legal authority to demand repayment from the borrower.

## Key Characteristics of a Promissory Note

- Unlike other documents related to property transactions, the promissory note **does not need to be recorded** anywhere.
<!-- - Its purpose is purely to create a **personal obligation** between you and the lender. -->
- Its purpose is purely to create a **personal obligation** between the borrower and the lender.

Knowledge of how a promissory note functions applies to many real estate financing transactions.
