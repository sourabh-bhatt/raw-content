# The Primary Mortgage Market in Real Estate Financing

<!-- In the realm of real estate financing, you'll encounter what's known as the **Primary Mortgage Market**. -->
The **Primary Mortgage Market** is a network in real estate financing. <!-- This is essentially a network populated by **direct lenders**, sometimes referred to as **prime lenders** or **originators**. These entities are your go-to sources for obtaining **mortgage loans**, as they provide the necessary cash directly to borrowers like yourself. -->
This network consists of **direct lenders**, sometimes referred to as **prime lenders** or **originators**. These entities provide **mortgage loans** by providing cash directly to borrowers.

## Role of Primary Lenders

- Some primary lenders choose to **hold onto the mortgages** they originate.
- However, the majority opt to **sell these loans** to other investors who operate within the **secondary mortgage market**.

## Why Do Lenders Sell Loans?

The primary reason lenders sell loans is **liquidity**:

- By selling off loans, lenders convert their tied-up investments into **liquid funds**.
- This liquidity enables them to provide funds for granting more loans.

## Primary Mortgage Market Cycle

<!-- Understanding this cycle is fundamental because: -->
This cycle:

- Explains how lenders manage to keep financing options flowing.
- Highlights the broader ecosystem of real estate financing.
- Shows the interconnectivity between the **primary** and **secondary mortgage markets**.

Example: A bank originates a mortgage loan and provides cash to the borrower. Instead of holding the loan until maturity, the bank sells the loan to an investor in the secondary market. The bank then uses the proceeds from the sale to issue new loans to other borrowers, maintaining a steady flow of mortgage financing.
