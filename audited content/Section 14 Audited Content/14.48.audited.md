# The Primary Mortgage Market in Real Estate Financing

<!-- In the realm of real estate financing, you need to familiarize yourself with the **primary mortgage market**. -->
The **primary mortgage market** is a network of **direct lenders**, often called **prime lenders** or **originators**, who provide **mortgage loans** directly to **borrowers**. <!-- These lenders play a critical role in the housing market, as they're the initial point of contact for securing a loan to purchase property. -->
These lenders serve as the initial point of contact for securing a loan to purchase property.

## Role of Primary Lenders

- Some primary lenders choose to **hold onto the mortgages** they issue.
- However, the majority **sell these loans to other investors**.

## Purpose of Selling Loans

- Selling loans allows lenders to **liquidate their initial investment**.
- This liquidation is a means to **acquire additional funds**.
- With these funds, lenders can **make more loans to other borrowers**.
