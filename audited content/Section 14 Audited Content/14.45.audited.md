# Understanding the Loan Estimate

<!-- The **Loan Estimate** is a crucial document in the mortgage application process. -->
The **Loan Estimate** is a document in the mortgage application process. It is required by the **TRID (TILA-RESPA Integrated Disclosure) Rule**, which combines requirements from TILA and RESPA.

## Purpose and Timing of the Loan Estimate

- The Loan Estimate provides an **estimate of loan terms and settlement costs** the borrower will likely pay.
- Lenders must provide the Loan Estimate **within three business days** of receiving a mortgage application.

## Nature of the Estimates

- The figures on the Loan Estimate are **estimates**, not final amounts.
- Actual costs may vary, but many fees have **tolerance limits** that restrict how much they can increase.

## Components of a Loan Estimate

The Loan Estimate includes:

- **Loan terms**: Interest rate, monthly payment, and loan amount.
- **Projected payments**: How payments may change over time.
- **Costs at closing**: Including loan costs and other costs.
- **Origination charges**: Fees charged by the lender.
- **Services you cannot shop for**: Required services with set providers.
- **Services you can shop for**: Services where you can choose providers.
- **Taxes and government fees**: Recording fees and transfer taxes.
