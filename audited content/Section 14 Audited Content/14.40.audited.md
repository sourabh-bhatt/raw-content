# Loan Servicing in Real Estate Financing

<!-- When you're diving into real estate financing, an important concept you'll encounter is **loan servicing**. It's crucial to understand that even after conventional lenders sell loans, they often still handle the servicing. -->
**Loan servicing** is a concept in real estate financing. Even after conventional lenders sell loans, they often still handle the servicing.

## What is Loan Servicing?

Loan servicing involves several responsibilities, including:

- **Collection of Monthly Payments**: Handling the collection of monthly payments from borrowers. This involves record-keeping and ensuring that payments are made on time.

- **Disbursing Funds for Property Taxes and Insurance**: Ensuring that property taxes and insurance premiums are paid on behalf of the borrower. This protects both the borrower and the lender's interests in the property.

- **Supervising the Loan**: Monitoring the loan's status to ensure everything proceeds according to the original terms.

- **Handling Delinquencies**: Addressing situations when borrowers fall behind on payments. This may involve restructuring the loan or pursuing other remedies as outlined in the loan agreement.

- **Managing Early Payoffs and Mortgage Releases**: Processing early loan payoffs or mortgage releases efficiently and correctly.

## Fees for Loan Servicing

For providing these services, a **collection fee** is charged. This fee compensates for the effort and resources spent managing these tasks, ensuring the loan remains healthy and that the rights and responsibilities of all parties are upheld.
