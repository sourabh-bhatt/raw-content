When planning subdivision developments and condominiums, developers encounter a concept known as **bulk zoning**. Bulk zoning helps control **building density** instead of focusing solely on the type of land use. It serves as a tool that governs how much of a plot can be covered by buildings and structures.

## What is Bulk Zoning?

Bulk zoning is also referred to as **area zoning** because it encompasses comprehensive restrictions that cover several aspects of a development, including:

- **Setback lines**: These dictate the distance a building must be from the property line.
- **Building height**: Ensures buildings do not exceed certain height limits.
- **Open areas**: Requirements for open spaces around structures.
