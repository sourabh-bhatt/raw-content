# Understanding Buffer Zones in Subdivision Developments and Condominiums

<!-- When planning **subdivision developments** and **condominiums**, understanding the concept of a **buffer zone** is essential. -->

When planning **subdivision developments** and **condominiums**, understanding the concept of a **buffer zone** is necessary.

## What is a Buffer Zone?

A buffer zone is a designated area that serves as a protective barrier between different types of land uses. It helps to mitigate potential negative impacts such as **noise** or **pollution** from one development affecting a neighboring area.

## Example Scenario

<!-- Consider you are a developer planning an **industrial park**. You might be required to establish a buffer zone between your proposed development and a neighboring **residential area**. Example: A developer planning an **industrial park** might be required to establish a buffer zone between the proposed development and a neighboring **residential area**. This buffer zone acts as a safeguard to ensure the residential community is not adversely affected by the industrial activities.

## How to Comply with Buffer Zone Requirements

<!-- To comply with buffer zone regulations, you could: -->

To comply with buffer zone regulations, developers can:

- Create a buffer zone featuring a **park**
- Use another form of **intermediate use** that separates the industrial park from the residential area
