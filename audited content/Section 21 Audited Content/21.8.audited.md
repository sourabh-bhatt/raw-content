# Understanding Buffer Zones in Subdivision and Condominium Planning

<!-- When you're planning **subdivision developments** and **condominiums**, it is essential to understand the concept of a **buffer zone**. -->

When planning **subdivision developments** and **condominiums**, understanding the concept of a **buffer zone** is necessary.

## What is a Buffer Zone?

<!-- A buffer zone is a **land use zone** that serves as a separator between two land uses that typically do not work well together. Think of a buffer as a peaceful mediator that helps prevent conflicts between different land uses. -->

A buffer zone is a **land use zone** that serves as a separator between two land uses that typically do not work well together. A buffer helps prevent conflicts between different land uses.

## Example of a Buffer Zone

- A park positioned between a **commercial area** and a **residential zone** acts as a buffer zone.
- This park provides a pleasant and logical transition between the two different uses.
