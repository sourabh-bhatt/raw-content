# Understanding Variance in Subdivisions and Condominium Developments

<!-- When planning subdivisions and condominium developments, understanding the concept of **variance** is vital. -->

When planning subdivisions and condominium developments, understanding the concept of **variance** is necessary.

## What is a Variance?

A *variance* is a special permission granted by local zoning authorities that allows a deviation from the strict application of zoning laws. It acknowledges that a one-size-fits-all approach to zoning might not accommodate every unique property or situation.

<!-- For example, imagine you come across a lot that measures one-and-a-half acres in a neighborhood where the zoning regulations require home sites to be at least two acres. In such a situation, to proceed with building, you must apply for and secure a variance. -->

Example: A developer comes across a lot that measures one-and-a-half acres in a neighborhood where the zoning regulations require home sites to be at least two acres. In such a situation, to proceed with building, the developer must apply for and secure a variance.

## The Variance Application Process

The process involves submitting a detailed application to the zoning board, explaining why the variance is necessary and how it aligns with community standards and interests. Key points include:

- Demonstrating that strict adherence to zoning regulations would create an unnecessary hardship.
