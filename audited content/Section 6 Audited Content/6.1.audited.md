# Understanding Covenants and Clauses in Mortgages and Deeds of Trust

**Mortgages** and **deeds of trust (DOTs)** contain **covenants** and **clauses** that define the terms of these agreements.

## Covenants

- **Covenants** are *promises* written into these contracts.
- They require one party to do or not do certain things.
- These promises ensure that all parties are aware of their obligations.
- Knowledge of covenants can prevent misunderstandings or breaches of contract.

## Clauses

- **Clauses** are the *conditions* stipulated in these agreements.
- They set out specific requirements or situations that could change the rights or duties of the parties involved.
- Clauses help navigate contract details effectively.
- This ensures all conditions are met and potential issues are addressed promptly.

## Application in Real Estate Transactions

**Covenants** and **clauses** in mortgages and DOTs serve several functions in real estate transactions. This knowledge will:

- Improve contract skills.
- Provide foundational knowledge needed for professional practice.
- Enable guidance of clients through complex transactions with confidence and expertise.
