# Understanding Mortgages and Lien Theory

In **mortgages**, **borrowers** (also called **mortgagors**) retain property ownership. **Lenders** (called **mortgagees**) hold **liens** on properties. A **lien** is a legal claim on property.

## Key Concepts

- The **lien** gives lenders the right to take action when borrowers don't pay.
- When borrowers don't make payments, lenders can start **foreclosure**.
- **Foreclosure** means lenders force property sales to recover owed money.

This system is called **lien theory**. It means the lender has a security interest in the property but the borrower keeps ownership.
