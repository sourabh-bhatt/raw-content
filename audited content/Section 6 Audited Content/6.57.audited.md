# Mortgages and Deeds of Trust in Real Estate Finance

**Mortgages** and **deeds of trust** create a **voluntary lien** on a property. This lien acts as security for the lender, ensuring a claim on the property until the borrower repays the loan in full.

## How Mortgages Work

- A mortgage is a **two-party agreement** between the borrower and the lender.
- When a borrower takes a loan to purchase property, this agreement gives the lender the right to foreclose if the borrower fails to fulfill loan repayment obligations.
- This failure to repay is commonly referred to as **defaulting on the loan**.

## How Deeds of Trust Work

- A deed of trust involves **three parties**: the borrower, the lender, and a third party called the **trustee**.
- The trustee holds **legal title** to the property until the loan is fully paid off.
- If the borrower defaults on the loan, the trustee—not the lender—has the authority to sell the property to recoup the loan amount.

These concepts frame the legal and financial structure of real estate transactions.
