# Handling Properties Under Financial Distress

When property owners cannot afford **mortgage payments** and amounts owed on **mortgages** exceed **property values**, selling properties outright to cover full amounts owed to lenders is not viable. 

## Foreclosure and Short Sale Processes

In such cases, properties must undergo either **foreclosure** or **short sale** processes because properties serve as **collateral** for debt. This process allows **lenders** to remove **liens**, enabling clear **title** transfer to new buyers.

This process affects transactions involving properties under **financial distress**.
