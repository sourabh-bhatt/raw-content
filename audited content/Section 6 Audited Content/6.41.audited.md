# Hypothecation in Real Estate Lending

**Hypothecation** is a lending arrangement where *borrowers* retain possession rights to *properties* used as *security* or *collateral* for *loans*.

## Key Concepts

- A common example of hypothecation is a **mortgage**.
- When borrowers adhere to **mortgage terms**, they maintain exclusive use and property benefits.
- **Lenders** allow property hypothecation, meaning properties secure loans while possession remains with borrowers.

Borrowers can continue living in or using properties while they serve as loan collateral.
