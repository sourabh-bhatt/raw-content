# Satisfaction of Mortgage

The **Satisfaction of Mortgage** concept operates within real estate processes.

## What Happens After the Final Mortgage Payment?

After final mortgage payments, the following occurs:

- **Lenders** acknowledge payment completion by providing canceled **mortgage notes** to borrowers.
- Borrowers receive the **satisfaction of mortgage** document.

## Recording the Satisfaction of Mortgage

This document requires prompt action:

- Borrowers must **record the satisfaction of mortgage document without delay**.
- This step legally indicates full mortgage payment on properties.
- Recording clears the **lien** from the property's **title**.
- This process ensures records accurately reflect property **ownership** free from mortgage obligations.
