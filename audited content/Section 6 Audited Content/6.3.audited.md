# Understanding Collateral in Real Estate

Collateral serves as a fundamental component in real estate transactions.

## What is Collateral?

*Collateral* refers to money or property that borrowers pledge to lenders as security for debt repayment. This provides assurance to the lender that valuable assets can be accessed if loan repayment issues occur.

## Collateral in Mortgage Lending

- **Mortgage lenders** reduce lending risk by requiring borrowers to pledge collateral as standard industry practice.
- The purchased property typically serves as the **collateral** for the mortgage loan.

## Implications of Defaulting on a Loan

Example:

- If you cannot make loan payments, the lender can foreclose on the pledged property and sell it.
- This process helps the lender recover losses from unpaid loans.

This mechanism demonstrates the serious implications of mortgage **default** and the need for consistent loan repayment.
