# Short Sales: An Alternative to Foreclosure

A **_short sale_** allows property sales for amounts less than mortgage balances with lender permission, serving as a foreclosure alternative.

## What is a Short Sale?

In a short sale:

- The **lender** agrees to accept less money than the mortgage balance to release the **lien** on the property.
- The new owner can take over the **title** of the property without the lender's lien attached.

## Borrower's Responsibility and Deficiency

Unless state laws specify otherwise, **borrowers** who cannot maintain mortgage payments may remain responsible for any remaining debt—called a **deficiency**—after property sale.

The lender has two options regarding this deficiency:

1. **Forgive the leftover debt**, eliminating repayment obligations.
2. Request written agreements to repay all or portions of this deficiency.

## Short Sale Applications

Short sales provide alternatives to **foreclosure** consequences while maintaining specific borrower responsibilities and outcomes.
