# Deed of Trust in Real Estate

A **Deed of Trust** functions as a **voluntary lien** on **real property** and shares similarities with **mortgages**.

## How a Deed of Trust Works

**Property owners** who borrow money enter contracts agreeing to forfeit property rights if they fail to repay debt according to **loan terms**.

## Key Difference: The Role of a Trustee

The deed of trust involves a **neutral third party** called a **trustee**. Unlike traditional mortgages where lenders directly hold liens, deed of trust arrangements operate differently:

- The trustee holds **legal title** to the **financed property** while the borrower retains **equitable title**.
- This arrangement continues until borrowers have **paid off the loan** in full.

This mechanism demonstrates the contractual arrangements that protect all parties in real estate lending.
