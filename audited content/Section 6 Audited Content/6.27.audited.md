# Understanding Interest in Mortgages and Deeds of Trust

**Interest** represents the cost of borrowing money within **mortgages** and **deeds of trust**. Lenders charge borrowers fees for money usage, whether for home purchases or real estate investments. Borrowers repay the borrowed amount (**principal**) plus additional fees calculated as percentages of **unpaid loan balances**, representing the **cost of borrowing**.

## Simple Interest Method

**Simple interest** calculates borrowing fees through a direct method:

- Interest charges base on remaining principal amounts - the unpaid loan portions.
- As borrowers repay loans and reduce principal, interest charges decrease accordingly.

This method ties borrowing costs directly to outstanding loan amounts. This concept supports loan management and real estate transaction navigation.
