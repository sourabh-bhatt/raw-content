# Understanding Home Equity Loans

**Home equity loans** can function as **first, second, or third mortgages** on properties. These loans offer either **fixed** or **adjustable interest rates** and are **secured by homeowner equity**. Home value minus outstanding mortgage balances serves as **collateral** for the loan.

## Loan Disbursement Options

Borrowers may receive:

- A **lump sum** from the lender, or
- The flexibility to **draw funds as needed** from a **line of credit**.

This flexibility makes home equity loans particularly appealing for various financial strategies, including the **consolidation of consumer debt**.

## Tax Advantages and Financial Strategies

- The **interest paid on a home equity loan may be tax-deductible if the loan is used to buy, build, or substantially improve the home that secures the loan**.
- **Interest on consumer debt such as credit cards or personal loans is not tax-deductible**.

Leveraging home equity can offer a financially advantageous way to manage or reduce debt by consolidating it under a **tax-deductible interest rate**.

These factors affect decision-making in personal and investment property finance management.
