# Understanding the Subordination Clause in Real Estate

The **subordination clause** operates within **mortgages** and **deeds of trust** frameworks.

## What is a Subordination Clause?

A subordination clause is a provision in a lease that makes the lease subordinate to (ranked below) any current or future mortgages on the property.

## Implications for the Lessee

**Lessees** who agree to subordination clauses accept that **mortgage holder** rights take priority over lease rights. This creates potential consequences:

- The lease could be terminated if the property goes into foreclosure.

## Subordination Clause Effects

This clause affects the priority of **financial obligations** on real estate properties. Lease positions relative to mortgages determine risk levels and decision-making factors.
