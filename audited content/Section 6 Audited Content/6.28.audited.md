# Understanding Mortgages

**Mortgages** involve contracts between **borrowers** (also known as **mortgagors**) and **lenders** (referred to as **mortgagees**). This agreement includes two components:

- A **promissory note**
- The **mortgage contract** itself

Through this contract, borrowers promise to repay borrowed amounts under specific terms, including **interest rates** and **payment schedules**, secured by purchased properties. This structure enables lenders to recover funds when borrowers fail to meet **repayment obligations**.
