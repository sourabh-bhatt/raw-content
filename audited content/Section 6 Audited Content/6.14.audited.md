# Purchase Money Mortgage (PMM) in Real Estate Transactions

A **Purchase Money Mortgage (PMM)** provides financing options when buyers cannot qualify for full loan amounts from traditional lenders.

## What is a Purchase Money Mortgage?

A PMM is a loan provided directly by the **seller** to the **buyer**. This arrangement allows buyers to finance purchase price portions through sellers instead of traditional lenders.

## How PMM Works

- Buyers often combine a **Purchase Money Mortgage** with a traditional bank loan to cover the total purchase price.
- This combination enables property purchases when traditional financing alone proves inadequate.

## Benefits of Using a PMM

- Helps buyers who have difficulty qualifying for full financing from traditional lenders.
- Provides sellers with an opportunity to facilitate the sale and potentially earn interest on the loan.

## PMM Applications

PMM arrangements serve transactions where buyer financing presents challenges. Real estate professionals use this option to assist clients in financing solutions.
