# Understanding Mortgages in Real Estate

**Mortgages** function as specific **loan** types secured by voluntarily placing **liens** on purchased properties when buyers lack sufficient upfront funds.

## How Mortgages Work

- **Property owners** enter contracts agreeing to borrow money.
- In return, borrowers voluntarily agree that failure to repay **debt** according to **loan agreement** terms results in property rights transferring to **lenders**.
- **Lenders** can take property ownership when borrowers cannot meet **payment obligations**.

## Mortgage Structure

**Mortgages** serve as property purchase foundations. They provide **equitable** arrangements by balancing buyer purchase needs with **lender** loan security requirements.

## Mortgage Applications

Mortgage knowledge supports real estate practice through:

- Client guidance through buying processes.
- Client decision-making support when securing purchase financing.
