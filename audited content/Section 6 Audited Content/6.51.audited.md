# Understanding the Mortgage Contract in Real Estate

A **mortgage contract** is a **security instrument** that makes **real property** act as **collateral** for the repayment of a debt.

- Example: A **promissory note** without an attached mortgage contract is simply a personal obligation with no collateral to back it up.
- Securing and recording a mortgage contract creates a **lien** on the real property.
- This lien means the property is legally tied to the debt, ensuring the lender has a **secured interest** in it until the debt is fully paid off.

The mortgage contract is a foundational element in real estate, underpinning the security of loans and facilitating the buying and selling of property.
