# Understanding Foreclosure in Real Estate

When a borrower defaults on loan payments, the result is a **foreclosure**.

## What is Foreclosure?

When someone fails to pay back their loan, the **lender** has the right to initiate a process to sell the property that was put up as **collateral** for the loan. The lender's goal is to recover the amount still owed on the loan.

## Variations in Foreclosure Process

- The rules and steps involved in a foreclosure can vary because they are determined by **state law**.
- This means the foreclosure process can look different depending on where the property is located.
- The specifics of these laws affect the **timeline** and **procedures** that must be followed during the foreclosure process.
