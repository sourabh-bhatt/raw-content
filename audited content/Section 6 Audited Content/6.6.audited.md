# Covenant of Good Repair in Mortgages and Deeds of Trust

The **Covenant of Good Repair** represents a commitment within **mortgages** and **deeds of trust** where **borrowers** agree to maintain mortgaged property in good condition.

This covenant establishes a **formal obligation** to prevent property deterioration.

## Key Obligations Under the Covenant of Good Repair

- Borrowers must **maintain the property** in good condition throughout the loan term.
- Borrowers cannot **remove or demolish structures**, including buildings or major improvements, without **prior lender approval**.

## Covenant Purpose and Benefits

- Lenders protect the **collateral value** securing the loan by preventing property deterioration from neglect or destruction.
- This commitment preserves the lending relationship.
- The covenant protects both borrower and lender **investments** in the property.

The Covenant of Good Repair preserves property value and establishes borrower responsibilities.
