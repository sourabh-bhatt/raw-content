# Exceptions to Virginia Property Disclosure Requirements

**Property sales** in Virginia include situations that do not require standard **Virginia Residential Property Disclosure Act** requirements. These exceptions apply to specific circumstances.

## Properties Exempt from Virginia Disclosure Requirements

- **New construction homes** that have never been occupied
- Properties sold at **foreclosure sales** or **tax sales**
- Properties transferred between **family members** as gifts or inheritance
- Properties sold by **court order** or through estate administration
- **Commercial properties** and properties with more than four residential units
- Properties where the seller has never occupied the property and has no actual knowledge of defects

## Disclosure Requirements for Exempt Properties

Exempt properties have modified disclosure requirements:

- The **Virginia Residential Property Disclosure Act** does not apply to these exempt properties
- However, sellers must still disclose any **known material defects** that could affect the property's value or safety
- Real estate agents must still provide **agency disclosure** forms regardless of property type
- All parties should document the exemption reason in the sales contract

These exceptions affect Virginia property transactions by establishing specific requirements and compliance procedures with Virginia real estate law.
