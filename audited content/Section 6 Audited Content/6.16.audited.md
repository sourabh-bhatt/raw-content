# Understanding Satisfaction in Mortgages and Deeds of Trust

The concept of **_satisfaction_** operates within **mortgages** and **deeds of trust (DOT)** frameworks.

## What is Satisfaction?

**Satisfaction** occurs when the borrower pays off the loan in full. This means the borrower has paid back all the money they owed. The lender then releases the lien on the property tied to the loan.

## Practical Application

Example: A client purchases a home under a **mortgage** or **deed of trust**. When you pay off the loan, satisfaction occurs.

**Satisfaction** is the process that:

- Clears the property's title
- Removes the lender's claim or interest
- Ensures the property is free and clear for sale

## Satisfaction Applications

Real estate agents use satisfaction knowledge to assist clients during buying processes. This awareness includes:

- If the property has any existing loans that need to be paid off
- When clients will own the property free and clear

This knowledge supports client guidance through purchase processes and clarifies property title ownership timing.
