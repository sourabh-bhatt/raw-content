# Power of Sale Clause in Real Estate Transactions

A **Power of Sale Clause** is an authorization typically included in a **Deed of Trust**. This clause gives **trustees** the legal right to sell financed properties when buyers fail to make loan payments.

## Title Theory State Applications

Virginia follows **title theory**. In title theory states, this clause allows lenders to sell defaulting buyer properties at **public auctions** without court orders.

## Power of Sale Clause Functions

- The Power of Sale Clause operates as a lending mechanism.
- It ensures lenders can recoup their losses on loans that have gone bad.
- This clause affects real estate transaction processes and outcomes.

Example: If a borrower defaults on their loan payments secured by a deed of trust containing a Power of Sale Clause, the trustee can sell the property at auction to recover the outstanding debt without lengthy court proceedings.
