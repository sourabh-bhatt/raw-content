# Understanding the Deed of Trust (DOT)

The **Deed of Trust (DOT)** functions as a contract among three parties:

- **Borrower (Trustor):** The person borrowing the money.
- **Lender (Beneficiary):** The party providing the loan.
- **Trustee:** A neutral third person who holds the legal title until the loan is paid off.

The Deed of Trust typically accompanies a **promissory note**. This note represents the borrower's promise to repay the loan under agreed terms and conditions.

These elements form the foundation of trust-based lending arrangements.
