# Fair Housing Laws and Down Payment Practices

## Understanding Fair Housing Compliance

**Increasing the amount needed for a down payment** with the intention of deterring a **protected class** of individuals from buying property is a violation of **fair housing laws**. These laws apply both at the **state level in Virginia** and at the **federal level**.

<!-- ## Importance of Fair Housing Laws -->

## Purpose of Fair Housing Laws

- Fair housing statutes are designed to **protect against discrimination** in housing.
- They promote **equal housing opportunities** for everyone, regardless of background.

- Compliance with these laws supports **ethical and legal** conduct in the real estate industry.
