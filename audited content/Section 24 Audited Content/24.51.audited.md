# Fair Housing Complaint Process and Determination

<!-- When it comes to **fair housing statutes**, it's crucial to understand how complaints regarding **discriminatory housing practices** are handled. -->
**Fair housing statutes** establish procedures for handling complaints regarding **discriminatory housing practices**. When someone files a complaint alleging discrimination in housing, a structured response process begins once this complaint reaches the relevant authority.

## Complaint Review Timeline

- The Virginia Fair Housing Office must take action within a strict timeframe of **30 days** from when the complaint is filed.
- During this period, the Virginia Fair Housing Office conducts a thorough analysis of the facts involved.
- This evaluation is not done in isolation; the Virginia Fair Housing Office works closely with relevant agencies to ensure the review is rigorous and fair.

## Determining Reasonable Cause

The core of this process is to decide whether there is **"reasonable cause"** to believe that a discriminatory housing practice has occurred or is likely to occur. This determination dictates whether further action is necessary.

## If No Determination is Reached Within 30 Days

- Sometimes, despite best efforts, the Virginia Fair Housing Office cannot reach a conclusive determination within the 30-day window due to complexities or other challenges.
- In such cases, both the complainant and the respondent (the party accused of discrimination) are notified in writing.
- This notification includes a detailed explanation of why a determination could not be made within the allotted time.
