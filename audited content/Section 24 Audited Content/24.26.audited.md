# Understanding Redlining and Fair Housing Laws

<!-- When learning about **fair housing statutes**, it's crucial to understand the concept of **redlining**. -->

**Fair housing statutes** include the concept of **redlining**. 

## What is Redlining?

Redlining is an illegal practice that involves denying a **mortgage loan** or **insurance policy** unfairly based on the **location of the property** rather than the **economic qualifications** of the person applying. This type of discrimination:

- Violates the **Fair Housing Act**
- Violates regulations set by the **Federal Housing Finance Agency (FHFA)**

## Legal Implications for Lenders

Lenders are prohibited from trying to reduce risk by setting **loan limits** on specific geographic areas. This means:

- Loan or policy decisions must be based on an individual's **financial ability**
- The **neighborhood** or location of the property should not influence lending decisions
