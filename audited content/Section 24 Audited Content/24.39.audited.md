# Fair Housing Violations and Their Consequences

<!-- When navigating the world of **fair housing** as part of your real estate journey, it's crucial to understand the potential consequences of violations. -->
**Fair housing** violations carry specific consequences and penalties.

## Penalties for Fair Housing Violations

<!-- If fair housing laws are not followed, the range of penalties can vary significantly, including: -->
Fair housing law violations result in various penalties, including:

- **Compensatory damages** awarded to victims for actual losses and *emotional distress* caused by the violation.
- **Injunctive relief**, where courts issue an order to rectify violations and ensure future compliance.
- Responsibility for **attorney fees** and various **legal costs**, increasing the financial burden.

## Increased Penalties for Patterns of Discriminatory Behavior

An administrative hearing that uncovers a pattern of discriminatory behavior results in more severe penalties:

- **First instance:** Civil penalties up to **$21,039**.
- **Three violations within seven years:** Penalties can increase up to **$105,194**.

## Escalated Penalties Determined by the Attorney General

If the Attorney General finds an ongoing pattern of discrimination, penalties escalate further:

- **First offense:** Civil penalties up to **$105,194**.
- **Subsequent violations:** Penalties double, reaching as high as **$210,388**.
