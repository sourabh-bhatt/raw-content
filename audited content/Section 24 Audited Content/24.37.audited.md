# Fair Housing Considerations in Housing Advertisements

<!-- When creating advertisements for housing, it's essential to be aware of certain practices that can lead to violations under **fair housing statutes**. When creating advertisements for housing, certain practices can lead to violations under **fair housing statutes**. One such practice is **discriminatory advertising**. Recognizing how this might appear in advertisements helps avoid these issues.

## Avoiding Discriminatory References

<!-- When drafting an advertisement, be cautious not to include phrases that reference specific landmarks or cultural sites, such as: -->
When drafting an advertisement, avoid including phrases that reference specific landmarks or cultural sites, such as:

- "One block past the synagogue"
- "Next door to Martin Luther King High School"

These references can subtly suggest that the property is more suitable for certain groups of people based on **racial, religious, or cultural backgrounds**, which constitutes a form of discrimination.

## Careful Use of Visual Content

Visual content in advertisements also requires careful consideration. For example, using an image that shows 10 young white adults, all holding cans of beer, to promote an apartment complex sends a particular message. Such imagery may imply that the community is exclusively youthful and perhaps unwelcoming to families or minorities.

This type of messaging can deter a diverse pool of potential renters, violating **fair housing laws** designed to ensure **equal opportunity** in housing for everyone.

## Promoting Inclusivity in Advertising

<!-- When crafting your housing advertisements, focus on inclusivity by: -->
When crafting housing advertisements, focus on inclusivity by:

- Avoiding references to specific landmarks that could imply a preference for or against certain groups.
- Choosing images that reflect a welcoming environment for all potential residents.

<!-- By following these guidelines, you contribute to promoting fairness and equality in housing opportunities. -->
Following these guidelines promotes fairness and equality in housing opportunities.
