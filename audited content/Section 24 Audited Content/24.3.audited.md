# Redlining and Fair Housing Compliance

**Redlining** is the practice of denying or limiting financial services to residents of certain areas based on race or ethnicity. For example, when lenders refuse to make loans or provide insurance in certain neighborhoods, often minority communities, this violates fair housing laws.

## Key Concepts

- **Protected Class**: Groups of people legally protected from discrimination under the Fair Housing Act.
- **Fair Housing Act**: A federal law that prohibits discrimination in housing-related transactions.
- **Discriminatory Practices**: Actions like increasing down payments to exclude certain buyers based on protected characteristics.
