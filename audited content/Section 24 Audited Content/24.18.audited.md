# Fair Housing Statutes and Their Impact on Real Estate Practices

<!-- When you're diving into the world of **fair housing statutes**, it's crucial to understand how these laws impact your interactions with potential buyers. -->

**Fair housing statutes** impact interactions with potential buyers.

## Financial Capacity vs. Discrimination

Consider this scenario:

<!-- - You are working with a customer who earns $40,000 annually.
- Choosing *not* to show them a house priced at $500,000 based on their financial capacity is **not** considered discrimination. -->

Example: When working with a customer who earns $40,000 annually, choosing *not* to show them a house priced at $500,000 based on their financial capacity is **not** considered discrimination.

This decision is grounded in financial practicality and is within the bounds of fair housing laws.

## Understanding Steering and Its Legal Implications

<!-- However, the situation changes if you decide not to show a house to a customer solely because they belong to a minority group. -->

However, the situation changes if professionals decide not to show a house to a customer solely because they belong to a minority group. This action is known as **steering**, which is a blatant violation of fair housing statutes.

- **Steering** refers to the practice of guiding potential homebuyers towards or away from certain neighborhoods based on their race, ethnicity, or another **protected characteristic**.
- Such practices undermine the essence of **equality** and **fairness**.
<!-- - Engaging in steering can subject you to serious **legal consequences**. -->
- Engaging in steering can subject professionals to **legal consequences**.
