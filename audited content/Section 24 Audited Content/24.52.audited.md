# Federal Fair Housing Act of 1968

<!-- When learning about the **Federal Fair Housing Act of 1968**, it's crucial to understand the following key points: -->
The **Federal Fair Housing Act of 1968** has several key provisions:

## Prohibited Discrimination

The Act prohibits any preferences, limitations, or discrimination based on the following protected classes:

- **Race**
- **Color**
- **Religion**
- **Sex** (which includes *gender*)
- **National origin**
- **Familial status** (families with children under 18, pregnant women)
- **Disability** (physical or mental impairments)

These protections apply when selling or renting most **residential dwellings** and **vacant land** intended for residential construction.

## Historical Note on Sex as a Protected Class

- Initially, **sex** was *not* recognized as a protected class under the Act.
- This changed in **1974**, when sex was added as a protected class through the Housing and Community Development Act.
