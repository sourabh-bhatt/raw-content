# Handling Complaints with No Reasonable Cause in Housing Discrimination

When accusations of **discrimination in housing** are examined, there are instances where the evidence is insufficient to support the claim. This situation is referred to as **"no reasonable cause"** to believe that any discriminatory act has occurred or might occur.

## Procedure Following a "No Reasonable Cause" Determination

- The authority responsible for investigating the complaint, often called the **Virginia Fair Housing Board**, will officially **dismiss the complaint**.
- This dismissal is not made lightly and must be communicated clearly.
- The Board is required to notify all parties involved about the dismissal.
- Additionally, the Board must make this information **accessible to the public** to ensure transparency.
- The notification to the involved parties must be completed **within twenty days** from the date the determination was made.
