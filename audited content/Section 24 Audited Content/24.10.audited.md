# Understanding the Fair Housing Act: Health and Safety Exemptions

<!-- When you're delving into the **Fair Housing Act**, it's crucial to understand its scope, especially regarding **health and safety**. -->

The **Fair Housing Act** includes scope provisions regarding **health and safety**.

## Exemptions Under the Fair Housing Act

- The act **does not require landlords** to rent a dwelling to an **individual with a disability** if their living there poses a **direct threat** to the **health or safety** of other people, based on an individualized assessment.
- Similarly, if an individual's occupancy could cause **significant physical damage** to other people's property, the act allows for an **exemption**.
- This exemption applies specifically to **disability-related situations** and requires a case-by-case evaluation.

## Balancing Rights and Safety

This part of the Fair Housing Act serves to balance the rights of individuals seeking housing against the need to maintain a **safe and secure environment** for all residents.
