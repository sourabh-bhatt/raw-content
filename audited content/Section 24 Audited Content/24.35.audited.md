# Fair Housing Amendments Act of 1988

<!-- When learning about the **Fair Housing Amendments Act of 1988**, it's crucial to understand that this legislation was enacted to strengthen the original **Fair Housing Act**. This was accomplished by: -->

The **Fair Housing Amendments Act of 1988** was enacted to strengthen the original **Fair Housing Act**. This was accomplished by:

- Increasing penalties for those who violate the law
- Introducing an administrative enforcement mechanism to ensure the law is followed
- Broadening protections to include individuals with **disabilities** and those with various **familial statuses**

## Key Provisions

- **Discrimination** against persons due to their disability or family composition is strictly prohibited.
