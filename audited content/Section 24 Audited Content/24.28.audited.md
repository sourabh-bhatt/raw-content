# Fair Housing Requirements: Equal Opportunity Posters and Affirmative Marketing

## Equal Opportunity Posters

<!-- As you explore the realm of **fair housing statutes**, one important aspect you'll encounter involves the display of **Equal Opportunity Posters**. You are required to showcase these posters prominently within: -->

**Fair housing statutes** require the display of **Equal Opportunity Posters**. These posters must be showcased prominently within:

- All business locations
- Model home sites
- Other relevant places

These posters affirm commitment to **equal housing opportunities**. Each poster must feature the official **Equal Opportunity slogan, statement, and logo**, serving as a visible pledge to uphold fairness and inclusivity principles in real estate practices.

## Affirmative Marketing Program for FHA-Financed Developments

<!-- When dealing with developments financed under the **Federal Housing Administration (FHA)**, you must actively participate in an **affirmative marketing program**. This program involves: -->

Developments financed under the **Federal Housing Administration (FHA)** require active participation in an **affirmative marketing program**. This program involves:

- Reaching out to a diverse cross-section of the community
- Preventing discrimination
- Promoting equal access to housing regardless of an individual's background

These efforts ensure compliance with legal obligations and enrich communities by fostering environments where everyone has equal opportunity to find housing. Affirmative efforts demonstrate commitment to fairness, equality, and respect for all individuals seeking housing.
