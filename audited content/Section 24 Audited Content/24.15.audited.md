# Fair Housing Laws and Housing for Older Persons

**Fair housing laws** include specifics regarding protection based on **familial status**, especially in relation to **housing for older persons**. This aspect is unique because these protections don't apply to certain types of housing designated for elderly individuals. Knowledge of these **exemptions** ensures compliance with fair housing laws.

## What Constitutes "Housing for Older Persons"

This can include:

- Housing provided by **state or federal programs** aimed explicitly at assisting elderly people, as defined by those programs.
- Housing intended exclusively for residents who are **62 years of age or older**.
- Complexes where at least one resident in each unit is **55 years of age or older**.

## Criteria for Exemption from Familial Status Protection

For housing to qualify as appropriate for older persons and be exempt from familial status protection, two critical criteria must be met:

1. At least **80% of the units** must have at least one person who is **55 years of age or older** living in them.
2. The **owner or manager** of the housing must have published and adhere to policies and procedures that show a clear intention to provide housing specifically for people who are **55 years of age or older**.
