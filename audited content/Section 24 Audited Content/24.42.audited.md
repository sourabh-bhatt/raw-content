# Fair Housing Laws: Federal and Virginia State Protections

<!-- When you learn about **fair housing laws**, you need to understand that **states must follow federal laws**. -->
**Fair housing laws** require that **states must follow federal laws**.

## Federal Fair Housing Laws and Protected Classes

The **Federal Fair Housing Act** protects certain **protected classes**. These include:

- **Race**
- **Color**
- **Religion**
- **National origin**
- **Sex** (including pregnancy)
- **Familial status**
- **Disability**

<!-- You must know these protected classes. They show what types of discrimination are illegal in housing. -->
These protected classes define what types of discrimination are illegal in housing.

## State-Level Extensions: The Example of Virginia

Federal laws set the minimum standards. **States can add more protections** beyond federal requirements. Virginia has expanded its fair housing laws to also protect people based on:

- **Source of income**
- **Military status**
- **Age**

## Compliance Requirements

Both federal and state fair housing laws must be followed. This avoids discrimination in housing practices and creates fair housing opportunities for everyone.
