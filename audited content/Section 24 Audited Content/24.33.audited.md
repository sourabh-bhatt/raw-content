# Roles and Powers of the Virginia Real Estate Board and Virginia Fair Housing Office

**Fair housing** involves understanding the roles and powers of both the **Virginia Real Estate Board** and the **Virginia Fair Housing Office**. These agencies monitor fair housing laws, ensuring everyone has equal access to housing opportunities without discrimination.

## Authority and Complaint Handling

The Virginia Fair Housing Office has the authority to initiate and handle complaints related to housing discrimination. The Virginia Real Estate Board handles complaints related to real estate licensing and professional conduct. For example, if someone feels they've been unfairly treated or denied housing based on discrimination, the Virginia Fair Housing Office steps in to address such concerns. They are not just passive receivers of complaints; they actively investigate any allegations of **fair housing law** violations.

## Investigation and Resolution Process

If an investigation uncovers issues, these agencies do not immediately resort to legal action. Instead, they attempt to resolve disputes through dialogue and **conciliation**, which is similar to **mediation**. The goal is to reach an agreement that satisfies all parties involved in a less confrontational way.

However, if conciliation fails, these agencies can:

- Issue a **formal charge**
- Refer the case to the **Attorney General**, who can pursue legal action

## Upholding Fair Housing Principles

Beyond complaint handling and investigations, these agencies serve as guardians of **fair housing principles**. They are empowered to take necessary actions to uphold and enforce fair housing laws effectively. This includes:

- Making and updating regulations to address evolving housing needs and challenges
