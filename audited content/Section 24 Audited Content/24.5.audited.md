# Reasonable Modifications in Fair Housing

## Understanding Reasonable Modifications

<!-- In the context of **fair housing**, understanding what constitutes **reasonable modifications** is crucial. -->

In the context of **fair housing**, **reasonable modifications** are defined by specific criteria. 

## Examples of Reasonable and Unreasonable Modifications

- If a tenant with disabilities installs **grab bars** in their apartment to ensure their safety, the landlord may ask for these grab bars to be removed when they move out. This is because removing grab bars does not significantly alter the structure of the property.

- However, it becomes unreasonable to ask a tenant to restore a widened doorway to its original width upon leaving. Widening a doorway often requires significant **structural changes**, and reverting such modifications could not only be costly but challenging, making it an unreasonable request.
