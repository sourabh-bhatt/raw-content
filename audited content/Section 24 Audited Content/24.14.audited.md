# Formal Charges and Actions on Housing Discrimination Complaints

**Note**: Housing discrimination complaints in Virginia are handled by the **Virginia Fair Housing Office** and the **U.S. Department of Housing and Urban Development (HUD)**, not the Virginia Real Estate Board. The Real Estate Board handles violations of real estate licensing laws.

## Housing Discrimination Complaint Process

If an attempt to resolve a housing discrimination complaint through **conciliation** fails, the **Virginia Fair Housing Office** or **HUD** will take action on behalf of the person who has been wronged. The agency will:

- Create a **formal charge**
- Move forward with enforcement action as needed

<!-- > It is important to note that a **formal charge** cannot be created regarding an act of **housing discrimination** once a **civil lawsuit** has been filed by the wronged party, whether that lawsuit is based on federal or state fair housing laws. -->

> Note that a **formal charge** cannot be created regarding an act of **housing discrimination** once a **civil lawsuit** has been filed by the wronged party, whether that lawsuit is based on federal or state fair housing laws.

## Process When a Formal Charge is Made

- The charge will clearly outline why the agency believes housing discrimination has occurred. This belief is based on the final report from an **investigation**.
- The charge is not limited to the original complaint. It can include findings that go beyond the original allegations.

## Notification of Parties Involved

After the charge is created, the agency must provide a copy to:

- The person or entity accused (the **respondent**)
- The individual who has been harmed and filed the complaint

This step ensures that all parties know about the allegations and the findings of the investigation. This sets the stage for further legal action if necessary.
