# Exemptions for Housing for the Elderly under Fair Housing Statutes

<!-- When you're learning about **fair housing statutes**, it's crucial to understand the specific **exemptions** that apply to **housing for the elderly**. **Fair housing statutes** include specific **exemptions** that apply to **housing for the elderly**. These exemptions affect how properties are marketed and to whom they can be rented. Two main categories of exemptions exist:

## 1. Housing Communities for Residents 62 Years or Older

- These communities **require all residents** to be **62 years of age or older**.
- These communities are **exempt from the familial status rules** in the **Fair Housing Act**.
- This means they can legally **restrict residency to older adults**, providing a living environment tailored to their needs.

## 2. Housing Communities with Residents 55 Years or Older

- In these communities, **at least one person in each unit must be 55 years or older**.
- Also, **at least 80% of the units** must have someone 55 years or older living there.
- To qualify for this exemption, communities must:
  - Follow these age rules strictly.
  - Offer **services and amenities specially designed for older residents**, such as:
    - Common dining halls
    - Bus services for transportation
    - Recreational facilities geared towards older adults
  - Give priority to people who meet the age requirements when renting units.
