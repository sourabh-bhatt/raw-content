# The Fair Housing Act

The **Fair Housing Act** ensures *equality* and *fairness* in the housing market. It covers the sale or rental of residential housing. **HUD** defines residential housing as a **dwelling**.

## Definition of Dwelling

The term *dwelling* includes:

- Buildings where people live, like houses and apartments.
- Vacant land planned for homes.

## Clarification

- **Commercial properties** are *not* covered under this definition.
