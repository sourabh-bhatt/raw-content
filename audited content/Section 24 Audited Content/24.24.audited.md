# Fair Housing Laws: Federal and State Regulations

<!-- As you dive into the realm of **fair housing**, it's crucial to understand the landscape of both **federal** and **state laws** designed to ensure everyone has equal and fair opportunities to obtain housing. These laws specifically aim to protect certain groups of people, known as **protected classes**. -->

**Fair housing** encompasses both **federal** and **state laws** designed to ensure everyone has equal and fair opportunities to obtain housing. These laws protect certain groups of people, known as **protected classes**.

## Federal Fair Housing Laws

The cornerstone of federal fair housing regulation is administered by the **Department of Housing and Urban Development (HUD)**, with enforcement power shared between HUD and the **Department of Justice**. These federal laws set the baseline standards for fair housing protections across the country.

## State Fair Housing Laws

- Most states have their own set of fair housing laws.
- These laws are generally modeled after federal guidelines but may vary.
- **State laws cannot offer lesser protections than federal laws**; they cannot undercut federal requirements.
- States can impose **more stringent requirements** or offer **broader protections** than those outlined at the federal level.
