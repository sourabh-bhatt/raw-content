**Fair housing laws** protect individuals from **discrimination** in housing-related activities based on specific **protected characteristics**. Virginia follows federal fair housing laws and adds additional state protections. Key unlawful practices under these statutes include:

## Unlawful Practices in Housing Transactions

1. **Refusal to Engage Based on Protected Characteristics**
   - It is illegal to refuse to sell or rent a property to someone because of their **race, color, national origin, religion, sex, familial status, disability, sexual orientation, gender identity, or source of funds**.
   - Decisions must be based solely on the individual's ability to meet tenancy or ownership obligations.

2. **Unequal Treatment in Terms and Conditions**
   - Offering different terms, conditions, or privileges based on characteristics such as **race, color, national origin, religion, sex, familial status, disability, sexual orientation, gender identity, or source of funds** is prohibited.
   - All potential renters or buyers must be treated equally, ensuring fairness and respect.

3. **Discriminatory Advertising**
   - Advertising that suggests a preference or restriction based on protected characteristics is forbidden.
   - Even indirect hints through certain words or symbols can be discriminatory.
   - Advertisements should focus on **property features and amenities**, not on the desirability of applicants based on personal attributes.

4. **Misrepresentation of Property Availability**
   - It is unlawful to mislead someone about the availability of a property for inspection, sale, or rent based on their protected characteristics.
   - Transparency and honesty are essential in all communications.

5. **Non-Discriminatory Membership in Real Estate Organizations**
   - Membership in real estate-related services or organizations must be open to all without discrimination.
   - Professional affiliations should reflect the diversity of the community served.

6. **Discriminatory Clauses in Housing Contracts**
   - Including clauses that discriminate or allow discrimination in housing contracts is illegal and unethical.
   - Every person's right to housing must be respected and protected in all agreements.

7. **Prohibition of Market Manipulation Tactics**
   - Actions like **blockbusting**, which manipulate housing markets based on neighborhood demographics, are illegal.
   - Such tactics prey on discriminatory fears and harm individuals and communities.

8. **Accommodations for Individuals with Disabilities**
   <!-- - It is important to allow and sometimes facilitate property modifications to ensure accessibility. -->
   - Property modifications must be allowed and sometimes facilitated to ensure accessibility.
   - Reasonable accommodations in policies and practices must be made.
   - New multifamily dwellings must include specific features to ensure accessibility for disabled persons.

These principles promote fairness and equality in housing. Fair housing laws protect everyone's right to a safe and comfortable home, free from discrimination, while ensuring lawful operation and avoiding legal repercussions.
