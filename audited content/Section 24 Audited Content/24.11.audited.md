# Occupancy Limits and Fair Housing Laws

## Scenario Overview

<!-- Imagine you're advising a family of eight who wishes to rent a one-bedroom apartment; however, there's a twist. -->

Example: When advising a family of eight who wishes to rent a one-bedroom apartment, there's a twist. The local zoning laws limit occupancy to **two individuals per bedroom**. In this scenario, the landlord has the right to refuse to rent to the family without breaching the **familial status protections** under both the **Virginia** and **Federal Fair Housing Acts**.

## Key Legal Considerations

- **Local zoning laws** set occupancy limits that can restrict the number of individuals allowed per bedroom.
- **Landlord rights** include enforcing these occupancy limits without violating fair housing protections.
- **Familial status protections** under the Virginia and Federal Fair Housing Acts do not override local occupancy regulations.
