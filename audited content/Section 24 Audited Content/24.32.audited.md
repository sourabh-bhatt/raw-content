Evaluating loan applications requires understanding legal protections provided by the **Fair Housing Act**. Key points include:

Example: When you notice the property is located in a neighborhood that has declined or "seen better days," it is **against the law** to deny a loan based solely on the property's location or the condition of the neighborhood.
