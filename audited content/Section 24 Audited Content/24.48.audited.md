# Understanding Occupancy Restrictions under the Fair Housing Act

<!-- When you're familiarizing yourself with the **Fair Housing Act**, it's crucial to understand the scope of **occupancy restrictions**. -->
The **Fair Housing Act** has specific provisions regarding **occupancy restrictions**. 

### Occupancy Standards

- The Fair Housing Act **does not prohibit states** from setting reasonable limits on how many people can live in a single dwelling.
- These **local occupancy standards** must be considered when guiding clients or managing properties.

### Balancing Compliance and Fair Housing Principles

- Compliance with local occupancy standards must be maintained while upholding the **principles and protections** offered by the Fair Housing Act.
- This balance maintains **fair and legal housing practices**.
