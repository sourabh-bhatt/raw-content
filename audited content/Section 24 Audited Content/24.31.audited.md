Example: When a private individual owns single-family homes, under certain conditions, they might be exempt from some Fair Housing Act protections. 1. **Ownership Limit**
   <!-- - You cannot own more than *three single-family houses* at one time. This limitation is crucial in determining whether the exemption applies to you. -->
   - Cannot own more than *three single-family houses* at one time. This limitation determines whether the exemption applies.

2. **Sales Limit**
   <!-- - You cannot sell more than *one single-family house* in any *two-year period* to qualify for this exemption. -->
   - Cannot sell more than *one single-family house* in any *two-year period* to qualify for this exemption.

3. **No Real Estate Agent Involvement**
   <!-- - You cannot use a *real estate agent or broker* in the transaction. The moment you decide to employ a real estate professional, this exemption no longer applies. -->
   - Cannot use a *real estate agent or broker* in the transaction. Employing a real estate professional disqualifies this exemption.

4. **Avoid Discriminatory Advertising**
   <!-- - Even if the other conditions are met, using *discriminatory advertising practices* disqualifies you from this exemption. It's essential to steer clear of any language or actions that could be interpreted as discriminatory to ensure you remain within the scope of this exemption. -->
   - Using *discriminatory advertising practices* disqualifies the exemption. Language or actions that could be interpreted as discriminatory must be avoided to remain within the scope of this exemption.
