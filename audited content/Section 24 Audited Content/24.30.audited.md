# Steering and the Fair Housing Act

<!-- In the world of **fair housing statutes**, it's important to familiarize yourself with a concept known as **_steering_**. This practice is considered an **illegal act** under the **Fair Housing Act**. -->

**Fair housing statutes** include the concept of **steering**. This practice is an **illegal act** under the **Fair Housing Act**.

## What is Steering?

**Steering** occurs when an agent:

- Discourages individuals belonging to **protected classes** from purchasing property in certain neighborhoods.
- Directs these individuals to specific properties or neighborhoods instead.

## Motive Behind Steering

The motive behind this discriminatory practice often relates to the **ethnic or cultural composition** of the neighborhood in question.
