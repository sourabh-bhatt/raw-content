# Understanding Blockbusting Under the Fair Housing Act

## What is Blockbusting?

<!-- In learning about the **Fair Housing Act**, it's crucial to understand the concept of **blockbusting**. -->

The **Fair Housing Act** includes the concept of **blockbusting**. This is considered an **illegal action** where someone attempts to persuade property owners to sell their homes by creating the fear that the neighborhood is on the verge of an undesirable change.

## Examples of Blockbusting Tactics

Common examples of blockbusting include:

- Suggesting that the arrival of minorities into a neighborhood is shifting its balance.
- Claiming that the entry of people from different nationalities is lowering the standards of the local school system.
- Insinuating that the overall safety or desirability of the area will decline due to demographic changes.

These tactics are used with the intent of scaring homeowners into selling their properties.
