# Owner-Occupant Exemption in Fair Housing Laws

Fair housing laws have specific **exemptions**, especially for **small-scale properties**. <!-- One key exemption you need to be aware of involves **owner-occupants**. -->

One key exemption involves **owner-occupants**.

## Owner-Occupant Exemption Explained

<!-- If you're dealing with someone who lives in their home but also sells or rents out rooms or units within that home, there's a particular exemption that comes into play, provided the property contains **four or fewer units**. -->

When someone lives in their home but also sells or rents out rooms or units within that home, a particular exemption applies, provided the property contains **four or fewer units**.

### Conditions for the Exemption

- The owner must actually live in one of the units as their **legal residence**.
- This rule is designed to cover smaller accommodations such as:
  - Small rooming houses
  - Individuals who rent out rooms to help cover expenses
  - Duplexes
