# Enforcing Fair Housing Laws: Legal Rights and Procedures

## Taking Legal Action
<!-- When it comes to enforcing **fair housing laws**, you have the right to take legal action on your own in **federal court** if you feel you've been wronged. -->

Enforcing **fair housing laws** includes the right to take legal action in **federal court** when violations occur. 

## Filing Deadlines
<!-- It is crucial to be mindful of the deadlines for filing a complaint: -->

Complaint filing deadlines include:

<!-- - You must file within **one year** with **HUD or the Virginia Fair Housing Office**.
- Alternatively, you have **two years** to file if you take your case directly to a **federal district court**. -->

- Filing within **one year** with **HUD or the Virginia Fair Housing Office**.
- Filing within **two years** when taking cases directly to **federal district court**.

Missing these deadlines could result in losing your right to pursue the issue legally.

## Civil Rights Act of 1866 Violations
For violations that fall under the **Civil Rights Act of 1866**, **federal court** is the only option for seeking justice. There is **no time limit** for filing under this act.

## Burden of Proof
<!-- If you decide to proceed with a lawsuit, the responsibility to prove the violation falls on you, as the **victim** or **plaintiff**. This means you will need to gather all necessary **evidence** to support your claim that your fair housing rights have been violated. -->

The responsibility to prove violations falls on the **victim** or **plaintiff**. All necessary **evidence** must be gathered to support claims that fair housing rights have been violated.
