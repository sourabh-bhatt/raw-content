# Leasing to Persons with Disabilities: Fair Housing Act Responsibilities

<!-- When leasing to persons with disabilities, you must understand the responsibilities mandated by the **Fair Housing Act**. This law prohibits discrimination. It also sets specific requirements that landlords must follow. -->

Leasing to persons with disabilities requires understanding responsibilities mandated by the **Fair Housing Act**. This law prohibits discrimination and sets specific requirements that landlords must follow.

## Reasonable Modifications

The Fair Housing Act requires landlords to allow **reasonable modifications** to the premises if these adjustments are necessary for persons with disabilities to fully enjoy the property. Examples of such modifications include:

- Installation of grab bars in bathrooms
- Ramps for wheelchair access

## Reasonable Accommodations

Landlords are also required to make **reasonable accommodations** in rules, policies, practices, or services when necessary to afford a person with disabilities the equal opportunity to use and enjoy a dwelling. Examples include:

- Making exceptions to a no-pets policy for individuals needing **service animals** or **emotional support animals**
