# Fair Housing and Religious Organizations

<!-- When learning about **fair housing statutes**, it's important to understand the rules regarding **religious organizations**, such as churches. -->
**Fair housing statutes** have specific rules regarding **religious organizations**, such as churches.

## Housing Exceptions for Religious Organizations

**Non-profit religious organizations** may offer housing exclusively to individuals who share their **religious faith**. However, this exception has clear boundaries:

- Organizations **may restrict housing based on religion**.
- Organizations **must not discriminate** against anyone based on their **race**, **color**, **sex**, **familial status**, **disability**, or **national origin**.

This distinction ensures that while religious fellowship can be fostered within housing initiatives, broader principles of **equality** and **non-discrimination** are upheld.
