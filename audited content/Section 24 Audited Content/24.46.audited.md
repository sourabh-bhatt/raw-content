# Understanding Steering and Fair Housing Laws

Example: An agent showing properties to a client who earns $40,000 annually. It's not against the rules if you choose not to introduce a $300,000 house to them, given the mismatch between their budget and the property's price.

## What Is Steering?

- **Steering** occurs when an agent decides not to show a house to a client based on their membership in a protected class.
- Steering also includes directing clients toward or away from certain neighborhoods based on protected class status.
- This practice is illegal and violates **fair housing laws** under both federal law and the Virginia Fair Housing Act.

## Fair Housing Compliance

- Steering is the primary cause of many fair housing lawsuits filed against agents.
- Decisions must be guided by **fairness and equality**, not by a client's race, ethnicity, or other protected class status.
- Violating these principles undermines the core of **fair housing statutes** and can lead to serious legal consequences.
