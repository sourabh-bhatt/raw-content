# Home Ownership Counseling and Lender Responsibilities

<!-- Understanding the intricacies of **home ownership** is crucial for you and your future clients. It's important to know that **lenders** have a responsibility to ensure that **borrowers** are fully aware of what they are getting into before agreeing to a loan. -->
**Lenders** have a responsibility to ensure that **borrowers** are fully aware of loan obligations before agreeing to a loan.

## Real Estate Settlement Procedures Act (RESPA) Requirements

The **Real Estate Settlement Procedures Act (RESPA)** mandates that lenders must provide the borrower with a list of federally-approved **home ownership counseling organizations** within three days of their **loan application**. This step provides borrowers with the resources they need to make informed decisions.

### Exceptions to the Requirement

- This disclosure requirement does **not** apply to all types of loans.
- Specifically, **reverse mortgages** and **time-share loans** are exempt from needing to disclose this information.

### Borrower's Responsibility

- Although lenders must provide the list, it is ultimately up to the borrower to decide whether to seek out and take the counseling offered.
