# Homesteading and Homestead Exemptions

## Homesteading Concepts

A **homestead exemption** is a legal protection provided by state laws. In Virginia, this protection helps homeowners protect their primary residence from certain creditors and provides property tax relief.

Virginia's homestead exemption protects against:

- **Unsecured creditors** (up to $5,000 in value)
- Provides **property tax relief** for elderly and disabled homeowners
- Protects the home as the **primary residence**

## Variations in Homestead Exemptions

Homestead exemptions vary from state to state. This variation includes:

- The amount of **acreage** covered
- The **value of homes** that the exemption encompasses
- The types of **creditor protection** offered
