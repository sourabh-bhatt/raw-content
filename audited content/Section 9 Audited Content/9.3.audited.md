# Enforcing a Lien Through Legal Proceedings

If a **borrower defaults on a loan** that's secured by a **lien** on property, enforcing that lien typically requires obtaining a **court order**. In this situation, the **creditor** must initiate **legal proceedings** and request a **court** to mandate the **sale of the property** in question.

## Court-Ordered Sale and Debt Settlement

- When a court orders that a property with **liens** should be sold to cover these **outstanding debts**, the sale proceeds are used to settle any liens against the property.
- The liens are paid off according to a predetermined **order of priority**.

This process clears all outstanding debts in a systematic and legally sanctioned manner.
