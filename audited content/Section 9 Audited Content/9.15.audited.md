# Understanding the Hierarchy of Liens in Real Estate

<!-- When you're diving into the realm of real estate, understanding the concept of **liens** and their operation by law is crucial. Let's explore how liens work, focusing on their **hierarchy**, as this knowledge helps you navigate various real estate scenarios effectively. -->
The concept of **liens** and their operation by law affects real estate practice. Liens operate according to a **hierarchy** that applies to various real estate scenarios.

## What Are Liens?

Properties may have financial claims against them, known as liens. Liens have a **pecking order** that dictates their priority. This order affects property sales or foreclosures, as it determines which debts get paid first from the sale proceeds.

## The Hierarchy of Liens
The priority of liens generally follows this order:

1. **Real Estate Property Tax Liens and Assessments**
   - These liens have the highest priority over all others, regardless of when other liens were recorded.
   - Their special status exists because property taxes fund local government services.

2. **Special Assessments**
   - These are charges for public improvements like sidewalks or street paving.
   - They rank second in priority after real estate taxes.

3. **Mortgages**
   - Mortgages are loans taken to purchase property.
   - Their priority ensures lenders can recoup their investment.

4. **Mechanic's Liens**
   - Claims made by contractors or workers who performed work on the property but have not been paid.
   - In Virginia, these liens have priority from the date work began, not when the lien was recorded.

5. **Judgment Liens and Other General Liens**
   - These liens are prioritized based on the order in which they were recorded.
   - The earlier a lien was recorded, the higher its priority.
