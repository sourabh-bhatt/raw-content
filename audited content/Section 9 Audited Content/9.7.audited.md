# Vendor's Lien in Real Estate Transactions

A **Vendor's Lien** is a specific, *equitable*, *involuntary lien* that a seller - called the **vendor** - holds against the property they have sold. This applies in situations where the entire **purchase price** has not been paid by the buyer.

## Understanding Vendor's Lien

- A **Vendor's Lien** does *not* grant the seller a direct interest in the property itself.
- Instead, it provides the seller with an *equitable right*, a kind of security or assurance.
- This right aims to protect the seller if the full amount of the purchase price remains unpaid.
