# Title Insurance: Protecting Property Title

<!-- **Title insurance** is a crucial *risk management* tool that provides you, as a *policyholder*, with limited protection against any hidden *title defects* or *encumbrances* that may surface after a real estate transaction has been completed. -->
**Title insurance** is a *risk management* tool that provides *policyholders* with limited protection against any hidden *title defects* or *encumbrances* that may surface after a real estate transaction has been completed.

## Purpose of Title Insurance

<!-- - Reimburses you for undisclosed claims or interests in the property's title. -->
- Reimburses policyholders for undisclosed claims or interests in the property's title.
- Covers issues such as *title defects*, *clouds on title*, or *encumbrances* that existed prior to the property's transfer.

## Title Insurance in Real Estate Transactions
