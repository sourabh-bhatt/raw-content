## Seller's Warranties in the Deed

The warranties the seller makes in the deed come in two main varieties:

- **General warranties**: Provide broad protection against title defects.
- **Special warranties**: Offer limited protection, covering only defects that arose during the seller's ownership.

<!-- Understanding the distinction between these warranties is key to assessing the level of protection. -->
The distinction between these warranties affects the level of protection.

## Handling Title Defects

The title clause accommodates potential issues in the process by:

- Granting the seller a **grace period** to remedy any title defects uncovered by the buyer's **title search**.
- Allowing the buyer to **terminate the contract** if these defects cannot be fixed.
