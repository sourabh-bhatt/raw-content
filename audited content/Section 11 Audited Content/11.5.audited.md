# Understanding the Real Estate Settlement Procedures Act (RESPA)

## Interaction Between Lenders, Agents, and Settlement Companies

<!-- When navigating the world of real estate, understanding the **Real Estate Settlement Procedures Act (RESPA)** is crucial. -->
The **Real Estate Settlement Procedures Act (RESPA)** affects real estate transactions.

- **Lenders** and **real estate agents** can suggest a **settlement company**.
- However, collecting a **fee** for recommending a settlement company is strictly prohibited.

This rule helps maintain fairness and transparency in the real estate process.

## Title Insurance Choices

- A **seller cannot force a buyer** to use a specific **title insurance company**.
- The buyer has the freedom to select a title insurance provider that meets their needs and preferences.

This ensures a fair and competitive market for title insurance.

## Financial Aspects: Property Taxes, Insurance Payments, and Escrow Accounts

- **Lenders** are limited in how much they can collect for **property taxes** and **insurance payments**.
- They may only collect:
  1. The portion of taxes and insurance due up to the **settlement date**.
  2. A **two-month reserve** to be deposited into an **escrow account**.

This reserve prevents the collection of excessive amounts, protecting financial interests.
