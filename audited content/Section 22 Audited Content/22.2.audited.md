# Limit on Disclosure Liability in Real Estate

## Understanding Limit on Disclosure Liability

<!-- In Virginia real estate law, an important concept to understand is the **Limit on Disclosure Liability**. This principle protects property owners from liability for wrong information that comes from outside sources. -->
The **Limit on Disclosure Liability** represents a fundamental principle in Virginia real estate law. This principle protects property owners from liability for wrong information that comes from outside sources. These sources could include:

- Public records
- Surveys
- Inspections

This protection only applies if the owner truly did not know the correct information. The owner cannot ignore facts on purpose.

## Owner's Responsibilities Regarding Disclosures

Sometimes an owner is asked about required disclosure information but does not know the answer. The owner can:

- Say they do not know the information.
- Provide an estimate of the needed information.

Any estimate must be:

- Clearly marked as an estimate.
- Reasonable.
- Based on what the owner actually knows.
