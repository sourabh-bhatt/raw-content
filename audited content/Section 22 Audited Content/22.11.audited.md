# Understanding Variances in Zoning Ordinances

<!-- When dealing with the rules of **land use**, it's essential to understand how **zoning ordinances** are applied. **Zoning ordinances** ensure uniformity and fairness under state and federal constitutions. Sometimes, strict application of these rules creates unfair hardships for property owners. This is where a **variance** comes into play.

## What is a Variance?

A **variance** is a special permission that allows for a deviation from the established **zoning laws** under specific and unique circumstances. State law sets strict criteria and processes for granting variances.

Local zoning laws may create hardship because of a property's unique attributes. In such cases, property owners might seek relief through a variance.

To be successful, property owners need to present a compelling case to the **zoning board**. They must prove two key points:

- The strict application of the zoning law imposes an **undue hardship** on the property.
- Granting the variance will not disrupt the overall **character and feel of the surrounding community**.

## Variance Applications

Variances provide relief when zoning laws create unfair situations. Navigating these scenarios carefully can help ensure that development and property use meet both individual needs and community standards.
