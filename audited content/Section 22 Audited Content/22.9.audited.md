**Spot Zoning** occurs when a small area, perhaps a single property or a few properties, is zoned differently from everything surrounding it. This typically happens when zoning laws are altered to allow a specific use of a property that doesn't conform to the zoning of the larger area.

## Understanding Spot Zoning

- **Spot Zoning** occurs when a small parcel or group of parcels is zoned differently from the surrounding properties.
- It allows a specific property use that is inconsistent with the broader zoning plan.

## Legal Challenges of Spot Zoning

Spot Zoning often encounters legal challenges because:

- It can be difficult to apply fairly.
- It may appear **arbitrary** or **discriminatory**.
- It creates exceptions for one property while nearby properties must adhere to different zoning standards.
