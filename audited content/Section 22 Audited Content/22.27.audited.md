# Real Estate Regulations for Environmentally Sensitive Land

<!-- When you work in real estate, you must understand the regulations concerning specific types of land. -->

Real estate professionals must understand regulations concerning specific types of land. Some areas have special rules because of their unique features or risks.

## Properties in High-Risk Areas

Properties located in **flood plains**, **coastal regions**, or areas prone to natural calamities such as **earthquakes** and **hurricanes** have special development rules to protect safety and the environment.

### Construction Material Guidelines

- Properties in flood plains might require **flood-proofing measures**.
- Buildings in earthquake-prone areas need to comply with **seismic building codes**.

These rules protect safety. They help buildings survive natural disasters.

### Restrictions on Construction Locations

- **Coastal shoreline** and **fault line setback restrictions** limit where construction can occur.
- These limits reduce damage from natural disasters. They also protect ecosystems and natural beauty.

### Development Prohibitions in Sensitive Environments

- In sensitive environments like **dunes** and **wetlands**, development may be entirely prohibited.
- These bans protect fragile ecosystems. These areas help with water purification and protect against natural disasters.
