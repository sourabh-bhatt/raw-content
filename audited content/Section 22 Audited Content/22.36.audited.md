# Understanding Environmental Liability in Property Development

<!-- When you work in property development, you need to understand **hazardous waste** on the property you want to buy. This is important. Property development requires understanding **hazardous waste** on properties for purchase. The **Comprehensive Environmental Response, Compensation, and Liability Act (CERCLA)** makes buyers responsible for environmental issues. One defense against liability for cleanup of contaminated property involves establishing status as an **"innocent purchaser."**

## Establishing the Innocent Purchaser Defense

<!-- To use this defense, your first step involves commissioning a **Phase I environmental study**. This study reviews existing data to assess the likelihood of contamination on the property. Using this defense requires commissioning a **Phase I environmental study**. This study reviews existing data to assess the likelihood of contamination on the property. If this study shows the presence of hazardous substances, a **Phase II study** becomes necessary. This study involves testing soil and water samples drawn from the property in question.

<!-- ## Steps to Protect Yourself from Liability -->

## Steps to Protect from Liability

- **Phase I Environmental Study:** Review of existing data to identify potential contamination risks.
- **Phase II Environmental Study:** If Phase I indicates risk, conduct physical testing of soil and water samples.

<!-- If both the Phase I and Phase II studies indicate that the property is free from contamination, you can claim the **"innocent purchaser"** defense. If both the Phase I and Phase II studies indicate that the property is free from contamination, buyers can claim the **"innocent purchaser"** defense. This claim could protect from liability for cleanup costs should the **Environmental Protection Agency (EPA)** discover hazardous waste on the property after purchase.
