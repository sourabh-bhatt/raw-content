# Disclosure Requirements for Properties in Tourism Activity Zones

<!-- When dealing with properties located in certain areas, particularly those within a **designated tourism activity zone**, there are specific legal obligations you must be aware of under Virginia law. -->

Properties located in certain areas, particularly those within a **designated tourism activity zone**, have specific legal obligations under Virginia law.

## Seller's Disclosure Obligations

<!-- If you own a **residential property** that is entirely or partially situated in a tourism activity zone, you are required to: -->

Owners of **residential property** that is entirely or partially situated in a tourism activity zone are required to:

- **Inform the buyer in writing** that the property is located within such a zone.
- Clearly explain what this designation means for the buyer as a potential homeowner.

## Key Information to Disclose

<!-- Your disclosure must cover the various impacts the buyer might experience due to the property's location, including: -->

The disclosure must cover the various impacts buyers might experience due to the property's location, including:

- The excitement and potential disruption caused by **special events and parades**.
- Possible **temporary street closures** that could affect daily commutes.
- The presence of vibrant **indoor and outdoor entertainment activities** in the area.

## Disclosure Requirements

<!-- Providing this information ensures buyers are fully informed about the unique characteristics of living in a tourism-driven area, allowing them to make well-rounded decisions regarding their potential new home. -->

Providing this information ensures buyers are fully informed about the unique characteristics of living in a tourism-driven area, allowing them to make informed decisions regarding their potential new home.
