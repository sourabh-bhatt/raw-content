# Waivers and Operating Permits for Onsite Sewage Systems in Property Transactions

<!-- When dealing with property transactions involving **onsite sewage systems**, you must understand **waivers** and their effects. -->

Property transactions involving **onsite sewage systems** require understanding **waivers** and their effects.

## Non-Transferrable Waivers

Waivers for **operating permits** for onsite sewage systems are **non-transferrable**. This means:

- When the property changes hands through sale or transfer, any waiver granted becomes **null and void**.

## Disclosure Requirements

The property owner must provide **full disclosure** using a specific form issued by the **Virginia Department of Professional and Occupational Regulation (DPOR)**. On this form, the owner must clearly state that:

- Once the property is sold or transferred, the operating permit is no longer valid if it was subject to a waiver.

## Regulatory Requirements by the Local Health Department

Before reinstating an operating permit, the **local health department** may require:

- Added treatment systems
- Pressure dosing systems
