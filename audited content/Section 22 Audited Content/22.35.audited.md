# Disclosure Requirements and "As Is" Sales in Virginia Residential Property Transactions

In Virginia, selling **residential property** provides two primary options for handling property condition information:

1. **Property Disclosure:** Complete Virginia's Property Disclosure Act form.
2. **"As Is" Sale:** Sell the property "as is" by providing the proper disclaimer to buyers.

## Virginia's Property Disclosure Act

- Virginia law requires sellers to either disclose or sell "as is" for most residential properties.
- The **Virginia Residential Property Disclosure Act** applies to sales of 1-4 family residential properties.
- Sellers must use Virginia's standardized disclosure form when choosing to disclose.
- These requirements apply whether or not a real estate agent is involved.

### Required Disclosure Areas Include:

- **Water and sewer systems**
- **Insulation condition**
- **Roofing condition** 
- **Plumbing systems**
- **Electrical systems**
- **HVAC systems**
- **Structural problems**
- **Land use restrictions**
- **Pest problems** like termites
- **Environmental hazards** like radon, asbestos, or lead-based paint

## Selling Property "As Is" in Virginia

- Selling "as is" means sellers will not make repairs or warranties about the property's condition.
- Sellers must provide buyers with Virginia's statutory "as is" disclaimer.
- This does **not** excuse sellers from disclosing known material defects required by other laws.
- Buyers assume responsibility for the property's condition after closing.

## Virginia Exemptions

Virginia's disclosure requirements do **not** apply to:
- New construction sold by builders
- Court-ordered sales (foreclosures, estate sales)
- Sales between family members
- Sales where buyer had right to inspect for 15 days or more

## Legal Protection

- Following Virginia's disclosure laws protects sellers from future liability.
- Proper disclosure or "as is" sale promotes fair dealing.
