**Client liability** under **Virginia law** affects real estate professionals navigating regulatory landscapes. The law defines the relationship between **clients** and **licensees**, especially when **misconduct** or **misrepresentation** occurs.

## General Rule About Client Responsibility

Virginia law includes a general rule:

- Clients are generally **not aware** of any wrongdoings committed by their licensees.
- These wrongdoings can include **negligent acts**, **intentional misconduct**, or **misrepresentation**.
- Therefore, clients are typically **not held accountable** for the missteps of their licensees, provided certain conditions are met.

## When the Rule Can Change

This rule can change in certain situations:

- If evidence shows that the client **knew**, or **reasonably should have known**, about the licensee's misconduct.
- If the client **took no action** to address the misconduct despite this knowledge.
