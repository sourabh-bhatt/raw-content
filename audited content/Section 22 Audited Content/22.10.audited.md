# Building Code Enforcement in Construction

## Role of Local Government
Local governments enforce building codes primarily through two mechanisms:

- **Building permits**
- **Inspections**

## Building Permits
Before construction begins, a **building permit** must be obtained. This permit acts as the local government's approval to start construction, ensuring that the planned building complies with all relevant local codes and regulations.

## Inspections During Construction
Obtaining the permit is only the first step. Throughout the construction process, local government inspectors conduct scheduled inspections at various stages. <!-- These inspections are critical to verify that the construction adheres to the building codes at every phase. -->
These inspections verify that the construction adheres to the building codes at every phase.

## Certificate of Occupancy
Once construction is complete, the building cannot be occupied immediately. A local official must issue a **certificate of occupancy**, which confirms that the project meets all applicable building codes following a final inspection. This certificate is mandatory before the building can be officially occupied.
