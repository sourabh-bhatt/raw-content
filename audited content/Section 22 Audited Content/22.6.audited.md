# Understanding Cooling Off Periods in Real Estate Transactions

**State and Federal laws**, particularly **condominium acts** and the **Truth in Lending Act (TILA)**, provide **buyers with specific rights in certain types of transactions**. However, **standard real estate purchase contracts do not have cooling off periods**. Cooling off periods apply only to specific transaction types.

## When Cooling Off Periods Apply

**Federal law provides cooling off periods for:**
- **Timeshare purchases** - 7 days to cancel
- **Door-to-door sales** - 3 days to cancel
- **Some loan transactions** under TILA

**Virginia condominium purchases provide:**
- Time to review disclosure documents
- Right to cancel based on document review
- This is different from a general cooling off period

## Facts About Real Estate Contracts

- **Standard real estate purchase contracts in Virginia do not have cooling off periods**
- Buyers cannot cancel for any reason without consequences
- Buyers can only cancel based on specific contract contingencies
- Common contingencies include financing, inspection, and appraisal
