# On-Site Sewage System Waivers Under Virginia Law

## Waivers for Sewage System Upgrades
<!-- When dealing with properties that have an **on-site sewage system**, Virginia law provides an important provision regarding system upgrades. For properties that have an **on-site sewage system**, Virginia law provides a provision regarding system upgrades. If the sewage system is not functioning properly and requires more advanced treatment than it is currently designed for, or if there is a new need for **pressure dosing**, the homeowner can request a special permission known as a **waiver** from these upgrade requirements.

## Recording and Limitations of the Waiver
- Once granted, the **waiver** is officially recorded in the **property records**.
- The waiver is tied specifically to the **current owner** of the property.
- If the property is sold or transferred, the waiver becomes **invalid**.

## Implications for New Owners
- The **new owner** is responsible for complying with the additional treatment or pressure dosing requirements that were previously waived.
