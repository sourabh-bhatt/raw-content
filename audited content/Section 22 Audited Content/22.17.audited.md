# Disclosure Requirements for Defective Drywall in Virginia Property Transactions

<!-- When you're dealing with property transactions in Virginia, there's an important disclosure you need to be aware of, especially concerning **defective drywall**. Virginia property transactions require specific disclosure concerning **defective drywall**. If a seller knows that the property contains defective drywall, they **must inform the prospective buyer in writing**.

## What Qualifies as "Defective Drywall"?

There are two main criteria:

- **Substantial Product Hazard:** If the **US Consumer Product Safety Commission** has marked the drywall as a substantial product hazard, it is considered defective.
- **Elemental Sulfur Content:** If the drywall contains more than **10 parts per million of elemental sulfur**, it qualifies as defective. This is particularly relevant for some drywall manufactured in China and imported into the United States between 2004 and 2007.
