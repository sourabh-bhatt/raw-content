# Police Power in Land Use Regulation

Police power is the government's authority to make laws for legitimate land use goals. These laws must serve the public's *health*, *safety*, and *general welfare*. The foundation of police power is rooted in the **United States Constitution**, which grants this power to individual states.

## Delegation of Authority

- States delegate police power authority to **counties** and **municipalities**.
- This delegation allows for more localized control over land use.

## Police Power as a Regulatory Umbrella

Police power acts as an umbrella term encompassing various public controls through which governments regulate land use. These controls ensure the well-being of the community by:

- Regulating how land is developed
- Managing land use
- Influencing real estate operations
