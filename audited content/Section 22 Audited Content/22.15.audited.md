# Zoning Administration and Due Process

## Understanding Due Process in Zoning

**Zoning administration** includes the concept of **due process**. Most communities have established **boards of zoning appeals**, which serve property owners.

## Role of Boards of Zoning Appeals

- These boards serve as the first line of defense for property owners who disagree with **local zoning ordinances**.
- They provide a structured avenue for airing grievances and seeking resolutions.
