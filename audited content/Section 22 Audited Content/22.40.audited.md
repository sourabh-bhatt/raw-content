# Understanding the Role of Local Building Departments in Construction Projects

<!-- When you're considering any construction project, one of the first steps you must take involves understanding the role of **local building departments**. These entities are fundamental in enforcing **zoning regulations**. -->

Construction projects require understanding the role of **local building departments**. These entities enforce **zoning regulations**.

## Building Permits

<!-- Before you can proceed with specific construction activities, you must obtain a **building permit**. This permit serves as official approval, confirming that your proposed structure aligns with both the **permitted zoning** for the area and **local building codes**. -->

Specific construction activities require obtaining a **building permit**. This permit serves as official approval, confirming that proposed structures align with both the **permitted zoning** for the area and **local building codes**.

## Consequences of Non-Compliance

<!-- - If your proposed structure does not align with these regulations, the building permit will be denied.
- This denial effectively halts your development plans. -->

- If proposed structures do not align with these regulations, the building permit will be denied.
- This denial effectively halts development plans.
- Proceeding without securing a building permit is not just a minor oversight; it constitutes a violation of most **zoning ordinances**.
