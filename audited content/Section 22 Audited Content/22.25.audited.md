# Virginia Time Share Properties

**Time share properties** in Virginia allow multiple individuals to own a single property together, granting each person the right to use the property during designated time intervals. This arrangement typically covers five or more time periods spread out over at least five years.

## Developing a Time Share Property

<!-- If you're considering developing a time share property, there are specific steps to follow: -->

Developing a time share property requires specific steps:

- **Register with the Virginia Real Estate Board** by submitting an application.
- Provide a **Public Offering Statement**.
- Record the **time share instruments** at a local level. These instruments outline:
  - Property details
  - Structure and duration of time share periods
  - Common expenses
  - Management plans
- **Establish an association** for managing the property before registration.

## Converting Existing Structures to Time Share Ownership

For those interested in converting existing structures into time share ownership, similar protocols apply with additional tenant considerations:

- Provide a **90-day notice** to current tenants informing them of the conversion.
- Offer tenants an **exclusive purchase option** valid for 60 days following the notice.
- Tenants renting on a month-to-month basis must receive at least **120 days' notice** to vacate if they decline to purchase.

## Selling Time Share Units

### Initial Sales (New Constructions)

- A **Public Offering Statement** is mandatory.
- Contracts must inform buyers of their **right to rescind** the agreement within **10 days** after ratification.
- Sales must adhere to marketing standards, avoiding misleading statements or promises.

### Resales

- The current unit owner must provide potential buyers with a **resale certificate** issued by the association, which may require a preparation fee.
- The resale certificate should include:
  - Financial information
  - Existing judgments or liens
  - A **Buyer's Acknowledgment Form**
- Buyers have a **right of rescission** extending **10 days** after receiving all necessary information.

## Governance and Ownership Changes

- The **declarant** retains control of the time share until **75% of the units are sold**.
- Afterward, a **time share association** formed by unit owners assumes management responsibilities.
- Owners can change the nature of ownership by a vote requiring a **67% majority**.

## Legal Considerations

- Any legal actions related to inaccuracies in the project must be initiated within **two years** from the contract date.
