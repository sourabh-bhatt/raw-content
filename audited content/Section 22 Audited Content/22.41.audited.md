# Disclosures Related to Building and Zoning Violations in Virginia

<!-- When dealing with properties in Virginia, it is crucial to understand the requirements concerning **disclosures related to building or zoning violations**. -->

Virginia properties require understanding the requirements concerning **disclosures related to building or zoning violations**. These disclosures ensure the integrity of the transaction and adherence to legal requirements.

## Building Code Violations

- Any ongoing enforcement actions highlighting violations against the **Uniform Statewide Building Code** must be openly disclosed.
- These violations are serious because they affect the **safe, decent, and sanitary living conditions** of the property.
- Any condition that compromises these living standards must be transparently communicated to potential buyers.

## Zoning Violations

- Any ongoing local **zoning ordinance violations** that have not been resolved in a timely manner must also be disclosed.

## Disclosure Process

- The Virginia Real Estate Board (**VREB**) requires disclosure of these violations, though it does not mandate a specific standardized form for building and zoning violations.
- Sellers must disclose these violations through appropriate disclosure documents or statements.
