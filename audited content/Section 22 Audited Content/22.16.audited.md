# Building Codes in Construction

<!-- When learning about **building codes**, understand that you're delving into a critical aspect of regulatory statutes. Building codes serve as a legitimate way for the state to exercise its **police power**. -->

**Building codes** represent a fundamental aspect of regulatory statutes. Building codes serve as a legitimate way for the state to exercise its **police power**. 

## Purpose of Building Codes

<!-- Essentially, these codes are put in place to safeguard **public health and safety**. Their primary role is to set forth the basic standards that everyone must adhere to in construction projects. These standards cover a variety of important areas including, but not limited to: -->

These codes safeguard **public health and safety**. Their primary role is to set forth basic standards that everyone must adhere to in construction projects. These standards cover various areas including:

- The quality of **building materials**
- The specifics of **electrical wiring** setups
- **Plumbing requirements**
- The **load limits** that structures must be able to support
