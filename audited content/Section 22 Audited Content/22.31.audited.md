# Methamphetamine Disclosure Requirements in Virginia Real Estate Transactions

<!-- When dealing with property transactions in Virginia, it is crucial to understand the **methamphetamine disclosure** requirements. -->

Virginia property transactions require understanding of **methamphetamine disclosure** requirements.

## Disclosure Obligation for Property Owners

If a residential property was previously used for methamphetamine production, the following applies:

- The owner **must disclose** this information to any potential buyer.
- Disclosure is required **unless the property has been cleaned** according to the **Department of Health's guidelines**.
- This disclosure must be provided through a **written notice**.
- The Virginia Real Estate Board provides a **designated form** specifically for this purpose to ensure uniformity and clarity.

## Purpose of the Disclosure Requirement

This requirement exists for two primary reasons:

1. **Safety:** Residual contamination from methamphetamine production can pose serious health risks to occupants.
2. **Transparency:** It ensures buyers are fully informed about the property's history, promoting ethical and lawful real estate practices.

Complying with these disclosure requirements applies to all parties involved in Virginia real estate transactions.
