# Understanding Do Not Call Laws in Real Estate Marketing

<!-- When marketing real estate services in Virginia, you must follow **"Do Not Call" laws**. These laws protect consumers from unwanted phone calls. Real estate agents must understand these rules to avoid legal problems and protect their license. -->

Marketing real estate services in Virginia requires following **"Do Not Call" laws**. These laws protect consumers from unwanted phone calls. Real estate agents must understand these rules to avoid legal problems and protect their license.

## Key Federal Rules to Know

There are two main federal rules for Do Not Call regulations:

### 1. Entity-Specific Do Not Call Rules
<!-- - These rules apply to your real estate brokerage.

- These rules apply to real estate brokerages.
- Brokerages must keep their own list of people who asked not to be called.
- If someone asks to be on the Do Not Call list, brokerages must honor that request.
- Brokerages cannot call these people for sales or promotions.

### 2. The National Do Not Call Registry
- This is a nationwide list.
- People can register their phone numbers to avoid unwanted calls.
<!-- - You must check this registry regularly.
- You cannot call people on this list for promotional purposes. -->

- Real estate professionals must check this registry regularly.
- Real estate professionals cannot call people on this list for promotional purposes.

## Virginia Real Estate Requirements

As a Virginia real estate licensee, you must:
- Follow both federal Do Not Call laws and Virginia Real Estate Board regulations
- Understand that violations can result in license discipline
- Maintain compliance as part of your professional responsibilities
