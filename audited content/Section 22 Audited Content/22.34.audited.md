# Understanding CERCLA and Environmental Liability in Commercial Real Estate

<!-- When dealing with **commercial properties contaminated by hazardous waste**, it is essential to understand a key piece of federal legislation: **CERCLA**, also known as the **"Superfund."** This law places important obligations related to **mitigation**, **abatement**, and **cleanup** on those identified as responsible for the contamination. -->

**Commercial properties contaminated by hazardous waste** require understanding of federal legislation: **CERCLA**, also known as the **"Superfund."** This law places obligations related to **mitigation**, **abatement**, and **cleanup** on those identified as responsible for the contamination.

## Liability Under CERCLA

- **Responsible parties**: Not only the parties that caused the contamination are held accountable.
- **Property owners**: Owners themselves can also be targeted for cleanup costs, even if they did not cause the contamination.

## Role of the Environmental Protection Agency (EPA)

- The **EPA** is authorized to:
  - Step in and clean up hazardous waste sites.
  - Bill property owners for the cleanup efforts.

## Financial Implications

- Cleanup costs can be extremely high, sometimes reaching up to **$25 million**.
