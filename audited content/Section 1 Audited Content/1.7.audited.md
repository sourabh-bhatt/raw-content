# Understanding Equity in Real Estate

## What is Equity?

When someone owns a property, the **equity** is essentially the cash value of this property once all debts tied to it are subtracted, including any **mortgage** that might be outstanding. Equity serves as a real-time measurement of the **financial stake** in the property.

## How Equity Grows

Equity increases when:

- More mortgage payments are made, thereby reducing the debt.
- The property's value appreciates over time.

## Why Equity Matters

This concept is important because it reflects the actual **ownership value** in the property. This figure can influence future financial decisions, such as:

- Selling the property.
- Leveraging the equity for **loans** or other **investments**.
