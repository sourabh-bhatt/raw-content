# Understanding Industrial Real Estate

## What Are Industrial Properties?
Industrial properties serve as the powerhouse behind the scenes of the economy. These properties are not just about manufacturing and warehouses; they encompass a broad range of facilities including:
- Mines
- Plants
- Utilities
- Vacant land ripe for industrial development

**Examples include:**

- **Warehouses** equipped with loading docks
- **Manufacturing buildings** designed to house overhead cranes due to their substantial height
- **Office showrooms** that blend spacious warehouse features with a small portion dedicated to office space

## Specialized Industrial Buildings

Industrial properties also include specialized buildings designed for **research and development**, containing laboratory spaces. These are particularly interesting because they blend the industrial aspect with the high-tech needs of research disciplines.

## Environmental Considerations

Industrial property professionals need to be aware of an important layer of complexity: **environmental laws**. Industrial properties can be directly linked to environmental hazards due to the nature of activities performed on them. Being familiar with laws governing hazardous waste, such as **CERCLA** (Comprehensive Environmental Response, Compensation, and Liability Act) - commonly referred to as the **Superfund** - is essential.

This act, along with other state and federal environmental laws, plays a critical role in the operations and transactions involving industrial properties.
