# The Role of Commercial Banks in Real Estate

## Commercial Banks as Support for Commerce

- **Commercial banks** are the backbone of support for commerce-related activities.
- They provide **loans** that fuel business ventures, home improvements, and short-term construction projects, known as **interim financing**.
- The primary source of funds for these loans comes from **demand deposits**, which are the checking accounts that individuals and businesses maintain with the banks.
- This source of funding is key because it influences the bank's **liquidity** and approach to lending.

## Liquidity and Lending Practices

- Compared to **Savings and Loan associations (S&Ls)**, commercial banks need to maintain a higher level of liquidity.
- This is because their loan portfolios are rich in **short-term loans** expected to yield high returns.

## Involvement in the Residential Real Estate Market

- Commercial banks play a multi-faceted role in the residential real estate market.
- One key activity is participating in the **secondary market**:
  - They sell the home loans they've originated.
  - They also purchase packages of loans at a discount.
- This movement of loans is critical to ensuring liquidity and keeping the **mortgage market** active.

## Commercial Banks and Large-Scale Real Estate Developments

- Larger commercial banks often operate through **bank holding companies** and **trust departments**.
- These arms engage directly in financing and owning significant real estate developments such as:
  - Shopping centers
  - Large apartment complexes
  - Resort properties
- Their involvement highlights the importance of commercial banks in shaping both **residential** and **commercial real estate** markets.
