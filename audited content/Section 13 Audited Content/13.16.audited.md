# Understanding Assessed Value in Real Estate

<!-- In real estate, understanding the concept of **Assessed Value** is crucial. -->
**Assessed Value** represents a key concept in real estate.

## What is Assessed Value?

<!-- - **Assessed Value** is the value that your local government assigns to a property. -->
- **Assessed Value** is the value that local government assigns to a property.
- It determines the **property taxes** that will be owed.

## Relationship Between Assessed Value and Market Value

- Assessed values are typically a percentage of the property's **market value**.
- This means assessed values are generally lower than what the property might sell for on the open market.
