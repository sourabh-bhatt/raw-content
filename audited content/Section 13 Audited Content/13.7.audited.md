**Usury laws** are state laws designed to prevent excessively high interest rates from being charged. These laws set a maximum limit on the **interest rates** that can be applied to **loans**.

## Virginia Usury Laws

In Virginia, usury laws are found in **Virginia Code Â§ 6.2-303**. Virginia sets specific limits on interest rates:

- **General maximum rate**: 12% per year
- **Small loans under $2,500**: Up to 36% per year
- **Certain licensed lenders**: May charge higher rates under specific regulations
