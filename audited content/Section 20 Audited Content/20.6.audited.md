# Calculating Price Per Square Foot in Real Estate

## Understanding Price Per Square Foot

<!-- When calculating the **price per square foot**, you're taking the **sales price** and dividing it by the **square footage** of the living space, also referred to as the **Gross Living Area**. -->

When calculating the **price per square foot**, divide the **sales price** by the **square footage** of the living space, also referred to as the **Gross Living Area**.

## Example Calculation

<!-- Imagine a house with 2,000 square feet of space sells for $200,000. To find the price per square foot: -->

Example: A house with 2,000 square feet of space sells for $200,000. To find the price per square foot:

- Divide $200,000 by 2,000 sq. ft.
<!-- - This gives you **$100 per square foot**. -->
- This equals **$100 per square foot**.

## Additional Considerations

<!-- While the price per square foot provides a rough estimate of the property's value, it's important to understand that this figure alone does not capture the full picture. Several factors can influence the valuation beyond just square footage: -->

While the price per square foot provides a rough estimate of the property's value, this figure alone does not capture the full picture. Several factors can influence the valuation beyond just square footage:

- Presence of **detached buildings**, **garages**, or a **swimming pool**
- Use of **superior construction materials**
- Additional **amenities** such as high-end appliances and upgrades

<!-- These elements can significantly affect the final value of the property. Therefore, while the price per square foot is a helpful starting point, always consider the entire property and its unique features when determining value. -->

These elements can affect the final value of the property. While the price per square foot is a helpful starting point, appraisers should consider the entire property and its unique features when determining value.
