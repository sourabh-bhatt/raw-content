# Functional Obsolescence in Real Estate

<!-- **Functional obsolescence** occurs when a property or improvement devalues due to its inability to serve its intended purpose effectively. **Functional obsolescence** occurs when a property or improvement devalues due to its inability to serve its intended purpose effectively. This situation can arise from outdated design elements or because the property has aged. Functional obsolescence directly impacts a property's **market value** and attractiveness.

## Causes of Functional Obsolescence

- **Outdated design elements:** Example: A house designed decades ago may have a layout that no longer meets modern living standards, such as:
  - Insufficient number of bathrooms
  - A kitchen isolated from the rest of the living space

- **Aging property components:** Essential parts of the property may become outdated or less efficient over time, reducing the property's appeal and utility.

## Role in Property Evaluation

Recognizing functional obsolescence supports property evaluation of current and future potential. By identifying these factors, appraisers can:

- Better advise on the property's **marketability**
- Recommend potential **upgrades** to enhance its value

Example: When assessing a home with an outdated floor plan, suggesting renovations like adding bathrooms or opening the kitchen to the living area can help restore or increase the property's value.
