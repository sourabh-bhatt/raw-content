# Understanding the Principle of Conformity in Property Appraisal

<!-- When you're appraising both **residential** and **income-producing properties**, it's essential to understand the concept of **conformity**. Let's dive into what this means for you. -->

When appraising both **residential** and **income-producing properties**, understanding the concept of **conformity** is necessary.

## What is Conformity?

<!-- Imagine you're evaluating a home that blends well with its surroundings â€“ similar in style and construction to its neighbors. This harmony often translates into a higher value for the property compared to a home that sticks out because of its dissimilar style and construction. -->

Example: A home that blends well with its surroundings - similar in style and construction to its neighbors - often has higher value than a home that stands out due to dissimilar style and construction.

- Properties that **conform** to the neighborhood's general aesthetic and architectural standards are more appealing to a broader base of buyers.

## Why Conformity Matters in Appraisal

<!-- Understanding and applying the principle of conformity can significantly influence your appraisals, ensuring that they accurately reflect the property's appeal and, by extension, its **market value**. -->

The principle of conformity influences appraisals by ensuring they accurately reflect the property's appeal and **market value**.
