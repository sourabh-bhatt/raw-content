# Comparative Market Analysis (CMA) in Real Estate

**Comparative Market Analysis (CMA)** is a tool real estate agents use when estimating the market value of properties. Unlike a formal appraisal, real estate agents can conduct a CMA to assist sellers in setting a listing price or to help buyers understand what they might offer on a property.

The CMA process is based on the **principle of substitution**. This principle asserts that a buyer won't pay more for a property than they would for a similar, substitute property.

## Distinguishing CMA from Other Valuations

<!-- It's helpful to know the distinction between a CMA, a Broker Opinion of Value (BOV) or Broker Price Opinion (BPO), and a formal appraisal: -->

The distinction between a CMA, a Broker Opinion of Value (BOV) or Broker Price Opinion (BPO), and a formal appraisal:

- **CMA**: Typically performed by real estate agents to aid sellers in pricing their property or to guide buyers in deciding on an offer price. It is an informal estimate of market value based on comparable sales.

- **BOV or BPO**: Often requested by lenders, especially in situations like a short sale. Similar to a CMA, it is an informal valuation but specifically carried out for the lender's benefit rather than a client's.

- **Appraisal**: A formal and detailed valuation conducted by a licensed professional. This process is more thorough and is typically required by lenders before approving a loan.
