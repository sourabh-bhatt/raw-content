# Understanding Contribution in Property Appraisal

<!-- When appraising both **residential** and **income-producing properties**, it's important to understand the concept of **contribution**. -->

When appraising both **residential** and **income-producing properties**, appraisers must understand the concept of **contribution**.

## What is Contribution?

<!-- **Contribution** refers to the value that an **improvement** adds to the overall **market value** of a property. However, it's important to remember that not every improvement will increase a property's value by the cost of the improvement itself. This is because the value of an improvement is determined by what it actually contributes to the property's worth in the eyes of potential buyers. -->

**Contribution** refers to the value that an **improvement** adds to the overall **market value** of a property. Not every improvement will increase a property's value by the cost of the improvement itself. The value of an improvement is determined by what it actually contributes to the property's worth in the eyes of potential buyers.

## Evaluating Property Improvements

<!-- As you assess a property, consider the following: -->

When assessing a property, consider the following:

- Which enhancements are likely to increase the property's **market attractiveness**?
- How will these improvements affect the property's overall **value**?

Examples of common improvements include:

- Adding a new bathroom
- Renovating the kitchen
- Installing **energy-efficient windows**

<!-- The key takeaway is to recognize that the actual increase in market value may not always align with the cost of the improvement. Your goal should be to identify improvements that offer the most significant **contribution** to the property's overall value. -->

The actual increase in market value may not always align with the cost of the improvement. The goal is to identify improvements that offer the most significant **contribution** to the property's overall value.

<!-- By doing so, you can make informed decisions and provide accurate recommendations to clients considering property improvements. -->

This approach supports informed decisions and accurate recommendations to clients considering property improvements.
