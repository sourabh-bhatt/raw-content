# Regression and Progression in Property Valuation

<!-- When you're assessing the value of **residential** and **income-producing properties**, it's crucial to understand how the quality of neighboring properties impacts their value. This is where the concepts of **regression** and **progression** come into play. Let's dive into these concepts. -->

When assessing the value of **residential** and **income-producing properties**, understanding how the quality of neighboring properties impacts their value is necessary. The concepts of **regression** and **progression** apply here.

## Regression

<!-- Regression happens when you're looking at a very **luxurious house** that's situated in a modest neighborhood. Because it's surrounded by homes that are less expensive and perhaps not as well-appointed, the value of this luxurious house tends to decrease. It's like a shining star dimming its light to match the surrounding stars. The principle here is that the high-end features and amenities of the luxurious house won't be as valued because they're not common in the area. Its value will be pulled down towards the more modest range of values in that neighborhood. -->

Regression happens when a **luxurious house** is situated in a modest neighborhood. Because it's surrounded by homes that are less expensive and perhaps not as well-appointed, the value of this luxurious house tends to decrease. The high-end features and amenities of the luxurious house won't be as valued because they're not common in the area. Its value will be pulled down towards the more modest range of values in that neighborhood.

## Progression

<!-- On the flip side, **progression** is at work when a modest home finds itself in a neighborhood filled with more expensive homes. Here, the modest home's value receives a boost. It's akin to a small plant growing taller and healthier in a thriving garden. Despite the home itself not having the high-end features of its neighbors, its value progresses upwards. The reason? Being in a coveted area surrounded by luxurious homes makes it more desirable, and as a result, its value is elevated towards the higher end of the spectrum. -->

**Progression** occurs when a modest home is located in a neighborhood filled with more expensive homes. The modest home's value receives a boost. Despite the home itself not having the high-end features of its neighbors, its value progresses upwards. Being in a coveted area surrounded by luxurious homes makes it more desirable, and its value is elevated towards the higher end of the spectrum.

## Property Evaluation Considerations

<!-- For you, understanding these principles is key when evaluating properties. Not only do the characteristics of the property itself matter, but also the context of its location and the qualities of the neighboring properties. This understanding will enable you to make more accurate appraisals and serve your clients effectively. -->

Understanding these principles applies when evaluating properties. The characteristics of the property itself matter, as well as the context of its location and the qualities of the neighboring properties. This understanding enables more accurate appraisals and effective client service.
