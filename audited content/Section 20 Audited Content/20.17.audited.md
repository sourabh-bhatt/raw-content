# Understanding Appraised Value in Real Estate

<!-- When you're learning about appraising **residential** and **income-producing properties**, it's crucial to understand the concept of **appraised value**. Think of it as an educated guess made by an **appraiser** on the **market value** of a property. -->

When learning about appraising **residential** and **income-producing properties**, understanding the concept of **appraised value** is necessary. **Appraised value** represents an educated estimation made by an **appraiser** on the **market value** of a property.

This is not just any guess, though. Appraisers use **analytical procedures** to come up with this number. They'll look at a variety of factors affecting the property's value to ensure their estimation is as accurate as possible.
