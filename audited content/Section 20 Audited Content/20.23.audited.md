In real estate, understanding how to evaluate the worth of properties is necessary, especially for income-generating properties like apartment complexes, office buildings, or shopping centers. The **Income Approach** provides a method for this evaluation.

## What is the Income Approach?

<!-- The **Income Approach** focuses on figuring out what a property's future income might look like and what that means for the property's value today. The **Income Approach** focuses on determining what a property's future income might look like and what that means for the property's value today. This involves predicting how much money a property will make and then working backward to understand what that future income is worth in present terms.

## The Income Approach Principle

<!-- ## Why is the Income Approach Important? -->

<!-- This method hinges on a principle called **Anticipation**, which means the market value of a property reflects the present worth of all the money it's expected to bring in over its useful life. In other words, the earnings a property generates basically determine its worth on the market. -->

This method is based on a principle called **Anticipation**, which means the market value of a property reflects the present worth of all the money it's expected to bring in over its useful life. The earnings a property generates determine its worth on the market.

## When to Use the Income Approach

This approach is especially useful when dealing with properties specifically meant to generate income, such as:

- Industrial parks leasing space to businesses
- Office buildings filled with tenants
- Retail hubs bustling with shoppers

The Income Approach provides a clear process to establish what these properties are worth based on their earning potential.
