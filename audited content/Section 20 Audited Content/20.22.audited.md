# Highest and Best Use in Property Appraisal

<!-- When appraising **residential** and **income-producing properties**, you need to understand **Highest and Best Use**. This principle is important for determining property value. When appraising **residential** and **income-producing properties**, understanding **Highest and Best Use** is necessary for determining property value. It means finding the **legal** and **reasonable use** of a property that, at the time of appraisal, is most likely to yield the **highest net return**.

## Highest and Best Use Principles

<!-- ## Importance of Highest and Best Use -->

- The Highest and Best Use is not about finding just any profitable use for the property.
- It focuses on identifying the specific use that **maximizes the property's current value**.
- This involves assessing:
  - The **physical possibilities** of the property.
  - The **zoning laws** that apply.
  - Whether the potential use is **financially feasible** and **maximally productive**.

## Applying the Principle

<!-- When you understand this principle, you will look at properties not just as they are now, but as they could be used for their **best purpose**. This understanding will enable you to: -->

Understanding this principle means looking at properties not just as they are now, but as they could be used for their **best purpose**. This understanding enables appraisers to:

- Make informed decisions.
- Offer expert advice on property value.
<!-- - Differentiate yourself in the real estate field. -->
- Differentiate themselves in the real estate field.

Example: A vacant lot zoned for commercial use might currently be used as a parking area, but its Highest and Best Use could be developing a retail center if that use yields a higher net return and complies with zoning regulations.
