**RESPA** stands for the *Real Estate Settlement Procedures Act*. This federal law is overseen by the **Consumer Financial Protection Bureau (CFPB)**. RESPA regulates closing procedures. Its core objective is to ensure that lenders provide buyers or sellers with complete details of all **settlement costs**. RESPA also prevents unfair practices by lenders.

## Key Aspects of RESPA

- RESPA does **not** dictate the prices for settlement services.
- It provides regulations about real estate closings and settlement agents.
