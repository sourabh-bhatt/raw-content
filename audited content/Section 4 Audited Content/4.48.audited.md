# Rescission in Real Estate Transactions

<!-- When you're working with contracts, you need to understand **rescission**. -->

**Rescission** is a legal concept in contract law.

## What is Rescission?

Certain laws give buyers the right to cancel some contracts. The **Truth in Lending Act (TILA)** provides a **rescission period** for specific loan transactions. Virginia laws also provide rescission periods for **time-share** and **condominium purchases**.

## The Rescission Period

<!-- During the rescission period, you can cancel the agreement **without any penalty**. Think of it as a *cooling-off period*. This allows you to reconsider your decision. You can back out without facing legal or financial consequences. -->

During the rescission period, buyers can cancel the agreement **without any penalty**. This *cooling-off period* allows reconsideration of the decision without facing legal or financial consequences.

Under federal law, borrowers have **3 business days** to rescind certain loan transactions. This applies to refinancing and home equity loans secured by the primary residence.

In Virginia:
- **Condominium purchases**: 5-day rescission period
- **Time-share purchases**: 7-day rescission period

## Points to Remember

- The rescission period applies to specific transactions governed by federal and Virginia laws.
- It grants the right to cancel contracts without penalty within a set timeframe.
- It protects consumers from rushed financial decisions.
- Different transactions have different rescission periods.
