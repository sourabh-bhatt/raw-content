# 4.66 Disclosure Requirement for Transfer of Residential Real Property

The **transfer of residential real property** with **one to four dwelling units** requires specific **disclosure requirements**. These requirements apply to various transaction types, including:

-   **Sale**
-   **Exchange**
-   **Installment land sales contract**
-   **Lease with an option to buy**

This disclosure requirement applies regardless of whether a **licensed real estate broker** or **salesperson** participates in the transaction. The requirement ensures **transparency** and fulfills legal **obligations** in real estate transactions.
