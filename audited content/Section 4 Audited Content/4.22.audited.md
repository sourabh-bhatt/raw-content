# Understanding Property Condition in Real Estate Contracts

<!-- When you're dealing with **contracts**, it's crucial to understand the concept of **property condition**. -->
The concept of **property condition** affects real estate contracts. 

Most contracts will stipulate that on the **closing date**, the property needs to be in a condition that's *substantially the same* as it was when the contract was first signed by the involved parties. This clause takes into consideration **normal wear and tear**, which is expected over time.
