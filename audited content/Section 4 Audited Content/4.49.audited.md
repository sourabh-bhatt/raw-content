# Preparing Disclosures for Clients in Virginia Real Estate

<!-- When you're preparing **disclosures** for your clients, you must ensure that these disclosures are provided in a **written format**. This is crucial for clarity and **record-keeping**. -->

**Disclosures** for clients must be provided in a **written format** for clarity and **record-keeping**.

## Flexibility Under Virginia Law

Virginia law allows for some flexibility in how these disclosures are presented:

- Real estate professionals can use a **standard format** as outlined by the state.
- Alternatively, disclosures may be included with other disclosures.

## Ensuring Disclosures Are Conspicuous

<!-- The key requirement is to ensure that disclosures are **conspicuous**. To achieve this, consider the following methods: -->

Disclosures must be **conspicuous**. Methods to achieve this include:

- Use a **bold font**
- Use ALL CAPITAL LETTERS
- Underline the text
- Place the disclosure within a box

These techniques make the information clearly visible and understandable, minimizing the potential for oversights or misunderstandings.
