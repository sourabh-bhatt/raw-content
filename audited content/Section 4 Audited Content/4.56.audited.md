# Contract Assignments in Real Estate

<!-- When you're navigating through contracts, it's vital to understand the concept of **assignments**. -->

Contract **assignments** involve the transfer of contractual rights and interests. 

## What is an Assignment?

An **assignment** is the process where one party (known as the **assignor**) transfers their rights, title, or interest in a contract to another party (called the **assignee**). This can cover a broad spectrum of rights, but **not all contracts can be assigned** to somebody else. The capability to assign a contract largely hinges on:

- What the contract is about
- The specific terms agreed upon by the parties involved

## Restrictions on Assignments

- **Personal service contracts** typically do **not** allow assignment because these contracts are highly personal in nature.
- The original parties to the contract have the power to decide if the contract is assignable. They can specify within the contract whether:
  - Assignments are authorized
  - Assignments are absolutely prohibited
  - Permission from one party is required before an assignment can occur

## Example: Mortgage Assignments

In the case of **mortgages**, there are often specific provisions included that explicitly:

- Authorize the assignment of the mortgage to another party
- Prohibit the assignment of the mortgage

Overlooking these provisions can lead to legal and financial complications.
