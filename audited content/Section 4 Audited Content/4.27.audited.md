# Property Descriptions in Purchase Agreements

<!-- When drafting **purchase agreements**, it's crucial to include a clear **description of the property** in question. -->
When drafting **purchase agreements**, include a clear **description of the property** in question. This can be approached in two ways:

## Types of Property Descriptions

- **Legal Description**
  - Provides precise details such as **metes and bounds**.
  - Outlines the property's specific physical **boundaries and dimensions**.
  - <!-- Understanding and articulating these details is vital to avoid ambiguity about the property's limits and size. -->
  These details help avoid ambiguity about the property's limits and size.

- **Non-Legal Description**
  - Typically refers to the property's **address**.
  - Easier to understand for those unfamiliar with property description nuances.
  - Less precise and may not provide all necessary details for certain transactions.

## Accurate Property Descriptions

Incorporating the correct property description method into the purchase agreement:

- Safeguards both parties involved in the transaction.
- Eliminates potential disputes over the exact land being referred to.
- Serves as a cornerstone of any solid agreement.
