# Understanding Rescission in Real Estate Contracts

<!-- When you're learning about contracts, it's essential to understand the concept of **rescission**. Rescission is essentially the **cancellation or termination of a contract**. But it's not just about stopping your future obligations under the contract. -->

**Rescission** is the cancellation or termination of a contract. When a contract is rescinded, it's as though the contract never existed, and both parties are returned to their positions before the contract was formed.

## Ways Rescission Can Occur

- **Mutual Agreement:** Both parties agree to cancel the contract. This agreement to rescind can be a separate agreement or a clause included in the original contract.

Example: If you and the other party realize the deal isn't going to work out as expected, you both may agree to call it off and pretend it never happened.

- **Unilateral Right to Rescind:** One party has the right to rescind the contract alone. This usually happens after a **breach of contract** by the other party or due to some other legal reason that invalidates the contract. The party not at fault may also seek **damages**.

- **Legal Rescission:** The law itself cancels the contract in certain circumstances. This provides another way a contract may be rescinded without direct agreement between the parties.

## Condition for Rescission

Rescission is only possible if both parties can be returned to their **pre-contract positions**. If this isn't feasible, then rescission might not be an option.
