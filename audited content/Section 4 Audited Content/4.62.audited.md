# 4.62 Impact of Death on Contracts

The impact of the death of one of the parties to a contract depends on the type of contract.

*   **Purchase Agreements:** Generally, a contract to buy or sell real estate is **binding on the estate** of the deceased party. If the seller dies, the estate is obligated to sell. If the buyer dies, the estate is obligated to buy.
*   **Personal Service Contracts:** Contracts that require the personal services of a specific individual (e.g., a listing agreement or a buyer representation agreement) usually **terminate** upon the death of that individual (or the broker).
*   **Leases:** Under the Virginia Residential Landlord and Tenant Act, a lease is deemed terminated as of the date of death of a sole tenant, though the estate may remain liable for actual damages.

**Key Distinction:**
*   **Real Estate Contract:** Binding on heirs/estate.
*   **Agency Agreement:** Terminated by death.
