# Understanding Offers in Real Estate Contracts

## What is an Offer?

<!-- When you're learning about contracts, it's essential to grasp the concept of an **offer**. Think of an offer as a promise one party makes to another, stating they're willing to enter into a binding agreement based on specific terms or conditions. -->
An **offer** is a promise one party makes to another, stating they're willing to enter into a binding agreement based on specific terms or conditions. 

Example: Saying, *"I will buy your house for $200,000"* is making an offer.

## From Offer to Contract

<!-- Once you present an offer, it can evolve into a **contract** if the other party **accepts** it. -->
Once presented, an offer can evolve into a **contract** if the other party **accepts** it. However, for that to happen, the offer must be supported by **consideration** (something of value exchanged between the parties) and be free of any **contractual defenses** that could invalidate the agreement.

## Termination of Offers

<!-- Understanding how offers **terminate** is crucial for ensuring effective contract management and avoiding potential legal pitfalls. -->
Offers **terminate** in various ways and don't stay open indefinitely. <!-- Keep this in mind as you navigate through the complexities of contracts in your future endeavors. -->
