# Disclosures and Disclaimers in Contracts

**Disclosures** or **disclaimers** must be clearly communicated to the **buyer** before they make an **acceptance** decision. If this step is overlooked, the buyer retains the right to **terminate the agreement** without penalty by sending a timely **written notice** to the seller.

## Concepts

- **Timing and Transparency**: Making all necessary disclosures upfront adheres to **ethical standards** and maintains the integrity of the **transaction**.
- **Building Trust**: Clear communication builds **trust** and minimizes the risk of **disputes** in contractual agreements.

## Practical Reminder

- Make all disclosures and disclaimers **before the buyer accepts**.
- This practice builds a foundation of trust that benefits all parties involved.
