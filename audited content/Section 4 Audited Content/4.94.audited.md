# 4.94 Understanding Contracts in Real Estate Transactions

**Contracts** for buying or selling property involve a transition from an **offer** to a legally binding agreement.

## From Offer to Purchase Agreement

Example: A property interests a client for purchasing. An offer is submitted, and once both sides - the buyer and seller - sign this document, it transforms into a **purchase agreement**, also referred to as a **sales contract** or **contract for sale**. This document serves multiple functions:

-   Outlines the terms of the sale, including the **price** and the **closing terms**
-   Clearly identifies the property in question
-   Gives the buyer **equitable interest** in the property

## Contract Contingencies

Specific conditions within the contract, known as **contingencies**, must be met before the contract can be fully executed or performed. These contingencies could include:

-   Securing **financing**
-   Completion of a satisfactory **home inspection**
-   Sale of an existing home

These elements provide protection and ensure that all necessary steps are taken before the property changes hands.

Careful navigation of these contracts and full understanding of each clause benefits transaction outcomes.
