# 4.72 Duress in Contract Law

**Duress** occurs when someone pressures another party through physical means or threatening behavior during contract formation. This pressure creates situations where the pressured party feels compelled to agree without genuine choice.

## Legal Effects of Duress

-   **Duress** prevents fair or genuine agreement between parties
-   Contracts signed under such circumstances are **voidable**
-   The person under duress can choose to void the contract

## Meeting of the Minds Requirement

-   A **meeting of the minds** requires genuine consent for legal enforceability
-   Without genuine consent, contracts cannot be enforced

This concept protects parties from legal obligations arising from agreements made without genuine consent.
