# 4.80 Understanding Consideration in Contracts

The principle of **consideration** serves as a core concept in **contracts**.

## What is Consideration?

**Consideration** is essentially what motivates both parties to enter into a contract. It means that for a contract to be valid, each party must agree to either do something or refrain from doing something.

-   This could be as simple as exchanging money for a property.
-   It involves a mutual exchange of promises or actions.

## Legal Requirements

Consideration forms the backbone of any **contractual agreement**. This represents the reciprocal exchange between parties where each gives something to receive something else.

Without consideration, the contract lacks the essential reason for its existence and is therefore not valid.
