# 4.99 Seller Financing: Understanding the Land Contract (Contract for Deed)

In **seller financing**, the **Land Contract**, also known as a **Contract for Deed**, represents one method. In this arrangement, the buyer receives *equitable title* once the deal closes. The seller retains *legal title* to the property until the buyer makes all payments.

## Key Differences Between Land Contract and Mortgage

-   In a **land contract**, the buyer gets *equitable title* right away, but the seller retains *legal title* until full payment.
-   With a **mortgage**, the lender usually transfers legal title to the buyer, with the lender holding a lien.

## Risks and Buyer Protections

-   If the buyer stops making payments on a land contract, they have fewer protections than with a traditional mortgage.
-   The buyer takes on more risk in a land contract agreement.

These differences enable buyers to understand seller financing options.
