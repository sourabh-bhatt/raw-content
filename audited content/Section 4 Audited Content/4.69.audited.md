# 4.69 Understanding Fraud in Real Estate Contracts

## Definition of Fraud

Fraud makes contracts invalid. It occurs when someone uses **deceit** to gain an unfair advantage. The **intention** behind an action determines if it is fraudulent. Not all deceit is fraudulent - **unintentional deceit** falls under **misrepresentation**, not fraud.

## Forms of Fraud in Real Estate Transactions

Fraud manifests in various forms in real estate transactions, including:

-   Knowingly making **false statements** about a property for sale.
-   Deliberately **hiding relevant facts** concerning the property.

## Consequences of Fraud

Fraud consequences include:

-   Courts may award **damages** to the injured party.
-   Contracts becoming **void** or **voidable**. This depends on the type of fraud:
    -   **Fraud in the factum**: Fraud aimed at the nature of the agreement itself.
    -   **Fraud in the inducement**: Fraud aimed at influencing the decision to enter into the contract.

## Legal Framework

These concepts enable identification and handling of fraudulent scenarios, protecting all parties in real estate transactions.
