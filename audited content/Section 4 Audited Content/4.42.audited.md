# Fraud in the Inducement in Real Estate Contracts

<!-- When learning about contracts, it's important to grasp the concept of **Fraud in the Inducement**. -->

**Fraud in the Inducement** involves an *intentional act of deceit* - whether through **false statements** or **hiding information** - pertaining to facts or circumstances surrounding the contract. Someone is misled about the true nature of the deal, and this deceit prompts them to agree to the contract.

## Points about Fraud in the Inducement

- The deceit involves facts or circumstances related to the contract.
- The party is misled into entering the agreement based on false or hidden information.
- As a result, the contract becomes **voidable**, meaning the deceived party has the legal right to cancel the agreement.

## Example

Consider a situation where a seller **misrepresents key information** about what is being sold. This misrepresentation can lead the buyer to enter into a contract under false pretenses.
