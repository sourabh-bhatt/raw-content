# Understanding the Concept of Impossibility in Contracts

<!-- In understanding **contracts**, it's crucial for you to grasp the concept of **impossibility**. **Impossibility** is a defense mechanism that releases a party from fulfilling their obligations under a potentially binding contract due to circumstances that make legal performance unfeasible.

## How Impossibility Works

- When an unforeseen event makes it impossible to perform contractual duties, the affected party may be excused from those duties.
- This principle protects parties from being held liable when performance is literally impossible.

## Example

Example: Take a situation where a buyer is expected to close on a home. If that home were to be completely destroyed by fire before the deal finalizes, it would be literally impossible for the buyer to proceed with the purchase as initially agreed. In such scenarios, the principle of **impossibility** comes into play, shielding the buyer from any legal obligation to carry out the terms of the contract that have become unachievable.
