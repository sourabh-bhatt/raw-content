# 4.86 Understanding Contract Timelines and "Time Is of the Essence" in Real Estate

**Contract timelines** involve how deadlines are typically interpreted and enforced in real estate transactions.

## Reasonable Time for Contract Deadlines

-   Generally, deadlines within contracts suggest a **"reasonable time"** for fulfillment.
-   These deadlines are not meant to be ultra-strict or exact to the minute.
-   For example, if a contract states that a transaction should close on **July 15**, it means the closing is expected around that date or within a reasonable timeframe afterward—not necessarily exactly on July 15.

## Precise Timing Requirements

-   In real estate, **precise timing** may be required for certain deadlines.
-   To ensure strict adherence to specific dates, parties include a **"time is of the essence"** provision in the contract.

### What Does "Time Is of the Essence" Mean?

-   This phrase signals a clear agreement that the specified deadlines **must be met exactly as laid out**.
-   Missing these deadlines, even by a small margin, can be considered a **breach of contract**.

### Example Scenario

Example: A contract sets the closing date for **November 3, 2009, at 12:00 pm**, and explicitly states **"time is of the essence."** If either party arrives at the closing **five minutes late (12:05 pm)**, this constitutes a breach of the contract. The inclusion of **"time is of the essence"** turns every second into a potential deal-breaker or deal-maker.

The **"time is of the essence"** concept ensures transactions proceed smoothly and as expected, avoiding surprises or misunderstandings related to timing.
