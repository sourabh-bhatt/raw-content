# 4.90 Understanding Acceptance of Offers in Real Estate

**Acceptance** of **offers** can be made through various methods.

## Methods of Acceptance

-   If there isn't a particular method of acceptance outlined in the offer, it can be accepted **verbally or in writing**.
-   However, in Virginia real estate transactions, **written acceptance** is required for the contract to be **enforceable** under the Statute of Frauds.
-   While verbal acceptance may create a contract, only written acceptance ensures the agreement can be enforced in court.

## Legal Requirements for Acceptance

-   This knowledge guides interactions and communications during **negotiations**.
-   Understanding legal acceptance methods enables effective management of offers and responses.
-   **Written acceptance** ensures enforceability under Virginia law.
-   Proper **contractual procedures** require adherence to these acceptance requirements.
