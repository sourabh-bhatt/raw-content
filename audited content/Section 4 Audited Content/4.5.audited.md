# Understanding Rejection in Real Estate Contracts

<!-- When learning about **contracts**, it's crucial to understand the concept of **rejection**. When an **offer** doesn't meet needs or expectations, the recipient can terminate this offer. This act of terminating the offer is known as **rejection**. An offer can be rejected in two ways:

## Ways to Reject an Offer

1. **Direct Rejection**: This is where the **offeree** explicitly communicates to the **offeror** (the person who made the offer) that they are declining their offer. This makes the intention clear and leaves no room for misunderstanding.

2. **Counteroffer**: This might seem like accepting the offer, but in reality, it's a form of rejection. By making a **counteroffer**, the offeree is essentially saying that the original offer isn't acceptable, but they're still interested in negotiating. A counteroffer cancels the original offer and puts a new proposal on the table.
