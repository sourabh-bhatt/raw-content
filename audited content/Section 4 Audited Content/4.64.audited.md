# 4.64 Counteroffers

A **counteroffer** is a rejection of the original offer and the creation of a new offer.

*   **Rejection:** When a seller (or buyer) changes any term of an offer, they are effectively rejecting the original offer. The original offer is dead and cannot be accepted later unless revived by the offeror.
*   **New Offer:** The counteroffer becomes a new offer that the other party can accept, reject, or counter.
*   **Role of Agent:** Real estate agents must present all written counteroffers to their clients promptly.
*   **Mirror Image Rule:** For a contract to be formed, the acceptance must match the offer exactly (Mirror Image Rule). Any deviation constitutes a counteroffer.

**Process:**
1.  Buyer makes offer.
2.  Seller changes price and initials the change (Counteroffer).
3.  Buyer accepts the new price or counters again.
