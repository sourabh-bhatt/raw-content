# Understanding Counteroffers in Real Estate Negotiations

<!-- When you receive an **offer** and decide not to accept it as it stands, but rather propose something different, you are essentially making a **counteroffer**. -->
When a party receives an **offer** and decides not to accept it as it stands, but rather proposes something different, they are essentially making a **counteroffer**. 

## What is a Counteroffer?

- A counteroffer is a new proposition that voids any previous offer made.
- <!-- For example, if as a **seller**, you respond to the initial offer with a different one, what you're presenting is referred to as a counteroffer. -->
Example: If as a **seller**, a party responds to the initial offer with a different one, what they're presenting is referred to as a counteroffer.
