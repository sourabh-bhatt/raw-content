# 4.100 Understanding the Parties in a Purchase Agreement

A **purchase agreement** involves two main parties: **the buyer** and the person selling the property (**the seller**). In more formal terms, these roles are often referred to as the **vendee** and the **vendor**, respectively.

-   The **vendee** is the party showing an interest in acquiring the property.
-   The **vendor** is the individual or entity looking to sell the property.

These roles govern the structure of **real estate transactions**.
