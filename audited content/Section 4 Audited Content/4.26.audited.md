# Understanding Contracts in Real Estate

<!-- As you dive into the concept of **contracts**, it's essential to grasp that a contract is a **legally binding agreement** reached between people who have **legal capacity**. -->
A contract is a **legally binding agreement** reached between people who have **legal capacity**. These people agree to either do something or refrain from doing specific designated actions. A legally binding contract requires both parties to understand and agree on the same thing—often referred to as the **"meeting of the minds"**—between the parties involved.

In real estate, contracts form the backbone of almost every transaction. Real estate includes a variety of contracts:

- **Listing contracts**
- **Sales contracts**
- **Option contracts**
- **Leasing contracts**
- **Development contracts**
- **Installment sales contracts**
- **Financing contracts**
