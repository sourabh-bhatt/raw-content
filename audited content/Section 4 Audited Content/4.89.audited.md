# 4.89 Bilateral Contracts in Real Estate

**Bilateral contracts** are agreements where *both sides make promises to each other*.

## Understanding Bilateral Contracts

In real estate, **real estate sales contracts** demonstrate this concept:

-   The **buyer** promises to purchase the property.
-   The **seller** promises to sell the property to the buyer.

This *mutual exchange of promises* makes the contract **bilateral**, with both parties committing to specific actions or obligations.

These contracts provide a clear framework of expectations for each party involved in the transaction.
