# Statute of Frauds in Real Estate Transactions

<!-- When you're diving into real estate transactions, one crucial legal principle you need to be familiar with is the **statute of frauds**. -->
The **statute of frauds** is a legal principle that applies to real estate transactions. 

## Key Principle

This law requires that any contract for the **sale or transfer of land**, or any **lease of land for more than one year**, must be:

- Documented in writing
- Signed by the parties involved

in order to be legally enforceable.

## Statute of Frauds Requirements

Even if an oral agreement regarding land sale seems to have all the necessary components to form a valid contract, courts will **not recognize it** unless it meets the written requirement set by the statute of frauds. 

This requirement ensures:

- Clarity in real estate transactions
- Reduction of misunderstandings
- Prevention of fraudulent claims about the terms of the sale
