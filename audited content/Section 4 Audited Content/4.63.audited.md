# 4.63 Purchase Agreement

The **Purchase Agreement** (also known as the Contract of Sale, Sales Contract, or Offer to Purchase) is the most important document in a real estate transaction. It sets the terms and conditions of the sale.

*   **Bilateral Contract:** It is a bilateral contract because both parties (buyer and seller) make promises to each other (buyer promises to pay, seller promises to transfer title).
*   **Executory Contract:** Once signed by both parties, it becomes an **executory contract**, meaning the parties have duties to fulfill before closing (e.g., securing financing, inspections).
*   **Key Terms:**
    *   Price and terms of sale.
    *   Property description.
    *   Closing date.
    *   Conditions and contingencies.
    *   Type of deed to be conveyed.

**Note:** The purchase agreement does not transfer title; the deed does that at closing.
