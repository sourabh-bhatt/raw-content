# 4.98 Contract Expiration and Termination in Real Estate Agreements

The concept of **expiration** applies to **contracts**. Virginia has specific laws about contract termination.

## Termination Date Requirements

-   In certain cases, Virginia law requires that a specific **termination date** be explicitly stated within the contract itself.
-   This means the contract must outline a clear end date, after which the agreement is no longer valid.

## Contracts Without a Specified End Date

-   When a contract does **not** specify an end date, Virginia law imposes what is known as a **"reasonable period of time"** for the agreement's duration.
-   The term *reasonable* means the duration should be sensible and justifiable, considering the nature of the agreement and the intentions of the parties involved.

## Legal Framework for Contract Duration

-   The duration of a contract impacts the parties' **responsibilities, rights, and obligations**.
-   Contracts that follow Virginia law regarding expiration safeguard party interests and protect the **legal validity** of the agreement.

Proper consideration of the **expiration clause** ensures compliance with Virginia real estate law. This knowledge enables the creation of effective and legally sound agreements.
