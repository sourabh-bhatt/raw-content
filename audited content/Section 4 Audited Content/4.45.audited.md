# The Contracting Process in Real Estate

<!-- When you start the **contracting process**, you begin by making an **offer**. It's then up to the **seller** to decide whether to **accept** your offer and enter into a **contract**, also known as a **purchase agreement**, with you.

Initially, when the purchase agreement is signed only by you, it stands merely as an **offer to purchase**. This initial step is crucial because it sets the stage for **negotiations** and the eventual formation of a **legally binding agreement** if the seller agrees to your terms. -->

The **contracting process** begins by making an **offer**. The **seller** decides whether to **accept** the offer and enter into a **contract**, also known as a **purchase agreement**.

When the purchase agreement is signed only by the buyer, it stands as an **offer to purchase**. This step sets the stage for **negotiations** and the eventual formation of a **legally binding agreement** if the seller agrees to the terms.
