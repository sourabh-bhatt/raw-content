# 4.75 Understanding Gift Promises and Consideration in Contracts

**Gift promises** are typically unenforceable due to the lack of consideration in contracts.

## What Are Gift Promises?

A gift promise occurs when someone promises to give another party something as a gift with nothing expected in return. From a contractual standpoint, this lacks a key element - **consideration**.

## The Role of Consideration

**Consideration** is what makes a contract legally binding. It involves something of value that is exchanged between the parties. This could be:

-   Money
-   Services
-   An agreement to perform (or not to perform) a particular action

Essentially, it is a reciprocal promise or a legal obligation that binds both sides.

## Why Gift Promises Are Unenforceable

-   Without **consideration** - without that exchange of something valuable - there is no legal foothold to enforce the promise.
-   A promise to give a gift, without expecting anything in return, cannot be enforced by law.

## Real Estate Applications

Every agreement in **real estate** must embody a clear exchange to be legally recognized and upheld.
