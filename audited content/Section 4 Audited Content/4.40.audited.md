# Understanding Acceptance in Real Estate Contracts

<!-- When you're delving into contracts, one key concept you must grasp is **acceptance**. Think of acceptance as officially expressing your desire to enter into a contract that aligns with a particular **offer**. -->

**Acceptance** is officially expressing the desire to enter into a contract that aligns with a particular **offer**.

## Points About Acceptance

- An **offer** needs to be accepted *exactly* as it is laid out.
- If the offer does not specify a particular **method of acceptance**, there is flexibility.
- Acceptance can be made in any reasonable manner, such as:
  - Confirming agreement in a face-to-face conversation.
  - Signing written documents, like a **real estate purchase agreement**.
