# 4.83 Understanding Mutual Agreement and Rescission in Contracts

The concept of **mutual agreement** applies particularly in the context of **rescission** in **contracts**.

## What is Rescission?

**Rescission** is a process where both parties involved agree to cancel the contract without any breach occurring. When both parties decide that canceling the contract is the best course of action, both must show a clear intent to do so.

## How Rescission Occurs

-   Both parties must mutually agree to cancel the contract.
-   The cancellation must be consensual and legal.
-   Alternatively, a **court order** can also result in the cancellation of the contract, but this usually happens under different circumstances.

Mutual agreement for rescission involves both parties coming together to nullify the contract by agreement, ensuring that the cancellation is consensual and legal.
