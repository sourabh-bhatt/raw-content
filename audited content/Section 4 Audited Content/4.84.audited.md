# 4.84 Understanding Offers with Deadlines

An **offer** with a **deadline** will automatically **terminate** if not accepted before the specified time. Setting this deadline provides a strategy to manage control over the offer once it has been made.

This deadline creates a timer on the decision-making process, establishing urgency for **timely responses** in **negotiation** scenarios.

Understanding deadline mechanics enables effective offer handling and prevents missed opportunities due to overlooked timing requirements.
