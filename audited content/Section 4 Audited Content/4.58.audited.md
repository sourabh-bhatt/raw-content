# Breach of Contract and Remedies

<!-- When delving into **contracts**, it's essential to understand the provision that clearly outlines your rights and obligations should one party not fulfill their part of the agreement. -->

Contract provisions outline rights and obligations when one party does not fulfill their part of the agreement. This segment identifies what actions or inactions are considered a **breach of contract** - in other words, a **default**.

## Concepts

- **Breach of Contract (Default):** Occurs when a party fails to perform their contractual duties as agreed.
- **Remedies Available:** 
  - **Specific Performance:** Enforcing the contract's completion as originally agreed.
  - **Damages:** Seeking compensation for losses incurred due to the breach.
