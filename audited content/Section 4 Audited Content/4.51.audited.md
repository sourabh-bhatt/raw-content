# Risk of Loss Clause in Real Estate Contracts

<!-- In learning about contracts, it's important for you to understand the **"Risk of Loss" clause**. -->

The **"Risk of Loss" clause** is a contract provision that allocates responsibility for property damage. 

## What is the Risk of Loss Clause?

This clause typically states that the **seller** is responsible for bearing any risk of loss due to events like **fire** or other unexpected incidents that happen during the **executory period** of the contract.

- The executory period refers to the time after the contract has been signed but before the ownership has officially changed hands.

## Implications of the Clause

- If something happens to the property during this period, the seller is still liable for those damages.
- This protects the buyer from being responsible for any unforeseen damages that occur before officially taking over the property.
