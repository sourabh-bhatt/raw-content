# Shared Appreciation Mortgage (SAM)

A **Shared Appreciation Mortgage (SAM)** is a financing option for potential homeowners who may not qualify for traditional loans.

## What is a Shared Appreciation Mortgage?

A SAM is a type of lending arrangement where the lender provides a loan at **interest rates below the current market level**. In return, the lender receives a **share of the profits** when the financed real estate is sold in the future.

## Key Features of a SAM

- The lender offers a loan with **below-market interest rates**.
- The lender's compensation comes from a **portion of the property's future sale profits**.
- The terms must be clearly outlined in the **mortgage or Deed of Trust** to prevent misunderstandings.

## Benefits of a SAM

For borrowers who may be denied traditional mortgages due to financial history or income constraints, a SAM can provide:

- Access to the property market despite qualifying challenges.
- An alternative financing option that balances lower interest costs with shared future profits.

SAMs provide an alternative financing solution for borrowers with qualifying challenges.
