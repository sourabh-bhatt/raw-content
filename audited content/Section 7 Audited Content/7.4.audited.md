# Jumbo Loans: Non-Conforming Mortgage Loans

A **Jumbo Loan** exceeds the conforming loan limits set by the **Federal Housing Finance Agency (FHFA)**. This type of loan doesn't conform to usual standards, making it a non-conforming loan.

## Conforming Loan Limits

- The FHFA sets conforming loan limits each year
- In most areas, the 2024 limit is $766,550 for a single-family home
- High-cost areas have higher limits, up to $1,149,825
- Virginia has both standard and high-cost area limits depending on the county

## Jumbo Loans and Risk

- Not all Jumbo Loans are risky or subprime
- Some Jumbo Loans may fall into the subprime category due to less favorable terms and risky lending
- However, many Jumbo Loans come with appealing terms and are not inherently risky

## Qualification Criteria for Jumbo Loans

To qualify for a Jumbo Loan, borrowers must meet specific criteria:

1. **Excellent Credit**: Borrowers need excellent credit scores, typically 700 or higher
2. **Solid Income**: Borrowers must have enough income to comfortably manage loan payments
3. **Low Debt-to-Income Ratio**: Usually 43% or lower
4. **Substantial Down Payment**: Often 10-20% or more
5. **Cash Reserves**: Lenders may require 2-6 months of mortgage payments in reserves

Lenders require assurance that providing such a loan amount does not pose undue risk.

## Considerations

- Jumbo Loans are relevant for borrowers interested in properties that exceed typical market offerings
- Borrowers must have the financial backbone to support these larger loans
- Interest rates may be slightly higher or lower than conforming loans depending on market conditions

Jumbo Loans apply to high-value property transactions that exceed conforming loan limits.
