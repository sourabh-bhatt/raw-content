# Buydown Mortgages: Reducing Monthly Payments During High Interest Rates

A **buydown mortgage** reduces monthly payments during periods of high interest rates.

## What is a Buydown Mortgage?

This approach involves prepaying a part of the interest rate to decrease monthly payments during the early years of the loan.

## How Does It Work?

- When interest rates are high, a buydown mortgage provides financial relief.
- **Builders** may adopt this strategy to help potential buyers qualify for loans during high interest rate periods.

## Considerations

- The relief provided by a buydown mortgage is **temporary**.
- Once the specified period for the lowered interest ends, the rate will climb back up to its original figure.

This strategy provides borrowers time to adjust before facing the full mortgage interest rate.
