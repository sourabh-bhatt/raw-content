# Open-End Mortgages

## What is an Open-End Mortgage?

An **open-end mortgage** is a loan secured by **real property** where the **lender** specifies the **maximum amount** that the **borrower** can borrow. This type of mortgage offers flexibility by allowing the borrower to draw funds up to this maximum limit at various times as needed.

## Key Advantages

- Borrowers only pay **interest** on the actual amount borrowed at any given time, not on the maximum loan limit.
- This makes it an attractive option for financing projects that require incremental funding.

## Practical Example

Example: A builder financing a construction project:

- The builder needs funds to complete different phases of the project.
- With an open-end mortgage, the builder can borrow money incrementally, aligned with the progress of the construction.
- This approach is efficient and cost-effective because interest is charged only on the funds actually used.

## How It Works

- Functions similarly to an **open line of credit**.
- Offers flexibility in **fund utilization**.
- Keeps interest payments tied directly to the borrowed amount rather than the maximum loan limit.
