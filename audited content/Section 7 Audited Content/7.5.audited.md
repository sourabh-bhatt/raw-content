# Purchase Money Mortgage

A **Purchase Money Mortgage** is an agreement that happens directly between a **seller** and a **buyer**.

## Key Features of Purchase Money Mortgages

- The buyer can either combine this mortgage with an **institutional mortgage** or choose not to.
- This flexibility allows for structuring deals that can be customized to the specific financing needs of the parties involved.

**Purchase Money Mortgages** provide flexibility in real estate financing arrangements.
