# Renegotiable Rate Mortgage: An Adjustable Rate Mortgage (ARM)

A **renegotiable rate mortgage** is a type of **adjustable rate mortgage (ARM)** that is a long-term loan offering **short-term renewal periods**. Typically, these periods range between **3 to 5 years**, during which the **interest rates** are subject to renegotiation.

## How It Works

- At each adjustment period over the life of the loan, **payments are recalculated or amortized** based on the renegotiated interest rate.
- Despite these adjustments, the **total amount of the mortgage remains unchanged**.

This setup provides a blend of **predictability and flexibility** depending on market interest rate trends and borrower financial strategy.
