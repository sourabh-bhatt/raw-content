# Fixed-Rate Mortgage

A **Fixed-Rate Mortgage** features an **interest rate** that remains unchanged throughout the duration of the **loan term**. Typically, these terms can be for 10, 15, or even 30 years.

With a **Fixed-Rate Mortgage**, **monthly payments** toward the **principal** and **interest** do not increase over time, providing **financial stability** and predictability.
