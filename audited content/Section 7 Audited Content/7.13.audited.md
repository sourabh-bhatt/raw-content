# Negative Amortization in Mortgages

**Negative amortization** occurs in a lending agreement where payments vary and are not enough to cover all the **interest charges** that accrue, instead of making equal payments throughout the life of the loan.

## How Negative Amortization Works

- Payments only cover a part of the interest owed.
- No portion of the payment goes towards reducing the **loan principal**.
- The unpaid interest is added to the loan's principal balance.

This means that, instead of the loan balance decreasing over time - as expected with a **fully amortized loan** - it actually increases.

## Key Implications

- Borrowers pay lower monthly amounts in the short term.
- The total amount owed grows over the loan term, despite making regular payments.
- This impacts how the **loan balance** changes over time in any **mortgage agreement**.
